# Expedition Checklist

## Before departure

- What is my primary objective?
- Where is the destination?
- What movement type is legal?
- What route/site path will I expose?
- Who is the critical character?
- What is my recovery plan?
- What is the follow-up objective?

## During the expedition

- Has the objective changed?
- Has the company changed?
- Is the next site still worth visiting?
- What future value am I spending?
- Has the route created movement debt?
- Am I still inside my deadline zone?

## Before recovery

> Can I safely visit one more productive site?

Recover because continuing no longer advances the mission—not merely because the company is uncomfortable.
