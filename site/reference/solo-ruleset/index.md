# Which Solo Rules Are We Using?

This page is the rules contract for the Academy. It separates three layers that must not be mixed silently:

1. **Normal MECCG rules** — movement legality, site paths, Hazard Limits, card text, timing, attacks and site encounters.
2. **The historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C** — the source of the 30-card Hero Resource and 30-card Hazard teaching lists.
3. **The Academy's solo teaching procedure** — the solitaire adjustments and the Book of Mazarbul mission used for the first expedition.

The historical Challenge Deck was not designed specifically for this exact teaching scenario.

## Rules used for the first Academy game

| Topic | Academy choice |
|---|---|
| Hero play deck | Shuffle **36 cards**: the fixed 30-card Resource list plus the six character/Wizard cards listed on the Hero Deck page. No Hazards go in this deck. |
| Hazard deck | Use the separate fixed 30-card teaching list; shuffle it. |
| Location deck | Keep the 15 site cards separate. Do not shuffle them as a play deck. |
| Starting pool / setup | Use the five starting Dwarves and two Cram items specified by Challenge Deck C. The six character/Wizard cards are in the Hero play deck, not this starting group. Gandalf is the Academy's Wizard avatar; he is not one of the five starting Dwarves. Pallando ×3 belongs to the historical Challenge Deck C reference, not the active Gandalf-led deck. |
| Hand size | Use the standard solitaire hand size of **5** when the hand is reset. Do not use the normal multiplayer hand size of 8 for this teaching setup. |
| Movement and card play | Follow the normal MECCG rules and current card text. |
| Hazard draw | Use the solitaire formula below, not the normal multiplayer draw alone. |
| Hazard Limit | Follow the normal rules. The solo draw does not increase the limit. |
| Unplayed drawn Hazards | Discard them at the end of the Movement/Hazard Phase. |
| Hazard deck exhausted | Shuffle its discard pile to form a new Hazard deck when required to continue drawing. |
| Defeated Hazard creatures | This first mission is not a kill-MP challenge. Follow normal rules for unique creatures; put defeated non-unique creatures in the Hazard discard so they can return when the Hazard deck is recycled. Do not count kills as progress toward the Book mission. |
| First-game objective | Complete the teaching expedition to acquire Book of Mazarbul at Moria, then record the arrival and recovery decision. This is a tutorial mission, not a claim that the full generic solo campaign victory condition has been played. |

If a card's printed wording or a current ruling differs from a shorthand explanation on an Academy page, use the card and rules.

## Why the active Hero play deck contains 36 cards

The historical Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C list contains 30 Resources plus eight character/Wizard cards, including Pallando ×3. The Academy keeps the locked 30 Resource list and the five non-Wizard characters from that pool, but uses **one Gandalf Wizard card** instead of the historical Pallando package. The active Hero play deck is therefore **36 cards**. This is legal because the solo procedure requires a play deck of at least 30 cards; it does not require the historical 38-card count.

## The solo Hazard draw

The Academy adopts the established MECCG solitaire draw procedure described in the solo rules: draw **2 + twice the number of Hazard cards normally drawn for the relevant site**.

For the Game 1 journey to Moria, the normal Hazard draw number is **3**, so:

**2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards.**

The number used for the normal draw depends on the standard rules for that movement. For example, when moving to a non-Haven site, the draw is normally based on the new site; when moving to a Haven, the normal rules use the site of origin. Apply the normal rule first, then double that number and add two for the solo draw.

**Twilight exception:** the Challenge Deck C Hazard list contains two Twilight cards. Twilight can be played at any point during either player's turn, cancels one Environment card in play or declared earlier in the same chain of effects, and does **not** count against the Hazard Limit. It is otherwise a Hazard, and it can also be played as a Resource. It still needs a legal Environment target.

### Draw is not the Hazard Limit

The base Hazard Limit is the company size or two, whichever is greater; **Hobbits and Orc scouts each count as half a character, rounded up**. A five-character company containing no Hobbits or Orc scouts therefore has a base Hazard Limit of **5** for its Movement/Hazard Phase, unless an applicable rule or effect changes it. The limit is fixed for that phase.

- **8 cards drawn:** the cards available for the solo Hazard side to consider under this procedure.
- **5 Hazard Limit:** the normal maximum number of Hazard plays that count against the limit for this company and phase.

Drawing eight cards never permits eight ordinary Hazard plays against a five-character company.

## How to play the drawn Hazards fairly

The solitaire instruction is to play **as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within the company's Hazard Limit**, choosing the combination most disadvantageous to the Hero company.

1. Inspect the full drawn hand.
2. Check every card's key, target, printed conditions and timing.
3. Reject cards that are illegal in the actual state; never invent legality to fill a slot.
4. Compare the impact of the remaining legal options, including valid sequencing or combinations.
5. Play as many as the procedure permits within the Hazard Limit.
6. Discard the drawn cards that were not played at the end of the phase.

Do not automatically play the first five cards. Do not leave a useful legal slot empty just to make the Hero turn easier. Equally, do not invent extra attacks, ignore a restriction, or assume two effects combine unless their text and the relevant rulings allow it.

For the step-by-step method, use the [Hazard Player guide]({{ '/reference/hazard-player/' | relative_url }}) and the [Hazard Combination Practice Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-combo-lab/' | relative_url }}).

## Multiple companies — one Hazard phase per company

The solitaire instruction applies separately to **each company**. If you move two companies in the same turn, resolve two Movement/Hazard Phases in the order you choose. Calculate each solo Hazard draw from that company's own movement: if its destination is a Haven, normal rules use the site of origin's Hazard draw number. Each company has its own Hazard Limit. Do not pool the drawn cards or combine the limits.

Example from the Academy's Gandalf-led support-company plan:

- Five Dwarves move Rivendell → Moria: Moria's normal Hazard draw is 3, so draw 8; base Hazard Limit 5.
- Gandalf moves Grey Havens → Lórien: Lórien is a Haven, so use Grey Havens' normal Hazard draw of 2; draw 6; one-character company base Hazard Limit 2.
- Total: 14 cards drawn across two phases, with separate limits of 5 and 2.

Gandalf's +1 corruption-check modifier is not a hand-size effect. The Hero hand target remains five.

See [Gandalf and the Support Company]({{ '/decks/gandalf-support-company/' | relative_url }}) for the worked example.

## Movement rules remain normal

The Academy does not change the normal rules for:

- legal movement types and region adjacency;
- the four-region limit for normal Region Movement, including origin and destination;
- the prohibition on repeating a region in the declared Region Movement path;
- when movement is declared and when the site path is declared;
- creature-Hazard keying, legal targets and the Hazard Limit;
- card text, timing, combat and site resolution.

The [Hazard Legality Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/' | relative_url }}) provides a worked hand to practise keying and event timing. The [Site-Keying Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/site-keying-workshop/' | relative_url }}) teaches the case-sensitive difference between region and site keys. The [Free / Player-Directed Movement guide]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) explains the important distinction between the route used to move and the site path used for Hazard keying. The [Expedition Planner]({{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}) makes you record both before play.

## What this page does not claim

*The Road Goes Ever On*, second edition (April 2024), is a separate solo system with its own decks, components and procedures. The Academy does not import its rules piecemeal.

Likewise, this 30-Resource / 30-Hazard teaching setup does not automatically use the generic solitaire game's full victory condition. **Game 1 is a mission-based tutorial** centred on Book of Mazarbul. Before using the cards for a longer campaign, write down the campaign's victory condition, game-end condition and any additional scenario rules. Do not silently switch between a mission-based game and an MP race.
