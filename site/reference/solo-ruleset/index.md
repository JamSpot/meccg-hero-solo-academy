# Which Solo Rules Are We Using?

The Academy must not blur official two-player rules, historical Challenge Deck construction, and a separate fan-made solitaire system into one supposedly official ruleset.

## The Academy's teaching baseline

For the first lessons, the Academy uses:

- the historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C as the card-list baseline;
- the listed 30 Hero Resource cards and 30 Hazard cards;
- the normal MECCG rules for movement legality, site paths, Hazard Limits and card play;
- one explicitly identified solitaire draw procedure to give the solo Hazard player a workable hand.

This is a **teaching hybrid**. It is not a claim that the historical Challenge Deck was originally designed for this exact solitaire procedure.

## Solo Hazard draw used in Game 1

The Academy starter procedure adopts the draw rule in Simon Cogan's 2019 *Solo Variant Rules*:

**2 + (2 × the number printed on the destination Site card)**

For Moria, whose site number is 3, that is **8 Hazard cards**.

The normal two-player rules instead tell the Hazard player how many cards they may draw based on the site's darker box. Do not confuse that normal draw with the solitaire procedure above.

## Why not silently switch to a different solo system?

*The Road Goes Ever On*, second edition (April 2024), is a distinct solitaire system. Its setup includes a 40-card Player deck, a 40-card Hazard deck, a separate Long Event deck and a Quest deck. Its Hazard procedure also differs, using a face-down Hazard pile based on a different formula and additional rules involving Shadow Tokens.

That is a valid alternative, but it is not the system this 30-card teaching list currently implements. Importing isolated procedures from it without rebuilding the setup would make the guide inconsistent.

## What the official rules still control

The Academy does not override the official rules for:

- legal movement types and adjacency;
- the four-region limit for normal Region Movement;
- site-path declaration and creature-Hazard keying;
- the base Hazard Limit;
- card text, timing and legal targets;
- site and combat resolution.

The Council of Elrond rules explain that Region Movement includes the origin and destination regions, forbids repeated regions, and uses a declared site path to determine which creature Hazards can be keyed. The base Hazard Limit is set at the beginning of the company's Movement/Hazard Phase and remains fixed for that phase.

## Future decision

Before the Academy is labelled a complete campaign ruleset, it needs a single consolidated checklist covering hand size, reshuffling, character-target selection, site encounters, game end, victory conditions and all other solo-only procedures. Until then, this page records the current teaching choices openly.
