# Hazard Player

**Your job is not to kill characters. Make the Hero expedition less successful.**

Ask: *What can I do right now that causes the greatest damage to the company's ability to complete its objective?*

## Seven-step procedure

### 1 — CAN I PLAY IT?
Check site path, region, site, company and printed requirements.

### 2 — WHAT ARE THEY TRYING TO DO?
Identify the expedition objective.

### 3 — WHO MATTERS?
Identify the **critical character** whose disruption most threatens the expedition.

### 4 — WHAT HURTS MOST?
Compare legal Hazards by expedition disruption.

### 5 — WHO SHOULD BE HIT?
Ask who is easiest to hurt, most important, already damaged, needed immediately, and what happens if they are wounded.

### 6 — WHAT SHOULD COME NEXT?
Find the second Hazard that becomes stronger because of the first.

### 7 — IS IT WORTH THE LIMIT?
Do not assume the Hazard Limit should be filled. Stop when another card adds little meaningful disruption.

## Three questions

**WHO?** Who matters most?

**WHY?** Why does hurting them damage the expedition?

**WHAT NEXT?** Which Hazard becomes stronger after this one?

**Legal decision → Strategic decision → Rules resolution**
