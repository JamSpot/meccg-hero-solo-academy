# Expedition Planner — Table Worksheet

Use this page before every expedition. It turns the Hero player's plan and the solo Hazard player's response into one auditable sequence. You can use it with the physical region cards and the Academy's teaching decks; no proxies or extra cards are required.

The Academy uses **Region Movement** as the formal term. “Free movement” is a convenient teaching phrase, not permission to ignore adjacency, the region limit or the site-path rules.

## The one-minute Captain's check

Fill this in before declaring movement.

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **Current site and region** | |
| **Mission** — what concrete thing will this trip achieve? | |
| **Company condition** — size, tapped characters, wounds, key cards spent | |
| **Destination and destination region** | |
| **Movement type** — Starter, Region, or a specific special effect | |
| **Planned region sequence** — include origin and destination | |
| **Region count** — are there four or fewer, counting both endpoints? | |
| **Adjacency** — did I check each pair on the region cards? | |
| **Repeated region** — does any region appear twice? | |
| **Likely Hazard exposure** — which types/names can be keyed? | |
| **Site danger** — is there an automatic attack or other entry requirement? | |
| **Plan after arrival** — enter, recover, or reassess? | |
| **Next position** — where could the company go after this? | |

**Do not move on until the route is legal.** A promising destination cannot rescue an illegal route.

## Four separate decisions

Keep these steps distinct:

1. **Organization Phase:** choose the destination and declare the movement. A Region Movement route must already be capable of being legal.
2. **Movement/Hazard Phase — reveal:** reveal the new site.
3. **Site path:** for Region Movement, declare the actual region sequence. This sequence determines region types and names used for creature-Hazard keying; it does not itself move the company.
4. **Arrival and site entry:** resolve movement and Hazards, inspect the company's actual condition, and then decide whether to enter the site when the rules allow that decision.

If the movement is illegal when the new site is revealed, it is negated; the company remains at its origin but still conducts its Movement/Hazard Phase. See the [official rules for Movement/Hazard Phase]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) and the [Council of Elrond rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/).

## Worked plan A — Rivendell to Moria

**Mission:** acquire Book of Mazarbul.  
**Origin:** Rivendell, Rhudaur.  
**Destination:** Moria, Redhorn Gate.  
**Teaching Region Movement route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.

### Legality

- Three regions, counting both endpoints.
- No repeated region.
- Check every adjacency on the physical region cards.
- Within the normal four-region limit.

### Exposure and preparation

All three regions on this teaching route are Wilderness. A creature requiring two Wilderness regions may therefore be a legal option for the Hazard player, **if its printed keying and every other requirement are satisfied**. This is not a prediction that a particular creature will be drawn.

Under the Academy's chosen solo procedure, the Moria site number of 3 produces a draw of **2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards**. The five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of **5** for its Movement/Hazard Phase. These are different numbers: eight cards are available to consider, but the normal limit still controls how many Hazards may be played.

Before the company enters Moria, check its wounds and taps, the defensive cards still available, and Moria's automatic attack. Reaching the site does not guarantee that attempting the objective is wise.

**Debrief question:** if the company arrives too weak to attempt the Book, what did the route gain, and what did it cost? Record a concrete lesson rather than assuming the route was good or bad just because the company survived.

## Worked plan B — Moria to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

**Mission after the Book:** reassess and pursue a useful next Dwarf objective if the company is fit to continue.  
**Origin region:** Redhorn Gate.  
**Destination region:** Númeriador.

The direct sequence **Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador** contains five regions. It is not legal as one normal Region Movement.

A verified teaching alternative is two legs:

1. **Moria → Rivendell:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur.
2. **Rivendell → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold:** Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Check adjacency against the region cards before declaring either leg. Rivendell offers a Haven stop and a chance to consolidate, but it is an example—not a rule that every expedition must return to a Haven. If another intermediate site is available, compare its legality, value, exposure and recovery options.

**Debrief question:** is the extra stop wasted movement, or does it improve the company's condition and preserve a legal route? Answer from the actual state of your game.

## Worked plan C — the company arrives in poor condition

Imagine the company reaches its destination with its best fighter wounded, two characters tapped, and its main avoidance resource already spent.

Do not enter automatically. Check in this order:

1. Does the site have an automatic attack or other requirement?
2. Which characters can still contribute, and what is the likely cost of the attack?
3. What legal defensive or avoidance options remain?
4. How important and time-sensitive is the objective?
5. What happens if the company delays, recovers or chooses a different next position?
6. What information would change the decision?

There is no universal “always enter” or “always retreat” answer. The correct choice depends on the printed cards, current rules and state of the company.

## Solo Hazard decision worksheet

When you switch to the Hazard side, use the same declared site path and ask:

1. **Legal?** Check the printed key, target, timing and any other conditions.
2. **Useful?** Which legal play most threatens the Hero mission or the company's ability to attempt the site?
3. **Sequence?** Does an amplifier require another card, and can that combination be played legally in that order?
4. **Limit?** Count every Hazard that counts against the current company's limit.
5. **Best combination?** Under the Academy's selected solo procedure, choose the most disadvantageous legal combination within the limit.

For this Academy's solo draw procedure, draw **two plus twice the normal Hazard draw number for the relevant site**. First apply the normal movement rule to determine which site's draw number applies: a move to a non-Haven uses the new site, while a move to a Haven normally uses the site of origin. Undrawn or unplayed cards are not extra plays; follow the selected solo rules for discarding and reshuffling. The procedure is a labelled solitaire variant, not the standard multiplayer draw rule. Compare [My Solo Rules](https://meccg.wordpress.com/my-solo-rules/) with the [official MECCG rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) rather than blending their procedures silently.

## Five quick drills

Write your answer before consulting the [Route Lab model answers]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).

1. **Four or five?** Count the origin and destination in a proposed route of four regions, then in one of five.
2. **A repeated region:** explain why A → B → C → B → D is not a normal Region Movement route.
3. **Site path versus route:** explain when the Region Movement site path is declared and what it is used for.
4. **Eight drawn, five limit:** state what each number means and how you choose plays.
5. **Objective complete:** after acquiring Book of Mazarbul, name three facts that could change your next destination.

For each answer, give the rule or card text that supports it. If the map and a region card disagree about adjacency, use the region-card adjacency information.
