# Expedition Planner — Table Worksheet

Use this page before every expedition. It turns the Hero player's plan and the solo Hazard player's response into one auditable sequence. You can use it with the physical region cards and the Academy's teaching decks; no proxies or extra cards are required.

The Academy uses **Region Movement** as the formal term. “Free movement” is a convenient teaching phrase, not permission to ignore adjacency, the region limit or the site-path rules.

## The one-minute Captain's check

Fill this in before declaring movement.

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **Current site and region** | |
| **Mission** — what concrete thing will this trip achieve? | |
| **Company condition** — size, tapped characters, wounds, key cards spent | |
| **Destination and destination region** | |
| **Movement type** — Starter, Region, or a specific special effect | |
| **Planned region sequence** — include origin and destination | |
| **Region count** — are there four or fewer, counting both endpoints? | |
| **Adjacency** — did I check each pair on the region cards? | |
| **Repeated region** — does any region appear twice? | |
| **Likely Hazard exposure** — which types/names can be keyed? | |
| **Site danger** — is there an automatic attack or other entry requirement? | |
| **Plan after arrival** — enter, recover, or reassess? | |
| **Next position** — where could the company go after this? | |

**Do not move on until the route is legal.** A promising destination cannot rescue an illegal route.

## Check the actual Hero hand before choosing a route

The [Hero Opening Hand & Access Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hero-opening-hand-lab/' | relative_url }}) gives the five-card access probabilities and three worked hands. Record the actual objective card before you draw a route:

- Is the Resource actually in hand, or is there a legal way to find it?
- Is its printed playable site present in the 15-card Hero Location deck?
- Does the company have the skill, influence and untapped character needed?
- If the objective is absent, are you deliberately making a search expedition, pursuing another objective, or changing the mission?

Do not count a card as available merely because it is in the 36-card list. Mark it as **not seen**, **seen but discarded**, or **seen but unusable**; these are different deck-performance results.

## Four separate decisions

Keep these steps distinct:

1. **Organization Phase:** choose the destination and declare the movement. A Region Movement route must already be capable of being legal.
2. **Movement/Hazard Phase — reveal:** reveal the new site.
3. **Site path:** for Region Movement, declare the actual region sequence. This sequence determines region types and names used for creature-Hazard keying; it does not itself move the company.
4. **Arrival and site entry:** resolve movement and Hazards, inspect the company's actual condition, and then decide whether to enter the site when the rules allow that decision.

If the movement is illegal when the new site is revealed, it is negated; the company remains at its origin but still conducts its Movement/Hazard Phase. See the [official rules for Movement/Hazard Phase]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) and the [Council of Elrond rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/).

## Site-entry checkpoint — inspect the Hero site card

Arrival is not entry. After the Movement/Hazard Phase, check the actual Hero site card and then decide whether to enter. If you decline, the Site Phase ends immediately and the company cannot take other Site Phase actions that turn.

| Hero destination | Site-card numbers (opponent / you) | Hero draw | Solo Hazard draw | Site attack if entered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Moria | 3/2 | Up to 2 | 8 | Orcs — 4 strikes at 7 prowess |
| Goblin-gate (Hero site) | 2/1 | Up to 1 | 6 | Orcs — 3 strikes at 6 prowess |
| Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | 1/1 | Up to 1 | 4 | None |

These are Hero site-card values. The notation is **opponent draws / you draw**: the first number feeds the solo Hazard formula; the second is the Hero player's normal draw from the Hero play deck. A company with an avatar or a character of mind 3 or greater qualifies for the Hero draw and must draw at least one, up to the printed number. After the Movement/Hazard Phase, reset the Hero hand to five. The Blue Mountain card used by this Academy is a Free-hold in Númeriador with a 1/1 draw and no automatic attack; do not use Minion site data. Site automatic attacks do not consume Hazard Limit slots. A Hazard card that modifies an automatic attack is a separate Hazard play and normally does count. Complete the [Movement Draw Lab]({{ '/scenarios/movement-draw-lab/' | relative_url }}) before planning the longer campaign. The [Route Exposure Matrix]({{ '/scenarios/route-exposure-matrix/' | relative_url }}) then checks the actual baseline Hazard cards against all three connected legs; it shows why the core creature package remains legal across the Wilderness routes while the draw and site-attack pressure changes.

Use the [Site Entry & Automatic Attack Lab]({{ '/scenarios/site-entry-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/site-entry-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise the decision.

## Worked plan A — Rivendell to Moria

**Mission:** acquire Book of Mazarbul.  
**Origin:** Rivendell, Rhudaur.  
**Destination:** Moria, Redhorn Gate.  
**Teaching Region Movement route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.

### Legality

- Three regions, counting both endpoints.
- No repeated region.
- Check every adjacency on the physical region cards.
- Within the normal four-region limit.

### Why this is Region Movement, not direct Starter Movement

Moria's nearest Haven is **Lórien**, so Starter Movement cannot take the company directly from Rivendell to Moria. The Academy uses Region Movement for the direct teaching leg. The alternative is to travel Rivendell → Lórien first, then Lórien → Moria on a later turn using Starter Movement.

This is an important legality check: choosing the correct destination is not enough; the movement type must also be valid. Region Movement names all regions in the declared route, while Starter Movement uses the printed site path's region types and does not name intervening regions. That difference can change which named-region Hazards are available.

### Exposure and preparation

All three regions on this teaching route are Wilderness. A creature requiring two Wilderness regions may therefore be a legal option for the Hazard player, **if its printed keying and every other requirement are satisfied**. This is not a prediction that a particular creature will be drawn.

Under the Academy's chosen solo procedure, Moria's normal Hazard draw number of 3 produces a draw of **2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards**. The five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of **5** for its Movement/Hazard Phase. These are different numbers: eight cards are available to consider, but the normal limit still controls how many Hazards may be played.

Before the company enters Moria, check its wounds and taps, the defensive cards still available, and Moria's automatic attack (**Orcs: four strikes with 7 prowess**). Reaching the site does not guarantee that attempting the objective is wise. The automatic attack is a site encounter, not an extra Hazard play against the Hazard Limit.

**Debrief question:** if the company arrives too weak to attempt the Book, what did the route gain, and what did it cost? Record a concrete lesson rather than assuming the route was good or bad just because the company survived.

## Worked plan B — Moria to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

**Mission after the Book:** reassess and pursue a useful next Dwarf objective if the company is fit to continue.  
**Origin region:** Redhorn Gate.  
**Destination region:** Númeriador.

The direct sequence **Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador** contains five regions. It is not legal as one normal Region Movement.

A verified teaching alternative is two legs:

1. **Moria → Rivendell:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur.
2. **Rivendell → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold:** Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Check adjacency against the region cards before declaring either leg. Rivendell offers a Haven stop and a chance to consolidate, but it is an example—not a rule that every expedition must return to a Haven. If another intermediate site is available, compare its legality, value, exposure and recovery options.

**Debrief question:** is the extra stop wasted movement, or does it improve the company's condition and preserve a legal route? Answer from the actual state of your game.

## Worked plan C — the company arrives in poor condition

Imagine the company reaches its destination with its best fighter wounded, two characters tapped, and its main avoidance resource already spent.

Do not enter automatically. Check in this order:

1. Does the site have an automatic attack or other requirement?
2. Which characters can still contribute, and what is the likely cost of the attack?
3. What legal defensive or avoidance options remain?
4. How important and time-sensitive is the objective?
5. What happens if the company delays, recovers or chooses a different next position?
6. What information would change the decision?

There is no universal “always enter” or “always retreat” answer. The correct choice depends on the printed cards, current rules and state of the company.

## Worked plan D — Moria to Goblin-gate without a Haven stop

**Mission:** after acquiring Book of Mazarbul, consider a second objective: Gollum at Goblin-gate. Do this only if the company and campaign plan justify the risk.  
**Origin:** Moria, Redhorn Gate.  
**Destination:** Goblin-gate, High Pass.  
**Proposed Region Movement route:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass.

This is a non-Haven-to-non-Haven journey. It contains four regions including origin and destination, has no repeated region, and is within the normal limit **provided every adjacency is confirmed on the physical Region cards**. Do not declare it until you have checked those connections.

### What you gain and what you risk

- You do not spend a movement returning to Rivendell or another Haven first.
- The route names all four regions and exposes four Wilderness regions to relevant creature keying.
- Goblin-gate has an automatic attack of **Orcs — three strikes with 6 prowess**. Resolve that when entering the site; do not confuse it with the Hazard Limit.
- The Gollum objective is optional. If the company is wounded, tapped or short of defensive resources after Moria, the direct route may be too risky.

**Solo draw and entry choice:** The Hero version of Goblin-gate is **2/1**: Hero draw up to **1** card from the Hero play deck; normal Hazard draw **2**, so the Academy solo procedure draws **6**. The five-character company's base Hazard Limit is **5**. If the company enters Goblin-gate to attempt Gollum, it faces the automatic attack of three Orc strikes with 6 prowess. If it chooses not to enter, it does not face that automatic attack and may not take any other site-phase actions or play cards; it remains at Goblin-gate and can move again next turn.

**A second non-Haven leg:** from Goblin-gate, the company can move to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold via **High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**. This is another four-region route, all Wilderness. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold has a normal Hazard draw of **1**, so the solo draw is **4**; the Hero draw is up to **1 card** from the Hero play deck; it has no automatic attack. At Blue Mountain, store Book of Mazarbul during the **next turn's Organization Phase** after the bearer's corruption check; you cannot store it during the arrival-turn Site Phase. The site-type symbol is Free-hold [{F}], but the site is the named Dwarf-hold required by the Book's text.

**Captain's decision:** choose this route only if the next objective is worth the added exposure and the company can reasonably survive the arrival. If not, recover or choose another legal destination. A Haven stop is not compulsory, but the cost of skipping recovery must be counted.

### Storage is on the next Organization Phase

If the company reaches Blue Mountain during this turn's Movement/Hazard Phase, it cannot store Book of Mazarbul during the arrival-turn Site Phase. Storage is an Organization Phase action. At the start of the next turn, while the company is still at the Dwarf-hold, make the bearer's corruption check and store the Book if successful. You may then declare the next movement during that Organization Phase. Work through the [Book of Mazarbul — Second Expedition workbook]({{ '/scenarios/book-journey/' | relative_url }}) before using this as a campaign plan.

Source checks: [The Wizards movement and site-entry rules](https://meccg.com/rules/by-expansion/the-wizards/) and the [MECCG card database](https://www.councilofelrond.org/cards/). The Region cards confirm Redhorn Gate–Hollin–Rhudaur–High Pass and High Pass–Rhudaur–Arthedain–Númeriador. Verify the same adjacency on your physical Region cards before play.

## Worked plan E — Dwarves to Moria while Gandalf moves to Lórien

This is the advanced Challenge Deck C opening. It is not required for Game 1.

- Thorin's five Dwarves move Rivendell → Moria by Region Movement: normal Hazard draw 3, solo draw **8**, base Hazard Limit **5**.
- Gandalf is played at his home site, Grey Havens, as a separate company and moves Grey Havens → Lórien by Haven-to-Haven Starter Movement: Lórien is a Haven, so use Grey Havens' normal Hazard draw 2, solo draw **6**, base Hazard Limit **2** for the one-character company.

The two companies draw **14 cards in total** from the shared Hazard deck, but they resolve separate Movement/Hazard Phases with separate limits of five and two. The Hazard player must evaluate each company's own route and condition. Do not pool the cards or use a combined limit.

This plan protects Gandalf from Moria's site attack, but it does not make the Wizard safe: he faces a separate six-card solo Hazard draw alone. If Gandalf joins the Dwarves instead, the Moria draw remains eight but the company size and base Hazard Limit rise to six.

## Worked plan F — Lórien to The Wind Throne

This route is now available in the corrected 15-site Hero Location deck.

**Movement:** Starter Movement. The Wind Throne lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, so a company at Lórien can travel directly there. The printed site path is Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land; it is used for Hazard keying, not declared as a Region Movement route.

| Forecast | Result |
|---|---:|
| Site-card numbers (Hazard / Hero) | 2/2 |
| Hero draw | Up to 2 |
| Normal Hazard draw | 2 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **6** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Automatic attack if entered | Orcs — 3 strikes at 7 |

**Resource opportunity:** The site permits Information and minor/major Items. It is an additional legal destination for Durin's Axe and Hauberk of Bright Mail, but not for the Greater Item Wormsbane.

**Hazard exposure:**

- Cave Worm can be keyed to Grey Mountain Narrows.
- Seized by Terror can target an eligible character moving through the Shadow-land.
- Lesser Spiders, Neeker-breekers and Wargs have matching region/site keys.
- Giant Spiders is not legal: the path has only one Wilderness and is not in one of its special Mirkwood regions.
- Watcher in the Water is not legal: it requires two Wilderness plus a Coastal Sea, or its explicit Moria permission.
- Arouse Denizens is a possible later test because The Wind Throne is a Ruins & Lairs. Arouse Minions is not legal because the site is not a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold.

**Captain's decision:** The automatic attack is faced only if the company enters. If you decline, you avoid the Orc attack but take no site actions. Decide whether the Major Item opportunity is worth the six-card Hazard draw, the five-slot Hazard Limit and the possible Orc attack.

Use the [Location Deck Legality & Wind Throne Lab]({{ '/scenarios/location-deck-legality-lab/' | relative_url }}) to practise the full card-by-card decision.

## Solo Hazard decision worksheet

Before resolving the draw, work through the [Hazard Legality Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It demonstrates how the declared path changes creature legality and why support-event timing matters.

When you switch to the Hazard side, use the same declared site path and ask:

1. **Legal?** Check the printed key, target, timing and any other conditions.
2. **Useful?** Which legal play most threatens the Hero mission or the company's ability to attempt the site?
3. **Sequence?** Does an amplifier require another card, and can that combination be played legally in that order?
4. **Limit?** Count every Hazard that counts against the current company's limit.
5. **Best combination?** Under the Academy's selected solo procedure, choose the most disadvantageous legal combination within the limit.

For this Academy's solo draw procedure, draw **two plus twice the normal Hazard draw number for the relevant site**. Practise the calculations in the [Hazard Draw & Limit Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/' | relative_url }}), including the important case where a company moves to a Haven and the normal draw number comes from its site of origin. First apply the normal movement rule to determine which site's draw number applies: a move to a non-Haven uses the new site, while a move to a Haven normally uses the site of origin. Undrawn or unplayed cards are not extra plays; follow the selected solo rules for discarding and reshuffling. The procedure is a labelled solitaire variant, not the standard multiplayer draw rule. Compare [My Solo Rules](https://meccg.wordpress.com/my-solo-rules/) with the [official MECCG rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) rather than blending their procedures silently.

## Track the Hazard deck across the route

For the non-Haven route **Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold**, the five-Dwarf company draws six solo Hazards on the Goblin-gate leg and four on the Blue Mountain leg. These are two separate company Movement/Hazard Phases, each with its own base Hazard Limit of five. The total of ten cards is a route total, not one ten-card draw and not ten plays in one phase. At the end of each phase, ordinary unplayed draws go to the Hazard discard under the Academy supplement; if the draw deck runs out, recycle that discard. The Hero hand resets to five after each Movement/Hazard Phase and again at End of Turn. Use the [Solo Deck Cycle Lab]({{ '/scenarios/solo-deck-cycle-lab/' | relative_url }}) to practise the complete sequence.

## Worked route C — Lórien to The Wind Throne

**Mission:** test the optional Wind Throne expedition and its site-playable Resources; this is not the Book of Mazarbûl route.  
**Origin:** Lórien.  
**Destination:** The Wind Throne, Grey Mountain Narrows.  
**Movement:** Starter Movement, because Lórien is listed as the nearest Haven on the Hero site card.  
**Printed site path:** Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land.

### Draw and limit

- Hero draw: up to 2.
- Normal Hazard draw: 2.
- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 2) = **6**.
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- Five Dwarves plus Gandalf: **6**.
- Site automatic attack if entered: **three Orc strikes at 7**.

The destination's region is a Shadow-land, so Seized by Terror is legal against a non-Wizard character. Dori is the default target by probability: mind 1 gives a 33/36 (91.7%) chance of splitting him off. The destination site type is Ruins & Lairs, which is a different property; it makes Arouse Denizens a possible future variant card, not Arouse Minions.

### The double-River checkpoint

The baseline Hazard deck contains two Rivers. One Ranger must tap for each River. The starting company has Óin as its only Ranger; Gandalf is not a Ranger. If both Rivers are played, Óin cannot satisfy both and the entire Site Phase is skipped: the company cannot enter and does not face the automatic attack. Gildor is a Ranger in the active Hero deck and can provide a second untapped Ranger if legally recruited before departure. Tapping both Rangers satisfies the Rivers and allows entry, but leaves both tapped before the three Orc strikes at 7. If Seized by Terror is also available, the Hazard player should target a Ranger only when removing that Ranger leaves fewer untapped Rangers than River copies; otherwise Dori's higher split probability may be the stronger choice.

**Do not plan to play Book of Mazarbûl here.** It is a Special Item playable only at Moria; The Wind Throne lists Information and minor/major Items.

Work through the six-card Hazard hand and the exact target probabilities in the [Shadow-land Route & Seized by Terror Lab]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/' | relative_url }}) and [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).

## After the route is declared: choose the Hazard line

The site path controls creature keying; the relevant site's normal Hazard number controls the solo draw; company size controls the base Hazard Limit. Do not merge those three checks. ## Add a Ranger-capacity check before committing to a route

For a route with a site destination, record the following alongside the normal movement and Hazard calculations:

| Check | Your note |
|---|---|
| Does the printed/declared site path contain a Shadow-land or Dark-domain? | |
| Is Seized by Terror legal on this movement? | |
| How many Rangers are in the moving company? | |
| How many of those Rangers should still be untapped at the start of the Site Phase? | |
| Could one or two Rivers be played on the destination? | |

## Add a Seized by Terror aftermath check

When a route exposes a Shadow-land or Dark-domain, do not merely mark Seized by Terror as legal. Before committing to the move, note:

- the exact **site of origin** to which a successful target returns;
- which character is most likely to be targeted and what they carry;
- whether the target is a Ranger needed for River;
- whether the target is a follower or controls followers;
- whether a character-specific effect such as Promptings of Wisdom or Stealth changes which company remains protected.

A successful split does not give the target a second Movement/Hazard Phase or new Hazard Limit. The original company continues with its fixed limit; the returned company cannot take Site Phase actions that turn. Use the [Seized by Terror — Aftermath & Regrouping Lab]({{ '/scenarios/seized-terror-aftermath-lab/' | relative_url }}) before using a split as a route-denial tactic.
