# Rivendell → Moria

**Campaign:** Historical Dwarven Quest  
**Objective:** Acquire Book of Mazarbul  
**Start:** Rivendell, in Rhudaur  
**Destination:** Moria, in Redhorn Gate  
**Movement:** Region Movement  
**Teaching route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate

## 1. Legality

This route has three regions, counting both endpoints. The route is within the normal four-region maximum and has no repeated region. Check each adjacency against the region cards before committing to movement.

## 2. What the route exposes

The three regions are Wilderness. The declared Region Movement site path therefore contains three Wilderness regions and their names. This can make creatures with matching Wilderness requirements legal, including a creature requiring two Wilderness regions if its card text and all other conditions are satisfied.

Do not treat this as a promise that a particular Hazard will appear or can always be played. The Hazard card's printed keying and other requirements still control.

## 3. Timing

During Organization, declare Moria as the destination and ensure the movement can be legal. During the Movement/Hazard Phase, reveal Moria and declare the Region Movement site path. The site path determines much of the creature-Hazard exposure; it does not direct movement by itself.

## 4. The objective and the arrival decision

The route is only the travel plan. After the Hazard phase, inspect the company and then decide whether to enter Moria. Moria has an automatic attack, so arrival is not a promise that the company can safely attempt the objective.

Before entering, ask:

- Which characters are tapped or wounded?
- Which defensive resources remain?
- Can the company withstand the automatic attack and still attempt Book of Mazarbul?
- What is lost if the attempt is delayed?

## 5. The next expedition

If the Book is acquired, reassess instead of automatically pressing on. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a meaningful follow-up, but the direct route from Redhorn Gate to Númeriador is five regions. Plan a legal intermediate stop or identify a specific movement effect that changes the normal limit.

## Captain's decision

A good route has four values: **legality, exposure, destination reward and future position**. The route is good only if it serves the whole expedition.
