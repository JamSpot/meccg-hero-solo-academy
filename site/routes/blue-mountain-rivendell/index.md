# Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold → Rivendell — the direct return route

This route completes the Book of Mazarbûl expedition. After the Book has been stored at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, the company can return to Rivendell in **one legal Region Movement**. It does not have to visit Grey Havens first.

The example is important because Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold's printed nearest Haven is **Grey Havens**, not Rivendell. Starter Movement follows that printed relationship; Region Movement gives you a different legal route.

## The decision

**Starting point:** Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, Númeriador.  
**Objective:** return to Rivendell to recover, reorganise and plan the next expedition.  
**Company:** the five starting Dwarves.  
**Book:** already stored; it is no longer being carried.


## Option A — direct to Rivendell by Region Movement

Declare the three-region sequence:

**Númeriador → Arthedain → Rhudaur**

This is legal:

- Númeriador is adjacent to Arthedain.
- Arthedain is adjacent to Rhudaur.
- The route contains three regions, including both endpoints.
- No region is repeated.
- All three regions are Wilderness.

The Hero Region cards identify those adjacencies and types. Check the physical cards when laying out the route.

### Draws and exposure

Rivendell is a Haven. When a company moves to a Haven, the normal draw numbers come from its **site of origin**, not the destination Haven. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is the Hero site with a 1/1 draw.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw number | 1 |
| Hero draw | Up to 1 |
| Solo Hazard draw | 2 + (2 × 1) = **4** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack at Rivendell | None |

The company draws four cards from the separate Hazard deck. Its five-card Hazard Limit does not turn those four cards into five plays.

These Hazard Limit figures assume the five starting Dwarves only. If Gandalf joins them, he counts as a character for company-size purposes: the company has six characters and its base Hazard Limit becomes **6**. The site-card draw numbers do not change.


### What can the Hazard deck key?

The declared path is three Wilderness regions and includes the names Númeriador, Arthedain and Rhudaur.

| Hazard | Status on this route |
|---|---|
| Cave Worm ×3 | Legal by its named-region key to Númeriador |
| Giant Spiders ×3 | Legal by double Wilderness |
| Lesser Spiders ×3 | Legal by Wilderness |
| Neeker-breekers ×2 | Legal by Wilderness |
| Wargs ×2 | Legal by Wilderness |
| Watcher in the Water ×3 | Not legal: no Coastal Sea and the destination is not Moria |
| Seized by Terror ×2 | Not legal: no Shadow-land or Dark-domain |
| Despair of the Heart ×3 | Conditional: may target an eligible non-Wizard Dwarf |
| Full of Froth and Rage ×3 | Conditional: requires a Spider or Animal attack |
| Wake of War ×2 | Conditional: requires a Wolf, Spider or Animal attack |
| River ×2 | Site-targeted; check its target and effect before playing |
| Twilight ×2 | No target in the closed-sideboard baseline; no Environment is in play |

**13 of the 16 creature cards** in the fixed Hazard deck have a legal creature key on this direct route: Cave Worm, Giant Spiders, Lesser Spiders, Neeker-breekers and Wargs. This is a route-coverage count, not a prediction that all 13 will be drawn or played.

The direct route is short in draw volume, but it exposes both Cave Worm and Giant Spiders. The Hazard player still chooses only from cards actually drawn and must obey each card's timing, target and the company's Hazard Limit.

## Option B — Starter Movement through Grey Havens

The Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold card lists Grey Havens as its nearest Haven. Its printed site path is **Free-domain → Wilderness**. This is a legal Starter Movement to Grey Havens.

### Leg 1 — Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold → Grey Havens

Grey Havens is a Haven, so use the Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold origin draw numbers again.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw number | 1 |
| Hero draw | Up to 1 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **4** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack at Grey Havens | None |

The site path has one Wilderness and one Free-domain. Cave Worm can still be keyed to the named origin region Númeriador. Giant Spiders cannot use double Wilderness on this leg.

The route has **10 route-legal creature copies**: Cave Worm ×3, Lesser Spiders ×3, Neeker-breekers ×2 and Wargs ×2.

### Leg 2 — Grey Havens → Rivendell

The Grey Havens card gives the Haven-to-Haven site path to Rivendell as **Wilderness → Wilderness → Free-domain**. This is a separate movement on a later turn.

Rivendell is again the destination Haven, so the normal draw numbers come from the Grey Havens site card, which is 2/2.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw number | 2 |
| Hero draw | Up to 2 |
| Solo Hazard draw | 2 + (2 × 2) = **6** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack at Rivendell | None |

The two-Wilderness path makes Giant Spiders legal. Cave Worm is not keyed to Lindon or Rhudaur, so it is not legal on this second leg. The route has **10 route-legal creature copies**: Giant Spiders ×3, Lesser Spiders ×3, Neeker-breekers ×2 and Wargs ×2.

Across the two phases the Hazard side draws **10 cards in total** (four, then six). These are separate draws on separate turns, with a fresh Hazard Limit calculation for each phase. Do not combine them into one ten-card draw or one ten-play allowance.

## Compare the choices

| Decision | Direct Region Movement | Starter via Grey Havens |
|---|---:|---:|
| Turns to Rivendell | **1** | **2** |
| Solo Hazard cards drawn | **4** | **10** |
| Hazard phases | 1 | 2 |
| Route-legal creature copies | 13 on the direct route | 10 on each leg |
| Double Wilderness exposed? | Yes | Only on Grey Havens → Rivendell |
| Cave Worm by Númeriador | Yes | Only on Blue Mountain → Grey Havens |
| Automatic attack at destination | None | None |

**Default recommendation:** if the mission is simply to return to Rivendell, use the direct three-region Region Movement. It reaches the desired Haven in one movement and uses only four solo Hazard draws.

Choose Grey Havens first only when the stop itself has a specific campaign purpose, or when you deliberately prefer the first leg's narrower site path. That extra stop costs another turn and a separate six-card solo Hazard draw on the way to Rivendell.

Neither route is risk-free. The direct route gives the Hazard side a wider creature-key set in one phase; the Starter route draws more cards over two phases but exposes fewer creature copies on each individual leg.

## Recovery at the Haven

Reaching Rivendell does not heal wounded characters immediately. Characters who are still wounded when the company is at a Haven can heal during the next Untap Phase, becoming tapped as part of healing. They will need a later Untap Phase to become untapped again.

The same principle applies if the company stops at Grey Havens first. A Haven is a recovery point, but the extra Haven is not automatically worth an extra turn and another Hazard phase.

## Two important exclusions

- **Great-road is not available at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold.** It requires the company to start at a Haven. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a Free-hold, so use ordinary legal movement here.
- **Starter Movement cannot take the company directly from Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold to Rivendell.** The Blue Mountain site card lists Grey Havens as its nearest Haven. The direct one-turn route to Rivendell is Region Movement.
