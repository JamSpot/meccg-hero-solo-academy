# Iron Hill Expedition — Model Answer

## 1. Direct Starter Movement is legal

The Iron Hill Dwarf-hold card lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Lórien is a Haven, so the first Starter Movement condition is met.

The five symbols {w}{b}{b}{w}{w} do not make the movement illegal. The four-consecutive-region limit is specifically the Region Movement limit. Starter Movement uses the site path printed on the destination card and has no four-region maximum.

The Starter site path shows region types and the names of the origin and destination regions, but not the intermediate region names.

## 2. Region Movement directly from Lórien is illegal

The shortest adjacency chain from the Lórien region to Iron Hills is:

**Wold & Foothills → Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

That is five regions, including both endpoints. Region Movement allows no more than four consecutive regions unless a card effect explicitly increases the limit. Therefore this direct Region Movement is illegal.

## 3. Why the two legal paths key different Hazards

### Direct Starter Movement

The printed path types are Wilderness, Border-land, Border-land, Wilderness, Wilderness. There is no Shadow-land or Dark-domain, so Seized by Terror cannot be keyed. Cave Worm can still be keyed by the named destination region Iron Hills. Creatures keyed to Wilderness or Border-land can use the matching types.

### Region Movement via Eagles’ Eyrie

The declared path is Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills. The region types are Border-land, Shadow-land, Wilderness, Wilderness. Seized by Terror is legal because Grey Mountain Narrows is a Shadow-land and the company is moving through it. Cave Worm remains legal by the Iron Hills name.

The destination site is a Free-hold in both cases. Arouse Minions is therefore not legal on either final leg: its site-type requirement is Shadow-hold or Dark-hold. Passing through a Shadow-land does not satisfy a site-type requirement.

## 4. Draw calculations

The Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card has normal Hazard draw 3 and Hero draw 3.

- Direct Starter Movement: normal Hazard 3 → solo Hazard draw 2 + (2 × 3) = **8**; Hero draws up to **3**.
- Lórien → Eagles’ Eyrie: normal Hazard 1 → solo Hazard draw 2 + (2 × 1) = **4**; Hero draws up to **1**.
- Eagles’ Eyrie → Iron Hill: normal Hazard 3 → solo Hazard draw 2 + (2 × 3) = **8**; Hero draws up to **3**.

Route B therefore draws 12 solo Hazard cards across two movement/hazard phases and up to four Hero cards. Route A draws eight solo Hazard cards and up to three Hero cards. The base Hazard Limit is five for the five-Dwarf company in each phase; on the four-card Eagles’ Eyrie leg, only four cards have been drawn, so no more than four drawn cards can be played.

Neither destination has a printed automatic attack. That does not prevent the Hazard player from playing legal movement Hazards.

## 5. Faction influence check

Iron Hill Dwarves is a unique four-MP faction. It is playable at Iron Hill Dwarf-hold if the influence check is greater than 8. The faction's standard modification gives a Dwarf +2.

Thorin II has base direct influence 2 and +2 direct influence against Dwarves and Dwarf factions, for 4 against this faction when that direct influence is available. Glóin has base direct influence 2 and +1 against Dwarves and Dwarf factions, for 3 when available.

The current setup assigns Dori and Kíli under Thorin's direct influence and Óin under Glóin's. Their direct influence is therefore committed to followers. During the Organization Phase, the company may move those followers to general influence if the total mind does not exceed the starting 20 general influence.

The five Dwarves' total mind is exactly 20. If all five are moved to general influence:

- Thorin has 4 available direct influence against Iron Hill Dwarves, plus the faction's Dwarf standard modification +2. The check is 2D6 + 6 and must be greater than 8: a natural 3 or higher succeeds (35/36).
- Glóin has 3 available direct influence against Iron Hill Dwarves, plus the faction's Dwarf standard modification +2. The check is 2D6 + 5 and must be greater than 8: a natural 4 or higher succeeds (33/36).

The +2 direct-influence bonus Thorin and Glóin have specifically against the **Blue Mountain Dwarves** faction does not apply to Iron Hill Dwarves. Use only their general Dwarf-faction bonuses here.

These figures assume the influencer is untapped, the site is untapped, and the direct influence is genuinely available at the time of the attempt. If you keep the original follower assignments, do not add direct influence that is already committed.

## 6. Which route should the Hero choose?

If the only objective is to play Iron Hill Dwarves and there is no specific Resource reason to stop at Eagles’ Eyrie, the direct Starter Movement is normally the more efficient Hero choice:

- one movement/hazard phase instead of two;
- eight solo Hazard draws instead of twelve;
- no Shadow-land in the printed site path, so Seized by Terror is not legal;
- still allows Cave Worm to be keyed to Iron Hills.

The Eagles’ Eyrie route is a useful alternative only if the extra Site Phase has a real benefit. It also exposes Grey Mountain Narrows and makes Seized by Terror legal. It is not automatically safer merely because its final Region Movement uses four regions.

## 7. Hazard deck implications

The route confirms that Seized by Terror is not a universally dead card: it is inactive on the three opening campaign paths but becomes legal on the route through Grey Mountain Narrows. The proposed Arouse Minions swap does not help at Iron Hill because the destination is a Free-hold. Brigands is legal on both routes because each includes Border-land and/or Wilderness.

This is why the Academy keeps the baseline list fixed until the first three games are recorded and tests variants by route rather than declaring one universal Hazard list superior.
