# Moria → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

**Campaign:** Historical Dwarven Quest  
**Objective:** After acquiring Book of Mazarbul, consolidate the expedition and plan the next Dwarf objective.  
**Start:** Moria, in Redhorn Gate  
**Destination:** Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, in Númeriador

## The important legality check

Do not assume that Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is reachable from Moria in one normal Region Movement.

The direct westward sequence is:

**Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

That is five regions, including origin and destination. Normal Region Movement allows no more than four consecutive regions, so this is not a legal single movement without a specific effect that changes the limit.

## A two-leg teaching example

One clear, verifiable route is:

### Leg 1 — Moria to Rivendell
**Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur**

Three regions; check adjacency on the region cards. This reaches a Haven and creates a recovery/consolidation opportunity.

### Leg 2 — Rivendell to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold
**Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

Three regions; check adjacency and endpoints before declaring movement.

This example uses a Haven stop, but it does not mean every trip must return to a Haven. If another site in your Location deck provides a legal intermediate stop, compare its site value, route legality, Hazard exposure and recovery options before choosing it.

## Why the second expedition is different

Before acquiring the Book, the mission was **ACQUIRE**. After acquisition, reassess:

- Is the Book safe and does the company need to recover?
- Is Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold still the best next objective?
- Can the company legally reach it in one move, or does it need an intermediate site?
- Does the current company have the resources and condition for another site attempt?
- What is the next useful position if the route or company condition changes?

## Captain's lesson

The objective changing does not make the next destination automatically legal. **Re-plan from the company's actual current site**, count every region, and compare the whole expedition before moving.
