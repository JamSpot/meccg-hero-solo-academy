# Three-Turn Expedition — Worked Solo Run

This is the Academy's first **fully joined Hero-and-Hazard worked expedition**. It takes the fixed 36-card Hero deck and 30-card Hazard deck through the Book of Mazarbûl mission, then compares two legal ways to reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold without making a Haven return compulsory.

It is a teaching simulation, not a scripted result for your real game. The sample hands below are deliberately fixed so that you can study the decisions. For actual play, shuffle both decks and use the cards you really draw.

## What this exercise teaches

- How the Hero hand and Hazard hand are drawn from separate decks.
- Why adding Gandalf raises the company's Hazard Limit.
- How the Moria site path makes Watcher in the Water legal.
- How three Despairs can be spread across three different Dwarves.
- Why Wake of War adds prowess but not another strike to an attack that says each character faces one strike.
- Why the Moria automatic attack is separate from the Hazard Limit.
- How a direct non-Haven route compares with a route through Rivendell.
- Why Haven healing returns a wounded character to the tapped position, not the untapped position.
- Why Book of Mazarbûl's Sage hand-size bonus requires you to tap the Book during the Organization Phase.

## Prepare the table

Use the locked Academy baseline:

| Component | What to use |
|---|---|
| Hero play deck | 36 cards: 30 Resources, five non-Wizard characters and Gandalf |
| Hazard deck | 30 cards, separate from the Hero deck |
| Location deck | 15 Hero site cards |
| Starting company | Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin at Rivendell |
| Starting items | One Cram with Thorin II and one with Óin |
| Starting control | Thorin controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin |

For this worked line, use this **constructed opening Hero hand**:

- Book of Mazarbûl
- Gandalf
- Concealment
- Fellowship
- Risky Blow

Play Gandalf at Rivendell during the Organization Phase and join him to the five Dwarves. The company now has six characters, so its base Hazard Limit is six. The five starting Dwarves remain the same; Gandalf is an additional character, not a replacement. The Hazard Limit is fixed when this Movement/Hazard Phase begins, so it does not shrink during the phase if a character is later eliminated.

The Book is in hand, not in play. Gandalf is a Sage, but the Book's hand-size ability is not active merely because a Sage is present.

## Turn 1 — Rivendell to Moria

### Organization Phase

Declare Moria as the destination and use **Region Movement**.

**Declared route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.

There are three regions, including the origin and destination regions. Check the adjacency on the physical Region cards. The route is legal and contains no repeated region.

This is not direct Starter Movement: Moria lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, not Rivendell. The Region Movement route is therefore the appropriate direct journey.

### Movement/Hazard Phase

Reveal the Hero version of Moria. Its site-card numbers are **3/2** (Hazard player / Hero player).

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw | 3 |
| Solo Hazard draw | 2 + (2 × 3) = **8** |
| Hero draw | Up to **2** cards |
| Company size | 6 |
| Base Hazard Limit | **6** |

The Hero draw comes from the Hero play deck, which contains both Resources and character/Wizard cards. The Hazard draw comes from the separate Hazard deck. Do not combine the two hands.

### Worked eight-card Hazard draw

For this example, the eight Hazard cards are:

| Drawn card | Route / target check |
|---|---|
| Despair of the Heart ×3 | Legal on three different eligible non-Wizard, non-Hobbit Dwarves |
| Watcher in the Water | Legal because its card explicitly allows play at Moria |
| Full of Froth and Rage | Legal because Watcher is an Animal attack |
| Wake of War | Legal because Watcher is an Animal attack |
| Seized by Terror | **Illegal**: the declared route has no Shadow-land or Dark-domain region |
| Twilight | No legal target: the closed-sideboard baseline has no Environment in play |

The site type of Moria is Shadow-hold. That does **not** turn the site into a Shadow-land region for Seized by Terror. Region types and site types are different checks.

With Gandalf in the company, the Hazard Limit is six. The six legal cards can therefore all be played in this worked draw:

1. Play the three Despairs on **Dori, Kíli and Óin**. They are three different eligible characters; only one corruption card is being played on each character this turn.
2. Play Watcher in the Water at Moria.
3. Play Full of Froth and Rage.
4. Play Wake of War.

The three Dwarves each have a -1 corruption-check modifier. If any character in the company becomes wounded, each Despair target makes a corruption check. The Despair check occurs before the associated body check. If more than one character becomes wounded, the trigger can happen more than once.

Watcher in the Water gives **one strike to each character**. Full of Froth and Rage adds +2 prowess to the Animal attack; Wake of War adds another +1 prowess. The Watcher attack is therefore at **11 prowess**.

Wake of War's extra-strike modifier does not create a second strike for every character. The attack already says each character faces one strike; the current combat ruling says strike-count modifiers do not change the number of strikes for this form of attack. The +1 prowess still applies.

Because all six characters are facing a strike from this attack, do not plan on one of them being left outside the attack to support another strike. Resolve the attack using the normal strike sequence and current card wording.

The Hazard player has not played Seized by Terror or Twilight. Under the Academy's supplementary solo handling, those ordinary unplayed draws go to the Hazard discard at the end of this company's Movement/Hazard Phase. Twilight is normally outside the Hazard Limit, but it still needs a legal Environment target; it is not playable here.

### If Gandalf was not in the company

The Hazard draw remains eight because the site and route have not changed. The base Hazard Limit would be five for the five Dwarves.

In that version, the Hazard side must choose five of the six legal cards. A strong model choice is the three Despairs, Watcher and Full of Froth and Rage. It gives three separate corruption-check targets and makes the Watcher attack 10 prowess. Wake of War would add only +1 prowess to this particular attack; its extra strike has no effect on an attack that gives one strike to each character. This is a reasoned example, not a universal answer for every company condition.

### Site Phase — enter Moria or stop?

Moria's automatic attack is **four Orc strikes at 7 prowess**. It is a site attack, not one of the six Hazard plays and not part of the Hazard Limit.

Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War do not modify this Orc attack; their relevant bonuses are for Spider/Animal and Wolf/Spider/Animal attacks respectively.

After the Hazard phase, inspect the actual company:

- How many characters are wounded or tapped?
- Is Gandalf still able to act?
- Is there an untapped character who can play the Book?
- Is the company strong enough to face four Orc strikes at 7?

If you enter Moria and survive its automatic attack, play Book of Mazarbûl if an eligible untapped character can do so. Gandalf is the preferred bearer in this example if he is available to tap: he is a Sage and has +1 to his own corruption checks. If he cannot act, do not pretend a tapped or wounded character can tap to play the item.

If you decline to enter, you avoid the automatic attack, but the Site Phase ends immediately and no Site Phase actions are taken. The Book remains in hand; you will need to return to Moria to play it.

### Book and hand size

Book of Mazarbûl is a unique Special Item with 2 corruption points. It is worth 1 MP while in play and may be stored at a Dwarf-hold for 5 MPs.

If Gandalf bears it, the Sage ability is available, but it is **not automatic**. During a later Organization Phase, you must tap the Book to increase your hand-size target by one until the next Untap Phase. Merely having Gandalf in the company, or merely having him bear an untapped Book, does not change the hand-size target.

The first turn's Movement/Hazard hand reset is still to five: the Book was not in play and its Sage ability has not been activated. The End-of-Turn Phase is a second reset to the current target.

## Turn 2 — choose the next route

Assume the Book was acquired at Moria. The company now has a real choice. The next destination should be chosen from the company's condition, not from a rule that says it must return to a Haven.

### Route A — direct to Goblin-gate

**Region Movement:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass.

This is four regions, including both endpoints. All four are Wilderness regions, and the route is legal if each adjacency is confirmed on the Region cards.

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Destination | Goblin-gate |
| Site-card numbers (Hazard / Hero) | 2/1 |
| Hero draw | Up to 1 |
| Normal Hazard draw | 2 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **6** |
| Six-character Hazard Limit | **6** |
| Automatic attack if entered | Orcs — 3 strikes at 6 |

The company can use Goblin-gate as a waypoint without entering it. If it declines to enter, it avoids the Orc automatic attack but cannot take Site Phase actions there. If it wants to play Gollum, it must enter and face the automatic attack. Gollum is an Ally, not a character, so recruiting him does not increase company size or the next Hazard Limit.

### Route B — use Rivendell as a Haven stop

**Region Movement:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur.

This is three regions and is a legal route from Moria to Rivendell.

Rivendell is a Haven. Under the normal movement rule, a move to a Haven uses the site of origin's draw numbers. The origin is Moria, so this leg uses Moria's 3/2 numbers:

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Destination | Rivendell (Haven) |
| Normal Hazard draw | Moria's 3 |
| Hero draw | Up to 2 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **8** |
| Six-character Hazard Limit | **6** |

The Haven stop costs two more solo Hazard draws than the direct Goblin-gate leg. It also avoids Goblin-gate's optional site attack and gives the company access to Haven healing on the following Untap Phase.

**Haven healing is not a full untap.** A wounded character at a Haven heals to the tapped position during the Untap Phase. If the company moves out in that same turn, that character is healed but remains tapped; it does not become ready before the next Movement/Hazard Phase. To have that character untap normally, the company must remain at the Haven through the following Untap Phase.

## Turn 3 — reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

Both routes can reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold on Turn 3.

### From Goblin-gate

**Region Movement:** High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Four regions, no repeats, all Wilderness.

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Destination | Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold |
| Site-card numbers (Hazard / Hero) | 1/1 |
| Hero draw | Up to 1 |
| Normal Hazard draw | 1 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **4** |
| Six-character Hazard Limit | **6** |
| Automatic attack | None |

### From Rivendell

**Region Movement:** Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Three regions, no repeats, all Wilderness.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold lists **Grey Havens**, not Rivendell, as its nearest Haven. Do not use Starter Movement for this Rivendell-to-Blue-Mountain journey; use the legal three-region Region Movement route.

The destination's normal Hazard draw is still 1, so the solo Hazard draw is **4**. The Hero draw is up to one card, and the six-character Hazard Limit remains six.

### Compare the two expeditions

| Route after Moria | Turn 2 solo draw | Turn 3 solo draw | Two-leg total | Main trade-off |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain | 6 | 4 | **10** | Fewer Hazard draws; optional Goblin-gate Orc attack if you enter; no Haven recovery |
| Moria → Rivendell → Blue Mountain | 8 | 4 | **12** | Two extra Hazard draws; avoids Goblin-gate's site attack and offers Haven healing to the tapped position |

These totals are **draws across two separate Movement/Hazard Phases**, not one combined hand and not a permission to play ten or twelve Hazards in one phase. Each phase has its own six-card Hazard Limit.

Neither route is automatically safer. If the company is fit and wants to avoid the extra two Hazard draws, the direct route is attractive. If it is badly wounded and the Goblin-gate attack would be unacceptable, the Haven route may be worth the extra draw pressure. Remember that Haven healing does not make wounded characters untapped immediately.

## Turn 4 — store the Book

After arriving at Blue Mountain during Turn 3, the company has not yet stored the Book. Storage is an **Organization Phase** action, so it is attempted in the next turn's Organization Phase while the company is at the Dwarf-hold. It is not a Site Phase play; the company does not need to enter Blue Mountain just to store the Book.

- Make the required corruption check for the Book's bearer.
- If the check succeeds, store the Book in the Hero marshalling-point pile for 5 MPs.
- A stored Book is no longer borne by a character; its corruption points and Sage hand-size ability no longer apply.
- Do not claim the five storage MPs if the corruption check fails.

The Book's Sage ability should not be treated as a permanent +1 hand size. If you tapped it in an earlier Organization Phase, that increase lasts only until the next Untap Phase. Once the Book is stored, it is no longer a carried item and no longer supplies the hand-size ability.

## Practice — answer before opening the key

1. Why is Rivendell → Moria Region Movement rather than direct Starter Movement?
2. Why is Seized by Terror illegal on the first route even though Moria is a Shadow-hold?
3. Why does Wake of War add prowess to Watcher in the Water but not an extra strike?
4. What changes in the Hazard Limit if Gandalf is not in the Dwarf company?
5. Which Turn 2 route would you choose if two Dwarves are wounded and the company has already spent its best defensive Resource?
6. When does the Book's Sage hand-size ability become active?
7. Why is Book storage a Turn 4 Organization Phase action rather than a Turn 3 Site Phase action?

Write down the rule and card text that supports each answer, then use the [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/three-turn-expedition/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to check your reasoning.
