# Three-Turn Expedition — Model Answer

[Return to the Three-Turn Expedition workbook]({{ '/scenarios/three-turn-expedition/' | relative_url }})

## 1. The opening state

The sample Hero hand is deliberately constructed. It is not a typical guaranteed draw and must not be stacked into a real game.

Playing Gandalf at Rivendell adds a sixth character to the five-Dwarf company. The Moria solo Hazard draw remains eight because it is determined by the site-card Hazard number, but the base Hazard Limit rises from five to six.

The Book is still in hand. Gandalf's Sage skill does not increase the hand size until he bears the Book and the Book is tapped during an Organization Phase.

## 2. The eight-card Moria Hazard draw

| Card | Legal? | Model decision |
|---|---|---|
| Despair of the Heart ×3 | Yes, one on each of Dori, Kíli and Óin | Play all three; they are different eligible non-Wizard, non-Hobbit targets |
| Watcher in the Water | Yes, explicit Moria permission | Play |
| Full of Froth and Rage | Yes, Watcher is an Animal attack | Play |
| Wake of War | Yes, Watcher is an Animal attack | Play |
| Seized by Terror | No | Do not play: no Shadow-land or Dark-domain appears in the declared site path |
| Twilight | No target | Do not play: no Environment is in play |

The six legal cards use the six-character company's full Hazard Limit. The Hazard side does not get a seventh play because Twilight is outside the limit; Twilight still has to have a legal target. Here it has none. The base Hazard Limit was fixed at the start of the Movement/Hazard Phase; it remains six for this phase even if a character is eliminated during the Despair/Watcher sequence.

### Why three Despairs?

The three Despairs are placed on three different Dwarves. The rule is one corruption card per character per turn, not one per company. Each Despair must start a new chain of effects.

Dori, Kíli and Óin each have a -1 corruption-check modifier, so they are the more vulnerable starting targets. If any character in the company becomes wounded, each of the three targets makes a corruption check. The Despair ruling places that check before the body check. If several Despair checks are triggered, the Hero player resolves them one at a time in an order of their choice. Characters and Wizards in the company may tap to support a corruption check for +1 each, but each helper can contribute to only one of the checks. This corruption-check support rule is separate from combat support: a character assigned a strike cannot tap to support another character against that same attack.

### Why both attack modifiers?

Watcher in the Water is an Animal attack.

- Full of Froth and Rage: +2 prowess to Spider and Animal attacks.
- Wake of War: +1 prowess and +1 strike to Wolf, Spider and Animal attacks.
- Combined prowess increase: +3, so Watcher is at 11 prowess.

The attack says each character faces one strike. The current combat rule prevents a strike-count modifier from adding more strikes to this kind of attack. Wake's +1 prowess still applies; its extra-strike modifier does not. Wake of War's +1 Animal bonus does not require Doors of Night; Doors of Night changes its Wolf bonus. Full of Froth and Rage is discarded if a Spider or Animal attack is defeated.

If Gandalf is in the company, Watcher assigns one strike to each of the six characters. Gandalf cannot be targeted by Despair of the Heart because he is a Wizard, but he is still a character in the company and faces Watcher's strike.

### What if the company has only five Dwarves?

The Hazard Limit is five. A reasonable model line is:

- Despair of the Heart ×3
- Watcher in the Water
- Full of Froth and Rage

This gives three corruption-check targets and a 10-prowess Watcher attack. Wake of War would raise the Watcher to 9 if used instead of Full, because its extra strike does not affect the one-strike-per-character attack. The third Despair adds another target to every wound trigger, so this model chooses the third Despair over Wake's extra +1 prowess.

This is a tactical judgement for the example, not a universal priority list. If a particular character is one point from a critical combat threshold, the extra prowess may be more valuable. Reassess the actual company and hand.

## 3. The Moria automatic attack is separate

Moria's four Orc strikes at 7 prowess happen in the Site Phase only if the Hero company enters.

They do not consume Hazard Limit slots. Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War do not boost this Orc attack. The Hazard cards already played during the Movement/Hazard Phase do not automatically modify every later attack; use each card's printed race/attack conditions.

The Hero player can decline to enter after seeing the company's condition. If it declines, it cannot play the Book or take another Site Phase action that turn.

## 4. Book bearer and hand-size timing

Gandalf is the preferred bearer in this constructed hand if he remains untapped and can tap to play the Book. He is a Sage and has +1 to his own corruption checks.

The Book's hand-size ability is an action: tap the Book during the Organization Phase. It is not a passive bonus for simply being borne by a Sage.

Timeline:

1. **Turn 1 Site Phase:** play the Book at Moria. The Organization Phase has already passed, so the Book's hand-size action is not used this turn.
2. **Turn 2 Organization Phase:** if Gandalf still bears it, tap the Book to increase the hand-size target by one until the next Untap Phase.
3. **Turn 3 Untap Phase:** the temporary hand-size increase expires. The Book untaps normally if it is still in play.
4. **Turn 4 Organization Phase at Blue Mountain:** make the storage corruption check. If successful, place the Book in the MP pile for 5 MPs. Once stored, it is no longer borne and its Sage hand-size ability no longer applies.

If no Sage is available, the Book can still be played and stored. Among the five starting Dwarves, Thorin II and Glóin do not have the -1 corruption-check modifier printed on Dori, Kíli and Óin, so they are the safer non-Sage bearer choices. If a Sage joins later, transferring the Book is not free: the initial bearer must make the required corruption check.

## 5. Route comparison after Moria

### Direct route — Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain

**Turn 2:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass. Four regions, all Wilderness. Goblin-gate is 2/1, so the Hero draws up to one and the solo Hazard side draws six. The six-character Hazard Limit is six.

Goblin-gate's automatic attack is three Orc strikes at 6, but only if the company enters. Declining entry avoids the attack but also prevents site actions such as playing Gollum. Gollum is an Ally, not a character, so recruiting him does not increase company size or the Hazard Limit.

**Turn 3:** High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador. Four regions, all Wilderness. Blue Mountain is 1/1, so Hero draw up to one and solo Hazard draw four. There is no automatic attack.

Total solo Hazard draw across the two movement phases: **6 + 4 = 10**.

### Haven route — Moria → Rivendell → Blue Mountain

**Turn 2:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur. Three regions. Rivendell is a Haven, so use Moria's origin-site Hazard number of three: Hero draw up to two and solo Hazard draw eight.

**Turn 3:** Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador. Three regions. Blue Mountain is a non-Haven destination with a Hazard number of one: Hero draw up to one and solo Hazard draw four.

Total solo Hazard draw across the two movement phases: **8 + 4 = 12**.

The company may heal wounded characters at Rivendell during Turn 3's Untap Phase, but healing returns them to the tapped position. If they move to Blue Mountain in that same turn, they are still tapped during the next Movement/Hazard Phase. A full untap requires remaining at the Haven until the following Untap Phase.

## 6. What makes the route legal?

| Leg | Region sequence | Count | Region types |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Rivendell → Moria | Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate | 3 | Wilderness ×3 |
| Moria → Goblin-gate | Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass | 4 | Wilderness ×4 |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain | High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador | 4 | Wilderness ×4 |
| Moria → Rivendell | Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur | 3 | Wilderness ×3 |
| Rivendell → Blue Mountain | Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador | 3 | Wilderness ×3 |

The direct Moria-to-Blue-Mountain route would require five regions: Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador. It cannot be completed in one normal Region Movement. The Goblin-gate or Rivendell intermediate stop splits the journey into legal legs.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold lists Grey Havens as its nearest Haven. Rivendell → Blue Mountain is therefore Region Movement, not Starter Movement.

## 7. Hazard exposure after the first turn

The later routes remain Wilderness-heavy:

- Giant Spiders can use their double-Wilderness key.
- Lesser Spiders, Neeker-breekers and Wargs have relevant Wilderness keys.
- Cave Worm can use its named-region key when Redhorn Gate or High Pass is in the declared path.
- Watcher in the Water is not a generic double-Wilderness creature. It is not legal on the later legs: they have no Coastal Sea and do not end at Moria.
- Seized by Terror remains illegal on these routes because none contains a Shadow-land or Dark-domain region. A Shadow-hold site is not a Shadow-land region.
- Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War only modify the attack families named on their cards. They do not boost Cave Worm or the Orc automatic attacks at Moria and Goblin-gate.

This is why the route must be declared before the Hazard player selects creatures. A route can make a card legal or illegal even when the destination draw number stays the same.

## 8. Final decision

There is no universal winner between the two routes.

- Choose the direct Goblin-gate route when the company is fit, the lower two-leg Hazard draw matters, and you can decline the site attack if the company arrives too weak.
- Choose the Rivendell route when the Goblin-gate attack is unacceptable and the chance to heal wounds is worth two extra Hazard draws. Do not mistake healing to tapped for a full untap.
- In either case, the Book is stored at Blue Mountain during the next Organization Phase, not during the arrival-turn Site Phase.
