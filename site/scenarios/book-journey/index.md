# Book of Mazarbul — The Second Expedition

**Purpose:** turn the first Moria expedition into a complete campaign decision. You have acquired the Book. Now decide how to get it to a Dwarf-hold, whether to use Goblin-gate as a waypoint, and how to keep the Hazard side honest.

This exercise uses the Academy's fixed Challenge Deck C Hero and Hazard lists. It is conventional Hero solo play, **not Arda**. Keep the physical card lists unchanged while learning the route.

## Starting situation

Assume the following state at the end of Game 1:

- The company is at **Moria**, in **Redhorn Gate**.
- The company has successfully played **Book of Mazarbul**.
- The Book is borne by one of the Dwarves.
- All five starting Dwarves are still in the company.
- No additional card effect changes movement, Hazard draws, Hazard Limits, or site attacks.

If your real game ended differently, use the actual company size, wounds, taps, items and active effects instead of copying the assumptions.

### What is the new mission?

The Book is worth **1 MP while in play**. Its card text allows it to be stored at a **Dwarf-hold for 5 MP**. The Academy's planned destination is **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold**, in Númeriador.

The Book has **2 corruption points**. Storage is not automatic: the bearer must make the required corruption check. If the check succeeds, the Book is placed in your marshalling-point pile for its stored value. Do not add 1 MP and 5 MP together. The bearer choice matters: **Dori, Kíli and Óin each receive -1 to all of their corruption checks**. That can make one of them a better bearer than Thorin or Glóin, but weigh the modifier against who is most likely to be exposed to attacks. Add together all corruption points on the chosen bearer when checking the risk.

The Book's Sage-only hand-size ability is not part of this starting-company plan: none of the five starting Dwarves is a Sage.

## First, get the timing right

You acquired the Book during Moria's Site Phase. The Organization Phase for that turn has already passed, so you cannot go back and store it or declare a new destination.

Your next turn begins with the Untap Phase. Then, during the Organization Phase, declare the next movement. The Book stays with its bearer unless an effect changes that.

**Arrival at Blue Mountain and storing the Book are not the same action.** You arrive during a Movement/Hazard Phase. Storage is an Organization Phase action, so the normal sequence is:

1. Move to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold.
2. Complete that turn's Movement/Hazard and Site Phases.
3. At the start of the next turn, while the company is still at Blue Mountain, attempt to store the Book during the Organization Phase.
4. Resolve the bearer's corruption check. If successful, put the Book in your marshalling-point pile for 5 MP.
5. You may still plan the company's next movement during that Organization Phase.

Storage is not a Site Phase item play. Do not try to store the Book immediately after arriving in the Site Phase.

## Route A — stay away from a Haven

Use Goblin-gate as a non-Haven waypoint.

### Leg 1: Moria → Goblin-gate

**Declared Region Movement path:**

**Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass**

- Four regions, counting origin and destination.
- No repeated region.
- Every adjacent pair is confirmed by the Region cards.
- All four regions are Wilderness.
- Goblin-gate is in High Pass.

Goblin-gate's normal Hazard draw is **2**. The Academy's solo formula therefore draws:

**2 + (2 × 2) = 6 Hazard cards.**

With all five Dwarves still present, the normal base Hazard Limit is **5** for this Movement/Hazard Phase. Recalculate it if the company has changed.

### What can the Hazard player key here?

Check the actual card text, but the Challenge Deck C list gives several route-relevant options:

| Hazard | Why it is worth checking |
|---|---|
| **Cave Worm** | The declared path includes the named region **High Pass** (and Redhorn Gate). |
| **Giant Spiders** | Four Wilderness regions satisfy its double-Wilderness key. |
| **Lesser Spiders** | The path contains Wilderness. Do not rely on a Ruins & Lairs site key: Goblin-gate is a Shadow-hold. |
| **Wargs** | The path contains Wilderness. |
| **Neeker-breekers** | The path contains Wilderness. Read its one-strike-per-character wording exactly. |
| **Watcher in the Water** | Its double-Wilderness key is satisfied. Its special permission to be played at Moria is **not** the reason here: the new site is Goblin-gate. |
| **Seized by Terror** | **Not legal via this route:** there is no Shadow-land or Dark-domain region in the path. Goblin-gate's Shadow-hold site type does not substitute for a Shadow-land region. |

**Despair of the Heart** is a particularly relevant corruption threat: it can be played on a non-Wizard, non-Hobbit character, gives that character 2 corruption points, and causes a corruption check each time a character in that character's company becomes wounded. The Dwarves are eligible targets. **River** is a site-targeting short-event: if the company that moved to the site this turn does not tap a Ranger, it must do nothing during its Site Phase. The company may tap a Ranger by the beginning of the Site Phase to avoid that restriction; Óin is the starting company's Ranger. River counts against the Hazard Limit. **Full of Froth and Rage** and **Wake of War** have their own timing and attack-type effects. A route does not make an event automatically playable.

### Decide whether to enter Goblin-gate

Goblin-gate has an automatic attack of **Orcs — three strikes with 6 prowess**. This is a site attack, not a Hazard card and not part of the Hazard Limit.

At the Site Phase, choose deliberately:

- **Do not enter:** avoid the automatic attack. You cannot take site actions or play cards there during that Site Phase. Goblin-gate is simply your waypoint; you can move again next turn.
- **Enter:** face the automatic attack first. If the company survives and the site and card conditions allow it, you may attempt the optional Gollum objective.

Do not enter merely because you have arrived. Check the company's wounds, tapped characters, defensive Resources and the value of Gollum to the campaign.

## Leg 2: Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

If the company is still fit to continue, declare:

**High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

- Four regions, including origin and destination.
- No repeated region.
- Every adjacent pair is confirmed by the Region cards.
- All four regions are Wilderness.
- Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is in Númeriador.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold's normal Hazard draw is **1**, so the solo draw is:

**2 + (2 × 1) = 4 Hazard cards.**

If all five Dwarves remain, the base Hazard Limit is still **5**. The draw is four, so the Hazard side cannot play more than the four cards drawn; every card must still be legal.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a **Free-hold** by site type, but its name identifies it as a Dwarf-hold. Its Hero site card has no automatic attack. Its nearest Haven is Grey Havens; that does not prevent this legal Region Movement route.

### Hazard checks on the second leg

| Hazard | Why it is worth checking |
|---|---|
| **Cave Worm** | The path includes the named regions **High Pass** and **Númeriador**. |
| **Giant Spiders** | Four Wilderness regions satisfy double Wilderness. |
| **Lesser Spiders, Wargs and Neeker-breekers** | The path contains Wilderness; check the exact card key and target. |
| **Watcher in the Water** | Its double-Wilderness key is satisfied; the Moria-only site permission is not relevant at Blue Mountain. |
| **Seized by Terror** | **Not legal via this route:** all four regions are Wilderness, not Shadow-land or Dark-domain. |
| **River** | Playable on the destination site. Unless the company taps a Ranger by the beginning of its Site Phase, it must do nothing during that Site Phase. Óin can pay this cost if he is able to tap. The card does not rewrite the next turn's Organization Phase storage action. |

Blue Mountain has no automatic attack, but that does not make the journey safe: the company still faces the four-card solo Hazard draw on arrival.

## Route B — take the Haven recovery option

The alternative is to travel:

1. **Moria → Rivendell:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur.
2. **Rivendell → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold:** Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Both are three-region Region Movement routes. Check each adjacency on the physical Region cards.

The first leg ends at a Haven. Under the normal draw rule, a move to a Haven uses the site of origin for the draw number. Moria's normal Hazard draw is 3, so the solo draw is **8**. The second leg moves to Blue Mountain, whose normal draw is 1, so the solo draw is **4**.

At Rivendell, the next Untap Phase gives a recovery opportunity: tapped characters untap, while wounded characters are healed to the tapped position. A character healed this way does not also untap in that same Untap Phase. You can still move onward that turn if the company and plan allow it.

Moria lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, so do **not** use direct Starter Movement from Moria to Rivendell. The route above uses Region Movement.

## Compare the whole expedition

| Plan | First leg | Second leg | What you gain | Main cost |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Goblin-gate waypoint** | 6 Hazard cards | 4 Hazard cards | Two non-Haven legs; no Haven detour; may skip Goblin-gate's site attack by not entering. | No Haven recovery between legs; four-Wilderness exposure on both legs. |
| **Rivendell recovery** | 8 Hazard cards | 4 Hazard cards | Haven recovery opportunity between legs. | More Hazard cards on the first leg; requires a Haven stop. |

These are draws, not guaranteed plays. On each leg, apply the actual Hazard Limit, route keying, card text and company state. A five-character company normally has a base limit of five, but if a character is lost on the first leg, recalculate for the second.
