# Hero Resource Timing Workshop — know what your cards really do

This workshop uses the five starting Dwarves from Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C and the exact 30-card Hero Resource list. It is designed to be completed with the physical cards on the table.

The aim is to stop three common mistakes:

- treating a card's title as if it explains its rules;
- assuming a card is useful at every site or in every phase;
- spending a defensive card without accounting for the character, timing or Hazard-side cost.

Use the [Hero Deck Construction guide]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}) for the fixed list. Do not change the card quantities during this exercise.

## Starting company

| Character | Skills |
|---|---|
| Thorin II | Warrior / Scout / Diplomat |
| Dori | Warrior |
| Kíli | Warrior / Scout |
| Glóin | Warrior / Diplomat |
| Óin | Warrior / Ranger |

All five are Warriors. Thorin II and Kíli are Scouts. Óin is a Ranger. None is a Sage.

## Drill 1 — Which character can use the card?

For each card, identify at least one starting character who can use it, or explain why nobody in the starting company can.

1. Concealment
2. Lucky Search
3. Risky Blow
4. The Dwarves Are Upon You!
5. Marvels Told
6. River (as the Hazard player's card)

Do not answer from memory alone. Read the skill requirement and the starting character cards.

## Drill 2 — The five-card opening hand

You are at Rivendell with the five Dwarves. Your hand contains:

- Fellowship
- Concealment
- Book of Mazarbul
- Great-road
- A Friend or Three

For each card, decide:

- Is it playable now, or does it need a later phase/site/condition?
- Does it support the Book mission?
- What cost or future restriction comes with it?
- Would you play it now, hold it, or discard it?

Then explain whether Fellowship remains in play if you later add a character to the company.

## Drill 3 — Moria's automatic attack

The company reaches Moria. The site card shows **Orcs — four strikes with 7 prowess**.

The following Resources are in your hand:

- Not at Home
- Concealment
- The Dwarves Are Upon You!
- Risky Blow
- Hundreds of Butterflies

For each, decide whether it can affect the Moria attack and what the cost is.

**Do not confuse the site's automatic attack with a Hazard creature attack.** The attack is not a Hazard card and does not consume one of the Hazard Limit slots.

## Drill 4 — Search or secure the mission?

You are at Moria during the site phase. You have Book of Mazarbul and Lucky Search in hand. Thorin II and Kíli are both Scouts.

Decide:

1. Is Lucky Search's site condition met at Moria?
2. Can it find Book of Mazarbul?
3. What attack does the Scout face?
4. What happens to the item if the Scout is wounded?
5. Which Scout, if either, would you use, and why?

Then compare the side objective of finding a non-special item with the main objective of acquiring the Book.

## Drill 5 — Which Hazard events can Marvels Told remove?

Assume a Sage has joined the company in a later expedition. The Hazard side has these cards in play or being played:

- Full of Froth and Rage
- Wake of War
- River
- Seized by Terror
- Twilight

For each, state whether Marvels Told can remove it and why. Remember that Marvels Told has both a Sage requirement and a restriction on the type of Hazard event it can discard.

## Drill 6 — The hidden cost of Hundreds of Butterflies

The company has five characters and a base Hazard Limit of five. During its Movement/Hazard Phase, you consider playing Hundreds of Butterflies on a moving character.

Answer:

- What happens to that character?
- What happens to the company's Hazard Limit?
- Does the number of Hazard cards drawn change?
- Why might this Resource make the Hazard side stronger?

## Drill 7 — Great-road or normal Region Movement?

You are at Rivendell and want to reach Moria. Compare:

- using normal Region Movement to travel to Moria and remain there;
- playing Great-road and using its special return-to-Haven option.

State what Great-road requires, what extra opportunity it gives the Hazard side, and why the Academy does not use it in baseline Game 1 until its solitaire draw interaction is clarified.

## Drill 8 — Moria to Goblin-gate without a Haven

After acquiring the Book, you consider the route:

**Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass**

Answer:

1. How many regions are in the path?
2. What region type do all four share?
3. What is Goblin-gate's normal Hazard draw number, and how many cards does the Academy solo procedure draw?
4. What is the base Hazard Limit for the five Dwarves?
5. What is Goblin-gate's automatic attack?
6. Is Seized by Terror legal just because Goblin-gate is a Shadow-hold?
