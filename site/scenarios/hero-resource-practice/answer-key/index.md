# Hero Resource Timing Workshop — model answers

Use this after completing the [workshop questions]({{ '/scenarios/hero-resource-practice/' | relative_url }}). The aim is to explain the decision from the card text and current game state, not to memorise one scripted sequence.

## Drill 1 — Which character can use the card?

| Card | Correct check |
|---|---|
| **Concealment** | Scout only. Thorin II and Kíli are Scouts, so either can tap to cancel one attack against the company. |
| **Lucky Search** | Scout only. Thorin II and Kíli can attempt it, but only at a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold during the site phase, and they must face its uncancellable search strike. |
| **Risky Blow** | Warrior only against one strike. All five starting Dwarves are Warriors. It gives +3 prowess and -1 body against that strike. |
| **The Dwarves Are Upon You!** | The company must contain Dwarves and be facing an attack. All five starting characters are Dwarves, so all five receive +2 prowess and -1 body against that attack. |
| **Marvels Told** | Sage only. None of the five starting Dwarves is a Sage, so the starting company cannot use it. A Sage must first join. |
| **River** | This is a Hazard, not a Hero Resource. Its site effect can be cancelled by a Ranger in the company tapping, even at the start of the site phase. Óin is the starting Ranger. |

## Drill 2 — The five-card opening hand

- **Fellowship:** playable at Rivendell during the Organization Phase because the company has five characters. It gives +1 prowess and +1 to corruption checks to all characters and allies in the company. It is discarded if any character or ally joins or leaves, so plan company changes before relying on it.
- **Concealment:** hold it for a real attack. It requires a Scout to tap; Thorin II or Kíli can do this. Do not spend it merely because an attack might appear.
- **Book of Mazarbul:** the mission card is only playable at Moria during the site phase, so it is not playable at Rivendell.
- **Great-road:** it can be played only at the end of the Organization Phase on a company at a Haven. It allows the company to return to the Haven where it began at the end of the turn, but the Hazard side may draw up to twice the normal number of cards during the Movement/Hazard Phase. The solo rules do not explicitly state how to combine this modifier with the 2 + twice-normal solitaire draw, so the Academy keeps it out of baseline Game 1.
- **A Friend or Three:** it modifies one influence check or one corruption check by +1 for each character in the company. With five Dwarves, the modifier is +5. It is not a permanent +5 bonus and should be held for a meaningful check.

## Drill 3 — Moria's automatic attack

Moria's automatic attack is **Orcs — four strikes with 7 prowess**.

| Resource | Can it help? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Not at Home** | No, not against this attack. | It cancels Dragon, Drake or Troll attacks, not Orc attacks. Its alternative mode requires Gates of Morning, which is not in the main 30-card Resource list. |
| **Concealment** | Yes. | A Scout can tap to cancel one attack against the company. This can cancel the Moria automatic attack, but it costs the Scout's tap. |
| **The Dwarves Are Upon You!** | Yes. | The company contains Dwarves and is facing an attack; all Dwarves get +2 prowess and -1 body against it. |
| **Risky Blow** | Yes, against one assigned strike. | Choose a Warrior facing a strike; that strike gets +3 prowess and -1 body. It does not modify all four strikes. |
| **Hundreds of Butterflies** | Not as an attack modifier. | It can untap a moving character during the Movement/Hazard Phase, but it also increases the Hazard Limit by one. It does not cancel or weaken the site attack. |

The site automatic attack is resolved during the site encounter. It is separate from the Movement/Hazard Phase's Hazard Limit.

## Drill 4 — Search or secure the mission?

1. **Yes, the site condition is met.** Moria is a Shadow-hold [{S}], and Lucky Search can be used at a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold.
2. **No, it cannot find Book of Mazarbul.** Lucky Search finds a non-special item; Book of Mazarbul is a Special Item.
3. The Scout faces a single strike with prowess equal to **3 plus the number of cards revealed**.
4. If the Scout is wounded, the item found is discarded. The strike cannot be cancelled.
5. There is no automatic best Scout. Compare their current condition, their other planned uses, the likely search depth and the value of finding an ordinary item. Do not divert from the Book mission just because Lucky Search is available.

## Drill 5 — Which Hazard events can Marvels Told remove?

| Hazard | Can Marvels Told remove it? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Full of Froth and Rage** | Yes. | It is a Hazard permanent-event and is not an Environment. |
| **Wake of War** | Yes. | It is a Hazard long-event and is not an Environment. |
| **River** | No. | It is a short-event, not a permanent-event or long-event. |
| **Seized by Terror** | No. | It is a short-event. |
| **Twilight** | No. | It is an Environment card, which Marvels Told cannot discard. |

Marvels Told also requires a Sage to tap and make a corruption check modified by -2. It is not a free cancellation.

## Drill 6 — The hidden cost of Hundreds of Butterflies

It untaps the moving character, but increases the company's Hazard Limit by one. The Hazard draw count does **not** change.

For the five-Dwarf company:
- base Hazard Limit: 5;
- after Hundreds of Butterflies: 6;
- solo Hazard draw to Moria: still 8.

That extra limit slot can let the Hazard side play one more limit-counting card. The Resource may still be correct if the untapped character is needed for an important action, but it is not a free defensive benefit.

## Drill 7 — Great-road or normal Region Movement?

Normal Region Movement is the general rule that lets you travel between sites when the region route is legal. It does not require a Haven stop after every expedition.

Great-road is a specific short-event played at the end of the Organization Phase on a company at a Haven. It allows the Hazard side to draw up to twice the normal number of cards during that company's Movement/Hazard Phase, then permits the company to return to its starting Haven at the end of the turn without another Movement/Hazard Phase.

The card does not remove the need for a legal journey to the destination. It is a special sortie-and-return option with a substantial Hazard-side cost. Because the solitaire rules do not expressly resolve how its doubled draw interacts with the solo 2 + twice-normal formula, do not use it in the Academy's baseline Game 1.

## Drill 8 — Moria to Goblin-gate without a Haven

1. The route contains **four regions**, including origin and destination.
2. All four are **Wilderness**.
3. Goblin-gate's normal Hazard draw is **2**, so the Academy solo draw is **2 + (2 × 2) = 6**.
4. The five-character company's base Hazard Limit is **5**.
5. Goblin-gate's automatic attack is **Orcs — three strikes with 6 prowess**.
6. **No.** Seized by Terror requires a Shadow-land [{s}] or Dark-domain [{d}] region in the site path. Goblin-gate is a Shadow-hold [{S}] site. The uppercase site type does not satisfy the lowercase region-type condition.

The route is a useful example of non-Haven-to-non-Haven Region Movement, but it is not automatically safe. It exposes four Wilderness regions and draws six solo Hazards. The site attack is faced only if the company chooses to enter Goblin-gate.

## Drill 9 — Continue to Blue Mountain without a Haven

The route **High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador** is legal: each Region card lists the next region as adjacent, the route has four regions including both endpoints, and all four are Wilderness.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold has a normal Hazard draw of **1**, so the solo draw is **2 + (2 × 1) = 4**. The five-character company's base Hazard Limit is **5**. The site has no automatic attack.

The site card's site-type symbol is Free-hold [{F}], but the location is named **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold**. Book of Mazarbul's text says it may be stored at a Dwarf-hold; it does not require a particular site-type icon. This is the Dwarf-hold in the teaching Location deck.

Store the Book during the **Organization Phase** while the company is at the site. The bearer must make the normal corruption check required before storing an item. The Book has two corruption points, so the check is not automatic. If the check succeeds, the Book is placed in the marshalling-point pile for its **5 MP storage value**. Do not add the one-point in-play value to the five-point storage value.

The complete no-Haven campaign line is therefore:

1. **Moria → Goblin-gate:** Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass; solo draw 6. You may choose not to enter Goblin-gate and avoid its automatic attack, but you cannot play Gollum or take other site-phase actions there.
2. **Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold:** High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador; solo draw 4, no site automatic attack.
3. **Store Book of Mazarbul:** during the Organization Phase at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, after the bearer's corruption check.

This avoids a Haven return, but it does not avoid Hazard exposure. Both legs contain four Wilderness regions.

## Final checklist

- [ ] I matched each Resource to the skill of a character who can use it.
- [ ] I checked the phase and site requirements before playing it.
- [ ] I separated the site's automatic attack from Hazard plays.
- [ ] I counted the cost of tapping a Scout or Ranger.
- [ ] I remembered that Hundreds of Butterflies raises the Hazard Limit.
- [ ] I did not use Not at Home against an Orc attack.
- [ ] I did not treat a Shadow-hold site as a Shadow-land region.
- [ ] I used Great-road only as its printed special effect, not as a substitute for Region Movement.
