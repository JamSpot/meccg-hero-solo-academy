# Resource Route Planner — Choose the Expedition

The Hero Resource list contains more site-playable cards than one company should try to collect in a single campaign. This workbook teaches you to choose a useful objective, then plan a legal route and site attempt around the cards actually in your hand.

It uses the fixed 30-Resource Challenge Deck C-derived list and the Academy's Gandalf adaptation. **It does not change the 36-card Hero play deck or the 30-card Hazard deck.**

## 1. Resource target map

The historical *Challenge Decks Player's Guide* gives these suggested sites for the Dwarven Quest Resource cards. Treat the list as a destination guide, not a mandatory itinerary.

| Resource | Suggested target | Main planning point |
|---|---|---|
| **Book of Mazarbûl** | **Moria**; later store at a **Dwarf-hold**, such as Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | The first objective is to play it at Moria. The later storage expedition is a separate movement and corruption-check decision. |
| **Gollum** | **Goblin-gate** | Optional side objective. You must enter the site and survive its automatic attack before attempting to play the ally. |
| **Iron Hill Dwarves** | **Iron Hill Dwarf-hold** | This is a main-deck faction and requires an influence check. Do not confuse it with Blue Mountain Dwarves. |
| **Blue Mountain Dwarves** *(sideboard)* | **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold** | Not in the active 36-card main play deck. Use only after learning the avatar's sideboard-access action. |
| **Men of Dorwinion** | **Shrel-Kain** | A later faction objective; do not divert from the Book mission without a reason. |
| **Bow of Dragon-horn** | **Ovir Hollow**, or the *Bounty of the Hoard* line | Check the exact item and site conditions before committing to the trip. |
| **Magical Harp** | **Ovir Hollow**, or the *Bounty of the Hoard* line | A possible later item objective, not an automatic detour. |
| **Durin's Axe** | **Sarn Goriwing** or **Buhr Widu** | Powerful for a Dwarf bearer, but include its corruption points in the risk calculation. |
| **Hauberk of Bright Mail** | **Sarn Goriwing** or **Buhr Widu** | Compare the item's defensive value with the route and site danger. |
| **Enruned Shield** | **Caves of Ûlund** or **The Lonely Mountain** | Check the site's automatic attack and whether the company can still attempt the item. |
| **Wormsbane** | **Dead Marshes**, **Caves of Ûlund**, or **The Lonely Mountain** | A specialist weapon; pursue it when the route and expected threats justify it. |
| **Quickbeam** | **Wellinghall** | Ally objective with its own site and company requirements. |

**Important distinction:** *Iron Hill Dwarves* is in the active Resource list and is playable at Iron Hill Dwarf-hold. *Blue Mountain Dwarves* is a sideboard card and is playable at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. They are different factions at different sites.

## 2. What must be true before a Resource is played?

A site name in the target map does not by itself make a card playable.

- **Items and allies:** normally require an untapped company character and an untapped site during the Site Phase. The character taps to play the card; a successful play normally taps the site.
- **Factions:** require an untapped character, the appropriate site, and an influence check. Calculate the relevant direct influence and faction modifiers before attempting it.
- **Automatic attacks:** the company must enter the site and face its automatic attack before attempting ordinary site-phase Resources that tap the site.
- **Book storage:** storing Book of Mazarbûl is not an item play during the Site Phase. Its special Dwarf-hold storage is an Organization Phase action and requires the bearer to pass the storage corruption check.

Use the current [Council of Elrond rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) for the normal Site Phase and storage procedure, and the actual card text for each Resource.

## 3. Three priorities for the opening campaign

### Priority A — the Book mission

1. Start at Rivendell with the five Dwarves.
2. Move to Moria using the legal route in the [Expedition Planner]({{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}).
3. Check the company's condition before entering Moria and facing its automatic attack.
4. Attempt to play Book of Mazarbûl only if the company can reasonably survive the site attempt.
5. Record the bearer and all corruption points immediately.

### Priority B — optional Gollum

Goblin-gate is a legal non-Haven waypoint on the Book-storage route. If the company is healthy and Gollum is in hand, you can consider entering Goblin-gate and attempting the ally after its automatic attack. If the company is too weak, do not enter merely to use the card. A company that does not enter cannot play Gollum there during that Site Phase.

### Priority C — store the Book

The Academy's worked route is Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. The direct Moria-to-Blue-Mountain Region Movement would require five regions and is too long for one normal move. Once the company reaches Blue Mountain, store the Book during the next Organization Phase while still at the Dwarf-hold. The Book's special text permits storage at a Dwarf-hold even though the general storage rule normally refers to Havens.

## 4. Practice tasks

1. Mark each of these as **main play deck** or **sideboard**: Iron Hill Dwarves; Blue Mountain Dwarves; Book of Mazarbûl; Gollum.
2. You have Book of Mazarbûl in hand and the company is at Rivendell. What is the primary destination?
3. You reach Goblin-gate with two wounded Dwarves and Gollum in hand. What must you weigh before entering?
4. You reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold with the Book. Can you store it during that turn's Site Phase? When is the first normal storage opportunity?
5. You draw Iron Hill Dwarves while at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. Is that the correct site for the faction?
6. You have Men of Dorwinion in hand. Which site does the Challenge Deck player aid identify?
7. Why is it a mistake to plan a single route that tries to play every site-playable Resource in the deck?
