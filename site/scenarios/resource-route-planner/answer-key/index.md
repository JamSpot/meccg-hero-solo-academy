# Resource Route Planner — Model Answer

Use this after completing the [Resource Route Planner]({{ '/scenarios/resource-route-planner/' | relative_url }}). The goal is to make a focused expedition plan, not to visit every target site.

## 1. Main play deck or sideboard?

| Card | Correct zone |
|---|---|
| Iron Hill Dwarves | Main 30-Resource list |
| Blue Mountain Dwarves | Sideboard only |
| Book of Mazarbûl | Main 30-Resource list |
| Gollum | Main 30-Resource list |

Blue Mountain Dwarves is not a card you can expect to draw in the fixed 36-card Hero play deck. It requires the Wizard's sideboard-access action in an advanced game. The first three baseline games keep sideboard changes closed so the deck tests remain comparable.

## 2. Primary destination for Book of Mazarbûl

**Moria.** The Book is only playable there. The later storage destination is a Dwarf-hold, with Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold as the Academy's low-automatic-attack route objective. Playing the Book and storing it are two separate stages.

## 3. Gollum at Goblin-gate

Goblin-gate has an automatic attack of **three Orc strikes at 6 prowess**. The company must enter the site to attempt Gollum, and the automatic attack is resolved first. With two wounded Dwarves, check the likely strike assignments, available avoidance, taps, body-check risk, and whether Gollum is important enough to justify the danger. If the company declines to enter, it avoids the automatic attack but cannot play Gollum during that Site Phase.

## 4. Book storage timing

No. Storage is an Organization Phase action, not a Site Phase play. If the company arrives at Blue Mountain during a Movement/Hazard Phase, the first normal opportunity is the **next turn's Organization Phase**, while the company is still at the Dwarf-hold. The bearer makes the storage corruption check. A successful storage places the Book in the marshalling-point pile for **5 MP**; do not add its 1 MP in-play value to the stored value.

## 5. Iron Hill Dwarves at Blue Mountain?

No. The main-deck **Iron Hill Dwarves** faction is playable at **Iron Hill Dwarf-hold**. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is the target for the separate **Blue Mountain Dwarves** sideboard faction. Do not swap the names or site destinations.

## 6. Men of Dorwinion

The Challenge Deck C player aid identifies **Shrel-Kain** as its suggested site.

## 7. Why not pursue everything?

Each detour adds movement, Hazard exposure, a site-entry decision, and the risk of losing characters or spending key Resources. The company has a coherent primary mission: acquire the Book at Moria and store it at a Dwarf-hold. Gollum can be an optional objective if the company is fit. Factions and equipment at other sites are later opportunities when a route, hand and company state make them worthwhile. A card in hand is not a command to travel to its site.
