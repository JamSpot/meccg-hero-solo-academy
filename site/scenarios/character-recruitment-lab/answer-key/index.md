# Support Character Recruitment Lab — Model Answer

## Exercise 1 — play one character card

You cannot play Gandalf and Balin in the same Organization Phase. The normal character-play allowance is one character action per turn; playing the avatar uses that action.

For the Book of Mazarbûl mission, **Gandalf is the default first play** if he is available and can join the company at Rivendell. He supplies Sage, Scout and Diplomat skills, has +1 to his own corruption checks, and can use the Book's Sage-only ability if he bears it.

Balin remains a useful later recruit. If Gandalf travels with the company to Moria, Balin can be played at the avatar's current site on a later turn. The avatar's current site matters; do not assume that any Haven remains available for character play after an avatar is in play.

## Exercise 2 — avatar location restriction

1. **No, not merely because Rivendell is a Haven.** Once an avatar is in play, the character-play rule restricts where further characters can be played.
2. **Yes.** Moria is Gandalf's current site, so Gildor can be played there, subject to the normal influence and company rules.
3. **Potentially yes.** The rules also allow a character to be played under direct influence with an existing company. Check that company's actual unused direct influence and its follower assignments; do not count printed direct influence that is already committed.

## Exercise 3 — general influence

The starting company has seven unused general influence. With the original assignments unchanged:

| Recruit | Mind | GI remaining |
|---|---:|---:|
| Balin | 5 | **2** |
| Boromir II | 4 | **3** |
| Celeborn | 6 | **1** |
| Fíli | 2 | **5** |
| Gildor | 4 | **3** |

- **Sage:** Balin and Celeborn.
- **Best Dwarf-faction synergy:** Balin. He is a Dwarf Sage, gains +2 prowess against Orcs and has +1 direct influence against Dwarves and Dwarf factions.
- **Lowest mind:** Fíli (2).
- **Strongest Orc-combat contribution among the listed non-Sages:** Gildor has +2 prowess against Orcs. Fíli has +1. Both duplicate a skill already present (Ranger for Gildor, Scout for Fíli).

The table assumes each recruit is controlled by general influence and the original Thorin/Glóin assignments are retained. If you rearrange followers or use another legal control arrangement, recalculate.

## Exercise 4 — company size and Hazard Limit

| Company | Size | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Five starting Dwarves | 5 | **5** |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf | 6 | **6** |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf + Balin | 7 | **7** |

The normal maximum company size away from a Haven is **seven characters**. At a Haven the company-size restriction is different, but reorganise before leaving if the company would exceed the non-Haven limit.

Adding characters does **not** change the solo Hazard draw formula. That formula is based on the destination site's normal Hazard draw number. Adding characters **does** increase the base Hazard Limit because the limit is based on company size (minimum two). A larger company can therefore face more ordinary Hazard plays in the same Movement/Hazard Phase.

## Exercise 5 — a sensible Book-mission sequence

A straightforward default is:

1. **Play Gandalf first** at Rivendell if he is in hand. This supplies a Sage immediately and gives the Book a safer default bearer.
2. **Move the company with Gandalf** if the route and Hazard pressure justify it. The company is now six characters and its base Hazard Limit is six.
3. **Consider Balin on a later turn** if the extra Sage/Dwarf/Orc-fighting contribution is worth using the final company slot. With all seven characters together, the company is at the non-Haven size limit and its base Hazard Limit is seven.
4. **Do not add Gildor automatically.** His +2 prowess against Orcs is attractive, but the company already has a Ranger and a seven-character company cannot take another character away from a Haven.
5. **Recalculate before every movement.** Site draw determines the solo Hazard draw; company size determines the base Hazard Limit. They are separate numbers.

This is a default teaching line, not a mandatory script. If Gandalf is not drawn, Balin is the best non-Wizard Sage backup in this Dwarf package. If neither Sage is available, the company can still attempt the Book mission, but the Sage-only Book benefit is not available until a Sage is in the company and bears it.
