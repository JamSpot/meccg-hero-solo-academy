# Support Character Recruitment Lab

**Purpose:** practise when to bring Gandalf or another support character into the Dwarf company, how direct and general influence interact, and why adding characters also increases Hazard pressure.

This exercise uses the unchanged **36-card Hero play deck** and the five starting Dwarves. It is a conventional Hero solo exercise, not Arda.

## Starting position

- Five starting Dwarves are at Rivendell.
- No Wizard is in play.
- The starting direct-influence assignments are unchanged: Thorin controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin.
- Thorin II and Glóin have combined mind 13, leaving **7 general influence** from the starting 20.
- The Hero play deck contains Gandalf, Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli and Gildor.
- The company is five characters, so its base Hazard Limit is **5**.

The five Dwarves cover Warrior, Scout, Ranger and Diplomat skills, but **not Sage**. Gandalf, Balin and Celeborn are the Sage-capable cards in the active character package.

## Exercise 1 — Gandalf and Balin in the same hand

Your opening hand contains Gandalf and Balin. You are at Rivendell and have no Wizard in play.

1. Can you play both cards during this Organization Phase?
2. Which one is the stronger default first play for the Book of Mazarbûl expedition?
3. If you play Gandalf now and Balin remains in hand, can you play Balin on a later turn after the company has moved to Moria with Gandalf?

## Exercise 2 — the avatar's current site

Gandalf is already in play and travelling with the Dwarves. The company is now at Moria. You draw Gildor.

1. Can you play Gildor at Rivendell merely because Rivendell is a Haven?
2. Can you play Gildor at the avatar's current site, Moria?
3. What if a separate company at Rivendell has enough available direct influence to control Gildor?

## Exercise 3 — the seven-point influence budget

Assume the original Thorin/Glóin follower assignments remain unchanged and each recruit is controlled by general influence. Calculate the remaining general influence after adding each candidate separately.

| Recruit | Mind | GI remaining after recruitment |
|---|---:|---:|
| Balin | 5 | ? |
| Boromir II | 4 | ? |
| Celeborn | 6 | ? |
| Fíli | 2 | ? |
| Gildor | 4 | ? |

Then answer:

- Which two recruits provide Sage?
- Which recruit has the strongest Dwarf-faction synergy?
- Which is the cheapest recruit by mind?
- Which recruit improves Orc combat most without adding a new skill to the starting company?

## Exercise 4 — more characters, more Hazard pressure

Assume all characters join the same company:

| Company | Size | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Five starting Dwarves | 5 | ? |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf | 6 | ? |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf + Balin | 7 | ? |

1. What is the maximum company size away from a Haven, and what is the limit at a Haven?
2. Does adding characters change the solo Hazard **draw formula**?
3. Does it change the base Hazard **Limit**?

## Exercise 5 — choose a recruitment plan

You are preparing for the Book of Mazarbûl mission and have Gandalf, Balin and Gildor available over three turns. Choose a sensible recruitment order and explain:

- when the company gains a Sage;
- which character is the preferred Book bearer;
- how the company size changes;
- why the Hazard Limit must be recalculated before each movement.
