# First Session Practice Workbook

Use this after [Game 1 — First Expedition]({{ '/games/game-1/' | relative_url }}). Answer before opening the [answer key](answer-key/). For each exercise, record your decision, reason, risk and what would change your mind. Check printed card text whenever exact wording matters.

## 1. Count the regions
A → B → C → D. A and D contain the origin and destination; all pairs are adjacent and no region repeats. Is this within the normal Region Movement limit? What else must you check?

## 2. Five regions
A → B → C → D → E. Every connection is adjacent. Can this be one normal Region Movement? Why is adjacency not enough?

## 3. Repeated region
A → B → C → B → D. Is it legal if every individual connection is adjacent?

## 4. Rivendell to Moria
Your objective is Book of Mazarbul. The teaching route is **Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**. Identify origin, destination and region count. Give one benefit and one risk.

## 5. Draw versus limit
Under the Academy's solo procedure, Moria's site number is 3 and the Hazard draw is **2 + (2 × site number)**. The company has five characters. How many cards are drawn, and what is the base Hazard Limit?

## 6. Choose a Hazard
One drawn Hazard is illegal; another is legal but has little impact. What should you check before playing? Why is the biggest-looking attack not automatically best?

## 7. Sequence
You have a legal creature and a card that may strengthen it if the printed wording permits. What must you verify before choosing the order?

## 8. Reassess
The company reaches Moria, but characters are tapped or wounded and key defensive resources are spent. Name three things to inspect before attempting the objective.

## 9. After the Book
The company acquires Book of Mazarbul but is weakened. Another site offers some value without urgency. Give one reason to press on and one to recover.

## 10. Debrief
What rule did you forget? Which decision was hardest? What would you change on your next expedition?

The Academy uses a labelled teaching hybrid: the historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C card baseline, normal MECCG movement and Hazard rules, and the identified solo procedure on the [solo-rules page]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}). Use the [official rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) and card text for exact rulings.
