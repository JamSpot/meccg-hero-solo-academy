# First Session Practice Workbook

Twelve short exercises covering route legality, site paths, Hazard draw versus Hazard Limit, Hazard sequencing, site-entry decisions, changing objectives, and recovery. Answer before checking the key. For each, record your decision, reason, risk, and what would change your mind.

See the [solo ruleset]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}) and [Game 1]({{ '/games/game-1/' | relative_url }}). The Academy uses a labelled teaching hybrid; use printed card text and the [official rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) when exact wording matters.
