# First Session Practice Workbook — Answer Key

Try the [workbook questions]({{ '/scenarios/first-session/' | relative_url }}) before reading these model answers. These are reasoning guides, not substitutes for the printed card text or official rules.

## 1. Count the regions

**Model answer:** A → B → C → D has four regions, including the origin and destination. It is within the normal Region Movement limit if A contains the origin, D contains the destination, each adjacent pair is legal, and no region repeats.

## 2. Five regions

**Model answer:** No. A → B → C → D → E contains five regions. Adjacency alone does not overcome the normal four-region limit. A specific card effect would need to permit the longer movement.

## 3. Repeated region

**Model answer:** No. A → B → C → B → D repeats B. The normal Region Movement sequence cannot repeat a region even if each individual connection is adjacent.

## 4. Rivendell to Moria

**Model answer:** Rivendell is in Rhudaur; Moria is in Redhorn Gate. Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate contains three regions. The benefit is reaching Moria in one legal Region Movement. The cost is exposure: all three regions are Wilderness, and all three region names are declared for Region Movement keying. A double-Wilderness creature may be eligible if its own card text and all other conditions permit it.

## 5. Draw versus limit

**Model answer:** The solo draw is 2 + (2 × 3) = **8 Hazard cards**. A five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of **5**. The solo draw tells you how many cards are available; the Hazard Limit caps the number of Hazard plays that count against it.

## 6. Choose a Hazard

**Model answer:** First remove any illegal card from consideration. For each legal card, check its target/key, timing, card text, and effect on the company's mission. Choose the most damaging legal play rather than judging only by attack prowess.

## 7. Sequence

**Model answer:** Read the full printed wording of both cards. Confirm legal timing, target, keying and whether the first card changes the conditions for the second. Thematically related cards do not automatically form a legal combination. Keep within the Hazard Limit.

## 8. Reassess

**Model answer:** Inspect at least: (1) who is tapped or wounded; (2) who can still handle combat and the site's automatic attack; (3) which avoidance/defence resources remain in hand; (4) whether the objective can still be attempted; and (5) what recovery would cost or preserve.

## 9. After the Book

**Model answer:** Press on if the company remains capable, the next destination is genuinely valuable, and the route and Hazard forecast justify the risk. Recover if critical characters are wounded/tapped, key defences are spent, or the next reward is not urgent. Re-check route legality: the direct Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador line is five regions, so it is not a normal one-turn Region Movement to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold.

## 10. Debrief

There is no single correct reflective answer. Record one rule you forgot, the decision with the highest uncertainty, and one specific change to test on the next run. Change one thing at a time so you can tell whether the decision improved.

## Rules reminder

The Academy combines a historical Challenge Deck C card-list baseline, normal MECCG movement/Hazard rules, and a separately identified solo procedure. For movement, site paths and Hazard Limits, consult the [Council of Elrond Official Game Rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) and the [Academy solo ruleset]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}).
