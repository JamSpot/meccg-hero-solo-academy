# Route Lab — Practice Workbook

This workbook moves beyond memorising the Rivendell-to-Moria example. Use the physical Region cards or a legal map, and check card text whenever a Hazard or site rule matters. Write your answer before opening the [Route Lab answer key]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).

For each route, record: **destination purpose, movement type, legality, declared site path, Hazard exposure, arrival condition, next position**.

## Scenario 1 — Plan now, declare the site path later

During Organization, you declare Moria as the destination and plan Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate. When does the company reveal Moria, and when is the Region Movement site path declared?

## Scenario 2 — Three Wilderness regions

The company moves Rivendell → Moria via Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate. What region types and names are exposed? What must be checked before a double-Wilderness creature can be played?

## Scenario 3 — The tempting five-region trip

After acquiring Book of Mazarbul, you want to move directly from Moria to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. The proposed route is Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador. Is it legal as one normal Region Movement? What should you do instead?

## Scenario 4 — Same destination, different route

Two routes to the same destination are both legal. Route A names fewer regions; Route B gives a better next-turn position. List at least four factors to compare before choosing.

## Scenario 5 — Eight drawn Hazards, five slots

Your five-character company moves to Moria. The solo procedure draws eight Hazard cards. Explain how many cards are available, the base Hazard Limit, and how to choose plays.

## Scenario 6 — The legal but low-impact Hazard

One drawn card is illegal. Three are legal: a modest creature, a disruption event, and an amplifier that only helps if another legal card is played. What is your order of checks?

## Scenario 7 — The damaged expedition

The company reaches the destination, but its best fighter is wounded, two characters are tapped and the key avoidance card has been spent. The site still offers an objective. What do you inspect before entering?

## Scenario 8 — Objective complete, plan changed

Book of Mazarbul has been acquired. Your first plan was to continue west immediately. Explain why the objective change requires a fresh route plan and what you should check before choosing the next site.

## Scenario 9 — Map versus region card

The map appears to show two regions touching, but one region card does not list the other as adjacent. What is the correct action?

## Scenario 10 — Route debrief

After a game, your company survived but arrived too weak to attempt the objective. Record: (a) one route decision you made, (b) one Hazard exposure it created, (c) one alternative route or intermediate stop, and (d) the evidence that would make you choose the alternative next time.

## Scoring yourself

Give yourself one point for each of the following in every answer where relevant:

- legality checked before strategy;
- origin and destination counted;
- no repeated region;
- site path distinguished from physical movement;
- Hazard keying checked against card text;
- destination purpose stated;
- arrival and site entry treated as separate decisions;
- next position or recovery plan considered.

The aim is not to get a high score by guessing. The aim is to explain your reasoning clearly enough that another player could audit it.
