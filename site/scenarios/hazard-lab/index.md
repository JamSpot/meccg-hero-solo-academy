# Hazard Lab — Practice Workbook

A practical set of solo Hazard decision drills is being assembled here. Use the printed card text and official rules for every card-specific ruling.

## Exercise 1 — Eight cards, five slots

A five-character company moves to Moria. The Academy solo procedure draws eight Hazard cards. Only four of the eight are legal. How many can be played, and should a useful legal play be skipped just because it seems less dramatic?

## Exercise 2 — Double Wilderness

Rivendell to Moria uses Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate. All three are Wilderness. What must you check before playing a creature keyed to double Wilderness?

## Exercise 3 — Timing

When do you reveal the destination, and when do you declare the Region Movement site path?

## Exercise 4 — No movement

A company stays at its site. Does it have a site path? Which creature keys might still be available?

## Exercise 5 — Sequencing

A creature and an event might interact. What must you verify before spending two Hazard Limit slots?

## Exercise 6 — Objective pressure

A strong fighter and the character needed for the objective are both possible targets. How do you compare the plays?

## Exercise 7 — Route debrief

Record which regions made a creature legal and whether another legal route could have reduced exposure.
