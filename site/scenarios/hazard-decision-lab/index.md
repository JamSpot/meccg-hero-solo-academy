# Hazard Decision Lab — Choose the Strongest Legal Line

This lab teaches the solo Hazard player's most important judgement: **do not simply play the biggest-looking creature first. Build the most disadvantageous legal sequence from the cards actually drawn, while respecting the route, target, timing and Hazard Limit.**

The solo procedure says to draw (2 + 2 \times) the normal Hazard-player draw for the site, then play as many drawn Hazards as possible within the company's Hazard Limit. It also directs the player to choose the Hazards in the fashion most disadvantageous for the Hero company. The exercises below use constructed hands so you can practise decisions; they are not promised or stacked real draws.

## Before choosing a card: keep five numbers and facts separate

1. **Hero movement draw:** the Hero player draws from the 36-card Hero play deck using the lighter site-card number, if the company qualifies.
2. **Normal Hazard draw:** read the darker site-card number. For a move to a Haven, use the site of origin; otherwise use the new site.
3. **Solo Hazard draw:** (2 + 2 \times) the normal Hazard draw. This is how many cards you inspect from the separate Hazard deck.
4. **Hazard Limit:** normally the company size or two, whichever is greater (apply the relevant rounding and card effects). It is fixed for that company's Movement/Hazard Phase.
5. **Site automatic attack:** this is faced later in the Site Phase only if the Hero company enters. It is not one of the Hazard Limit slots.

The current [Council of Elrond rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) govern normal timing, creature keying and the fixed Hazard Limit. The original solitaire procedure is reproduced in [The Wizards rules](https://meccg.com/rules/by-expansion/the-wizards/); the supplementary end-of-phase discard and Hazard-deck recycling convention used by this Academy is explained in [My Solo Rules](https://meccg.wordpress.com/my-solo-rules/).

## The decision sequence

Use this order every time:

1. **Declare the route first.** Write the actual site path. Do not key a creature to a region that is merely nearby on the map.
2. **Sort the draw.** Mark each card legal, illegal, or legal only if a stated target/condition exists.
3. **Count slots.** Count ordinary Hazard plays against the fixed Hazard Limit. Remember explicit exceptions such as Twilight.
4. **Identify the expedition's weak point.** Is the Hero company trying to play an item, reach a faction, recover at a Haven, or simply reposition?
5. **Plan the sequence.** Events that modify an attack must be in effect before that attack resolves. A creature's attack resolves immediately when that creature is played.
6. **Choose the most damaging legal line.** Consider strike count, prowess, special effects, corruption triggers, and the Hero player's available skills/resources.
7. **Reassess after each attack.** Do not assume a support event remains in play after its printed discard condition occurs.

There is not one universal ranking of creatures. A single high-prowess strike may be worse for a company with a vulnerable low-body character; several lower-prowess strikes may be worse when the company has few untapped defenders. Use the actual company state.

## Drill 1 — Moria: corruption pressure and the Watcher

**Company:** Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin, Óin and Gandalf (six characters).

**Movement:** Rivendell → Moria by Region Movement, with the declared path Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate. The three regions are Wilderness; the destination is Moria.

**Site-card numbers:** Moria is 3/2 (opponent / Hero).

- Hero movement draw: up to 2 cards.
- Normal Hazard draw: 3.
- Solo Hazard draw: (2 + 2 \times 3 = 8).
- Base Hazard Limit: 6, fixed for this phase.

**Constructed eight-card Hazard draw:**

| Card | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Despair of the Heart | 3 |
| Watcher in the Water | 1 |
| Full of Froth and Rage | 1 |
| Wake of War | 1 |
| Seized by Terror | 1 |
| Twilight | 1 |
| **Total** | **8** |

Answer before opening the key:

- Which six cards can be played?
- Which three characters can receive Despair of the Heart?
- What happens to Watcher's strike count and prowess when both support events are active?
- Why is Seized by Terror not made legal by Moria being a Shadow-hold?
- If the Hero company enters Moria, can a wound from Moria's automatic attack trigger the Despairs?

## Drill 2 — Goblin-gate: sequence the creature package

**Company:** the same six-character Dwarf company with Gandalf.

**Movement:** Moria → Goblin-gate by Region Movement, with the path Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass. All four regions are Wilderness.

**Site-card numbers:** Goblin-gate is 2/1 (opponent / Hero).

- Hero movement draw: up to 1 card.
- Normal Hazard draw: 2.
- Solo Hazard draw: (2 + 2 \times 2 = 6).
- Base Hazard Limit: 6.

**Constructed six-card Hazard draw:**

| Card | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Cave Worm | 1 |
| Giant Spiders | 1 |
| Lesser Spiders | 1 |
| Wargs | 1 |
| Full of Froth and Rage | 1 |
| Wake of War | 1 |
| **Total** | **6** |

Answer before opening the key:

- Which region or site key makes each creature legal?
- Which two events should be active before the Spider/Wolf attacks?
- Which attacks receive both modifiers, and which receive only Wake of War?
- If the first Spider attack is defeated, what happens to Full of Froth and Rage before the next Spider attack?
- Is Goblin-gate's three-strike Orc automatic attack part of these six Hazard plays?

## Drill 3 — Blue Mountain: a low draw does not mean a low limit

**Company:** the same six-character company.

**Movement:** Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold by Region Movement, with the path High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador. All four regions are Wilderness.

**Site-card numbers:** Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is 1/1 (opponent / Hero).

- Hero movement draw: up to 1 card.
- Normal Hazard draw: 1.
- Solo Hazard draw: (2 + 2 \times 1 = 4).
- Base Hazard Limit: 6.

**Constructed four-card Hazard draw:**

| Card | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Cave Worm | 1 |
| Giant Spiders | 1 |
| Wargs | 1 |
| Seized by Terror | 1 |
| **Total** | **4** |

Answer before opening the key:

- Which three creatures have a legal key?
- Why is Seized by Terror illegal even though the company is travelling to a Dwarf-hold?
- Does the Hazard Limit of six allow you to invent two extra plays?
- Does Blue Mountain have a site automatic attack?

## Physical-table reminder

- Keep the Hero play deck and Hazard deck separate.
- Draw the full solo Hazard amount from the Hazard deck; do not put these cards in the Hero hand.
- Count the Hazard Limit separately from the draw.
- The site automatic attack is a later Site Phase encounter.
- Under the Academy's selected supplement, ordinary drawn-but-unplayed Hazards go to the Hazard discard at the end of the Movement/Hazard Phase. Save cards that require Doors of Night until Doors of Night is drawn, as the printed solitaire procedure instructs.
- When the Hazard deck is exhausted and more cards are needed, recycle its discard pile according to the Academy procedure.
