# Practice Scenarios

Start with the new [Hero Resource Timing Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hero-resource-practice/' | relative_url }}) and its separate [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hero-resource-practice/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It checks which starting Dwarf can use each Resource, the real limitations of Not at Home and Marvels Told, the extra Hazard slot created by Hundreds of Butterflies, and a two-leg non-Haven route to store Book of Mazarbul.

Start with the [First Session Practice Workbook]({{ '/scenarios/first-session/' | relative_url }}). It contains foundation exercises, with a separate [answer key]({{ '/scenarios/first-session/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).

Then use the [Route Lab workbook]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answers]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise movement timing, site-path declaration, Hazard exposure, route legality, and expedition reassessment.

Use the [Expedition Decision Lab]({{ '/scenarios/expedition-decision-lab/' | relative_url }}) to combine Hero hand management, route legality, Hazard exposure, site-entry decisions, and campaign planning. It includes a separate model-answer page.

Use the new [Route Risk Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/route-risk-workshop/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answers]({{ '/scenarios/route-risk-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare legal routes, account for the actual Hazard hand, weigh arrival condition and future position, and log evidence across three games.

Use the [Hazard Legality Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/' | relative_url }}) to sort a fixed eight-card hand against the Rivendell–Moria site path, choose five legal plays, and account for support-event timing. Open its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) only after making your decision.

Use the new [Site-Keying Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/site-keying-workshop/' | relative_url }}) to distinguish lowercase region-type keys from uppercase site-type keys. It uses Moria to demonstrate why a card can be legal through the route's Wilderness regions even though Moria is not the Ruins & Lairs site type printed on the Hazard.

Use the [Hazard Combination Practice Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-combo-lab/' | relative_url }}) when you are ready to test how the actual Dwarven Quest Hazard cards interact. It focuses on legality, timing, keying, support cards, and repeatable evidence rather than assumed combos.

## Curriculum

The practice bank builds progressively through Foundation, Movement, Hazard, Combat, Expedition, Compression, Position, Corruption, Abort, Campaign, and Masterclass. Only completed and published exercises count as part of the bank.

For each exercise, state the mission, identify legal options, explain exposure, identify the critical character, choose a plan, explain what would change it, and record the lesson.

The Academy uses a labelled teaching hybrid. See [Which Solo Rules Are We Using?]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}) before combining procedures from different solitaire systems.

## Test the teaching decks

Use the [Solo Playtest Protocol]({{ '/scenarios/solo-playtest/' | relative_url }}) to test the Hero and Hazard lists over three repeatable sessions before changing card quantities. It includes a route comparison scorecard, a recovery drill, and a three-game log.
