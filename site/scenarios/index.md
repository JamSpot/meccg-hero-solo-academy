# Practice Scenarios

The Academy practice bank contains **530 scenario exercises**, organised progressively rather than treated as one undifferentiated list.

## Curriculum

1. Foundation
2. Movement
3. Hazard
4. Combat
5. Expedition
6. Compression
7. Position
8. Corruption
9. Abort
10. Campaign
11. Masterclass

For each exercise: state the mission, identify legal options, explain exposure, identify the critical character, choose a plan, explain what would change it, and record the lesson.
