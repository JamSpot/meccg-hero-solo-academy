# Practice Scenarios

Start with the [First Session Practice Workbook]({{ '/scenarios/first-session/' | relative_url }}). It contains foundation exercises, with a separate [answer key]({{ '/scenarios/first-session/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).

The new [Hero Opening Hand & Access Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hero-opening-hand-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hero-opening-hand-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) calculate how often the fixed 36-card Hero deck presents Book of Mazarbûl, Gandalf, Sage access and site-playable Resources. It turns opening-hand probability into a practical decision about whether to recruit, move or pursue another objective.

The first Game 1 review exposed a common beginner trap: the Hero player and the solo Hazard side draw from **different decks** during a moving company's Movement/Hazard Phase. Complete the [Movement Draw Lab]({{ '/scenarios/movement-draw-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/movement-draw-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise the site-card notation, Hero draw, solo Hazard formula, Hazard Limit and five-card hand reset.

After your first game and the movement lesson, complete the [Hero Resource Timing Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hero-resource-practice/' | relative_url }}) and its separate [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hero-resource-practice/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It checks which starting Dwarf can use each Resource, the real limitations of Not at Home and Marvels Told, the extra Hazard slot created by Hundreds of Butterflies, and a two-leg non-Haven route to store Book of Mazarbul.

Use the [Resource Route Planner]({{ '/scenarios/resource-route-planner/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/resource-route-planner/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to turn the Challenge Deck C Resource target list into a focused campaign: Book first, optional Gollum, and later faction or item objectives only when the route justifies them.

Complete the [Location Deck Legality & Wind Throne Lab]({{ '/scenarios/location-deck-legality-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/location-deck-legality-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) before using the 15-site Location deck. It corrects the historical Dead Marshes entry (Minion site, not legal for a Wizard) and teaches the Lórien → The Wind Throne Starter Movement route, including its Shadow-land keying and Ruins & Lairs automatic attack.

Before storing the Book, complete [Corruption Check Lab — Choose the Book Bearer]({{ '/scenarios/corruption-check-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/corruption-check-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It teaches the direction of positive and negative modifiers, the safest bearer, support taps and Despair of the Heart's target restrictions.

After you understand the first expedition, complete [Gandalf — One Company or Two?]({{ '/scenarios/gandalf-company/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/gandalf-company/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It compares Gandalf joining the Dwarves with a separate Grey Havens → Lórien support company, including the separate solo Hazard draws and limits.

After completing the first Moria expedition, use the [Book of Mazarbul — Second Expedition workbook]({{ '/scenarios/book-journey/' | relative_url }}) and its separate [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/book-journey/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It connects the Hero mission, both non-Haven route legs, route-specific Hazard legality, Goblin-gate's optional site attack, the Haven alternative, and the correct timing for storing the Book at Blue Mountain.

Then follow the [Integrated Campaign Walkthrough]({{ '/games/campaign-walkthrough/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/games/campaign-walkthrough/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It combines the fixed decks, normal Hero card draws, solo Hazard draws, site-entry decisions, both route branches and the next-Organization-Phase storage check in one sequence.

Then work through the new [Three-Turn Expedition — Worked Solo Run]({{ '/scenarios/three-turn-expedition/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/three-turn-expedition/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It uses a constructed five-card Hero hand and eight-card Hazard draw to demonstrate the six-character Hazard Limit, the Despair/Watcher combination, Book bearer timing and the two routes to Blue Mountain.

After the Book campaign is familiar, run the [Full Solo Scoring Trial — Second Deck Exhaustion]({{ '/scenarios/full-solo-scoring-trial/' | relative_url }}). It switches from the mission tutorial to the printed solitaire scoring objective, explains MP sources, defeated-creature handling, and the difference between Hero-deck exhaustion and Hazard-deck recycling.

Before making your own entry decisions, complete the [Site Entry & Automatic Attack Lab]({{ '/scenarios/site-entry-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/site-entry-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). It separates arrival, the optional Site Phase entry decision, automatic attacks, Hazard Limit plays and Book storage timing, using the Hero versions of Moria, Goblin-gate and Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold.

Then use the [Route Lab workbook]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answers]({{ '/scenarios/route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise movement timing, site-path declaration, Hazard exposure, route legality, and expedition reassessment.

Use the [Expedition Decision Lab]({{ '/scenarios/expedition-decision-lab/' | relative_url }}) to combine Hero hand management, route legality, Hazard exposure, site-entry decisions, and campaign planning. It includes a separate model-answer page.

Use the new [Route Risk Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/route-risk-workshop/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answers]({{ '/scenarios/route-risk-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare legal routes, account for the actual Hazard hand, weigh arrival condition and future position, and log evidence across three games.

Complete the [Solo Deck Cycle Lab]({{ '/scenarios/solo-deck-cycle-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/solo-deck-cycle-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise both five-card Hero-hand resets, the separate Hazard discard, recycling when the Hazard deck empties, the official Doors of Night saved-card rule, and the difference between the Book tutorial and full scoring game.


Complete the [Hazard Draw & Limit Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) before the more complex Hazard workshops. It tests which site's normal draw number applies, the 2 + twice formula, company-size rounding, separate company limits, and the Hundreds of Butterflies / Twilight exceptions.

Use the [Hazard Legality Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/' | relative_url }}) to sort a fixed eight-card hand against the Rivendell–Moria site path, choose five legal plays, and account for support-event timing. Open its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) only after making your decision.

Use the new [Site-Keying Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/site-keying-workshop/' | relative_url }}) to distinguish lowercase region-type keys from uppercase site-type keys. It uses Moria to demonstrate why a card can be legal through the route's Wilderness regions even though Moria is not the Ruins & Lairs site type printed on the Hazard.

Use the [Site Path Tactics Lab]({{ '/scenarios/site-path-tactics-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/site-path-tactics-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare a five-symbol Starter Movement with a four-region Region Movement, calculate the separate solo draws and limits, and identify why Seized by Terror becomes legal only on the route through Grey Mountain Narrows.

Use the [Support Character Recruitment Lab]({{ '/scenarios/character-recruitment-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/character-recruitment-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise Gandalf/Balin timing, avatar-site restrictions, influence and company-size limits.

Use the [Route Exposure Matrix]({{ '/scenarios/route-exposure-matrix/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/route-exposure-matrix/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to audit every baseline Hazard against the three connected teaching routes, separate legal cards from conditional/dead draws, and choose the strongest legal five within the Hazard Limit.

Use the new [Hazard Deck Balance Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-deck-balance-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-deck-balance-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare the fixed 30-card Hazard baseline with the proposed two-card v1.1 experiment. It includes route-specific draw probabilities, a Moria pressure exercise, and a controlled test protocol. Keep the baseline unchanged for the first three recorded games.

Use the [Hazard Combination Practice Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-combo-lab/' | relative_url }}) when you are ready to test how the actual Dwarven Quest Hazard cards interact. It focuses on legality, timing, keying, support cards, and repeatable evidence rather than assumed combos.

Use the new [Iron Hill — River & Seized by Terror Route Lab]({{ '/scenarios/iron-hill-river-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/iron-hill-river-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare direct Starter Movement with a staged four-region Region Movement through Grey Mountain Narrows. It tests the exact Seized by Terror split odds, the one-Ranger-per-River ruling, the Site Phase denial decision, and why the direct route remains the better default for the Iron Hill faction objective.

## Curriculum

The practice bank builds progressively through Foundation, Movement, Hazard, Combat, Expedition, Compression, Position, Corruption, Abort, Campaign, and Masterclass. Only completed and published exercises count as part of the bank.

For each exercise, state the mission, identify legal options, explain exposure, identify the critical character, choose a plan, explain what would change it, and record the lesson.

The Academy uses a labelled teaching hybrid. See [Which Solo Rules Are We Using?]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}) before combining procedures from different solitaire systems.

## Test the teaching decks

Use the [Solo Playtest Protocol]({{ '/scenarios/solo-playtest/' | relative_url }}) to test the Hero and Hazard lists over three repeatable sessions before changing card quantities. It includes a route comparison scorecard, a recovery drill, and a three-game log.

Use the new [Hazard Decision Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-decision-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-decision-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise choosing the most disadvantageous legal Hazard sequence from constructed Moria, Goblin-gate and Blue Mountain draws. It separates route keying, solo draw volume, fixed Hazard Limit, support-event timing and Site Phase automatic attacks.

Use the [Shadow-land Route & Seized by Terror Lab]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise the Lórien → Wind Throne Starter Movement path, calculate Seized by Terror's 2D6 + mind odds, resolve one Ranger per River, and decide whether Gildor is worth recruiting as a second Ranger. It also separates the site's Shadow-land region from its Ruins & Lairs site type and explains why the Book of Mazarbûl cannot be played there.

Use the new [Ranger Capacity & River Denial Lab]({{ '/scenarios/ranger-capacity-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/ranger-capacity-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to compare five-, six- and seven-character companies, count the Rangers who can actually tap for River, and calculate the chance that the fixed Hazard deck can deny a Site Phase on six- and eight-card solo draws.


Complete the [Seized by Terror — Aftermath, Followers & Recovery Lab]({{ '/scenarios/seized-terror-aftermath/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/seized-terror-aftermath/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) after the Ranger Capacity Lab. It follows a successful split through carried items and allies, followers whose direct-influence control is broken, Fellowship's explicit discard clause, the return-to-origin rule, and the conditions for joining companies again.
