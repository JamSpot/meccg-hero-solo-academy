# Site-Keying Workshop — Model Answer

The central correction is simple:

**Moria is a Shadow-hold [{S}], not a Ruins & Lairs [{R}] site.**

The route Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate contains three Wilderness [{w}] regions. Several Challenge Deck C creatures are legal because of those route regions, not because Moria is a Ruins & Lairs site.

## 1. Card-by-card answer

| Hazard | Correct reason it is a candidate on this journey | Incorrect shortcut to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Cave Worm** | Its named-region key includes **Redhorn Gate**, the destination region in the declared site path. | Do not say this is an extra key unique to Region Movement. Redhorn Gate is also the destination region on a legal Starter Movement journey to Moria. |
| **Giant Spiders** | Its double-Wilderness key is met by the three Wilderness regions. | Its additional Ruins & Lairs / Shadow-hold / Dark-hold site keys apply in specified Mirkwood regions. Moria is not one of those regions, so do not use that special site-key text here. |
| **Lesser Spiders** | It can key to Wilderness [{w}], and this route contains three Wilderness regions. | Its uppercase {R} key is a Ruins & Lairs **site** key. Moria is {S}, not {R}. Its lowercase {s} key is a Shadow-land **region** type, not a Shadow-hold site type. |
| **Neeker-breekers** | It can key to Wilderness [{w}], which is present in the route. | Its uppercase {R} key does not match Moria's uppercase {S} site type. It is legal here via Wilderness, not via Moria's site type. |
| **Wargs** | Its region-type key includes Wilderness, which is present in the route. | Do not infer a site key from the card's creature theme; use its printed region key. |
| **Watcher in the Water** | It can key to double Wilderness, and its own text also says it may be played at Moria. Either is a valid reason to examine it here. | Do not generalise its explicit Moria permission to other creatures. |

## 2. The five key distinctions

### Lowercase {s} versus uppercase {S}

- **{s}** is a Shadow-land region type.
- **{S}** is a Shadow-hold site type.

They are different categories. A card with a lowercase {s} region key is not thereby keyed to every Shadow-hold site.

### Uppercase {R} versus Moria

- **{R}** means Ruins & Lairs site.
- Moria's site type is **{S}**, Shadow-hold.

Therefore, Lesser Spiders and Neeker-breekers cannot use an {R} site key at Moria. They are still legal candidates on the stated moving route because it contains Wilderness [{w}].

### Giant Spiders' additional site keys

Giant Spiders has a double-Wilderness key. Its card text also allows certain site-type keys in specified Mirkwood regions. Those extra keys do not turn every Shadow-hold into a valid site for Giant Spiders. Moria is outside the listed Mirkwood regions; use the double-Wilderness route key here.

### Watcher in the Water's explicit Moria permission

Watcher in the Water is the exception in this group: its printed text expressly permits it to be played at Moria. This is a card-specific permission, not a general rule that all Animal creatures can be played there.

### Cave Worm's named region

Cave Worm is keyed to Redhorn Gate among other named regions. Redhorn Gate is the region containing Moria and is included as the destination region. Therefore Cave Worm's key is available on this Moria journey.

Do not overstate what this proves: Starter Movement to Moria from Lórien also uses the destination region name Redhorn Gate. The extra intervening name exposed by the direct Region Movement route is Hollin, not Redhorn Gate. The fixed Challenge Deck C creatures in this exercise do not include a creature specifically keyed to Hollin.

## 3. Attack modifiers: one more important distinction

Both **Full of Froth and Rage** and **Wake of War** can affect the relevant Spider and Animal attacks, subject to their exact text and remaining in play.

- Giant Spiders and Lesser Spiders are Spider attacks. If both events are active, they receive +3 prowess and Wake of War's +1 strike, provided the attack is not otherwise protected by special wording.
- Watcher in the Water and Neeker-breekers specify that **each character faces one strike**. Under the official rule for attacks with one strike per character, a general strike-count increase does not add extra strikes to those attacks. Their prowess can still be modified by applicable effects.
- Full of Froth and Rage is discarded if a Spider or Animal attack is defeated. Recheck whether it remains in play before resolving the next attack.

Do not treat a support event as a permanent bonus for the entire phase if its own text tells you when it is discarded.

## 4. Why the distinction matters to fair solo play

A fair Hazard player must not:
- reject Lesser Spiders or Neeker-breekers by mistakenly thinking their only route key is {R};
- play them by incorrectly claiming Moria is {R};
- use Giant Spiders' special Mirkwood site-key clause at an unrelated site;
- overlook Watcher in the Water's explicit Moria wording;
- claim Cave Worm's Redhorn Gate key is unique to Region Movement.

The correct method is to identify the actual key that is satisfied, then compare the legal cards and choose the most disadvantageous combination allowed by the solo procedure and Hazard Limit.

## 5. Return to the table

Use this five-part check whenever you reveal a creature:

1. Is the key a **region type**, a **named region**, a **site type**, or an **explicit site name**?
2. Which actual region cards or destination site satisfy that key?
3. Does the card have additional restrictions or special wording?
4. Does its attack text change how strike modifiers apply?
5. Is it the strongest legal choice for the current company and objective?
