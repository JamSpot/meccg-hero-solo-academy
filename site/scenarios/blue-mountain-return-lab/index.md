# Blue Mountain Return & Haven Choice Lab

This exercise starts after the company has successfully stored Book of Mazarbûl at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. The Book is safe and the company must choose how to return to a Haven.

It tests a common mistake: assuming that the nearest-Haven relationship on a site card prevents a company from using Region Movement to reach a different Haven directly.

## Fixed game state

- Five starting Dwarves are together at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold.
- Book of Mazarbûl has already been stored.
- The Hero Location deck contains the Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold and the Hero Haven cards.
- The company is not using Great-road.
- Use the fixed 30-card Hazard deck.
- No special movement Resource or region-adjacency modifier is in play.

## Your choices

**Plan A:** move directly to Rivendell by Region Movement.

**Plan B:** use Starter Movement to Grey Havens, then move from Grey Havens to Rivendell on the following turn.

## Hazard-keying drill

For each route, mark each creature as legal or not legal. A creature must still be in the drawn Hazard hand.

1. Cave Worm — named-region key to Númeriador.
2. Giant Spiders — double-Wilderness key.
3. Lesser Spiders — Wilderness key.
4. Neeker-breekers — Wilderness key.
5. Wargs — Wilderness key.
6. Watcher in the Water — two Wilderness plus Coastal Sea, or its explicit Moria permission.

### Fixed four-card Hazard hand

For this drill only, assume the Hazard player has drawn exactly these four cards:

- Cave Worm
- Giant Spiders
- Watcher in the Water
- Seized by Terror

This is a deliberately fixed exercise hand, not a prediction of what a shuffled deck will produce. Apply the site path separately to each leg and mark which cards are actually playable.

Then answer:

- Why can Cave Worm be played on the first Starter leg but not the Grey Havens → Rivendell leg?
- Why does the direct Region Movement expose both Cave Worm and Giant Spiders?
- Why is Watcher in the Water not legal on any of these routes?
- Why is Seized by Terror not legal even though the company is moving through three regions on Plan A?

## The captain's decision

Write a short recommendation for the company. Your answer must distinguish:

- total turns;
- total solo Hazard cards drawn;
- Hazard phases and their separate limits;
- creature-key coverage;
- whether the extra Grey Havens stop has a mission purpose;
- when Haven healing actually occurs.

Do not answer only “the shortest route is best”. Explain what the extra stop buys you, if anything.
