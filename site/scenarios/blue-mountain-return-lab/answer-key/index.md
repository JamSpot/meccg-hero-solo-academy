# Blue Mountain Return & Haven Choice Lab — model answer

[Return to the exercise]({{ '/scenarios/blue-mountain-return-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. Plan A — direct Region Movement

The legal route is:

**Númeriador → Arthedain → Rhudaur**

The Region cards show the required adjacencies: Númeriador connects to Arthedain, and Arthedain connects to Rhudaur. Three regions are within the normal four-region maximum. No region repeats.

The three regions are all Wilderness, so the site path is **{w}{w}{w}**. Region Movement exposes all three region names as well as their types.

Rivendell is a Haven. The normal draw numbers therefore come from the site of origin, Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, not from Rivendell.

- Normal Hazard draw: **1**
- Hero draw: up to **1**
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 1) = 4**
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**

The company draws four Hazard cards. It cannot play more than the cards it drew, even though its base Hazard Limit is five.

The five-card Hazard Limit assumes only the five Dwarves. If Gandalf joins the company, he counts as a character for company-size purposes: six characters means a base Hazard Limit of **6**. The site-card draw numbers remain unchanged.


## 2. Plan B — Starter Movement via Grey Havens

### First leg: Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold → Grey Havens

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold lists Grey Havens as its nearest Haven. The printed site path is **{f}{w}** (Free-domain, Wilderness), so this is legal Starter Movement.

Grey Havens is a Haven. Use the Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold origin draw numbers:

- Normal Hazard draw: **1**
- Hero draw: up to **1**
- Solo Hazard draw: **4**
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**

The site path has only one Wilderness. Giant Spiders' double-Wilderness key is not met. Cave Worm is still legal because the site path includes the named origin region Númeriador.

### Second leg: Grey Havens → Rivendell

The Grey Havens card gives the site path to Rivendell as **{w}{w}{f}** (Wilderness, Wilderness, Free-domain).

Rivendell is a Haven, so use Grey Havens' draw numbers, **2/2**:

- Normal Hazard draw: **2**
- Hero draw: up to **2**
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6**
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**

This is a new Movement/Hazard Phase on a later turn. It has its own draw and its own Hazard Limit. The two legs therefore produce four plus six, or **ten Hazard cards across two phases**, not one ten-card phase.

The second Starter path has two Wilderness regions, so Giant Spiders is legal. Cave Worm is not: its named-region list does not include Lindon or Rhudaur.

## 3. Creature-key answers

| Creature | Direct Region route | Blue Mountain → Grey Havens | Grey Havens → Rivendell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cave Worm ×3 | Yes — Númeriador | Yes — Númeriador | No — no named key |
| Giant Spiders ×3 | Yes — three Wilderness | No — one Wilderness | Yes — two Wilderness |
| Lesser Spiders ×3 | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness |
| Neeker-breekers ×2 | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness |
| Wargs ×2 | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness | Yes — Wilderness |
| Watcher in the Water ×3 | No — no Coastal Sea or Moria | No | No |

### Fixed four-card Hazard hand

| Card | Direct Region route | Blue Mountain → Grey Havens | Grey Havens → Rivendell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cave Worm | Playable — Númeriador | Playable — Númeriador | Not playable — no named-region key |
| Giant Spiders | Playable — three Wilderness | Not playable — only one Wilderness | Playable — two Wilderness |
| Watcher in the Water | Not playable — no Coastal Sea or Moria | Not playable | Not playable |
| Seized by Terror | Not playable — no Shadow-land or Dark-domain | Not playable | Not playable |

With this fixed four-card hand, the Hazard player can play two cards on the direct route (Cave Worm and Giant Spiders), one on the first Starter leg (Cave Worm), and one on the second Starter leg (Giant Spiders). All other cards in the sample hand fail their active conditions. Each playable creature still counts against that phase's Hazard Limit.

That produces these route-legal creature-copy counts:

- Direct Region Movement: **13 of 16**.
- Blue Mountain → Grey Havens: **10 of 16**.
- Grey Havens → Rivendell: **10 of 16**.

These counts measure key legality, not the number of creatures actually drawn. The Hazard player must still draw the card and obey its other conditions.

Seized by Terror is illegal on all three paths because none contains a Shadow-land or Dark-domain. A three-region route is not automatically a dangerous region type: all three regions on Plan A are Wilderness.

## 4. Which route is better?

For the stated objective—return to Rivendell—the default is **Plan A**:

- one turn instead of two;
- four solo Hazard cards instead of ten;
- no automatic attack at the destination;
- a direct arrival at the preferred Haven.

Plan A does expose more creature copies in its one phase, including both Cave Worm and Giant Spiders. Plan B has a narrower first leg, but its second leg draws six cards and exposes Giant Spiders. The extra stop is justified only if Grey Havens itself is part of the next plan.

If Grey Havens itself is part of the next objective, the extra stop may be justified. It is not a free shortcut: it costs another turn and another Movement/Hazard Phase.

## 5. Haven healing

The company does not heal as soon as it reaches Grey Havens or Rivendell. Wounded characters can heal during the next Untap Phase while at a Haven; they become tapped as part of healing. They need a later Untap Phase to become untapped.

## 6. Why Great-road is not involved

Great-road is playable on a company that starts at a Haven. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a Free-hold, not a Haven. It cannot be used to make this departure.
