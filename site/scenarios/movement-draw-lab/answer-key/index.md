# Movement Draw Lab — Model Answer

## The rule in one sentence

For a moving Hero company, use the relevant site's **opponent draws / you draw** values: the Hero player draws normally from the Hero play deck using the second number, while the solo Hazard side separately draws **2 + twice the first number** from the Hazard deck; the Hazard Limit is calculated independently, and the Hero hand resets to five after the company's Movement/Hazard Phase.

## Exercise 1 — Rivendell to Moria

Moria is **3/2**.

- Hero draw: up to **2** cards from the Hero play deck. The five-Dwarf company qualifies because it contains characters with mind 3 or greater.
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 3) = 8** cards from the separate Hazard deck.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5** for the five-character company.
- Hazard cards do not enter the Hero hand.
- Reset the Hero hand to **5** after the company's Movement/Hazard Phase has finished, not before Hazard resolution.

## Exercise 2 — Moria to Goblin-gate

Goblin-gate is **2/2** and is a non-Haven destination.

- Hero draw: up to **2**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- The four regions are used for movement legality and Hazard creature keying. They do not multiply the draw.

## Exercise 3 — Goblin-gate to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

The Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is **1/1**.

- Hero draw: up to **1**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 1) = 4**.
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- If Gandalf joins the Dwarves, the company has six characters and its base Hazard Limit becomes **6**. The two draws do not change because the site-card numbers have not changed.
- If Gandalf travels separately, resolve a separate Movement/Hazard Phase for him. Do not combine the draws or limits.

## Exercise 4 — Moria to Rivendell

Rivendell is a Haven, so the normal movement draw uses the **site of origin**, Moria. Moria is **3/2**.

- Hero draw: up to **2**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **8**.
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**.

Do not use Rivendell's numbers merely because Rivendell is the destination. The Haven exception points back to the origin site.

## Exercise 5 — No movement

The normal rules say that a company that does not move does not draw cards based on movement. The printed solo paragraph does not provide a complete universal instruction for every stationary-company case. The Academy therefore does not turn the formula into an invented default. Use a scenario-specific instruction if the scenario wants a Hazard draw while a company stays put.

## Final check

> The Hero draw is up to the **you draw** number from the **Hero play** deck; the solo Hazard draw is **2 + twice the opponent-draw number** from the **separate Hazard** deck; the Hazard Limit is calculated from company size; after the Movement/Hazard Phase my Hero hand resets to **five**.
