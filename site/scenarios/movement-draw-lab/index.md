# Movement Draw Lab — two decks, two draws

This exercise fixes an easy-to-miss part of the solo turn: the Hero player still makes the normal movement draw from the Hero play deck. The solo Hazard side then draws from a completely separate Hazard deck using the solitaire formula.

Do not confuse either draw with the company's Hazard Limit.

## The site-card notation

The site-card draw values are commonly written:

**opponent draws / you draw**

- The first number is the normal Hazard-player draw.
- The second number is the normal Hero/resource-player draw.
- For a move to a **non-Haven**, use the destination site's numbers.
- For a move to a **Haven**, use the site of origin's numbers.

The Hero player may draw up to the second number if the moving company contains an avatar or a character with mind 3 or greater. A qualifying company must draw at least one card. The solo Hazard side separately draws:

**2 + (2 × the normal Hazard-player number)**

For the active one-Gandalf baseline, the Hero hand is reset to **five cards** after that company's Movement/Hazard Phase. If a card effect has changed the hand-size target (for example, Pallando or a Sage tapping Book of Mazarbûl), reset to the modified target instead.

## Exercise 1 — Rivendell to Moria

Your five starting Dwarves move from Rivendell to Moria by Region Movement.

Moria's Hero site card shows **3/2** (opponent draws / you draw).

Record:

1. How many Hero cards may you draw from the 36-card Hero play deck?
2. How many cards does the solo Hazard side draw from its separate 30-card Hazard deck?
3. What is the five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit?
4. Do the eight Hazard cards enter your Hero hand?
5. When do you reset the Hero hand, and what could change its five-card target?

## Exercise 2 — Moria to Goblin-gate

The five Dwarves move from Moria to Goblin-gate. The Hero version of Goblin-gate shows **2/1** (opponent draws / you draw).

Record the Hero draw, solo Hazard draw, base Hazard Limit and hand-reset point. Explain why the four-region route does not multiply either draw.

## Exercise 3 — Goblin-gate to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

The company moves to the Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. Its site card shows **1/1**.

Record the Hero draw, solo Hazard draw and base Hazard Limit. If Gandalf has joined the five Dwarves, what changes? If Gandalf travels as a separate company, should you combine the two companies' draws or limits?

## Exercise 4 — Moria to Rivendell

The company moves from Moria to Rivendell, which is a Haven. Use Moria's **3/2** values because the destination is a Haven.

Record both draws and the Hazard Limit. Explain why you do not use Rivendell's numbers for this movement.

## Exercise 5 — No movement

A company stays at its current site.

Under the normal Movement/Hazard rules, no cards are drawn based on movement. The printed solitaire paragraph does not clearly settle every stationary-company draw case. Do not invent a universal solo number. State what additional scenario instruction you would need before drawing from the Hazard deck.

## Final check

Complete this sentence:

> The Hero draw is ______ cards from the ______ deck; the solo Hazard draw is ______ cards from the ______ deck; the Hazard Limit is ______; after the Movement/Hazard Phase my Hero hand resets to ______.
