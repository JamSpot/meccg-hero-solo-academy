# Solo Deck Cycle Lab — Model Answer

Use this after completing the [Solo Deck Cycle Lab]({{ '/scenarios/solo-deck-cycle-lab/' | relative_url }}). Identify the rule source before moving a card between piles.

## 1. The zones

- Hero cards stay in the Hero play deck, Hero hand, Hero discard pile, or the appropriate in-play / marshalling-point zone.
- Hazard cards stay in the separate Hazard draw deck, Hazard discard pile, saved-for-Doors-of-Night area, in-play area, or the Hero marshalling-point pile when normal scoring rules put them there.
- A Hazard drawn for the solo phase never becomes part of the Hero hand.
- A long-event or permanent-event that remains in play is not available to be shuffled into the Hazard deck yet.

## 2. The five-card Hero hand

The correct reset points are:

1. Opening hand: five.
2. End of each company's Movement/Hazard Phase: reset to five (with any active hand-size modifier).
3. End-of-Turn Phase: reset to five again.

A movement draw can temporarily take the Hero hand above five. Do not discard down immediately after drawing; wait for the Movement/Hazard Phase reset. The Site Phase happens between the two reset points.

For the active Gandalf baseline, the target is five. The Book's hand-size modifier is active only after its Sage bearer taps the Book during the Organization Phase, and it lasts until the next Untap Phase. Merely bearing the Book does not change the hand target. Gandalf's corruption-check bonus is unrelated.

## 3. Moria: eight drawn, five limit

Moria's normal Hazard-player draw number is three:

**2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards.**

The five-Dwarf company's base Hazard Limit is five. The Hazard side evaluates all eight, plays the strongest legal combination it can within five ordinary limit-counting slots, and does not gain three extra plays because three additional cards were drawn.

## 4. Two non-Haven legs

| Leg | Normal Hazard number | Solo draw | Base limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Moria → Goblin-gate | 2 | **6** | **5** |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | 1 | **4** | **5** |
| **Total across two separate phases** | — | **10** | **5 per phase** |

The company resolves one Movement/Hazard Phase for each leg. The second phase has a new Hazard Limit calculation. The first phase's unused slots do not carry forward, and the second phase does not inherit the first phase's route or creature keys.

The qualifying five-Dwarf company may draw up to one Hero card on each leg because the Hero site cards are 2/1 at Goblin-gate and 1/1 at Blue Mountain. Those Hero draws are separate from the six- and four-card Hazard draws.

## 5. Unplayed Hazards and the Doors of Night exception

Under the Academy's adopted supplementary procedure, ordinary drawn-but-unplayed Hazards go to the Hazard discard at the end of the current company's Movement/Hazard Phase. They are not kept for the next leg.

There is one explicit exception in the printed solitaire paragraph: cards requiring Doors of Night are saved and played when a Doors of Night is drawn. Keep such cards in a separate marked area until that condition is met. When they become available, they still have to satisfy their own card text and the current Hazard Limit.

The active 30-card list has no Doors of Night and no card that requires it. **Wake of War is not a Doors of Night requirement.** Its basic bonus applies without Doors of Night; the Environment only increases the Wolf bonus.

**Twilight** is different again: it does not count against the Hazard Limit, but it still needs a legal Environment target. The closed-sideboard baseline has no Environment Resource in play, so a drawn Twilight with no Environment to cancel is an ordinary unplayable draw and is discarded under the Academy supplement.

## 6. Recycling when the Hazard deck runs out

If only three cards remain but the current phase requires eight:

1. Draw the three cards remaining in the Hazard draw deck.
2. If more cards are still required, shuffle the Hazard discard pile to form a new Hazard draw deck.
3. Draw the remaining five if the new deck contains enough cards.

Do not shuffle the cards currently being considered for this phase into the deck. Do not take long-events or permanent-events that are still in play. Do not pull a card out of the saved-for-Doors-of-Night area unless the Doors of Night condition has been met.

If the discard pile does not contain enough cards to complete the required draw, draw only what is actually available. Do not invent or duplicate cards.

## 7. Mission tutorial versus full scoring

| Situation | Where a defeated non-unique creature goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Book of Mazarbûl tutorial | Hazard discard | This mission does not award kill MPs; the selected solo supplement allows non-unique creatures to recycle |
| Full solo scoring game | Hero marshalling-point pile | Normal scoring rules award the creature's kill MPs and remove it from future Hazard cycles |

A unique creature follows the normal uniqueness restriction and cannot be brought back into play. The tutorial's recycling convention is not permission to replay a unique creature.

## 8. What the exercise should teach

- The Hero hand has two reset points per turn, not one.
- The Hazard draw and Hazard Limit answer different questions.
- Each company receives its own Movement/Hazard Phase, draw and limit.
- Ordinary unused Hazard draws are discarded under the Academy supplement.
- Doors of Night-dependent cards are saved under the official solitaire rule.
- Hazard deck recycling depends on the selected mission/scoring procedure.
- Route length does not multiply the draw; each separate leg has its own site-card calculation.
