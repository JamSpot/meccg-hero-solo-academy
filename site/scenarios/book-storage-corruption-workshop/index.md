# Book of Mazarbûl — Storage & Corruption Workshop

This workshop joins the Book mission, the free-movement route, the Hazard deck's Despair package and the Hero deck's corruption protection. It is an advanced practice module; **it does not change either locked deck**.

Use the fixed 36-card Hero play deck, the separate 30-card Hazard deck and the five-Dwarf starting company. The [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/book-storage-corruption-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) contains the calculations.

## 1. The mission has three separate stages

1. **Acquire the Book at Moria.** Book of Mazarbûl is a unique Special Item playable at Moria. While borne, it is worth 1 MP and gives its bearer 2 corruption points. Its card text permits it to be stored at a Dwarf-hold for 5 MPs.
2. **Reach a Dwarf-hold.** The Academy's practical route is Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold. Each leg is a legal four-region Region Movement. The direct Moria → Blue Mountain route needs five regions and is not a single legal Region Movement.
3. **Store the Book during an Organization Phase.** Storage is not a Site Phase action. If the company arrives at Blue Mountain during its Movement/Hazard Phase, its first storage opportunity is the next turn's Organization Phase. The company must still be at the Dwarf-hold when that Organization Phase begins.

The Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold Hero site is a **Free-hold** by its site-type symbol, but it is a Dwarf-hold by name and is the intended destination for the Book's special storage text. Its Hero site card has a 1/1 draw and no automatic attack.

The general storage rule normally names Havens; Book of Mazarbûl's own text supplies the Dwarf-hold exception. A successful storage corruption check moves the Book to the Hero MP pile for 5 MPs. The Book is then no longer borne: it remains in the marshalling-point pile, its 2 CP no longer burden a character, and its Sage hand-size ability is no longer active. Do not describe a stored card as “removed from play”.

## 2. Calculate the bearer’s actual check

Do not use the Book's 2 CP as the whole calculation unless it is the only corruption source the bearer controls.

**Modified roll = 2D6 + character corruption-check modifier + active company/card modifiers + support taps + any check-specific Resource.**

**Corruption-point total = the CP values on cards controlled by that character.**

Useful values in this Academy build:

| Character / card | Effect |
|---|---:|
| Gandalf | +1 to his own corruption checks |
| Thorin II, Glóin | 0 printed modifier |
| Dori, Kíli, Óin | -1 to their own corruption checks |
| Book of Mazarbûl | 2 CP while borne |
| Despair of the Heart | 2 CP on its target |
| Fellowship | +1 to all company characters' corruption checks per copy |
| A Friend or Three | +1 per character in the company to **one** corruption check |

Two Fellowship cards can stack. The original rules' worked example uses two copies for +2 to all company checks. Their company-wide check bonus is a modifier; do not add the company's Fellowship cards to an individual bearer's CP total.

A Friend or Three is a powerful one-check response: it adds +5 in the five-Dwarf company, or +6 if Gandalf has joined. The Hero deck contains two copies, but each copy modifies one check, not every check.

## 3. Despair of the Heart: why one wound can matter so much

Despair is a Corruption permanent-event. It gives its target 2 CP and makes that target check whenever a character in the same company becomes wounded. It cannot be duplicated on a given character, and only one corruption card may be played on each character per turn. The Hazard player can therefore spread the three copies across three different eligible Dwarves, but must play each copy as the first action in a new chain.

The Council of Elrond's CRF ruling says the Despair corruption check occurs **before the body check**. A wound to one character can therefore trigger checks for every surviving Despair target before that body check is resolved. If further characters become wounded later in the attack, those wound events can trigger further checks.

When several characters must check, resolve the checks one at a time in an order chosen by the Hero player. Each character tapped for support can add +1 to **one** check only, not to all of them. Do not confuse corruption-check support with combat strike support: the latter has its own rule about which characters may tap. A character tapped for a corruption check may be at -1 prowess when facing its own later strike.

## 4. The Hero has real counterplay

The Hazard side should be challenging, but it must account for the actual Resource cards and company state.

- **Fellowship ×2:** if played at a Haven on a company with at least four characters/allies, the two copies can provide +2 to every company member's corruption checks and +2 prowess. They are discarded if a character or ally joins or leaves the company.
- **A Friend or Three ×2:** each copy adds +1 for every character in the company to one check. Use it on the most dangerous check; do not apply it to all Despair targets.
- **Concealment ×3:** a Scout can tap to cancel one eligible attack. If the attack is canceled, it causes no wound and does not trigger Despair. It does not cancel every attack in the Hazard hand.
- **Marvels Told ×1:** if a Sage is available, it can discard a Hazard non-Environment permanent-event or long-event such as Despair. The Sage must make its required corruption check at -2, so this answer also has a cost.
- **Despair removal:** during the target's Organization Phase, it may tap and roll greater than 4 to remove Despair. If already tapped, or if it chooses not to tap, it can use the rules' -3 roll option. This is a removal roll, not a corruption check: do not add the character's printed corruption-check modifier or support taps to it.
- **Hundreds of Butterflies ×1:** this Resource short-event can increase the Hazard Limit against its moving company by one. It does not increase the number of Hazard cards drawn, and it is not an Environment, so it does not create a target for Twilight.

## 5. Company composition changes the pressure

The five Dwarves have a base Hazard Limit of five. If Gandalf joins them, company size and the base Hazard Limit rise to six. Hundreds of Butterflies can add one more slot in either case.

Joining Gandalf is not a free defensive upgrade:

- Gandalf cannot be targeted by Despair and has +1 to his own corruption checks.
- He is a Sage and can use the Book's hand-size ability while he bears it.
- He can tap to support one Dwarf corruption check.
- His joining the company discards any Fellowship cards on that company, removing their company-wide check and prowess bonuses.
- The larger company gives the Hazard side a higher base Hazard Limit.

If Gandalf is to bear the Book, decide before leaving Rivendell: companies can combine at a Haven, but Moria is not a Haven. Keeping him separate preserves the Dwarves' Fellowship but means he cannot bear their Book or support their checks.

## 6. Route numbers for the storage expedition

| Journey | Regions in the declared path | Hero draw | Normal Hazard draw | Solo Hazard draw | Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Moria → Goblin-gate | 4 | Up to 1 | 2 | **6** | 5 |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | 4 | Up to 1 | 1 | **4** | 5 |

The solo Hazard draw is **2 + twice the normal Hazard draw**. It is separate from the Hazard Limit and from any automatic attack. If Gandalf joins, use a base Hazard Limit of six; if Hundreds of Butterflies is played, add one to the applicable limit.

## 7. Practice questions

Use the [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/book-storage-corruption-workshop/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) only after attempting these.

1. The Book is borne by Kíli and is his only corruption source. What is his modified roll with no support? What happens on natural rolls of 2 and 3?
2. Repeat with Thorin II, Glóin and Gandalf as bearer. Which checks are safest without support?
3. Despair is played on the Book bearer. Calculate the new CP total and identify the change when the Book is successfully stored.
4. Three Despairs are played on Dori, Kíli and Óin. A Watcher in the Water strike wounds one character. How many checks are triggered, in what order are they resolved, and what can the Hero use to protect them?
5. Compare the five-Dwarf company with Gandalf joining. Account for the Hazard Limit, Fellowship, Gandalf's immunity to Despair, and the Sage ability.
6. The company arrives at Blue Mountain during its Movement/Hazard Phase. When can it first store the Book?
7. The Book bearer is already tapped and still has Despair. What removal option is available, and how is that roll different from the Book-storage corruption check?
8. The Hazard player has drawn three Despairs, Watcher, Full of Froth and Rage, and Wake of War. When can all six ordinary Hazard plays be legal against this company?

**Version note:** the current Council of Elrond consolidated corruption rule 7.1 contains a phrase about a modified result “less than two” that is not present in the older printed *The Wizards* outcome categories. For the narrow case where a CP-2 Hero with a -1 modifier rolls a natural 2 (modified result 1), the two texts appear to overlap. The Academy records that edge case explicitly rather than silently treating the sources as identical.
