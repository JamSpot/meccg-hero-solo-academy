# Book of Mazarbûl — Storage & Corruption Workshop: Model Answer

Use this answer key after attempting the [workshop]({{ '/scenarios/book-storage-corruption-workshop/' | relative_url }}). The fixed Hero and Hazard lists remain unchanged.

## 1. Book-only storage checks

Assume the Book is the bearer's only corruption-point source: **2 CP**. No Fellowship, A Friend or Three, other items or support taps are included in this first table.

| Bearer | Printed check modifier | Lowest modified result | Outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gandalf | +1 | 3 | Guaranteed success: even 2 + 1 is greater than 2 CP. |
| Thorin II / Glóin | 0 | 2 | Natural 2 equals 2 CP and fails; 1/36 discard, 35/36 success. |
| Dori / Kíli / Óin | -1 | 1 | Natural 2 gives 1 and natural 3 gives 2; 3/36 fail, 33/36 succeed. The severity of the natural-2 result is the source discrepancy below. |

For Kíli with no support:

- Natural 2 → modified 1.
- Natural 3 → modified 2.
- Natural 4–12 → modified 3–11, so the check succeeds.

Under the older printed *The Wizards* categories, a result one below CP is a discard, so both natural 2 and 3 discard Kíli. The current CoE consolidated rule 7.1 additionally says a modified result below 2 eliminates; that phrase overlaps with the one-below category for this exact result of 1. No specific CRF ruling resolving that overlap was found. The Academy flags it instead of pretending there is no source difference. The probability of failing the check is still 3/36 either way.

### Support and Fellowship

- One support character adds +1. For Kíli, that changes the net modifier from -1 to 0: only a natural 2 fails (1/36).
- Two support characters add +2. Kíli's net modifier becomes +1, so even a natural 2 succeeds.
- Thorin or Glóin need one support character for a guaranteed Book-only check.
- Gandalf needs no support for a Book-only check.
- One Fellowship gives +1 to every company character's checks. It changes Dori/Kíli/Óin from -1 to 0 and Thorin/Glóin from 0 to +1.
- Two Fellowship cards give +2 to every company character's checks. Dori/Kíli/Óin then have a net +1, making a Book-only check automatic.

The official *The Wizards* example explicitly assumes two Fellowship cards and applies +2 to the corruption-check modifier while leaving Frodo's CP total at 9. The Fellowship company bonus is not an additional CP burden on each character.

## 2. If Despair of the Heart is also on the Book bearer

Despair gives its target another 2 CP. The Book bearer now has **4 CP** until Despair is removed or the Book is stored.

| Bearer | Net check modifier | Success probability, no support | Failure outcomes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Thorin II / Glóin | 0 | 30/36 | Natural 2 (1/36) eliminates; natural 3–4 (5/36) discards. |
| Dori / Kíli / Óin | -1 | 26/36 | Natural 2–3 (3/36) eliminate; natural 4–5 (7/36) discard. |

For a -1 Dwarf with 4 CP, support changes the result as follows:

| Helpers tapped | Net modifier | Failing natural rolls | Failure probability |
|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | -1 | 2–5 | 10/36 |
| 1 | 0 | 2–4 | 6/36 |
| 2 | +1 | 2–3 | 3/36 |
| 3 | +2 | 2 | 1/36 |
| 4 | +3 | None | 0/36 |

The failure categories are important:

- With no helpers, natural 2 gives modified 1; natural 3 gives 2. Both are at least two below 4 CP, so they eliminate under the printed rule as well as the current CoE wording.
- Natural 4 gives 3 (one below CP) and natural 5 gives 4 (equal to CP); those are discard results for a Hero under the printed categories.
- With one helper, natural 2 gives 2 (two below CP), so that is the elimination result; natural 3–4 are discard results.
- With two helpers, natural 2–3 give 3–4 and are discard results.
- With three helpers, natural 2 gives 4 and is a discard result.
- Four helpers guarantee success under the stated assumptions.

A Friend or Three is a stronger no-tap answer for one check: in a five-character company it adds +5. Even a -1 Dwarf with 4 CP then has a minimum modified result of 6, which succeeds. If Gandalf has joined, the same card adds +6. The Hero deck has two copies, but each copy affects one check only.

## 3. Three Despair targets: resolve the checks separately

A wound to any character in the company triggers a corruption check for each surviving Despair target. The checks are resolved one at a time in an order chosen by the Hero player. A character tapped to support one check cannot provide another +1 to a later check.

For the five-Dwarf example with Despair on Dori, Kíli and Óin:

- Dori, Kíli and Óin each have -1 and 2 CP from Despair (unless one also carries the Book).
- Thorin II and Glóin are the other two characters.
- The Hero can choose the order of the three checks and allocate support across them. A target may support a *different* target's check if it is an eligible untapped character for that check; it cannot support its own check.
- A possible 2/2/1 allocation is Dori's check supported by Kíli and Thorin, Kíli's check supported by Óin and Glóin, then Óin's check supported by Dori. This taps all five characters. It illustrates the cost: characters tapped for the checks have -1 prowess when they face their own later strikes.
- If one target also bears the Book, use 4 CP for that target and allocate the support and any A Friend or Three to the most dangerous check. Do not add the Book's CP to the other two targets.

If the attack is Watcher in the Water, its “each character faces one strike” wording means one strike per character; Wake of War's extra-strike modifier does not add another strike to that attack. The prowess modifiers still apply. The CRF ruling makes Despair's check occur before the body check. A character wounded by a later strike can trigger another set of checks for any Despair targets still in play.

## 4. The Hazard line and the Hero's answers

The five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit is five. A six-card line of three Despairs + Watcher in the Water + Full of Froth and Rage + Wake of War needs six slots, so it is legal if:

- Gandalf has joined the company (base limit six); or
- the Hero has played Hundreds of Butterflies to increase the five-Dwarf company's limit from five to six.

If Gandalf joins, remember the other side of the trade-off: any Fellowship cards on the company are discarded because a character has joined. Gandalf is immune to Despair and can support one check, but his +1 modifier applies only to his own checks. If Hundreds of Butterflies is also played after Gandalf joins, the limit can reach seven.

The Hero's main deck has meaningful responses:

- Concealment can cancel one eligible creature attack if a Scout can tap. A canceled attack causes no wound.
- A Friend or Three can protect one corruption check by +5 in a five-character company, or +6 in a six-character company.
- Fellowship can add +1 per copy to every company check, but it is lost if a character or ally joins/leaves.
- Marvels Told can discard Despair if a Sage is present, but the Sage must make a corruption check modified by -2. It can also remove Full of Froth and Rage or Wake of War, but it cannot cancel a creature attack or a short-event such as River.

The Hazard player should choose the most disadvantageous **legal** combination after considering the Hero cards and current company state. The three-Despair line is a stress test, not a guaranteed wound: the Hero may cancel an attack, modify a check or remove a permanent-event.

## 5. How often are all three Despairs drawn?

On an initial draw of eight cards from a 30-card Hazard deck containing three Despairs:

| Draw event | Probability |
|---|---:|
| At least one Despair | 62.1% |
| At least two Despairs | 16.6% |
| All three Despairs | 1.4% |

These are exact hypergeometric probabilities for the initial 8-of-30 draw. They are not a whole-campaign prediction: cards left in play, discarded cards and the Academy's supplementary Hazard recycling change the pool on later phases. The result shows why three copies create a real but relatively rare high-pressure line, rather than making the three-Despair combination the expected opening.

## 6. Despair removal is not a corruption check

During the target's Organization Phase, the target may try to remove Despair by tapping and rolling greater than 4. If the target is already tapped, or chooses not to tap, the rules allow a -3 modifier instead.

- Tap and roll: natural 5–12 succeeds (30/36).
- Use the -3 option: the modified result must still be greater than 4, so the natural roll must be 8–12 (15/36).
- This is a removal roll, not a corruption check. Do not add the character's +1/-1 corruption-check modifier, Fellowship, A Friend or Three, or corruption-check support taps.
- If the -3 option is used for that card this turn, no further removal attempt for that card can be made that turn. A tap attempt may be repeated only if the character can untap and has not made a -3 attempt.

If Despair is removed before storage, the Book bearer returns from 4 CP to 2 CP. If it remains, make the Book-storage check against 4 CP.

## 7. The Book's Sage ability and storage timing

The Book's Sage-only ability requires its bearer to be a Sage. Gandalf qualifies; none of the five starting Dwarves does. The CRF 17 erratum says to tap **Book of Mazarbûl**, not its bearer, during the Organization Phase to increase hand size by one until the next Untap Phase.

Once stored, the Book is no longer borne and is removed from play for purposes other than MPs, stage points and uniqueness. It no longer provides the Sage hand-size effect and its 2 CP no longer count against its former bearer.

Storage at Blue Mountain occurs in the Organization Phase when the company is already at the Dwarf-hold. It is not an action that requires entering the site during that turn's Site Phase. If the company has just arrived, wait until the next turn's Organization Phase.

## 8. The Gandalf decision

If Gandalf joins the five Dwarves at Rivendell before they leave:

- Company size and base Hazard Limit rise from five to six.
- Fellowship on that company is discarded because a character joined.
- Gandalf cannot be targeted by Despair; his own corruption checks receive +1.
- Gandalf is a Sage and can use the Book's hand-size ability while he bears it.
- Gandalf can tap to support one other character's corruption check, but each helper can support only one check.

If Gandalf stays separate, the Dwarves keep any Fellowship bonuses and their base Hazard Limit stays five. Gandalf cannot support their checks or bear their Book while separate. Moria is not a Haven, so do not plan to combine the companies there.
