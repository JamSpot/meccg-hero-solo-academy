# 6I. Shadow-land Route & Seized by Terror Lab

This is an advanced, optional route exercise. It does not change Game 1 or either active deck. Use the corrected 15-card Hero Location deck, the fixed 36-card Hero play deck and the separate 30-card Hazard deck.

The purpose is to make you read a route in three different ways:

1. **Region type** — determines whether a region-keyed Hazard such as Seized by Terror is legal.
2. **Site type** — determines whether a site-keyed card such as Arouse Denizens is legal.
3. **Site-card draw numbers and company size** — determine the solo draw and the Hazard Limit separately.

## The route: Lórien to The Wind Throne

Use the Hero version of **The Wind Throne**.

| Site-card detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Site | The Wind Throne |
| Region | Grey Mountain Narrows |
| Region type | Shadow-land |
| Site type | Ruins & Lairs |
| Nearest Haven | Lórien |
| Printed Starter Movement site path | Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land |
| Draw numbers | Hazard 2 / Hero 2 |
| Playable resources | Information; minor and major Items |
| Automatic attack if entered | Orcs — 3 strikes at 7 prowess |

Lórien is listed as the nearest Haven, so a company at Lórien can use **Starter Movement** to reach The Wind Throne. The printed path is used for the site-path rules; it is not a list of five or four regions that you must declare as Region Movement.

The destination is in Grey Mountain Narrows, which is itself a Shadow-land. Therefore, even if you choose a legal Region Movement path instead, the destination region remains part of that path. You cannot make Seized by Terror illegal by choosing a different route into this particular site.

## Draw and limit — do not merge the numbers

The Wind Throne's normal Hazard draw is 2. The Academy's printed solitaire procedure therefore gives:

**Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 2) = 6 cards.**

The Hero player may draw up to 2 cards from the Hero play deck. The moving company qualifies because it contains an avatar or characters with mind 3 or greater.

The base Hazard Limit is set separately at the beginning of this company's Movement/Hazard Phase:

| Company at the start of the phase | Base Hazard Limit | Cards drawn by solo Hazard side | Maximum ordinary plays |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Five starting Dwarves | 5 | 6 | 5 |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| Five Dwarves + Gandalf + Gildor | 7 | 6 | 6 |

The base limit is fixed for that Movement/Hazard Phase. Do not recalculate it if a character later leaves the company. The company-size limit and the number of Hazard cards actually drawn are different constraints.

The table assumes the Hazard side has not accessed its sideboard during the turn's Untap Phase. If it has, the base Hazard Limit is halved, rounded up.

The three Orc strikes at 7 are a **Site Phase automatic attack**. They do not consume Hazard Limit slots. The company faces them only if it enters the site.

## Why Seized by Terror is legal here

Seized by Terror is playable on a non-Wizard character moving in a Shadow-land or Dark-domain. The Wind Throne Starter Movement path contains a Shadow-land, and the destination region Grey Mountain Narrows is also a Shadow-land.

This is a **region-type** condition. Do not confuse it with the site's Ruins & Lairs type:

- Seized by Terror is legal because the company is moving in a Shadow-land.
- Arouse Denizens would be legal at this Ruins & Lairs site if it were included in a Hazard variant.
- Arouse Minions is not legal at The Wind Throne because it requires a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold.
- The Wind Throne is not a Shadow-hold merely because it lies in a Shadow-land.

The Book of Mazarbûl is **not** playable here. It is a Special Item playable only at Moria; The Wind Throne lists Information and minor/major Items, not Special Items. This route is an optional later expedition for other objectives, not an alternative Book destination.

## Seized by Terror — choose the target, then calculate

The card makes a normal roll (2D6) and adds the target's mind. If the result is **less than 12**, the target splits into a different company and immediately returns to the original company's site of origin.

It does not wound or eliminate the character. It removes that character from the expedition and leaves him behind at the origin.

| Starting Dwarf | Mind | Roll needed to split | Chance of split |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dori | 1 | 2D6 result 2–10 | **33/36 = 91.7%** |
| Kíli | 3 | 2D6 result 2–8 | **26/36 = 72.2%** |
| Óin | 3 | 2D6 result 2–8 | **26/36 = 72.2%** |
| Glóin | 5 | 2D6 result 2–6 | **15/36 = 41.7%** |
| Thorin II | 8 | 2D6 result 2–3 | **3/36 = 8.3%** |
| Gandalf | — | Cannot be targeted | **Not eligible: Wizard** |

**Default Hazard-side target: Dori.** He has the lowest mind and the highest chance of being split off. A different target could still be tactically preferable if the company state makes that character more important, but do not overlook the probability difference.

The original company's base Hazard Limit was fixed at the start of the phase. Do not lower it mid-phase simply because Seized by Terror has separated a character.

## How often do the key cards appear?

The active 30-card Hazard deck has two Seized by Terror and two River.

For a fresh six-card draw without replacement:

| Event | Exact probability | Rounded |
|---|---:|---:|
| At least one Seized by Terror | 53/145 | **36.6%** |
| At least one River | 53/145 | **36.6%** |
| Both River copies | 3/87 | **3.45%** |
| At least one Seized by Terror and at least one River | 31/261 | **11.9%** |

If Seized by Terror is drawn and played on Dori, the unmodified roll splits him off 91.7% of the time. If both copies are drawn, each is a separate potential play; do not treat the one-copy roll probability as the exact combined chance for two attempts.

These are exact deck-composition probabilities. They are not win probabilities and do not mean a card will always be chosen when drawn.

## Practice hand A — Seized by Terror and two Rivers

For this constructed six-card exercise, draw:

- Seized by Terror
- Cave Worm
- Lesser Spiders
- River
- River
- Wargs

All six have a legal route or target opportunity on the Lórien → Wind Throne journey.

**Your decisions:**

1. Which character should the Hazard side normally target with Seized by Terror?
2. What is the chance that target splits off?
3. How many Hazard plays can be made against the five-Dwarf company?
4. What changes if Gandalf has joined before the Movement/Hazard Phase?
5. How many Rangers must the Hero player tap to satisfy both Rivers?
6. What happens if the company has only Óin as a Ranger?
7. What changes if Gildor has joined and both Óin and Gildor are untapped?
8. If the company cannot satisfy both Rivers, what happens to its entire Site Phase? Can it enter the site or face the automatic attack?

Do not assume that the company automatically enters the site. Resolve the movement and Hazards first, then apply the River requirement at the beginning of the Site Phase.

## Practice hand B — attack pressure

For a second constructed six-card draw, use:

- Seized by Terror
- Cave Worm
- Lesser Spiders
- Wargs
- Full of Froth and Rage
- Wake of War

All six are legal on this route. With Gandalf in the six-character company, the Hazard Limit is six and all six can be played. With only the five starting Dwarves, one of the six must be left unplayed.

Before looking at the answer key:

- Decide whether Seized by Terror should target Dori.
- Play the support events before the creatures.
- Remember that Full of Froth and Rage affects Spider and Animal attacks, not Wolves.
- Remember that Wake of War affects Wolf, Spider and Animal attacks.
- Check the discard condition of Full of Froth and Rage after every Spider or Animal attack.
- Decide which attack should be resolved first and explain why.

## What this teaches about the Hero deck

The five starting Dwarves contain only one Ranger: Óin. Gandalf does not add Ranger skill. The active 36-card Hero deck does contain Gildor Inglorion, a Warrior/Ranger with mind 4.

Gildor's Ranger skill is usually redundant because Óin already supplies it. **The Wind Throne route gives Gildor a specific second-Ranger role:** if Gildor is legally recruited into the company and remains untapped, he can help satisfy a second River.

There is a real cost:

- With only Óin, one River can be satisfied; two Rivers prevent Site Phase actions.
- With Óin and Gildor, both can be tapped to satisfy two Rivers.
- Both Rangers are then tapped before the company faces The Wind Throne's three Orc strikes at 7 if it chooses to enter.

Gildor is therefore a situational route-support recruit, not an automatic addition to the opening company. He is already in the 36-card deck; no deck change or purchase is required.
