# Shadow-land Route & Seized by Terror Lab — Model Answer

[Return to the exercise]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. Movement and draw calculations

Lórien is the nearest Haven listed on the Hero version of The Wind Throne. Starter Movement is therefore legal from Lórien.

The printed site path is Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land. The destination is in Grey Mountain Narrows, which is a Shadow-land. Seized by Terror is legal because the moving company is moving in a Shadow-land.

The site-card numbers are Hazard 2 / Hero 2:

- Normal Hazard draw: 2.
- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 2) = **6**.
- Hero draw: up to **2**; the company qualifies for the Hero draw.
- Five Dwarves: base Hazard Limit **5**.
- Five Dwarves plus Gandalf: base Hazard Limit **6**.
- Five Dwarves, Gandalf and Gildor: base Hazard Limit **7**, but only six Hazard cards were drawn.

The base Hazard Limit is set at the beginning of the Movement/Hazard Phase and stays fixed for that phase. If Dori is split off by Seized by Terror, do not recalculate the original company's limit.

The Site Phase automatic attack is three Orc strikes at 7. It is separate from the Hazard Limit and is faced only if the company enters.

## 2. Seized by Terror target

There is no universal target. **Dori is the best probability target** when the Hazard side wants the highest chance of separating a non-Wizard character and River is absent or already guarantees a skipped Site Phase. But if River is in the Hazard hand, first count the number of Rivers and the company's untapped Rangers.

- With one River and only Óin as an untapped Ranger, target **Óin**. If he splits off, no Ranger remains to satisfy River and the Site Phase is skipped. Óin's split chance is 26/36 (72.2%).
- With two Rivers and Óin plus Gildor as the only two untapped Rangers, target **Óin**. If he splits off, only Gildor remains; one Ranger cannot satisfy two Rivers, so the Site Phase is skipped. Óin is more vulnerable than Gildor (72.2% versus 58.3%).
- With two Rivers but only Óin as a Ranger, the Rivers already make the Site Phase impossible. Target **Dori** for the higher 91.7% chance of removing a character, unless Óin is otherwise the more valuable target.
- With one River and both Óin and Gildor untapped, splitting either Ranger leaves one Ranger for the one River. Target Dori or another tactically important character instead.
- With no River, Dori is the highest-probability target.

The roll is 2D6 plus the target's mind. The total must remain below 12 for the target to split. Dori's mind 1 means results 2–10 split him, giving 33 of 36 outcomes and **33/36 = 91.7%**.

The comparison is:

| Target | Mind | Split probability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dori | 1 | 91.7% |
| Kíli | 3 | 72.2% |
| Óin | 3 | 72.2% |
| Gildor | 4 | 58.3% |
| Glóin | 5 | 41.7% |
| Thorin II | 8 | 8.3% |
| Gandalf | Wizard | Not a legal target |

The character is not wounded or eliminated. The target splits into a different company and immediately returns to the original company's site of origin. The rest of the expedition is not described by the card as being returned.

## 3. Practice hand A — two Rivers

The six drawn cards are Seized by Terror, Cave Worm, Lesser Spiders, River ×2 and Wargs.

All six are legal:

| Card | Why it is legal |
|---|---|
| Seized by Terror | The site path contains a Shadow-land and the company has eligible non-Wizard characters. |
| Cave Worm | Grey Mountain Narrows is a named-region key. |
| Lesser Spiders | The route contains a Shadow-land and the destination is Ruins & Lairs. |
| River ×2 | Each copy can be played on the new site, where the company has moved this turn. |
| Wargs | The route contains Border-land and Shadow-land. |

### The River ruling

River is played on a site. If the company has moved to that site this turn, it must tap a Ranger for each River or do nothing during its Site Phase.

The ruling is not “tap one Ranger for all copies.” It is **one Ranger per River**. The Hero player has until the beginning of the Site Phase to satisfy the requirement and may tap the Ranger at the beginning of the Site Phase without entering. If any River remains unsatisfied, the Site Phase is wholly skipped: the company cannot enter the site, cannot take Site Phase actions and does not face the site's automatic attack.

The starting Dwarves have one Ranger: Óin. Gandalf is not a Ranger.

- With one River, Óin can tap and satisfy it.
- With two Rivers, Óin can satisfy only one. Without another untapped Ranger, the company must do nothing during its Site Phase.
- If Gildor is in the company and both Gildor and Óin are untapped, they can each tap to satisfy one River.

This is why Gildor's Ranger skill has a route-specific use even though Óin already supplies Ranger skill in the starting company. Gildor also has +2 prowess against Orcs, which helps against the site's three Orc strikes at 7. If he taps to satisfy River, the normal tapped-character penalty still applies, so the bonus is reduced but not erased.

### The cost of using Gildor

Gildor is a 4-mind Ranger in the active Hero play deck. If he is legally recruited before the expedition, he can provide the second Ranger. The company can then satisfy both Rivers, but both Rangers are tapped before entry.

If the company enters The Wind Throne, it must face three Orc strikes at 7. The tapped Rangers have the normal tapped-character prowess penalty if they are assigned strikes. The Hero player must compare the value of taking Site Phase actions against the weakened combat position.

If the company cannot satisfy both Rivers, its Site Phase is skipped in its entirety. It cannot enter The Wind Throne, cannot play a Resource there and does not face the three Orc automatic-attack strikes.

### Five-Dwarf versus six-character Hazard Limit

The solo Hazard side draws six cards in either case.

- Against five Dwarves, the Hazard Limit is five, so one of the six legal cards must remain unplayed.
- Against five Dwarves plus Gandalf, the Hazard Limit is six, so all six can be played.

The Hazard side should compare the three creature attacks rather than assume a single universal card to omit. Cave Worm is one strike at 16; Lesser Spiders is four strikes at 7; Wargs is two strikes at 9. The best five-card selection depends on the company's current tapped/wounded state, defensive Resources and the value of denying its Site Phase.

## 4. Practice hand B — support-event sequence

The second six-card draw is Seized by Terror, Cave Worm, Lesser Spiders, Wargs, Full of Froth and Rage, and Wake of War.

All six have legal route or target opportunities.

### Apply the support events correctly

Play Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War before the creatures.

- **Lesser Spiders** is a Spider attack. Full of Froth and Rage gives +2 prowess; Wake of War gives +1 prowess and +1 strike. Its four strikes at 7 therefore become **five strikes at 10**.
- **Wargs** are Wolves. Wake of War gives +1 prowess and +1 strike, changing two strikes at 9 to **three strikes at 10**. Full of Froth and Rage does not affect Wolves.
- **Cave Worm** is a Drake. Neither support event modifies it; it remains one strike at 16.

Full of Froth and Rage is discarded if a Spider or Animal attack is defeated. It is not discarded merely because a Wolf attack is defeated. This makes attack order important:

1. Resolve Wargs while Full of Froth and Rage is still in play; Wargs are Wolves, not Spiders or Animals.
2. Resolve Cave Worm; it is a Drake and does not trigger the Full of Froth discard condition.
3. Resolve Lesser Spiders with both support bonuses still active, unless another Spider or Animal attack has already been defeated.

If Lesser Spiders is defeated, Full of Froth and Rage is discarded. Wake of War remains according to its long-event duration.

This is a strong default sequence for this constructed hand, not a universal order for every company. If the company is especially vulnerable to Cave Worm's single 16-prowess strike, the Hazard player may choose to resolve that attack earlier.

### What changes when Dori is split off?

If Seized by Terror succeeds, Dori is no longer part of the moving company. The later creature attacks are resolved against the company that remains. The original company's Hazard Limit does not shrink during the phase; it was fixed at the start.

## 5. Probability checks

For a fresh six-card draw from a 30-card deck with two copies of a named card:

- Chance of at least one copy: **1 − C(28,6) / C(30,6) = 36.6%**.
- Chance of both copies: **C(28,4) / C(30,6) = 3.45%**.

Therefore:

- At least one Seized by Terror appears in 36.6% of fresh six-card draws.
- At least one River appears in 36.6%.
- Both River copies appear in 3.45%.
- At least one Seized by Terror and at least one River appear together in **31/261 (11.9%)** of fresh six-card draws.

If Seized by Terror is drawn and played on Dori, the unmodified split probability is 91.7%. If both copies are drawn, each is a separate potential play; the combined chance of success depends on whether the Hazard player uses one or both copies and on the result of each roll.

These figures measure card access and the printed roll, not game outcome.

## 6. The Book and site-type distinction

The Wind Throne is a Ruins & Lairs in a Shadow-land. It is not a Shadow-hold.

- Seized by Terror uses the Shadow-land region type.
- Arouse Denizens can target the Ruins & Lairs automatic attack if included in a later variant.
- Arouse Minions is not legal at this site.
- Book of Mazarbûl is a Special Item playable only at Moria, so it cannot be played at The Wind Throne.

The baseline Hazard deck does not contain Arouse Denizens. Do not add it during this exercise.

## 7. The Hero deck lesson

Gildor is already included in the six character/Wizard cards of the 36-card Hero play deck. He is not a required purchase or a deck-list change.

His Ranger skill is normally redundant beside Óin. The Wind Throne route reveals a specific reason to recruit him: he can be the second untapped Ranger needed to satisfy two Rivers. This is a conditional role, not a reason to recruit him automatically on every expedition.

## The return destination and what happens next

A successful target returns to the company's site of origin, which is Lórien in this exercise. The original company continues its Movement/Hazard Phase at The Wind Throne. The split character receives no new Hazard Limit or separate Hazard draw and cannot take Site Phase actions that turn. A Palantír borne by the target is not discarded merely because the character is no longer moving.
