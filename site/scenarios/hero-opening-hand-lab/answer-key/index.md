# Hero Opening Hand & Access Lab — Model Answer

[Return to the workbook]({{ '/scenarios/hero-opening-hand-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. Probability method

For a 36-card deck with k copies of a card or group, the chance of seeing at least one in n cards is:

**1 − C(36 − k, n) / C(36, n)**

This is a draw-without-replacement calculation. It is not the same as multiplying a single-card percentage by the number of draws.

## 2. Opening-hand answers

| Group | Copies | First 5 cards | First 7 cards |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One specific card | 1 | **13.9%** | **19.4%** |
| Two-copy card | 2 | **26.2%** | **35.6%** |
| Three-copy card | 3 | **37.0%** | **48.8%** |
| Any character/Wizard card | 6 | **62.2%** | **75.6%** |
| Any site-playable Resource | 12 | **88.7%** | **95.9%** |
| Any Sage-capable card | 3 | **37.0%** | **48.8%** |

The 12 site-playable Resources are eight Items, two Factions and two Allies. The 18 other Resources are supporting events. The six character/Wizard cards are in the same Hero play deck.

The expected number of site-playable Resources in the first five cards is 5 × 12/36 = **1.67**. That is an average across many shuffled opening hands; an individual hand may contain none or several.

## 3. After the Moria draw

Moria is 3/2. The Hero company may draw up to two cards from its Hero play deck, so a maximum draw means seven distinct cards have been seen in total.

- Chance of seeing Book of Mazarbûl by then: **19.4%**.
- Chance of seeing Gandalf by then: **19.4%**.
- Chance of seeing at least one of Gandalf, Balin or Celeborn: **48.8%**.
- Chance of seeing at least one of the 12 site-playable Resources: **95.9%**.

The Hero hand is then reset to its current target (normally five; six while the Book's Sage ability is active). The player may retain a key card and discard less useful cards. Do not leave all seven cards in hand, and do not mistake the seven-card “seen” sample for a seven-card hand.

The Hazard side separately draws eight cards: 2 + (2 × 3) = **8**. The five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit is **5**. If Gandalf or another character has joined the Dwarves before the phase, recalculate company size; a six-character company has a base Hazard Limit of six.

## 4. Hand A — Book and Gandalf

- Gandalf can be played at Rivendell, a Haven, during the Organization Phase.
- You may play only one character or Wizard in that Organization Phase; do not also play Balin. Other legal Organization actions remain available.
- If Gandalf joins the Dwarves, the company becomes six characters and its base Hazard Limit becomes six.
- Book of Mazarbûl is the mission Resource and is only playable at Moria.
- Concealment is a Scout response to an actual attack; retain it until a legal attack exists.
- Gandalf is a Sage, but the Book's hand-size ability does not apply until Gandalf actually bears the Book. His presence elsewhere in the company is not enough.

Check Fellowship and A Friend or Three against their exact conditions and the current company/check before use. Neither is an automatic pre-movement play merely because it is in hand.

## 5. Hand B — Book and Balin

- Balin may be played at Rivendell because it is a Haven.
- If Balin joins the Dwarves, the company becomes six characters and its base Hazard Limit becomes six.
- Marvels Told is Sage-only. It cannot be used until Balin is in play and able to tap; it also imposes its own corruption-check cost.
- Balin is a Sage. If he bears Book of Mazarbûl, he can tap it during the Organization Phase to increase the hand-size target by one until the next untap phase.
- If a Dwarf already bears the Book, transferring it to Balin at the same site requires a corruption check for the initial bearer. It is not a free transfer.

## 6. Hand C — competing objectives, no Book

- The Book mission cannot be completed from this hand because Book of Mazarbûl is absent.
- Gollum points to Goblin-gate; Iron Hill Dwarves points to Iron Hill Dwarf-hold. They are different objectives with different site and influence requirements.
- Not at Home is not a universal cancellation for Moria's Orc automatic attack. Its printed cancellation applies to Dragon, Drake or Troll attacks; its alternate automatic-attack option depends on Gates of Morning being in play. Moria's automatic attack is Orcs.
- A card for a destination does not make the route automatically worthwhile. Check the site is in the Location deck, the route is legal, the draw numbers, Hazard keys, automatic attack, required skill/influence and next position.
- The Hero must decide whether to preserve the Book mission and continue drawing, or explicitly switch to another objective. Do not silently count Gollum or Iron Hill as completing the Book mission.

## 7. Why a stationary company does not cycle the Hero deck

Normal rules give no movement-based draw when a company does not move. The printed solo procedure is applied by the Academy as two Hazard draws for a stationary company (normal draw zero, then 2 + 2 × 0), but the Hero makes no movement draw. The Academy labels this a literal application of the solitaire wording, not a separate Council ruling; a scenario-specific instruction can override it.

## 8. What the evidence says about the deck

A singleton appearing in only 13.9% of opening hands is normal for a one-copy card; it is not evidence of a deck defect. The site-playable package is dense enough that nearly nine in ten opening hands contain at least one such Resource, but the particular site and objective still matter.

Record these separately:
- not seen;
- seen and retained;
- seen but discarded;
- seen but no legal opportunity arose;
- played successfully;
- attempted and failed.
