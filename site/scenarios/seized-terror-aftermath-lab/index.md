# 6L. Seized by Terror — Aftermath & Regrouping Lab

This advanced lab follows **Seized by Terror** beyond the dice roll. It explains where the split character goes, which company continues to face Hazards, what happens to carried cards and followers, and how the characters can meet again.

It does not change the 36-card Hero deck, 30-card Hazard deck, or 15-card Hero Location deck.

## The card's complete consequence

Seized by Terror is playable on a non-Wizard character **with a mind value** moving in a Shadow-land or Dark-domain. The target rolls 2D6 and adds mind; a Ringwraith, which has no mind value, cannot be targeted. If the result is **less than 12**, the target splits into a different company and immediately returns to the original company's **site of origin**.

That means:

- The target returns to the site from which the moving company began this Movement/Hazard Phase.
- It is **not** a return to the target's home site or the nearest Haven.
- The rest of the original company continues its current Movement/Hazard Phase at the revealed destination.
- The returned company's site path immediately disappears and its Movement/Hazard Phase ends. It does not start a second phase, draw a second hand of Hazards, or receive a new Hazard Limit.
- The returned company cannot initiate actions during its Site Phase.

The distinction between the two companies is essential. The original company remains the current company for the Movement/Hazard Phase; the returned character is no longer moving.

## What happens to the Hazard Limit?

The original company's base Hazard Limit was fixed at the beginning of its Movement/Hazard Phase. It does not shrink when a character splits away.

CoE Rulings Digest #52, Query 42, explicitly answers that the split-off character does **not** receive a new Hazard Limit of two. Do not create a second Hazard allowance for the character returning to the origin.

The Hazard player may continue to use the original moving company's remaining Hazard Limit against the company that is still moving, subject to normal keys, targets and timing.

## Carried cards and effects

The character remains in play, so do not discard cards merely because the character has split away.

| Card or effect | What happens |
|---|---|
| An item borne by the split character | Remains with that character unless a specific card effect says otherwise. |
| Palantír of Amon Sûl / Palantír of Osgiliath | CoE Digest #120, Query 2, explicitly says it is not discarded when Seized by Terror succeeds: the character is no longer considered to be moving. If that character attempts to move alone in a later Movement/Hazard Phase, the Palantír is discarded. |
| A short-lasting effect that says “his company” or “the character's company” | Follows the named character into the new company. |
| A short-lasting effect that simply affects “the company” without naming a character | Spreads across the resulting companies under CoE Digest #71, Query 9. |
| A resource permanent-event or ongoing effect targeting the original company as an entity | Stays with the original company under the company-splitting rule. |
| A hazard permanent-event on the original company | The Hazard player chooses which resulting company it accompanies under the company-splitting rule. |

If you want to transfer an item away from the split character, the two characters must be at the same site. They may be in different companies, but the normal Organization Phase transfer procedure and the giver's corruption check still apply. You cannot transfer the item while the two groups are at different sites.

Do not treat every company effect as interchangeable. Read the exact wording: **“his company”** and **“the company”** can produce different results after a split.

## Followers: who travels with whom?

A follower must remain stacked under the character controlling it. The rules also explicitly allow a character and that character's followers to be moved together between companies during Organization.

Apply those rules carefully:

- **If Seized by Terror targets a controlling character:** that character's followers remain stacked under the controller and accompany that controller's new company back to the site of origin.
- **If Seized by Terror targets a follower:** the follower splits away from its controller and returns to the site of origin. The controller is now at a different site, so the follower is no longer controlled by that character's direct influence.

When a follower is removed from direct-influence control outside the Organization Phase, its mind is not immediately charged against general influence. At the next Organization Phase, it must be placed under general influence or under a character with sufficient direct influence at the same site; otherwise it is discarded at the end of that phase.

This is a consequence of the general follower rules, not a separate Seized by Terror card erratum. If an unusual company composition or a special effect changes the situation, check those rules separately.

## What if the site-of-origin card has already left play?

A return-to-origin effect does not fail merely because the site card has been removed. CoE Digest #125, Query 6, rules that when a company must return to its site of origin and that site card is no longer in play, the site card (or resource card acting as the site) is returned to play in the same orientation it left, even if it had reached the discard or out-of-play pile. This is different from a company simply losing its site card without a return effect; in that case the characters are discarded.

## Can the two groups immediately rejoin?

Not simply because the split character has returned.

At the end of all Movement/Hazard Phases:

- Companies at the same non-Haven site must join. If joining would violate company-composition rules, one company of the Hazard player's choice must return to its site of origin. Companies at the same Haven may join or not as the resource player chooses.
- Companies at the same Haven may join if the resource player chooses.
- Companies at different sites remain separate.

In the usual Lórien → The Wind Throne example, the main company continues to The Wind Throne while the split character returns to Lórien. They are at different sites, so they do not automatically rejoin. Plan a later return journey or another legal effect if you need to regroup them.

A company returned to its site of origin cannot take Site Phase actions that turn. The split character does not get to enter the origin site and play a Resource just because the card returned him there.

## River: count the Rangers who remain with the moving company

This is where the return effect matters to the Hero player's plan.

On Lórien → The Wind Throne, the printed Starter Movement path contains a Shadow-land, so Seized by Terror is legal. The destination has a normal Hazard draw of two, giving a six-card solo Hazard draw. The five starting Dwarves have only one Ranger: Óin.

| Hazard decision | Result |
|---|---|
| Seized by Terror splits Óin | Óin returns to Lórien. No Ranger remains with the moving company at The Wind Throne. One River cannot be cancelled. |
| Seized by Terror splits Dori | Dori returns to Lórien. Óin remains with the moving company and can tap for one River. |
| Two Rivers are played against the five-Dwarf company | One Ranger cannot satisfy two Rivers, regardless of which character Seized by Terror targets. |
| Óin and Gildor are both untapped Rangers; one River is played | If one is split away, the other can still tap for River. |
| Óin and Gildor are both untapped Rangers; two Rivers are played | Both are needed. Splitting either one leaves too few Rangers. |

The split changes who is available at the destination; it does **not** change the Hazard Limit that was set for the original company.

## Promptings of Wisdom — the important exception

Promptings of Wisdom can cancel Hazard effects that force **the Ranger's company** to return to its site of origin. Its timing matters.

CoE Digest #210, Query 6 (9 August 2025), clarifies two cases:

1. **Seized by Terror targets the Ranger who has Promptings of Wisdom.** If that Ranger taps in response to the effect that would return him, Promptings cancels the return. The Ranger continues to the new site alone, without a separate Movement/Hazard Phase, and rejoins the company at the end of the turn's Movement/Hazard Phases. The protection follows that Ranger; the rest of the original company is no longer protected by it.
2. **Seized by Terror targets a different character.** Promptings remains with the company containing its Ranger. It does not protect the other character from being split and returned to the site of origin.

This is not a general immunity to Seized by Terror. It is a specific response to the return effect, and the chain of effects must be resolved in the correct order.

## Practice questions

Answer before opening the model answer.

1. The five starting Dwarves move from Lórien to The Wind Throne. Seized by Terror targets Óin. The roll is 8; Óin adds mind 3. Does he split? Where does he go?
2. The Hazard player then plays one River. Can the company at The Wind Throne satisfy it?
3. Instead, Seized by Terror targets Dori. The roll is 10; Dori adds mind 1. Does the company still have a Ranger for River?
4. Does the split-off character receive a separate two-card or two-Hazard-limit Movement/Hazard Phase?
5. A split character bears Palantír of Amon Sûl. Is it discarded immediately? What happens if he tries to move alone next turn?
6. The target is a follower controlled by a character who continues to the destination. What happens to the direct-influence relationship?
7. The target is the controlling character with two followers. Who accompanies him back to the origin?
8. A Ranger with Promptings of Wisdom is targeted by Seized by Terror. What must he tap in response to cancel the return, and which company remains protected?
9. The main company and the returned character are at different sites at the end of all Movement/Hazard Phases. Can they simply join?
10. Which part of the original company's Hazard Limit remains available after the split?
11. If the site-of-origin card has already left play when the return effect resolves, what happens to that site card?
