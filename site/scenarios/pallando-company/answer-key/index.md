# Pallando — Model Answer

## 1. Plan A: Pallando joins the Dwarves

The company contains five Dwarves plus Pallando: **six characters**.

Moria is a non-Haven destination with a normal Hazard draw number of 3. The Academy's solitaire procedure is:

**2 + (2 × normal Hazard draw) = 2 + (2 × 3) = 8 cards.**

The base Hazard Limit is the company size or two, whichever is greater. Six characters therefore give a base Hazard Limit of **6**.

This creates one Moria Movement/Hazard Phase: eight cards drawn, up to six ordinary Hazard plays, subject to legality and card text.

The main cost is strategic, not just numerical: Pallando is travelling with the Dwarves into Moria and is exposed to the same Hazard phase and the site's automatic attack. A Wizard's elimination ends the game.

## 2. Plan B: separate companies

### Thorin's Dwarves

The Dwarves remain a five-character company.

- Route: Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.
- Destination: Moria.
- Normal Hazard draw number: 3.
- Solo Hazard draw: **8**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.

### Pallando

Pallando is a one-character company at his printed home site, Grey Havens. He moves to Lórien using Starter Movement because both sites are Havens and each site card lists a path to the other.

Lórien is a Haven destination. Under normal rules, the Hazard draw is based on the **site of origin**, not the new Haven. The origin is Grey Havens, whose normal Hazard draw number is 2.

- Route: Grey Havens → Lórien, using the printed Haven-to-Haven site path.
- Normal Hazard draw number used: **Grey Havens 2**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6**.
- Company size: 1.
- Base Hazard Limit: **2**, because the normal minimum is two.

### Combined total — but not a combined phase

Plan B draws **8 + 6 = 14 Hazard cards** across two separate Movement/Hazard Phases.

| Company | Cards drawn | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Five Dwarves to Moria | 8 | 5 |
| Pallando to Lórien | 6 | 2 |
| **Total** | **14** | **Two separate limits: 5 and 2** |

The Hazard deck is shared, but each company is assessed separately. Do not pool the 14 cards and choose seven for whichever company you prefer. Resolve the Dwarves' phase and Pallando's phase as separate company encounters, using the relevant route, target, timing and limit for each. The solo procedure directs you to play as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within that company's own limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination.

## 3. Pallando's hand-size effect

The solitaire base hand size is five. Pallando's card says his controlling player may keep one more card than normal. While Pallando is in play, the Academy hand target is therefore **six cards** at the normal hand resets.

This is a Hero hand-size effect. It does not change the number of cards drawn from the separate Hazard deck or increase a company's Hazard Limit.

## 4. Which plan is better?

There is no automatic winner.

### Plan A — join the Dwarves

**Advantages**
- One company and one Movement/Hazard Phase.
- Pallando contributes another character to combat.
- The Wizard travels with the protection of the Dwarves.

**Costs**
- Hazard Limit rises from five to six.
- Pallando is exposed to the Moria journey and automatic attack.
- The Wizard's survival is now tied to the main expedition's danger.

### Plan B — separate companies

**Advantages**
- Pallando avoids Moria's automatic attack.
- His company has its own lower Hazard Limit of two.
- He reaches Lórien, where he can develop a support company and recruit followers.

**Costs**
- The Hazard deck must supply 14 cards across the two phases.
- Pallando faces six drawn Hazards alone, with only the normal minimum limit of two.
- A solo Wizard company has no companion to absorb or share the danger.
- The Hazard deck will cycle more quickly.

For a cautious first campaign, it is reasonable to keep Game 1 focused on the Dwarves and introduce Pallando's separate company only after you are comfortable resolving one company at a time. Once you do use Plan B, do not soften the Hazard choices: apply the two separate limits honestly.

## 5. What happens next?

The Challenge Deck C Player Guide recommends Pallando at Lórien building a company from the play deck. His support objectives include:

- Gollum at Goblin-gate;
- Quickbeam at Wellinghall;
- Book of Mazarbul at Moria if Thorin's Dwarves have not already acquired it;
- later bringing Blue Mountain Dwarves from the sideboard into the Hero play deck or discard pile.
