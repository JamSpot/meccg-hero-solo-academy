# Historical Pallando Scenario — Model Answer

> **Historical reference only.** The active Academy uses Gandalf, not Pallando.

The historical Pallando example has the same company sizes and solo draw calculations as the Gandalf exercise:

- Five Dwarves to Moria: draw 8, base Hazard Limit 5.
- Pallando alone, Grey Havens to Lórien: Lórien is a Haven, so use Grey Havens' normal Hazard draw number 2; draw 6, base Hazard Limit 2.
- Total: 14 cards across two separate Movement/Hazard Phases, with separate limits of 5 and 2.

The historical Pallando card lets the controller keep one more card than normal, so the five-card solo Hero hand target becomes six while Pallando is in play.

**Do not transfer that ability to Gandalf.** Gandalf's card instead gives +1 to all his corruption checks and does not change hand size.
