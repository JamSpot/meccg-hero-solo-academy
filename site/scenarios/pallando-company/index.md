# Historical Pallando Scenario — Archived

> **Not part of the active Gandalf-led Academy course.** The Academy's Wizard is Gandalf. This page is retained only as a historical comparison with the Pallando-based Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C.

The old Pallando exercise compared:

- Pallando joining the five Dwarves at Rivendell and moving to Moria as a six-character company; or
- Pallando forming a separate one-character company at Grey Havens and moving to Lórien while the Dwarves moved to Moria.

Historical calculations:

| Company | Solo Hazard draw | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Five Dwarves to Moria | 8 | 5 |
| Pallando alone, Grey Havens → Lórien | 6 | 2 |

The total 14 cards are drawn across two separate company phases, not one combined Hazard hand. Pallando's card raises the Hero hand target from five to six. Do not apply that hand-size effect to Gandalf.

For the active exercise, use [Gandalf — One Company or Two?]({{ '/scenarios/gandalf-company/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/gandalf-company/answer-key/' | relative_url }}).
