# Pallando — One Company or Two?

**Purpose:** practise the first important Wizard decision in Challenge Deck C. You must compare the risk of keeping Pallando with the Dwarves against using him as a separate support company.

This is a scenario exercise, not a change to the published card list or the normal rules.

## Starting situation

- Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin are at Rivendell.
- Thorin controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin.
- The two starting Cram items are assigned as listed in Challenge Deck C.
- Pallando is in your hand. No Wizard is currently in play.
- Your Hero hand target is five before Pallando enters play.
- The 30-card Hazard deck is shuffled and separate.
- Your Location deck contains the listed sites, including Grey Havens, Lórien and Moria.

Your main mission remains **Book of Mazarbul at Moria**. The Challenge Deck C player guide recommends using Pallando as a support company at Lórien, but you must decide whether that is worth the extra Hazard exposure in this solo procedure.

## Decision 1 — Where can Pallando be played?

Pallando's printed home site is Grey Havens. A Wizard may be brought into play at his home site or at Rivendell, provided you do not already have a Wizard in play.

You may not play another character and Pallando in the same Organization Phase: playing a character or Wizard is one character-play activity for that phase.

## Decision 2 — Choose a company plan

### Plan A — Pallando joins the Dwarves

Place Pallando in Thorin's company at Rivendell and move together to Moria using the Dwarves' Region Movement route:

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

Calculate the new company size, solo Hazard draw and Hazard Limit.

### Plan B — Pallando forms a separate company

Play Pallando at his home site, **Grey Havens**, as a separate company. Move him to Lórien using the Haven-to-Haven Starter Movement path printed on the site cards. Move the five Dwarves from Rivendell to Moria using their Region Movement route.

Calculate each company's solo Hazard draw and Hazard Limit separately.

## Your tasks

Before opening the answer key, write down:

1. How many characters are in each company under Plan A?
2. How many Hazard cards does Plan A draw for Moria?
3. What is Plan A's base Hazard Limit?
4. Under Plan B, which site's normal Hazard draw number is used for Pallando's move to Lórien, and why?
5. How many Hazard cards does Pallando's company draw?
6. What is the Hazard Limit for a one-character Wizard company?
7. What is the total number of Hazard cards drawn across both companies in Plan B?
8. Why is that not one combined Hazard Limit?
9. What happens to your Hero hand target while Pallando is in play?
10. Which plan better protects the Wizard, and what extra risk does that plan create?

**Do not choose the plan that merely gives the Heroes fewer Hazards.** Choose the plan you can justify from the card text, movement rules, solo procedure and actual company state.
