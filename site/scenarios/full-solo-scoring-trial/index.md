# Full Solo Scoring Trial — play for the second exhaustion

Use this after you understand Game 1, the Book of Mazarbul expedition and the integrated Campaign Walkthrough. This is the step from a mission-based tutorial to the **printed MECCG solitaire scoring objective**.

The official solitaire objective is to accumulate as many marshalling points (MPs) as possible by the time your **Hero play deck is exhausted for the second time**. This is a score challenge, not a fixed 25-MP win threshold. Do not confuse it with the multiplayer Free Council triggers.

## 1. Keep the Academy decks unchanged

Use the physical cards already listed in the Academy:

| Component | What to use |
|---|---|
| Hero play deck | **36 cards**: 30 Resources, five non-Wizard characters and one Gandalf Wizard card |
| Hazard deck | **30 separate Hazards** from Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C |
| Location deck | The 15 Hero site cards, kept separate and unshuffled |
| Starting company | Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin at Rivendell |
| Starting items | Cram with Thorin II and Cram with Óin |
| Hero hand | Five cards |
| Sideboard | Leave it closed for this first scoring trial |

Do not put Hazards into the Hero play deck. Do not add Pallando to the active Gandalf build. No proxies or substitute cards are needed.

The five starting Dwarves have a combined mind of 20, so they form a legal starting group. The six character/Wizard cards in the Hero play deck are drawn and played later only when their normal requirements are met.

See [Hero Deck Construction]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}) and [Hazard Deck Construction]({{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }}) for the exact card lists.

## 2. What changes from the Book tutorial?

The normal MECCG rules still govern movement, site paths, card legality, combat, site entry, corruption and scoring. The official solitaire procedure changes the deck arrangement, hand size and Hazard draw.

There is one important difference from the Academy's first mission:

| Situation | Book of Mazarbul tutorial | Full scoring trial |
|---|---|---|
| Main objective | Acquire the Book and learn the expedition loop | Accumulate as many MPs as possible by second Hero-deck exhaustion |
| Defeated non-unique Hazard creature | Academy mission procedure: place it in the Hazard discard so it can return when that deck is recycled | Normal scoring: put a defeated creature in the Hero MP pile and count its printed kill MPs |
| Hazard deck exhausted | Recycle its discard pile under the Academy's published solo supplement | Same recycling supplement, but creatures already scored in the Hero MP pile stay out |
| End of game | Mission debrief and recovery decision | Second exhaustion of the Hero play deck |

Do not carry the tutorial's special treatment of defeated non-unique creatures into the scoring trial. In scoring mode, defeating a Hazard creature is one of the ways the Hero side earns MPs.

An attack is defeated only when all strikes directed against the company are defeated. A canceled or ineffectual strike means the attack was not defeated. If a creature has multiple attacks, each must be defeated before the creature awards its kill MPs.

## 3. Run each turn in the normal order

Use the normal six phases:

1. **Untap:** untap cards and heal eligible wounded characters at a Haven.
2. **Organization:** play eligible characters, reorganize, transfer or store items, and declare each company's movement.
3. **Long-event:** resolve the normal long-event cleanup and play any eligible Resource long-events.
4. **Movement/Hazard:** resolve a separate phase for each company.
5. **Site:** decide whether each company enters its current site; if it enters, face the site's automatic attacks before attempting site Resources.
6. **End of turn:** complete the normal cleanup and reset the Hero hand to five.

Do not skip the decision to enter a site. Arrival during the Movement/Hazard Phase is not the same as entering during the Site Phase.

## 4. Calculate both movement draws and the Hazard Limit

For each company that moves, identify the relevant Hero site card first.

- Moving to a **non-Haven**: use the destination site's two draw numbers.
- Moving to a **Haven**: use the site of origin's two draw numbers.
- Site-card draw numbers are commonly written **opponent draws / you draw**. The first is the normal Hazard-player number; the second is the Hero/resource-player number.
- The Hero player draws from the Hero play deck, up to the **you draw** number, if the company contains an avatar or a character with mind 3 or greater. A qualifying company must draw at least one card.
- The solo Hazard side separately draws from the Hazard deck using the first number:

**Solo Hazard draw = 2 + (2 × normal Hazard-player draw number)**

A company that does not move has no normal movement draw under the ordinary rules. The generic printed solitaire paragraph does not fully settle every stationary-company case; use a scenario that states its procedure rather than inventing a universal number.

The Hazard Limit is a third, separate calculation. Under the current normal rules, the base limit is the company's size or two, whichever is greater; Hobbits and Orc scouts count as half a character, rounded up. The base limit is fixed at the beginning of that company's Movement/Hazard Phase. A card such as Hundreds of Butterflies can modify the limit, but does not change either draw.

### Worked calculations

| Journey | Site-card numbers (opponent / you) | Hero draw | Solo Hazard draw | Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rivendell → Moria | 3/2 | Up to **2** | **8** | **5** |
| Moria → Rivendell | 3/2 (use Moria, the origin) | Up to **2** | **8** | **5** |
| Moria → Goblin-gate | 2/2 | Up to **2** | **6** | **5** |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | 1/1 | Up to **1** | **4** | **5** |

After each company's Movement/Hazard Phase, reset the Hero hand to its current target: **five** for the active Gandalf baseline, modified by any active hand-size effect such as Pallando or a Sage tapping Book of Mazarbûl. Do not confuse that hand reset with either draw, and do not put Hazard cards into the Hero hand.

If Hundreds of Butterflies increases the five-Dwarf company's limit by one, the Hazard side may have up to six limit-counting plays from the cards already drawn, if six legal plays exist. The draw does not increase. Twilight remains the explicit exception: it does not count against the Hazard Limit, but it still needs a legal Environment target.

For each draw, inspect every card's key, target, timing and conditions. Play as many drawn Hazards as possible within the limit, choosing the combination most disadvantageous to the current company. Never invent a key or play an illegal card merely to fill a slot.

## 5. Keep the two discard-and-recycle systems separate

There are two decks with different jobs:

### Hero play deck

When the Hero play deck is exhausted for the **first time**, follow the normal deck-exhaustion procedure: return site cards in the discard pile to the Location deck, then shuffle the other eligible cards in the Hero discard pile to form the next Hero play deck.

Cards in play remain in play. Cards in the Hero MP pile and out-of-play pile do not return to the Hero play deck. A character discarded rather than eliminated follows the normal character rules; an eliminated character remains out of play.

When the Hero play deck is exhausted for the **second time**, stop the scoring trial and total the MPs.

### Hazard deck

The Hazard deck is separate. Under the Academy's explicitly labelled supplement from [My Solo Rules](https://meccg.wordpress.com/my-solo-rules/), drawn-but-unplayed Hazards are discarded at the end of that company's Movement/Hazard Phase, and the Hazard discard is shuffled to form a new Hazard deck when required.

In scoring mode, defeated Hazard creatures are in the Hero MP pile, not the Hazard discard. They do not return when the Hazard deck is recycled. The Hazard deck can therefore become more event-heavy as the game progresses. That is an intended consequence of earning kill MPs, not a reason to put defeated creatures back.

## 6. Understand the Hazard deck's starting balance

The 30-card Challenge Deck C Hazard list contains:

- **16 creatures** — Cave Worm ×3, Giant Spiders ×3, Lesser Spiders ×3, Neeker-breekers ×2, Wargs ×2 and Watcher in the Water ×3.
- **14 non-creature Hazards** — Despair of the Heart ×3, Full of Froth and Rage ×3, River ×2, Seized by Terror ×2, Twilight ×2 and Wake of War ×2.

That is a 53% creature / 47% event starting mix, close to the approximate 50/50 balance suggested by the independent solo-deck guide. The list contains **no Dragon creatures**; Cave Worm is a Drake. It therefore gives the course creature pressure and attack combinations without making the opening Hazard deck Dragon-heavy.

In the full scoring trial, each defeated creature that enters the Hero MP pile reduces the creature count available for later Hazard cycles. Record this change rather than silently replacing cards.

## 7. Choose objectives from the hand and the map

Do not travel to every site just because it is in the Location deck. Look at the Resources in your five-card hand, identify what can realistically be played, and then choose a nearby legal destination.

Useful objectives already supported by this teaching deck include:

| Resource | Site objective |
|---|---|
| Book of Mazarbul | Moria; later store it at a Dwarf-hold for 5 MP |
| Gollum | Goblin-gate or Moria |
| Iron Hill Dwarves | Iron Hill Dwarf-hold |
| Men of Dorwinion | Shrel-Kain |
| Quickbeam | Wellinghall |
| Bow of Dragon-horn / Magical Harp | Ovir Hollow, subject to the exact card and site conditions |
| Durin's Axe / Hauberk of Bright Mail | Sarn Goriwing or Buhr Widu |
| Enruned Shield | Caves of Ûlund or The Lonely Mountain |
| Wormsbane | Dead Marshes, Caves of Ûlund or The Lonely Mountain |

Use the [Free / Player-Directed Movement guide]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) and [Expedition Planner]({{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}) before committing. Normal Region Movement is limited to four consecutive regions, including both endpoints, with no repeated region and legal adjacency. Starter Movement remains limited by the nearest-Haven conditions on the actual site cards.

A Haven is a useful recovery option, not a mandatory stop after every expedition. Compare the route's Hazard draw, creature keys, site automatic attack, objective value, arrival condition and next position.

## 8. Record the score honestly

Use the printed MP values and card instructions. The following categories help keep the tally auditable:

| MP source | What to record |
|---|---|
| Characters | Printed character MPs for characters still eligible to count |
| Items | In-play or stored value as appropriate; a stored item uses its stored value, not both values added together |
| Factions | Printed faction MPs after a successful influence attempt |
| Allies | Printed ally MPs |
| Defeated Hazard creatures | Printed kill MPs when the attack is actually defeated |
| Other Resources | MPs earned by following the card's own instructions |

For Book of Mazarbul, count **1 MP while it is in play**. If successfully stored at a Dwarf-hold, count **5 MP instead**; do not add the two values together.

### Scoring record

At setup, the five starting Dwarves represent **7 printed character MPs**: Thorin II 3, Dori 0, Kíli 1, Glóin 2 and Óin 1. Record those MPs while the characters remain eligible to count, and update the total if a character later leaves play.

| Checkpoint | Hero play-deck exhaustion | Character MPs | Item / stored MPs | Faction MPs | Ally MPs | Hazard kill MPs | Other MPs | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Start | 0 | **7** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **7** |
| First exhaustion | 1 | | | | | | | |
| Second exhaustion | 2 | | | | | | | |

Also record:

- How many times the Hazard deck recycled.
- How many Hazard creatures were defeated and moved to the Hero MP pile.
- Which routes were legal and which were actually used.
- Which Resources remained dead in hand or became useful later.
- Which company decisions preserved or lost future opportunities.

## 9. Check yourself before starting

1. **Five Dwarves to Moria:** how many Hazard cards are drawn, and what is the base Hazard Limit?  
   **Check:** eight cards are drawn; the base limit is five.

2. **Moria to Rivendell:** which site's normal Hazard number applies?  
   **Check:** Moria, because Rivendell is the destination Haven; the solo draw is eight.

3. **Three Hazard creatures are defeated:** where do they go in this scoring mode?  
   **Check:** the Hero MP pile, where their printed kill MPs count. They do not return to the Hazard deck.

4. **The Hazard deck runs out:** does the scoring trial end?  
   **Check:** no. Recycle its eligible discard under the Academy supplement. The game-end clock is the Hero play deck.

5. **The Hero play deck runs out for the first time:** does the scoring trial end?  
   **Check:** no. Reshuffle the eligible Hero discard to form the next Hero play deck.

6. **The Hero play deck runs out for the second time:** what happens?  
   **Check:** stop and total the score.
