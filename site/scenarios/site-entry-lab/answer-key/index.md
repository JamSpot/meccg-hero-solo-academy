# Site Entry & Automatic Attack Lab — Model Answer

Use this after completing the [workbook]({{ '/scenarios/site-entry-lab/' | relative_url }}). The route and entry decisions are examples; the best choice depends on the actual company condition and cards drawn.

## 1. Keep three different limits and attacks separate

- **Normal site Hazard draw number:** printed on the site card and selected by the normal movement rules.
- **Solo Hazard draw:** 2 + twice that normal Hazard number.
- **Hazard Limit:** normally the company's size or two, whichever is greater, with the applicable rounding and card effects.
- **Automatic attack:** a site attack faced when the company chooses to enter. It is not a Hazard card and does not consume a Hazard Limit slot.

For the five-character starting Dwarf company, the base Hazard Limit is five. The solo draw does not increase it.

## 2. Use the Hero site cards

The Academy's Hero Location deck gives these values:

| Site | Normal Hazard / Hero draw | Solo Hazard draw | Automatic attack when entered |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Moria | 3 / 2 | 8 | Orcs — 4 strikes with 7 prowess |
| Goblin-gate | 2 / 1 | 6 | Orcs — 3 strikes with 6 prowess |
| Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | 1 / 1 | 4 | None on the Hero site card |

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a Hero Free-hold in Númeriador. Do not import the different Minion site data into this Hero/Wizard project.

## 3. Drill 1 — Rivendell to Moria

Moria is a non-Haven destination, so its own normal Hazard draw number applies: three.

- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 3) = **8**.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- Hero Resource draw: up to **2**.
- Site automatic attack if entered: **four Orc strikes with 7 prowess**.

The eight drawn cards are the solo selection pool. The five-slot Hazard Limit is the cap on ordinary limit-counting Hazard plays. The four automatic Orc strikes are resolved later, in the Site Phase, if the company enters Moria. They are not four additional Hazard plays.

The company should enter only if the remaining characters, taps, wounds and defensive Resources give a reasonable chance of surviving the attack and playing Book of Mazarbul. If the company is too weak, declining entry is legal and may be strategically correct.

## 4. Drill 2 — Declining to enter Moria

If the company does not enter:

- it does not face Moria's automatic attack this Site Phase;
- it cannot attempt to play Book of Mazarbul there this turn;
- it cannot take other Site Phase actions or play cards during that Site Phase;
- it remains at Moria and may choose to enter on a later turn.

The automatic attack is faced again whenever the company later chooses to enter. Defeating it on an earlier visit does not permanently remove it from the site.

The correct choice depends on the actual state. Do not enter automatically just because the objective is available, and do not decline automatically just because the site attack looks severe.

## 5. Drill 3 — Goblin-gate as a waypoint

Goblin-gate is a non-Haven destination with a normal Hazard draw of two:

- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 2) = **6**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- Hero Resource draw: up to **1**.
- Automatic attack if entered: **three Orc strikes with 6 prowess**.

If the company enters, it must face the Orc attack before taking site actions such as attempting Gollum. If it declines to enter, it avoids the attack but cannot attempt Gollum or take other Site Phase actions. It remains at Goblin-gate and may enter on a later turn.

## 6. Drill 4 — Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

The Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold site card is a Free-hold in Númeriador. Its normal draw is one Hazard and one Hero Resource card.

- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 1) = **4**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- Hero Resource draw: up to **1**.
- Site automatic attack: **none on the Hero site card**.

The four-card solo Hazard draw still happens because the company is moving to the non-Haven site. The absence of an automatic attack does not make the movement/Hazard Phase safe or remove the Hazard Limit.

Book of Mazarbul can be stored at a Dwarf-hold for five marshalling points. Storage happens during the Organization Phase and requires the bearer's corruption check. Once the company is at Blue Mountain at the start of a later Organization Phase, the company need not enter the site solely to store the Book. The site is named a Dwarf-hold even though its printed site type is Free-hold.

## 7. Drill 5 — Hazard Limit classification

| Situation | Counts against the Hazard Limit? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Moria's four automatic Orc strikes | No | They are the site's automatic attack, not a Hazard card. |
| Goblin-gate's three automatic Orc strikes | No | They are the site's automatic attack, not a Hazard card. |
| A creature Hazard legally played against the moving company | Yes | It is a Hazard play and normally uses one slot. |
| A Hazard event that legally modifies a site's automatic attack | Yes | It is still a Hazard play; the site attack itself remains separate. |
| Twilight cancelling a legal Environment target | No ordinary slot | Twilight has a specific exception and does not count against the Hazard Limit. It still needs a legal target and correct timing. |

## 8. Drill 6 — Hazard timing before site entry

A Hazard that legally targets or modifies the automatic attack at the company's new site may be played during the Movement/Hazard Phase, before the Hero player decides whether to enter during the Site Phase. The official rules explicitly allow this even if the company later declines to face the automatic attack.

This does not make the automatic attack mandatory. It means the Hazard side may make a legal play against the attack while the company is moving, and the Hero player then makes the separate entry decision. The Hazard card must still meet its printed conditions and normally counts against the Hazard Limit.

## 9. The practical decision sequence

At the table, use this order:

1. Finish the Movement/Hazard Phase.
2. Record the actual wounds, taps, eliminated characters and remaining Resources.
3. Read the destination site's automatic attack, if any.
4. Decide whether to enter.
5. If entering, resolve the automatic attack and any eligible on-guard attacks.
6. Only after successful entry, take Site Phase actions that require entry.
7. Keep Organization Phase actions—such as storing Book of Mazarbul—separate from Site Phase entry.
