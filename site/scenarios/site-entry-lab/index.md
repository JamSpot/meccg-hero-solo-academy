# Site Entry & Automatic Attack Lab — arrival is not entry

This lesson teaches one of the most important table decisions in MECCG: **reaching a site does not force you to enter it**. You decide whether to enter during that company's Site Phase, after the Movement/Hazard Phase has finished.

Use the Hero site cards in the Academy's 15-site Location deck. This is a Hero/Wizard project: do not substitute Minion versions of sites.

## What you will learn

By the end of this lab, you should be able to:

- distinguish a movement/Hazard draw from a site's automatic attack;
- calculate the solo Hazard draw and the company's Hazard Limit separately;
- decide whether to enter Moria or Goblin-gate after seeing the company's actual condition;
- understand what the company gives up when it declines to enter;
- identify which actions require successful site entry;
- store Book of Mazarbul at Blue Mountain during the correct Organization Phase.

## The four rules to keep separate

### 1. Movement/Hazard Phase

If the company moves, resolve movement, declare the site path, set the Hazard Limit, draw cards and play legal Hazards. Under the Academy's solo procedure, the Hazard side draws:

**2 + (2 × the normal Hazard draw number for the relevant site).**

The Hazard Limit is a separate ceiling. A five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of five. The solo draw does not turn that limit into eight.

### 2. Arrival

After the Movement/Hazard Phase, the company has reached its new site. Arrival does not itself trigger the site's automatic attack.

### 3. Site Phase — enter or do nothing

When the company's Site Phase begins, choose one:

- **Enter the site:** face its automatic attack(s), including any eligible on-guard attack, then take permitted site actions if the company successfully enters.
- **Do not enter:** the Site Phase ends immediately. The company does not face the site's automatic attack and cannot take any other action or play cards during that Site Phase. It remains at the site and may enter on a later turn.

A site automatic attack must be faced every time the company chooses to enter, even if it was defeated on a previous visit.

### 4. Site actions and Book storage are not the same thing

A resource such as an item, faction or ally that is played during the Site Phase normally requires a successful site entry, an untapped character and an untapped site, plus the card's own conditions.

Book of Mazarbul has a special instruction: it may be stored at a **Dwarf-hold** for five marshalling points. Storage is an Organization Phase action and requires the bearer's corruption check. It is not a Site Phase resource play. Once the company is at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold at the start of a later Organization Phase, you do not need to enter the site solely to store the Book.

See the [Council of Elrond rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) for the Site Phase and storing rules, and the [full card spoiler list](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/MeCCG%20Full%20Spoiler%20List.pdf) for the Hero site and Book card text.

## Use the Hero site cards — not Minion site data

The Academy's Location deck uses the Hero versions of these sites:

| Hero site card | Site type and region | Normal Hazard draw | Normal Hero draw | Automatic attack when entered |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Moria | Shadow-hold, Redhorn Gate | 3 | 2 | Orcs — 4 strikes with 7 prowess |
| Goblin-gate | Shadow-hold, High Pass | 2 | 1 | Orcs — 3 strikes with 6 prowess |
| Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | Free-hold, Númeriador | 1 | 1 | None on the Hero site card |

**Alignment warning:** the Hero Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold card is a Free-hold in Númeriador, with a normal draw of 1/1 and no automatic attack. A different Minion site version exists with different printed data. It is not part of this Academy. If a reference gives Blue Mountain a Dwarven automatic attack or a 2/2 draw, check that you have not accidentally consulted the Minion site.

## Draw, limit and site attack — five examples

For the standard five-Dwarf company, the base Hazard Limit is five in each example.

| Journey | Which normal site draw applies? | Solo Hazard draw | Hero Resource draw | Site attack if entered |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Rivendell → Moria | Destination Moria: 3 Hazard / 2 Hero | 8 | 2 | 4 Orc strikes at 7 |
| Moria → Goblin-gate | Destination Goblin-gate: 2 / 1 | 6 | 1 | 3 Orc strikes at 6 |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain | Destination Blue Mountain: 1 / 1 | 4 | 1 | None |
| Moria → Rivendell | Destination is a Haven, so use origin Moria: 3 / 2 | 8 | 2 | None at Rivendell |
| Rivendell → Blue Mountain | Destination Blue Mountain: 1 / 1 | 4 | 1 | None |

The site attack is **not** a Hazard card and does not consume a Hazard Limit slot. A Hazard card that modifies or adds to an automatic attack is still a Hazard play and must obey its own wording and the Hazard Limit.

## Drill 1 — Arrive at Moria

Your five Dwarves move from Rivendell to Moria using the verified Region Movement route:

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

You draw eight Hazard cards under the Academy solo procedure. The base Hazard Limit is five.

Before reading the model answer, write down:

1. Why are eight cards drawn?
2. Why is the base Hazard Limit still five?
3. Which automatic attack must be faced if you enter Moria?
4. Is that attack part of the five-card Hazard Limit?
5. What would make you decline to enter after the Hazard phase?

Do not assume that surviving the Hazard phase means the company is healthy enough to face four Orc strikes.

## Drill 2 — Moria: enter now or wait?

The company arrives at Moria with one Dwarf wounded, two characters tapped and the most useful defensive Resource already spent.

Choose a decision and justify it using the actual board state:

- **Enter:** attempt the Book now and face the four Orc strikes at 7 prowess.
- **Do not enter:** avoid the automatic attack, but give up the Book attempt and all other Site Phase actions this turn.

There is no universal answer. The decision depends on the remaining company, the Book's urgency, the available defensive cards and the cost of another turn.

If you do not enter, the company remains at Moria. It may enter on a later turn, but the automatic attack must be faced again when it chooses to enter.

## Drill 3 — Goblin-gate as a waypoint

The company has acquired Book of Mazarbul and moves from Moria to Goblin-gate:

**Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass**

The solo Hazard draw is six and the base Hazard Limit is five.

At the Site Phase, decide whether to:

- enter Goblin-gate and face three Orc strikes at 6 prowess, allowing a Gollum attempt if all card conditions are met; or
- decline to enter, avoid the site attack and take no other Site Phase actions.

Goblin-gate can therefore be a waypoint without being explored. Do not claim the Gollum objective if you did not enter and make the required play.

## Drill 4 — Blue Mountain is different

The company moves from Goblin-gate to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold:

**High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

The Hero Blue Mountain site card gives a normal Hazard draw of one, so the solo Hazard draw is four. The five-character base Hazard Limit remains five.

There is no automatic attack on the Hero Blue Mountain site card.

Answer these questions:

1. Does the company still face the four-card solo Hazard draw on arrival?
2. Does it face a site automatic attack if it enters?
3. Must it enter during the arrival-turn Site Phase just to store Book of Mazarbul next turn?
4. When does the storage check happen?

## Drill 5 — What belongs to the Hazard Limit?

Classify each item:

| Situation | Does it consume a Hazard Limit slot? |
|---|---|
| Moria's four automatic Orc strikes | |
| Goblin-gate's three automatic Orc strikes | |
| A creature Hazard legally played against the moving company | |
| A Hazard event that legally modifies a site's automatic attack | |
| A legal Twilight played as an Environment-cancelling Hazard | |

Use the actual card text and the [Hazard Draw & Limit Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/' | relative_url }}). Remember that Twilight has its own exception and does not count against the ordinary Hazard Limit.

## Drill 6 — Hazard timing before the entry decision

The company is moving to Moria. The Hazard side draws a card that can legally modify Moria's automatic attack.

Can the Hazard side play it during the Movement/Hazard Phase even though the Hero player might later choose not to enter Moria?

Check the card's exact target and timing. The official rules clarify that a Hazard may target an automatic attack at the company's new site during the Movement/Hazard Phase, even if the company ultimately declines to face that attack. Such a Hazard play still counts against the Hazard Limit unless the card or rules say otherwise.
