# Iron Hill — River & Seized by Terror Route Lab — Model Answer

[Return to the exercise]({{ '/scenarios/iron-hill-river-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. Direct Starter Movement

Lórien is the nearest Haven listed on the Hero Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card. The company can therefore use Starter Movement.

The printed site path is Wilderness → Border-land → Border-land → Wilderness → Wilderness. It contains no Shadow-land or Dark-domain. The destination is in Iron Hills, a Wilderness region. **Seized by Terror is illegal on this route.**

Do not count the Free-hold site symbol as a region type. Site type and region type are separate properties.

The normal Hazard draw at Iron Hill is 3, so the solo Hazard draw is:

**2 + (2 × 3) = 8 cards.**

The Hero draw is up to 3. The five-Dwarf company's base Hazard Limit is 5, set at the start of the Movement/Hazard Phase. Iron Hill has no automatic attack.

River is legal because it is playable on a site. If played, it requires one Ranger per copy before the company can take its Site Phase actions.

## 2. The supplementary route via Eagles’ Eyrie

Lórien → Eagles’ Eyrie is legal Starter Movement because Eyrie lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Its normal Hazard draw is 1 and Hero draw is 1:

- Solo Hazard draw: 2 + (2 × 1) = **4**.
- Hero draw: up to **1**.
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- No site automatic attack.

The next leg is Region Movement:

**Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

This is four consecutive adjacent regions, with both endpoint regions included and no repetition. Their types are Border-land, Shadow-land, Wilderness and Wilderness.

Grey Mountain Narrows is the decisive difference: **Seized by Terror is legal** on this final leg because the company is moving through a Shadow-land. Cave Worm also has its named Iron Hills key; Giant Spiders can use the double-Wilderness key.

The Iron Hill destination still has normal Hazard draw 3, so the final leg draws **8** solo Hazard cards and up to **3** Hero cards. The five-Dwarf Hazard Limit is 5.

Across the two separate movement/hazard phases, the route draws **4 + 8 = 12** solo Hazard cards and up to **1 + 3 = 4** Hero cards. These are separate phases on separate turns, not one combined twelve-card draw. The first phase's Hazard Limit does not carry over to the second; it is set again at the start of the later phase.

Eagles’ Eyrie is not in the locked first-play 15-site Location deck and offers no playable Resource category for the active deck. The route is a supplementary rules exercise, not a recommended detour for the Iron Hill faction campaign.

## 3. Seized by Terror calculation

The target rolls 2D6 and adds mind. A result below 12 splits the target into a new company and immediately returns that character to the original company's site of origin.

For Óin (mind 3):

- 2D6 must be 2 through 8.
- There are 26 such outcomes out of 36.
- Split probability: **26/36 = 72.2%**.

For Dori (mind 1):

- 2D6 must be 2 through 10.
- There are 33 such outcomes out of 36.
- Split probability: **33/36 = 91.7%**.

For Gildor (mind 4):

- 2D6 must be 2 through 7.
- There are 21 such outcomes out of 36.
- Split probability: **21/36 = 58.3%**.

A successful split is not a wound and does not tap the character. The targeted character is separated and returned to the origin; the rest of the company continues its movement.

## 4. River timing and Site Phase denial

The current River erratum says it is playable on a site. If a company has moved to that site this turn and does not tap a Ranger, it must do nothing during its Site Phase.

The CRF ruling clarifies:

- The Ranger may be tapped at the beginning of the Site Phase without entering.
- One Ranger must be tapped for **each River** played on the site.

Therefore:

- One River requires one Ranger.
- Two Rivers require two Rangers.
- If the company cannot meet the number of Rangers required, it must do nothing during its Site Phase. It cannot enter Iron Hill or attempt Iron Hill Dwarves.
- Because Iron Hill has no automatic attack, the River interaction is about denying the faction and other site actions, not avoiding an automatic attack.

## 5. Correct Hazard target

### Five Dwarves, one River, one Ranger

The company has only Óin as a Ranger. If the Hazard player has one River and Seized by Terror, target Óin.

If Óin splits (72.2%), no Ranger remains. River then prevents the company's Site Phase. If the Seized roll fails, Óin remains and can tap at the beginning of the Site Phase to satisfy River.

### Five Dwarves, two Rivers, one Ranger

The two Rivers already require two Rangers, but the starting company has only Óin. The Site Phase is denied even if Seized by Terror fails. The Hazard player should normally target Dori (91.7%) to maximize the chance of separating another character, rather than spending the Seized attempt on Óin when River already makes entry impossible.

### Óin and Gildor both untapped, one River

There are two available Rangers for one River. If Seized by Terror splits one Ranger, the other can still tap to satisfy River. Splitting a Ranger does not deny the Site Phase. The Hazard player should usually target Dori (91.7%) or another tactically valuable character instead.

### Óin and Gildor both untapped, two Rivers

There are exactly two Rangers for two Rivers. If Seized by Terror splits Óin, only Gildor remains, which is one Ranger short. Target Óin: his mind 3 gives a 72.2% split chance, better than Gildor's 58.3%.

### Only one Ranger remains untapped

Count only Rangers who can actually tap. If one Ranger has already been tapped, split the remaining available Ranger when a River still needs to be satisfied. A Ranger already tapped cannot be tapped again to meet River.

## 6. Hero-side counterplay and the active deck

Gildor is already in the 36-card Hero play deck. He supplies a second Ranger, but does not make the company immune to the combination:

- Against one River, two untapped Rangers provide redundancy; Seized can remove one and the other can satisfy River.
- Against two Rivers, two Rangers are only just enough. Seized can split one and leave the company one Ranger short.

The current Hero Resource list has no direct cancellation for these two short-events:

- Marvels Told only discards a Hazard non-Environment permanent-event or long-event; River and Seized by Terror are short-events.
- Concealment cancels an attack, not River or Seized by Terror.
- Not at Home cancels specified Dragon/Drake/Troll automatic attacks or attacks keyed to a site; it does not cancel either effect.

The best baseline defence is to choose the direct Starter Movement route when it serves the objective, because that route does not contain a Shadow-land or Dark-domain. On a route where Seized is legal, preserve the required Rangers until the Site Phase and reassess the actual number of Rivers.

## 7. Why the detour is not the default

The direct Starter Movement route reaches the same Free-hold in one Movement/Hazard Phase, draws eight solo Hazard cards, and makes Seized by Terror illegal. The supplementary Eyrie route takes two phases, draws twelve solo Hazard cards, and exposes the company to Seized by Terror on the final leg. Eyrie has no playable Resource category for this deck.

Therefore, for the actual Iron Hill Dwarves objective, **direct Starter Movement from Lórien is the stronger default route**. The staged route is useful because it teaches the difference between Starter and Region Movement and provides a controlled Seized/River rules exercise. It is not a route improvement.

## 8. Deck decision

Do not change the Hero or Hazard list because of this exercise. The 36-card Hero play deck already contains Gildor, and the 30-card Hazard deck already contains River ×2 and Seized by Terror ×2. First record how often these cards actually appear and whether River meaningfully denies the faction attempt during the baseline or a deliberately configured supplementary test.
