# 6J. Iron Hill — River & Seized by Terror Route Lab

This advanced exercise compares two legal ways to reach the **same Iron Hill Dwarf-hold**. It shows why a route is not merely a way to reach a site: the declared site path changes which Hazards can be played.

The lesson uses only cards already in the fixed Hero and Hazard lists. It does **not** change Game 1, the 36-card Hero play deck, the 30-card Hazard deck, or the 15-site first-play Location deck.

## The destination

The Hero Iron Hill Dwarf-hold is a **Free-hold** in the **Iron Hills** region.

| Site detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Site type | Free-hold |
| Region | Iron Hills — Wilderness |
| Nearest Haven | Lórien |
| Normal Hazard / Hero draw | 3 / 3 |
| Automatic attack | None |
| Main campaign objective | Iron Hill Dwarves faction — 4 MPs |

Do not confuse the site's name with its site type. “Dwarf-hold” is part of the name; the actual site symbol is Free-hold. It is not a Shadow-hold, Dark-hold or Ruins & Lairs.

## Route A — direct Starter Movement from Lórien

Lórien is the nearest Haven printed on the Hero Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card, so Starter Movement is legal.

The printed site path is:

**Wilderness → Border-land → Border-land → Wilderness → Wilderness**

That is five region-type symbols. It is still legal Starter Movement: the four-region maximum applies to normal Region Movement, not Starter Movement. A direct Region Movement from Lórien to Iron Hills would require five regions including both endpoints, so it is not legal.

| Measure | Direct route |
|---|---:|
| Movement/Hazard Phases | 1 |
| Hero draw | Up to 3 |
| Normal Hazard draw | 3 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **8** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack | None |
| Seized by Terror | **Illegal** |

Why Seized by Terror is illegal: the Starter Movement site path contains no Shadow-land or Dark-domain. The destination Iron Hills is Wilderness. A site type never substitutes for a region type.

**River is still legal.** River is playable on a site, and Iron Hill is the new site this turn. The company must tap one Ranger for each River on that site or do nothing during its Site Phase.

## Route B — stage at Eagles’ Eyrie, then use Region Movement

This is a supplementary map exercise. Hero Eagles’ Eyrie is **not** in the locked 15-site first-play Location deck. Use the genuine Hero site card only if you deliberately build a separate test Location deck; do not alter Game 1.

### Leg 1: Lórien → Eagles’ Eyrie

Eagles’ Eyrie is a Hero Free-hold in Anduin Vales, with Lórien as its nearest Haven. Starter Movement is legal.

| Measure | Lórien → Eagles’ Eyrie |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard / Hero draw | 1 / 1 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **4** |
| Hero draw | Up to 1 |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | 5 |
| Site automatic attack | None |

The site has no playable Resource category in this card pool, so it is a waypoint for the movement exercise, not a rewarding stop. You do not need to enter it merely because you arrive there.

### Leg 2: Eagles’ Eyrie → Iron Hill Dwarf-hold

Use Region Movement through this exact four-region sequence:

**Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

| Region | Type | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Anduin Vales | Border-land | Starting region |
| Grey Mountain Narrows | Shadow-land | Makes Seized by Terror legal |
| Northern Rhovanion | Wilderness | Meets one Wilderness key |
| Iron Hills | Wilderness | Destination region; named key for Cave Worm |

All four regions are adjacent in sequence, neither endpoint is omitted, and no region repeats. This is legal Region Movement.

| Measure | Eagles’ Eyrie → Iron Hill |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard / Hero draw | 3 / 3 |
| Solo Hazard draw | **8** |
| Hero draw | Up to 3 |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack | None |
| Seized by Terror | **Legal** |

The route passes through Grey Mountain Narrows, a Shadow-land. Seized by Terror can therefore target an eligible non-Wizard character in the moving company. River can also be played on the new Iron Hill site.

### Compare the actual cost

| Measure | Direct Starter | Via Eagles’ Eyrie |
|---|---:|---:|
| Movement/Hazard Phases | 1 | 2 |
| Solo Hazard cards drawn | **8** | **12** |
| Hero cards drawn, maximum | 3 | 4 (1 + 3) |
| Base Hazard Limit per phase | 5 | 5 |
| Seized by Terror on final leg | No | Yes |
| River at Iron Hill | Yes | Yes |
| Iron Hill automatic attack | None | None |

The detour adds a separate movement/hazard phase and four more solo Hazard draws. It does not improve the Iron Hill faction objective, and Eagles’ Eyrie has no site-playable Resource for this deck. Do not take this detour in a real campaign merely to visit another site.

## The River–Seized by Terror combination

The active Hazard deck contains **two River** and **two Seized by Terror**.

- **River** is a short-event playable on a site. If a company moved to that site this turn, it must tap a Ranger for each River played there or do nothing during its Site Phase.
- **Seized by Terror** is a short-event playable on a non-Wizard character moving in a Shadow-land or Dark-domain. The target rolls 2D6 and adds his mind. If the result is less than 12, he splits into a separate company and immediately returns to the original company's site of origin.

The River erratum/ruling allows the Ranger to be tapped at the beginning of the Site Phase, without first entering the site. It requires **one Ranger for each River**. If the requirement is not met, the company must do nothing during its Site Phase: it cannot enter Iron Hill or attempt the Iron Hill Dwarves faction.

The two cards attack different parts of the turn:

- Seized by Terror removes a character from the moving company and sends that character back to the origin.
- River checks whether enough Rangers remain to permit any Site Phase actions at the destination.

Both are ordinary Hazard plays and count against the Hazard Limit. Against the five starting Dwarves, the limit is five. The solo side draws eight cards on the Iron Hill leg but may play no more than five ordinary Hazards, assuming the Hazard player did not access the sideboard during the turn's Untap Phase. If the sideboard was accessed, the base limit is halved, rounded up. The limit is fixed at the start of the Movement/Hazard Phase, even if Seized by Terror later splits a character away.

## Exact Seized by Terror probabilities

The result is **2D6 + target mind**. A total below 12 splits the character.

| Character | Mind | 2D6 result that splits | Probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dori | 1 | 2–10 | **33/36 = 91.7%** |
| Óin | 3 | 2–8 | **26/36 = 72.2%** |
| Gildor | 4 | 2–7 | **21/36 = 58.3%** |
| Glóin | 5 | 2–6 | **15/36 = 41.7%** |
| Thorin II | 8 | 2–3 | **3/36 = 8.3%** |
| Gandalf | — | Not eligible | Wizard |

A failed split does not wound or tap the target. It simply means the split effect does not occur.

### How often do River and Seized appear together?

In an **isolated fresh eight-card draw** from the 30-card Hazard deck, the chance of at least one River and at least one Seized by Terror is **802/3915 = 20.5%** (about one in five). The chance of seeing both River copies is **28/435 = 6.44%**. These are deck-composition probabilities, not win probabilities. They are not the exact probability for the second leg of a continuous two-leg expedition, because the first leg changes which cards remain in the draw pile and discard pile.

## Hazard-side decision rule: count Rangers before choosing a target

The starting five Dwarves have only one Ranger: **Óin**. Gandalf is not a Ranger. Gildor Inglorion, already in the active Hero play deck, is a second Ranger if he has been legally recruited and is in the company.

| Company and Hazard hand | Strong default choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Five Dwarves; one River + Seized by Terror | Target Óin | If Óin splits (72.2%), no Ranger remains for River; Site Phase is denied |
| Five Dwarves; two Rivers + Seized by Terror | Play both Rivers; usually target Dori | One Ranger cannot satisfy two Rivers even if Seized fails; Dori is the more reliable split (91.7%) |
| Óin + Gildor both untapped; one River + Seized | Usually target Dori | If one Ranger splits, the other can still satisfy the single River |
| Óin + Gildor both untapped; two Rivers + Seized | Target Óin | If Óin splits (72.2%), only one Ranger remains for two Rivers |
| Only one Ranger is untapped; one River + Seized | Target that Ranger | Removing the only available Ranger denies the Site Phase |
| All available Rangers already tapped; River present | River already denies the Site Phase | Use Seized on Dori or another valuable target instead |

This is a tactical default, not an automatic script. Recheck the actual number of Rivers, untapped Rangers, other targets, and the remaining Hazard Limit before committing the line.

## What the Hero deck can and cannot do

Gildor is the useful built-in redundancy: he is already in the 36-card Hero play deck and supplies a second Ranger. With Óin and Gildor both untapped, one River cannot be used with Seized by Terror to deny the Site Phase by splitting just one of them. Two Rivers are different: if one Ranger is split, one Ranger cannot satisfy both copies.

The current Hero Resource list has **no direct cancellation for either short-event**:

- Marvels Told discards a Hazard non-Environment permanent-event or long-event; it does not discard River or Seized by Terror, which are short-events.
- Concealment cancels an attack, not these effects.
- Not at Home cancels specified Dragon, Drake or Troll automatic attacks, or an attack keyed to a site; it does not cancel River or Seized by Terror.

Therefore, the main Hero-side answers are route choice, keeping Rangers available, and not assuming a site action will be possible. The direct Starter Movement route avoids Seized by Terror altogether.

## Practice — make the decision before opening the answer key

1. You have the five starting Dwarves at Lórien and the direct Iron Hill Starter Movement site path. Is Seized by Terror legal? Explain using the region types, not the site's name.
2. You take the supplementary route through Eagles’ Eyrie. Which named region makes Seized by Terror legal on the final leg?
3. The Hazard hand contains one River and Seized by Terror. Óin is your only Ranger. Which character should the Hazard side target, and what is the split probability?
4. The company contains Óin and Gildor, both untapped, and the Hazard hand has one River and Seized by Terror. Can splitting one Ranger deny the Site Phase?
5. The same two Rangers are present, but the Hazard hand contains two Rivers and Seized by Terror. Which Ranger is the stronger target, and why?
6. If River remains unsatisfied, can the Hero player enter Iron Hill and attempt the faction anyway?
7. How many Hazard cards are drawn on each route, and what is the five-Dwarf Hazard Limit?
8. Does Marvels Told cancel River or Seized by Terror? Why not?
9. Is the Eagles’ Eyrie detour a good default campaign route? Give the actual cost and reward.

**Do not change either active deck after this exercise.** Record whether the route choice and River interaction materially affected a real playtest before considering a card substitution.
