# Site Path Tactics Lab — Starter vs Region Movement

**Purpose:** learn why the same destination can expose different Hazard cards depending on the movement method. This is an advanced exercise after the [Free Movement guide]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}).

The rules used here are the current Council of Elrond movement and creature-keying rules, combined with the Academy's clearly labelled solo Hazard draw procedure. It is not an Arda exercise.

## The rule distinction

A **movement route** is how the company legally gets from its origin region to its destination region. A **company site path** is the information used to determine which creatures can be keyed to that moving company.

### Starter Movement

- Check the nearest-Haven conditions on the actual Hero site cards.
- Use the printed site-path region types.
- The company site path identifies the origin and destination region names, but not the names of intervening regions.
- The normal four-region maximum does **not** apply to Starter Movement.

### Region Movement

- Include the origin and destination regions.
- Every adjacent pair must be legal on the Region cards.
- No region may repeat.
- Use no more than four consecutive regions unless a specific effect changes that limit.
- The company site path includes the types and names of every region in the declared sequence.

**Timing:** during the Organization Phase, choose the destination and declare the movement. The proposed Region Movement must be capable of being legal. During the Movement/Hazard Phase, reveal the new site and declare the actual Region Movement site path. It may differ from the site's printed nearest-Haven path, but it must have been legal when movement was initially declared.

The site path does not itself move the company. It determines the route information used for Hazard creature keying.

## Case A — Lórien to Iron Hill Dwarf-hold by Starter Movement

Use the **Hero** Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card:

- Site type: Free-hold
- Region: Iron Hills
- Nearest Haven: Lórien
- Site-card numbers: 3 Hazard / 3 Hero
- Automatic attack: none

Lórien is a Haven and is listed as Iron Hill Dwarf-hold's nearest Haven, so Starter Movement is legal. The printed path has five region-type symbols:

**Wilderness → Border-land → Border-land → Wilderness → Wilderness**

That five-symbol path is legal. Do not apply the Region Movement four-region maximum to Starter Movement.

The Starter Movement company site path contains those five region types and the names of the origin and destination regions: **Wold & Foothills** and **Iron Hills**. It does not name the three intervening regions.

| Measure | Direct Starter Movement |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw | 3 |
| Academy solo Hazard draw | **8** |
| Hero draw | Up to **3** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack | None |

Route-keying consequences:

- **Cave Worm:** legal by the named Iron Hills region.
- **Giant Spiders:** legal by double Wilderness; the path contains three Wilderness regions.
- **Seized by Terror:** not legal; the path has no Shadow-land or Dark-domain.
- **Arouse Minions:** not legal; Iron Hill is a Free-hold, not a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold.
- **Brigands:** would be legal by Border-land or Wilderness, but it is only a proposed future variant and is not in the baseline Hazard deck.

The solo draw is 2 + (2 × 3) = **8**. The five-card Hazard Limit is a separate restriction.

## Case B — direct Region Movement from Lórien to Iron Hill

The shortest legal adjacency sequence is:

**Wold & Foothills → Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

This contains **five** regions, including both endpoints. It is therefore too long for normal Region Movement. Do not skip a region unless the Region cards actually show a legal adjacency.

The fact that Case A is legal does not make this Region Movement legal: Case A uses Starter Movement and the printed site path.

## Case C — stage at Eagles' Eyrie, then use Region Movement

This is a legal two-movement demonstration of the route and site-path difference. It is **not** the recommended route for the current Iron Hill objective.

### Leg 1: Lórien → Eagles' Eyrie

Use Starter Movement. The Hero Eagles' Eyrie site card is a Free-hold in Anduin Vales, lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, has 1/1 site-card numbers and no automatic attack.

| Measure | Lórien → Eagles' Eyrie |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw | 1 |
| Academy solo Hazard draw | **4** |
| Hero draw | Up to **1** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack | None |

**Important site check:** the Hero Eagles' Eyrie card lists no playable Resource category. The current Academy Resource deck therefore has no item, faction or ally to play there. It is a waypoint in this exercise, not a Resource stop. Do not enter merely because the site is a Free-hold.

### Leg 2: Eagles' Eyrie → Iron Hill Dwarf-hold

Use Region Movement:

**Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

This is four consecutive regions, includes both endpoints, repeats none, and each adjacency is shown on the Region cards.

| Region | Region type |
|---|---|
| Anduin Vales | Border-land |
| Grey Mountain Narrows | Shadow-land |
| Northern Rhovanion | Wilderness |
| Iron Hills | Wilderness |

| Measure | Eagles' Eyrie → Iron Hill |
|---|---:|
| Normal Hazard draw | 3 |
| Academy solo Hazard draw | **8** |
| Hero draw | Up to **3** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Site automatic attack | None |

The declared site path includes **Grey Mountain Narrows**, a Shadow-land. **Seized by Terror is now a legal candidate** against an eligible non-Wizard character, subject to its exact target and resolution conditions.

Cave Worm is also legal by the named Iron Hills or Grey Mountain Narrows region. Giant Spiders is legal by double Wilderness. Arouse Minions remains illegal because the destination is a Free-hold.

Across the two legs, the solo Hazard side draws **4 + 8 = 12 cards**. These are two separate Movement/Hazard Phases, each with its own Hazard Limit of five. Do not add the two limits together and call it one ten-card limit. The Hero side may draw up to **1 + 3 = 4 cards** across the two legs.

The route is deliberately less efficient for the current objective: it draws four more solo Hazards than direct Starter Movement, takes an extra movement phase, and the waypoint has no playable Resource category in this deck. Its value here is teaching how a Region Movement site path can expose a region name and type that Starter Movement does not.

## Hazard-side exercise — the same hand, two different paths

Assume the Hazard side draws these eight cards for the final movement into Iron Hill:

- 2 Seized by Terror
- 2 Cave Worm
- 2 Giant Spiders
- 1 Full of Froth and Rage
- 1 Wake of War

For each route, identify which cards are legal before choosing plays.

**On Case A (direct Starter Movement):**

- Both Seized by Terror copies are illegal because there is no Shadow-land or Dark-domain in the site path.
- Cave Worm and Giant Spiders have matching keys.
- Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War can support the Spider attacks if their conditions and timing are met.
- The base Hazard Limit is five. Choose the most disadvantageous legal combination; do not play an illegal card just to fill a slot.

**On Case C, Leg 2 (Region Movement through Grey Mountain Narrows):**

- Seized by Terror becomes legal against an eligible non-Wizard character because the site path contains a Shadow-land.
- Cave Worm remains legal by named region.
- Giant Spiders remains legal by double Wilderness.
- The support events still need their proper Spider-attack targets and timing.
- The base Hazard Limit is still five. Eight cards drawn does not mean eight ordinary Hazard plays.

Full of Froth and Rage and Wake of War both count against the Hazard Limit. Full of Froth and Rage must be checked again after each Spider or Animal attack because it is discarded if such an attack is defeated. Do not assume its bonus survives for a later attack.
