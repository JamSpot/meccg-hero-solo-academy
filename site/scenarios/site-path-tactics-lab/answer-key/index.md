# Site Path Tactics Lab — Model Answer

[Back to the Site Path Tactics Lab]({{ '/scenarios/site-path-tactics-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. Five symbols can be legal Starter Movement

The four-region maximum applies to normal Region Movement. Lórien is a Haven and is listed as the nearest Haven on the Hero Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card, so Starter Movement is permitted. The printed five-symbol site path is used as printed.

## 2. The direct Region Movement is illegal

The shortest adjacency sequence from Wold & Foothills to Iron Hills is:

**Wold & Foothills → Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills**

It contains five regions, including origin and destination. Normal Region Movement allows no more than four consecutive regions. The five-symbol Starter path does not override this separate limit.

## 3. The site paths expose different Hazard keys

### Direct Starter Movement

The site path has Wilderness, Border-land, Border-land, Wilderness and Wilderness. It identifies Wold & Foothills and Iron Hills by name, but not the intervening region names.

- Cave Worm is legal by the named Iron Hills region.
- Giant Spiders is legal by double Wilderness.
- Seized by Terror is illegal because no Shadow-land or Dark-domain appears.
- Arouse Minions is illegal because the destination site is a Free-hold.

### Region Movement via Eagles' Eyrie

The final leg's site path is Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills. It includes the names and types of all four regions.

- Seized by Terror is legal against an eligible non-Wizard character because Grey Mountain Narrows is a Shadow-land.
- Cave Worm is legal by Iron Hills or Grey Mountain Narrows.
- Giant Spiders is legal by the two Wilderness regions.
- Arouse Minions is still illegal because it keys to a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold site, while Iron Hill is a Free-hold.

This is the key lesson: a region type and a site type are different things. A Shadow-land in the path does not turn a Free-hold destination into a Shadow-hold.

## 4. The draw and Hazard Limit calculations

| Movement | Normal Hazard draw | Solo Hazard draw | Hero draw | Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Lórien → Iron Hill (Starter) | 3 | **8** | Up to 3 | 5 |
| Lórien → Eagles' Eyrie (Starter) | 1 | **4** | Up to 1 | 5 |
| Eagles' Eyrie → Iron Hill (Region) | 3 | **8** | Up to 3 | 5 |

The Academy solo draw is 2 + (2 × normal Hazard draw). The two-leg route therefore draws 12 Hazard cards across two separate phases. Each phase has its own base Hazard Limit of five. The first leg draws only four cards, so at most four of those drawn cards can be played in that phase.

The Hazard Limit is not the number of cards drawn. The solo Hazard side must choose the most disadvantageous legal combination within the limit.

## 5. Eagles' Eyrie is only a waypoint for this deck

The Hero Eagles' Eyrie site card has no playable Resource category. The current Hero Resource list has no card that can be played there as an item, ally, faction or information resource. It has no automatic attack, but that does not create a site reward.

So the staged route is useful as a teaching demonstration of a legal four-region Region Movement and the extra Seized by Terror key. It is not a better Iron Hill expedition for the current deck: it adds a movement phase and four more solo Hazard draws without providing a Resource action at the waypoint.

## 6. The eight-card Hazard hand

On the direct Starter path, both Seized by Terror cards are illegal. The two Cave Worms, two Giant Spiders and the two support events have matching route or attack conditions, subject to their exact timing and target restrictions. The Hazard player has five ordinary slots and should select the most disadvantageous legal combination.

On the Region Movement final leg through Grey Mountain Narrows, Seized by Terror becomes legal against eligible non-Wizard characters. All eight cards are potential candidates, but the Hazard Limit remains five. The Hazard player must still respect card duplication restrictions, timing, targets, and the fact that Full of Froth and Rage is discarded if a Spider or Animal attack is defeated.

Do not automatically play the first five cards drawn. First establish legality, then choose the strongest legal sequence.

## 7. Practical route recommendation

For the current Iron Hill Dwarves objective, the direct Starter Movement from Lórien is the more efficient route:

- one movement phase instead of two;
- eight solo Hazard draws instead of twelve;
- no Shadow-land or Dark-domain key for Seized by Terror;
- Cave Worm remains legal by the Iron Hills destination name;
- no automatic attack at Iron Hill.

Use the Eagles' Eyrie route only if a future deck or scenario gives the waypoint a real purpose, or when deliberately practising the Region Movement/site-path distinction. It is not necessary to take this detour merely to avoid returning to a Haven.
