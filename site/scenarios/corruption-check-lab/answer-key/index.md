# Corruption Check Lab — Model Answer

## 1. Gandalf is the safest bearer when he is present

Book of Mazarbul gives 2 corruption points. Gandalf has **+1 to his corruption checks**, and that modifier is added to the 2D6 roll.

- Lowest natural 2D6 roll: 2.
- Gandalf's modifier: +1.
- Lowest modified roll: **3**.
- Book's CP total: **2**.

Because 3 is greater than 2, a Book-only check by Gandalf is guaranteed to succeed. He does not need a helper under this assumption. If Gandalf controls any other card with corruption points, add those points and recalculate.

Gandalf is also a Sage, so he can tap Book of Mazarbul during the Organization Phase to increase the Hero hand size by one until the next Untap Phase. This hand-size increase comes from the Book's text, not from Gandalf's Wizard card.

## 2. If Gandalf is absent, Thorin II or Glóin are safer than the -1 Dwarves

Thorin II and Glóin have no printed corruption-check modifier. With only the Book's 2 CP:

- no helper: only a natural 2 fails (1/36);
- one helper: the +1 support makes the lowest modified roll 3, so the check is guaranteed.

Dori, Kíli and Óin each have **-1**. A negative modifier lowers the roll and is a penalty. Do not treat Kíli's Scout skill or Óin's Ranger skill as a corruption-check bonus.

## 3. Kíli's risk calculation

Kíli has the Book's 2 CP and a -1 modifier.

| Other characters tapped to support | Final modifier | Lowest modified roll | Failure risk |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | -1 | 1 | Natural 2 or 3 fails: 3/36. |
| 1 | 0 | 2 | Natural 2 fails: 1/36. |
| 2 | +1 | 3 | Guaranteed success against 2 CP. |

The earlier numerical calculation for Kíli was correct; the earlier recommendation that his -1 made him a safer bearer was not. A -1 is a penalty.

## 4. Despair of the Heart makes Kíli a dangerous target

Despair of the Heart gives its eligible target another 2 corruption points, taking Kíli from 2 CP to **4 CP**. It also causes a corruption check whenever a character in his company becomes wounded.

| Helpers tapped | Net modifier on Kíli | Lowest modified roll | Failure risk against 4 CP |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | -1 | 1 | Natural 2–5 fail: 10/36. |
| 1 | 0 | 2 | Natural 2–4 fail: 6/36. |
| 2 | +1 | 3 | Natural 2–3 fail: 3/36. |
| 3 | +2 | 4 | Natural 2 fails: 1/36. |
| 4 | +3 | 5 | Guaranteed success. |

The target may tap during his Organization Phase and attempt to remove Despair with a roll greater than four. If it remains, use the full 4 CP total for later checks.

**Despair of the Heart cannot be played on Gandalf**, because he is a Wizard. If Gandalf carries the Book, the Hazard player must choose another legal non-Wizard, non-Hobbit, non-Ringwraith target. The repeated checks apply to that target, not to Gandalf.

## 5. The cost of tapping helpers

Each other character tapped to support a corruption check adds +1 to the roll. Those helpers remain tapped if the company moves later in the same Organization Phase. A tapped character faces strikes with the normal -1 prowess consequence.

Therefore:
- Gandalf with only the Book needs no helper.
- A zero-modifier Dwarf needs one helper for a guaranteed Book-only check.
- Kíli needs two helpers for a guaranteed Book-only check.
- If Despair remains on Kíli, even more support is needed.

Do not tap characters automatically. Compare the residual corruption risk with the combat risk created by moving with tapped characters.

## 6. The correct table decision

- **Gandalf present:** he is the strongest default bearer, provided his total CP is only the Book's 2 or otherwise remains manageable.
- **Gandalf absent:** prefer Thorin II or Glóin for corruption safety; both have no printed modifier.
- **Kíli, Dori or Óin:** their -1 modifier is a penalty. Use one only if another tactical reason outweighs the corruption risk.
- **Despair of the Heart:** legal against the non-Wizard Dwarves, not Gandalf.

This is how the Hero player and Hazard player keep the game fair: the Hero player calculates the actual check, while the Hazard player chooses the strongest legal target rather than pretending every character is equally vulnerable.
