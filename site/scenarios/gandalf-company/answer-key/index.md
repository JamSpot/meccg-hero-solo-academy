# Gandalf — Model Answer

## 1. Plan A: Gandalf joins the Dwarves

The company contains five Dwarves plus Gandalf: **six characters**.

Moria is a non-Haven destination with a normal Hazard draw number of 3. The Academy's solitaire procedure is:

**2 + (2 × normal Hazard draw) = 2 + (2 × 3) = 8 cards.**

The base Hazard Limit is the company size or two, whichever is greater. Six characters therefore give a base Hazard Limit of **6**.

The company has one Moria Movement/Hazard Phase: eight cards drawn, up to six ordinary Hazard plays, subject to legality and card text.

The main cost is that Gandalf is exposed to the Moria journey and its automatic attack. His prowess is 6 and body is 9, but his corruption checks are modified by **+1**. A Wizard's elimination ends the game.

## 2. Plan B: separate companies

### Thorin's Dwarves

- Route: Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.
- Destination: Moria.
- Normal Hazard draw number: 3.
- Solo Hazard draw: **8**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.

### Gandalf

Gandalf is played at Grey Havens, a Haven covered by his printed home-site instruction (Any Haven). He moves to Lórien using Starter Movement because both sites are Havens and each site card lists a path to the other.

Lórien is a Haven destination. Under normal rules, the Hazard draw is based on the **site of origin**, not the new Haven. The origin is Grey Havens, whose normal Hazard draw number is 2.

- Route: Grey Havens → Lórien, using the printed Haven-to-Haven site path.
- Normal Hazard draw number used: **Grey Havens 2**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6**.
- Company size: 1 Wizard.
- Base Hazard Limit: **2**, because the normal minimum is two.

### Combined total — but not a combined phase

Plan B draws **8 + 6 = 14 Hazard cards** across two separate Movement/Hazard Phases.

| Company | Cards drawn | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Five Dwarves to Moria | 8 | 5 |
| Gandalf to Lórien | 6 | 2 |
| **Total** | **14** | **Two separate limits: 5 and 2** |

The Hazard deck is shared, but each company is assessed separately. Do not pool the 14 cards or choose seven for whichever company you prefer. Resolve each company phase in turn, using its own route, target, timing and limit. The solo procedure directs you to play as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within that company's own limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination.

## 3. Gandalf's corruption modifier and hand size

Gandalf's card gives **+1 to all of his corruption checks**. It does not increase your hand size. Your Academy Hero hand target remains **five cards**.

Pallando's card has a different ability: it lets his controller keep one more card than normal. That Pallando ability is not part of the active Gandalf-led build.

Kíli has a -1 modifier to his corruption checks, so he remains the preferred default bearer for Book of Mazarbul. If Gandalf bears the Book, his +1 modifier makes his check harder.

## 4. Which plan is better?

There is no automatic winner.

### Plan A — join the Dwarves

**Advantages**
- One company and one Movement/Hazard Phase.
- Gandalf contributes his four skills and combat prowess.
- He travels with the Dwarves' protection.

**Costs**
- Hazard Limit rises from five to six.
- Gandalf is exposed to the Moria journey and automatic attack.
- His +1 corruption-check modifier remains a liability.

### Plan B — separate companies

**Advantages**
- Gandalf avoids Moria's automatic attack.
- His company has its own lower Hazard Limit of two.
- He reaches Lórien and can develop a support company.

**Costs**
- The Hazard deck must supply 14 cards across the two phases.
- Gandalf faces six drawn Hazards alone, with only the normal minimum limit of two.
- A one-character Wizard company has no companion to share the danger.

For a cautious first campaign, it is reasonable to keep Game 1 focused on the Dwarves and introduce Gandalf's separate company after you are comfortable resolving one company at a time. Once you use Plan B, do not soften the Hazard choices: apply the two separate limits honestly.

## 5. What happens next?

The historical Challenge Deck C Player Guide's Pallando strategy is a reference, not the active Wizard instruction for this Academy. The Gandalf-led Academy adapts the support-company idea to Gandalf's actual card text.
