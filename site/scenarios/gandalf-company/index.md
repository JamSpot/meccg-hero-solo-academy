# Gandalf — One Company or Two?

**Purpose:** practise the Gandalf-led Academy's first important Wizard decision: keep Gandalf with the Dwarves, or build a separate company at a Haven?

This is an Academy adaptation of the historical Dwarven Quest resource and Hazard teaching lists. The Wizard is Gandalf, not Pallando.

## Starting situation

- Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin are at Rivendell.
- Thorin controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin.
- The two starting Cram items are assigned as listed.
- Gandalf is in your hand; no Wizard is currently in play.
- Your Hero hand target is five.
- The 30-card Hazard deck is shuffled and separate.
- The Location deck contains Grey Havens, Lórien and Moria.

Your main mission remains **Book of Mazarbul at Moria**.

## Decision 1 — Where can Gandalf be played?

Gandalf's printed home site is **Any Haven**. A Wizard may be brought into play at his home site or at Rivendell, provided you do not already have a Wizard in play. Gandalf does not need influence to control himself.

You may not play another character and Gandalf in the same Organization Phase: playing a character or Wizard is one character-play activity for that phase.

## Decision 2 — Choose a company plan

### Plan A — Gandalf joins the Dwarves

Play Gandalf at Rivendell and place him in Thorin's company. Move together to Moria using:

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

Calculate the new company size, solo Hazard draw and Hazard Limit.

### Plan B — Gandalf forms a separate company

Play Gandalf at **Grey Havens** as a one-character company, then move to Lórien using the Haven-to-Haven Starter Movement path. At the same time, move the five Dwarves from Rivendell to Moria using their Region Movement route.

Calculate each company's solo Hazard draw and Hazard Limit separately.

## Your tasks

Before opening the answer key, write down:

1. How many characters are in Plan A's company?
2. How many Hazard cards does Plan A draw for Moria?
3. What is Plan A's base Hazard Limit?
4. Under Plan B, which site's normal Hazard draw number is used for Gandalf's move to Lórien, and why?
5. How many Hazard cards does Gandalf's company draw?
6. What is the Hazard Limit for a one-character Wizard company?
7. What is the total number of Hazard cards drawn across both companies in Plan B?
8. Why is that not one combined Hazard Limit?
9. Does Gandalf increase your hand size? What does his card actually modify?
10. Why is Gandalf a better default Book bearer than Kíli?

**Do not choose the plan that merely gives the Heroes fewer Hazards.** Choose the plan you can justify from the card text, movement rules, solo procedure and actual company state.
