# Ranger Capacity & River Denial Lab — Model Answer

[Return to the exercise]({{ '/scenarios/ranger-capacity-lab/' | relative_url }})

## 1. One River against the five starting Dwarves

Óin is the only Ranger in the five-Dwarf starting company. If he is untapped, he may tap at the beginning of the Site Phase to satisfy one River. The company may then proceed with its Site Phase, although Óin is now tapped and cannot make an influence attempt or play a Resource that requires an untapped character.

## 2. Two Rivers against the five starting Dwarves

Two Rivers require two Rangers. The starting company has only Óin. It cannot satisfy both requirements, so it must do nothing during its Site Phase. It cannot enter the site or attempt Iron Hill Dwarves.

The Hazard player does not need Seized by Terror to deny the Site Phase in this case. If Seized is also available, targeting Dori is normally the better split attempt because Dori's mind 1 gives a 91.7% split chance, compared with Óin's 72.2%.

## 3. Add Gandalf, but not Gildor

The company grows from five to six characters, so its base Hazard Limit rises from five to six. Its Ranger count remains one: Gandalf is a Scout, not a Ranger. One River can still be satisfied by Óin; two Rivers still cannot.

This is the central distinction: company size changes the Hazard Limit, but only Ranger skill changes the number of River costs the company can pay.

## 4. Add Gildor

The company has six characters and two Rangers: Óin and Gildor. Its base Hazard Limit is six. One River can be satisfied by either Ranger; two Rivers can be satisfied by tapping both, provided both are untapped and remain in the company.

This six-character example is the Dwarf/Gildor moving company while Gandalf is in a separate company. That separate company has its own Movement/Hazard Phase and Hazard Limit. If Gandalf joins the Dwarves and Gildor, the moving company has seven characters and a base Hazard Limit of seven.

## 5. One River, Seized by Terror, and two untapped Rangers

If Seized by Terror targets Óin or Gildor, the other Ranger remains untapped and can satisfy one River. If the targeted Ranger bears Promptings of Wisdom, it may already have been activated earlier in the turn, or he must tap it in response to cancel the return; he still cannot satisfy River. With one Seized copy, the Hazard player cannot deny the Site Phase using one River and two Rangers. If both Seized copies are drawn, both Rangers must be split successfully; a failed first attempt followed by a retry on the same Ranger still leaves the other Ranger available.

The Hazard player should usually target a different valuable character, such as Dori, if the aim is to maximise the chance of a split rather than deny the Site Phase.

## 6. Two Rivers, Seized by Terror, and two untapped Rangers

Two Rivers require both Rangers. If Seized by Terror successfully splits either Ranger, only one remains and the company cannot satisfy both River costs. Óin is the stronger target because his mind is lower. If the first Seized attempt fails and a second copy is available, retry Óin; a single successful split is enough when there are two Rivers.

- Óin, mind 3: 26/36 = **72.2%** split chance.
- Gildor, mind 4: 21/36 = **58.3%** split chance.

The Hazard player should normally target Óin.

## 7. Seven characters but only two Rangers

The seventh character does not satisfy a River. River counts Rangers, not total characters. The seven-character company has a base Hazard Limit of seven, but still has only two Rangers. It can satisfy two Rivers, not three.

## 8. One Ranger already tapped before the Site Phase

Only untapped Rangers can tap to satisfy River. If one of Óin and Gildor is already tapped, only the other can be used. One River can still be satisfied by the remaining untapped Ranger; two Rivers cannot. If both Rangers are already tapped, even one River prevents the Site Phase.

## 9. Direct Iron Hill versus the Eyrie route

The direct Lórien → Iron Hill Starter Movement path is Wilderness → Border-land → Border-land → Wilderness → Wilderness. It contains no Shadow-land or Dark-domain, so Seized by Terror is illegal.

The final Region Movement leg from Eagles’ Eyrie declares Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills. Grey Mountain Narrows is a Shadow-land, so Seized by Terror is legal on that leg.

The route does not change the number of Hazards drawn: Iron Hill's normal Hazard draw is three, so the solo draw is eight. It changes the keys that are available.

## 10. Drawing both cards is not the same as denying the Site Phase

A River and Seized by Terror in the Hazard hand create an opportunity, not an automatic denial.

- With one Ranger and one River, the Seized split must succeed against that Ranger.
- With two Rangers and one River, one Ranger can still satisfy River after the other splits.
- With two Rangers and two Rivers, splitting either Ranger is enough to leave too few Rangers.
- With two Rivers and only one Ranger, River already denies the Site Phase without a split roll.

Always count the Rangers who can actually tap at the beginning of the Site Phase.

## Probability calculations

The active 30-card Hazard deck has two River, two Seized by Terror and 26 other cards. The following are exact fresh-draw calculations.

### One Ranger

A Site Phase denial is available if either:

- both River copies are drawn (automatic denial with one Ranger), or
- exactly one River and at least one Seized by Terror are drawn, and at least one Seized split roll succeeds against the sole Ranger.

For Óin, the split chance is 26/36. Seized by Terror has no Cannot be Duplicated clause. After a failed short-event resolves and is discarded, a second copy can be played on the same Ranger in the same Movement/Hazard Phase. With two copies, the chance of at least one successful split is 1 − (10/36 × 10/36) = 91.98%.

| Draw size | Chance of denial |
|---|---:|
| 6 cards | 17063/147987 = **11.53%** |
| 8 cards | 63026/317115 = **19.87%** |

### Two untapped Rangers

A Site Phase denial is available if either:

- both Rivers and at least one Seized are drawn, with at least one Ranger successfully split; or
- one River and both Seized copies are drawn, and both Rangers are successfully split.

With two Rivers, one successful split is enough. If the first Seized roll against Óin fails, the second copy can retry Óin; the chance of at least one success is:

**1 − (10/36 × 10/36) = 91.98%.**

With one River, both Rangers must be split. The two Seized copies must succeed against different Rangers; if the first fails, retrying the same Ranger cannot deny the Site Phase. The chance both split is:

**26/36 × 21/36 = 42.13%.

| Draw size | Chance of denial |
|---|---:|
| 6 cards | 6227/591948 = **1.05%** |
| 8 cards | 17797/634230 = **2.81%** |

These figures assume both Rangers begin untapped, no other effect has already tapped a Ranger, the Hazard player chooses the River/Seized line, no sideboard access has halved the Hazard Limit, and all cards come from a fresh shuffled deck. Promptings of Wisdom used in response to a successful Seized against its bearer is included only as a return cancellation: the Ranger still taps and is unavailable for River. Promptings activated earlier in the turn is excluded, because that Ranger is already tapped before the Hazard line. They are not the probability of winning the game, nor the exact probability of a later draw in a continuous expedition.

The one-Ranger profile is more vulnerable because two Rivers alone can deny the Site Phase. A second Ranger sharply reduces the number of River/Seized combinations that can do so. A larger company that adds no Ranger increases the Hazard Limit but does not improve River capacity.

## Teaching conclusion

**Recruit for the skill you need, not merely for a larger company.** Gandalf is valuable for his Wizard and Sage abilities, but he does not answer River. Gildor is the card in the current support package that adds a second Ranger. Even then, preserve both Rangers: two Rivers require both, and Seized by Terror can split one away.

## Promptings of Wisdom and the probability results

The active Hero deck contains Promptings of Wisdom, so the Ranger's return to origin is not automatic in every case. If the targeted character is the Ranger bearing Promptings, the Ranger can tap and make a corruption check to cancel the return effect. The split remains, but he continues to the new site without a separate Movement/Hazard Phase and rejoins before the Site Phase. Because he is tapped, he cannot satisfy River. If Seized targets a different character, that character returns while Promptings stays with the Ranger's original company. This is why the lab's River-denial probabilities remain valid: Promptings can preserve arrival, but it does not provide an untapped Ranger for River. See CoE Rulings Digest #210, Question 6.
