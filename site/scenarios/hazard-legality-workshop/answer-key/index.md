# Hazard Legality Workshop — Model Answer

Read this after completing the [workshop]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-legality-workshop/' | relative_url }}). The point is to show the reasoning process, not to memorise a card order for every game.

## 1. Check legality first

For the declared Region Movement path **Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**, all three regions are Wilderness, and the named region Redhorn Gate is part of the site path. Moria is the destination site.

| Card | Why it is a legal candidate in this scenario |
|---|---|
| Cave Worm ×2 | Cave Worm may be keyed by name to Redhorn Gate. Region Movement includes the names of all regions in the declared site path, so each copy has a valid named-region key. |
| Giant Spiders | Its double-Wilderness key is satisfied by the three Wilderness regions. Its additional site-type keys apply only at Ruins & Lairs, Shadow-holds or Dark-holds in specified Mirkwood regions; Moria is not one of those regions, so use the route's double-Wilderness key here. |
| Lesser Spiders | Its keys include Wilderness and Shadow-land region types, plus a Ruins & Lairs [{R}] site key. It is legal here via the route's Wilderness [{w}] regions. **Moria is a Shadow-hold [{S}], not a Ruins & Lairs site**, so do not use Moria as the {R} key. |
| Neeker-breekers | Its keys include several region types, including Wilderness, plus a Ruins & Lairs [{R}] site key. It is legal here via the route's Wilderness [{w}] regions. **Moria is a Shadow-hold [{S}], not a Ruins & Lairs site**, so do not use Moria as the {R} key. Its one-strike-per-character wording must be applied exactly. |
| Watcher in the Water | It can key to double Wilderness and may also be played at Moria. Either route/site condition makes it a candidate here. |
| Full of Froth and Rage | It is a permanent-event that improves Spider and Animal attacks. The drawn hand contains attacks it can affect, so it has a potential effect. It cannot be duplicated while an instance is already having an effect. |
| Wake of War | It is a hazard long-event that improves Wolf, Spider and Animal attacks. It has a potential effect on the drawn attacks and can be played during the company's Movement/Hazard Phase as a hazard long-event. It cannot be duplicated while an instance is already having an effect. |

This is a deliberately constructed hand where **all eight cards have a legal use**. In an ordinary draw, do not assume this: verify every card's actual key, target, timing and conditions. An unused Hazard Limit slot never makes an illegal card legal.

## 2. A strong five-card line

For the stated five-Dwarf starting company, a strong model line to test is:

1. **Full of Froth and Rage**
2. **Wake of War**
3. **Giant Spiders**
4. **Lesser Spiders**
5. **Watcher in the Water**

This uses all five available slots. Both support events improve the relevant attacks while in effect; the Spider attacks also benefit from the extra strike provided by Wake of War. Watcher in the Water has each character face one strike, so the official rules' special rule for attacks that specify one strike per character means a strike-count modifier does not add extra strikes to that attack. Its prowess can still be modified.

The two Cave Worms and Neeker-breekers are legal but are left out of this model line because the limit is full. Cave Worm's single, very high-prowess strike is a real alternative to evaluate—not a card you should dismiss automatically. If the company's condition, available Resources or relevant modifiers change, compare the actual strike-by-strike consequences again. The solo rule requires the most disadvantageous legal combination for the *current* board, not a memorised five-card script.

## 3. Sequence matters

**Full of Froth and Rage** is discarded if a Spider or Animal attack is defeated. Therefore, do not assume its +2 prowess will necessarily remain for every later attack.

After resolving each Spider or Animal attack:

1. Determine whether the attack was defeated under the rules.
2. If it was defeated, discard Full of Froth and Rage as its text directs.
3. Recalculate the effects still active before resolving the next attack.
4. Keep Wake of War's separate effect in account for as long as it remains in play.

Choose the order by considering the actual company, not by following a fixed order from this answer key. A different company condition may make a different order more damaging.

## 4. Why the route mattered

If the company had used Starter Movement, the site path would show the relevant region types and the names of the origin and destination regions, but not the names of intervening regions. Region Movement includes the names of all regions in the declared path.

Cave Worm is legal because Redhorn Gate is explicitly part of the declared site path. However, Redhorn Gate is the destination region for Moria, so this named-region key would also be available on a legal Starter Movement approach to Moria from Lórien. Do not claim Cave Worm proves an extra named-region exposure unique to Region Movement. The general rule remains: match the actual declared site path and the printed key; a named intervening region only adds an option when a Hazard specifically names it.

## 5. Final fairness check

Before you resolve the hand, confirm:

- [ ] The route was chosen before looking at the Hazard hand.
- [ ] Every creature has a legal key.
- [ ] I distinguished lowercase region types from uppercase site types; Moria is {S}, not {R}.
- [ ] Every event meets its own conditions and timing.
- [ ] Each ordinary limit-counting play is counted against the five-slot Hazard Limit; Twilight is the explicit no-limit exception if its target is legal.
- [ ] Attack and support effects are recalculated after each resolution.
- [ ] No illegal card was added merely to fill a slot.
- [ ] No useful legal play was skipped just to protect the Heroes.
