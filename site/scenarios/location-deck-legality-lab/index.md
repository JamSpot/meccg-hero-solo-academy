# Location Deck Legality & Wind Throne Lab

This lab corrects a serious site-card problem in the historical Challenge Deck C-derived list and adds a practical Starter Movement route that gives the Hazard deck a different set of legal attacks.

## 1. The Dead Marshes correction

The earlier 15-site list included **Dead Marshes**. That card is a **Minion** site. Under the current official rules, a Wizard's Location deck may contain only Hero site cards. There is no Hero Dead Marshes site card in the current official card database.

Do not put Dead Marshes in this Hero Location deck. The Academy replaces it with the **Hero version of The Wind Throne** from *The Wizards*. There is also a separate Minion version of The Wind Throne from *The Lidless Eye*: check the alignment printed on the physical card.

The corrected 15-site list is on the [Hero deck page]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}).

## 2. The Wind Throne — read the actual Hero site card

| Attribute | Hero version |
|---|---|
| Card identity | The Wind Throne — Hero version, *The Wizards* |
| Nearest Haven | Lórien |
| Region | Grey Mountain Narrows |
| Site path from Lórien | Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land |
| Site type | Ruins & Lairs |
| Site-card numbers | Hazard 2 / Hero 2 |
| Playable | Information; minor and major Items |
| Automatic attack | Orcs — 3 strikes at 7 prowess |

This site is a deliberate teaching choice: it adds a Shadow-land to the printed site path without adding another Dragon site.

## 3. Why Lórien → The Wind Throne is Starter Movement

The company is at Lórien, a Haven. The Wind Throne lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, so the company may use Starter Movement directly to it.

The printed **Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land** sequence is the site's site path. It is used for creature keying and movement exposure; it is not a list of three Region Movement cards that you must declare. The four-region limit for Region Movement is not the test here.

## 4. Calculate all three numbers

The Wind Throne's site-card numbers are **2/2** (normal Hazard-player draw / Hero-player draw).

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Hero draw | Up to **2** from the Hero play deck |
| Normal Hazard draw | **2** |
| Solo Hazard draw | **2 + (2 × 2) = 6** from the separate Hazard deck |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |
| Automatic attack if entered | Orcs — **3 strikes at 7** |

Drawing six Hazard cards does not permit six ordinary Hazard plays. The five-Dwarf company can normally face up to five Hazard plays that count against its Hazard Limit. The Orc automatic attack is separate and is faced only if the company enters the site.

## 5. What the baseline Hazard deck can key here

The site path is Wilderness / Border-land / Shadow-land, the destination is a Ruins & Lairs, and the named region is Grey Mountain Narrows.

| Card | Wind Throne legality | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cave Worm | **Legal** | Its named-region key includes Grey Mountain Narrows. |
| Lesser Spiders | **Legal** | It can use the Wilderness, Shadow-land or Ruins & Lairs key. |
| Neeker-breekers | **Legal** | The route/site supplies several of its printed region/site keys. It does not affect Wizards. |
| Wargs | **Legal** | Wilderness, Border-land and Shadow-land are all present. |
| Seized by Terror | **Legal** | An eligible non-Wizard, non-Ringwraith character is moving through a Shadow-land. |
| Giant Spiders | **Not legal** | The path has only one Wilderness, not the two required by its deep-Wilderness key; the special Mirkwood keys do not apply in Grey Mountain Narrows. |
| Watcher in the Water | **Not legal** | It requires two Wilderness regions plus a Coastal Sea, or its explicit Moria permission. This path has one Wilderness, no Coastal Sea, and is not Moria. |
| River | **Legal** | It can target the destination site. Óin may tap as a Ranger at the start of the Site Phase to cancel it. |
| Despair of the Heart | **Conditional** | It can target an eligible Dwarf; it does not need a route key. |
| Full of Froth and Rage | **Conditional** | It needs a Spider or Animal attack, such as Lesser Spiders or Neeker-breekers. |
| Wake of War | **Conditional** | It needs a Wolf, Spider or Animal attack, such as Wargs, Lesser Spiders or Neeker-breekers. |
| Twilight | **No target in baseline** | There is no Environment in the active Hero deck or baseline Hazard deck. |

The baseline has **22 of 30 cards with a route or target opportunity** on this route. The eight route-inactive cards are Giant Spiders ×3, Watcher in the Water ×3 and Twilight ×2. This is a route-specific composition measure, not a prediction of whether the company will win.

## 6. Site type is not region type

The Wind Throne is a **Ruins & Lairs** site. Its site path includes a **Shadow-land** region.

Those facts do different jobs:

- **Seized by Terror** checks the region type, so the Shadow-land makes it legal against an eligible character.
- **Arouse Denizens** (not in the baseline) can modify an automatic attack at a Ruins & Lairs, so it is a candidate here.
- **Arouse Minions** (not in the baseline) requires a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold site. It is **not** legal here. A Shadow-land in the route does not turn a Ruins & Lairs into a Shadow-hold.

If Arouse Denizens is tested in a later variant, the Wind Throne's automatic attack becomes **three Orc strikes at 10 prowess** for the turn. It does not gain extra strikes. Arouse Denizens counts against the Hazard Limit. The company can still decline to enter the site and avoid the automatic attack, but then it takes no site actions.

## 7. Hero Resource opportunities

The Wind Throne permits Information and minor/major Items. It is therefore an additional legal destination for the Major Items **Durin's Axe** and **Hauberk of Bright Mail**.

It is **not** a Greater Item site. Do not send Wormsbane or another Greater Item there. In the active list, Wormsbane remains a Caves of Ûlund or The Lonely Mountain objective.

## 8. Practice tasks

Before opening the model answer, write down:

1. Why is Dead Marshes excluded from the Hero Location deck?
2. Which physical version of The Wind Throne belongs in the deck?
3. Why is Lórien → The Wind Throne Starter Movement rather than Region Movement?
4. Calculate Hero draw, solo Hazard draw and Hazard Limit for the five Dwarves.
5. Explain why Seized by Terror is legal but Watcher in the Water is not.
6. Explain why Arouse Denizens could modify the automatic attack but Arouse Minions could not.
7. Which of Durin's Axe, Hauberk of Bright Mail and Wormsbane can be played there?
8. Would you enter the site if the Hazard side has a legal creature, River and an attack modifier?
