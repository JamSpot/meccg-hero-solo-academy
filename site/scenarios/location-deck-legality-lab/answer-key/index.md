# Location Deck Legality & Wind Throne Lab — Model Answer

## 1. Dead Marshes is not a Wizard site

The current official rules restrict a Wizard Location deck to Hero site cards. The Dead Marshes site card is Minion-alignment. It is not a legal card in this Hero Location deck. The historical Challenge Deck C-derived player aid is preserved as a source, but its Dead Marshes entry is corrected for the Academy.

Use the **Hero** version of The Wind Throne from *The Wizards*. Do not use the separate Minion version from *The Lidless Eye*. The two cards share a name but have different alignments and nearest Havens.

## 2. The Wind Throne's printed facts

The Hero version lists:

- Nearest Haven: Lórien.
- Region: Grey Mountain Narrows.
- Site path: Wilderness, Border-land, Shadow-land.
- Site type: Ruins & Lairs.
- Draw numbers: 2 Hazard / 2 Hero.
- Playable: Information and minor/major Items.
- Automatic attack: Orcs, three strikes at 7 prowess.

These are the card's printed facts; do not infer site type from the region path.

## 3. Movement and draws

Lórien is a Haven and is printed as The Wind Throne's nearest Haven. The company can therefore use Starter Movement directly from Lórien to The Wind Throne. The five-symbol/four-region Region Movement limit is not involved.

The draw calculations are:

- Hero: up to **2** from the Hero play deck.
- Normal Hazard: **2**.
- Solo Hazard: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6** from the Hazard deck.
- Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- Site automatic attack: three Orc strikes at 7, only if the company enters.

The Hazard draw and Hazard Limit are separate. The automatic attack is also separate from the Hazard Limit.

## 4. Creature keying and other Hazard targets

| Card | Answer |
|---|---|
| Cave Worm | Legal by the named region Grey Mountain Narrows. |
| Lesser Spiders | Legal by Wilderness, Shadow-land or Ruins & Lairs key. |
| Neeker-breekers | Legal by its region/site keys. It does not affect Wizards. |
| Wargs | Legal by Wilderness, Border-land or Shadow-land. |
| Seized by Terror | Legal against an eligible character because the site path contains a Shadow-land. |
| Giant Spiders | Illegal: the path has only one Wilderness and Grey Mountain Narrows is not one of its special Mirkwood keys. |
| Watcher in the Water | Illegal: the card requires two Wilderness plus a Coastal Sea, or may be played at Moria. The Wind Throne path has one Wilderness, no Coastal Sea, and is not Moria. |
| River | Legal on the destination site. Óin can tap as a Ranger at the start of the Site Phase to cancel it. |
| Despair of the Heart | Conditional on an eligible target in the company. |
| Full of Froth and Rage | Conditional on a Spider or Animal attack being present. |
| Wake of War | Conditional on a Wolf, Spider or Animal attack being present. |
| Twilight | No target in the closed-sideboard baseline. |

The route has **22/30** baseline Hazard cards with an opportunity. The eight cards without an opportunity are Giant Spiders ×3, Watcher in the Water ×3 and Twilight ×2.

## 5. Arouse Denizens versus Arouse Minions

The Wind Throne is a **Ruins & Lairs**, so Arouse Denizens is the matching candidate. It may increase one automatic attack at that site by +3 prowess. The Orc attack becomes three strikes at 10 prowess for the turn; the number of strikes does not change. Arouse Denizens is not part of the 30-card baseline and counts against the Hazard Limit if tested.

Arouse Minions is not legal on this site. It requires a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold. The fact that the route passes through a Shadow-land is irrelevant to that site-type requirement.

This is the central distinction:

- **Shadow-land** is a region type and can make Seized by Terror legal.
- **Shadow-hold** is a site type and can make Arouse Minions legal.
- **Ruins & Lairs** is the Wind Throne's site type and can make Arouse Denizens legal.

Do not treat those three labels as interchangeable.

## 6. Resource destinations

Durin's Axe and Hauberk of Bright Mail are Major Items, so the Wind Throne's Major Item permission makes it a legal alternative site for either card.

Wormsbane is a Greater Item. The Wind Throne does not permit Greater Items, so it is not a legal destination. The historical Dead Marshes suggestion is invalid too: the site is Minion-alignment and its playable categories are only minor/major Items. Use Caves of Ûlund or The Lonely Mountain for Wormsbane in this Location deck.

## 7. A high-pressure Hazard line in a later variant

If the Hazard deck later adds Arouse Denizens and draws the following five cards against the moving five-Dwarf company, all five can be legal and fit the five-card Hazard Limit:

1. **Cave Worm** — keyed to Grey Mountain Narrows.
2. **Seized by Terror** — targets an eligible Dwarf moving through the Shadow-land.
3. **River** — targets The Wind Throne and threatens the Site Phase unless Óin taps to cancel it.
4. **Arouse Denizens** — boosts the Ruins & Lairs automatic attack from three Orc strikes at 7 to three at 10.
5. **Despair of the Heart** — targets an eligible Dwarf and adds corruption pressure if a character in the company is wounded.

This is a constructed teaching hand, not a promise that these cards will be drawn together. It demonstrates why the route is a useful later test. If the Hero player declines to enter, the automatic attack is not faced and no site action is taken.

## 8. What changes in the Academy

The active Location deck remains 15 cards. Dead Marshes is removed and the Hero version of The Wind Throne is added. The 36-card Hero play deck and 30-card Hazard baseline are unchanged.

The original three baseline campaign routes remain available. The Wind Throne is an optional, more demanding expedition that exposes the Hazard deck to a Shadow-land and a Ruins & Lairs site.
