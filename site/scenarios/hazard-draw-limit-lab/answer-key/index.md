# Hazard Draw & Limit Lab — Model Answer

Use this only after completing the workbook. The key is to apply the ordinary movement draw rule first, then the solo formula, then the company's Hazard Limit.

## The calculation method

1. Determine whether the destination is a Haven.
2. If it is a Haven, use the origin site's normal Hazard draw number; otherwise use the destination site's number.
3. Calculate **2 + (2 × normal number)**.
4. Calculate company size, counting each Hobbit and Orc scout as half and rounding up.
5. Set the base Hazard Limit to the greater of company size or two.
6. Apply any relevant modifiers. Do not confuse a modifier to the limit with an additional card draw.

## Workbook A — draw and limit

| Journey | Site supplying normal number | Normal number | Solo calculation | Solo draw | Company | Base limit |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Rivendell → Moria | Moria (non-Haven destination) | 3 | 2 + (2 × 3) | **8** | Five Dwarves | **5** |
| Moria → Rivendell | Moria (Haven destination; use origin) | 3 | 2 + (2 × 3) | **8** | Five Dwarves | **5** |
| Moria → Goblin-gate | Goblin-gate (non-Haven destination) | 2 | 2 + (2 × 2) | **6** | Five Dwarves | **5** |
| Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold | Blue Mountain (non-Haven destination) | 1 | 2 + (2 × 1) | **4** | Five Dwarves | **5** |
| Grey Havens → Lórien | Grey Havens (Haven destination; use origin) | 2 | 2 + (2 × 2) | **6** | Gandalf alone | **2** |

The Moria → Rivendell example is the important Haven check. Do not use Rivendell's normal number for that movement: because the destination is a Haven, the normal two-player draw rule uses the site of origin, Moria.

## Workbook B — company size

| Company | Effective size | Base Hazard Limit | Explanation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| One Wizard | 1 | **2** | The minimum is two. |
| Two normal characters | 2 | **2** | Company size equals two. |
| Three normal characters and one Hobbit | 3.5 → 4 | **4** | The Hobbit counts as half; round the total up. |
| Four normal characters and one Hobbit | 4.5 → 5 | **5** | Round 4.5 up to five. |
| Four normal characters and two Orc scouts | 5 | **5** | The two Orc scouts together count as one. |
| Five normal characters | 5 | **5** | Five is greater than the minimum of two. |

The base limit is not simply the printed number of character cards if a company includes Hobbits or Orc scouts.

## Workbook C — Hundreds of Butterflies

1. **Eight cards were drawn.** The draw is based on Moria's normal Hazard draw number of three: 2 + (2 × 3) = 8.
2. **The base Hazard Limit was five.** The company contains five normal characters.
3. **The modified Hazard Limit becomes six.** Hundreds of Butterflies increases the limit against that company by one.
4. **No additional card is drawn.** The solo draw was already determined; Hundreds of Butterflies changes the limit, not the draw.
5. **Up to six limit-counting Hazard plays** can be made, provided six legal plays are available and the selected solo procedure requires playing as many as possible.
6. **Twilight does not use one of those six slots.** It does not count against the Hazard Limit, but it still needs a legal Environment target and must follow its timing.

This is the important Hero/Hazard interaction: the Hero player receives an untap, but the Hazard side gets another slot. The solo draw remains eight.

## Workbook D — fixed base limit

1. **No.** The base Hazard Limit was set at the beginning of the company's Movement/Hazard Phase and remains fixed for that phase, even if a character is eliminated during it.
2. **Yes, the calculation could differ.** If the character had left before the base limit was set, the company size at the setting step would be lower, so calculate from that current company size.
3. The setting step is a defined rules step. Recounting after each strike would incorrectly change a limit that the rules say remains fixed. Apply later card effects separately if they modify the limit.

## Workbook E — separate companies

| Company | Solo draw | Base limit | Why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Five Dwarves → Moria | **8** | **5** | Moria is a non-Haven destination with normal draw number 3; company size is five. |
| Gandalf → Lórien | **6** | **2** | Lórien is a Haven, so use Grey Havens' normal draw number 2; Gandalf is one character but the minimum limit is two. |

The solitaire procedure is applied separately to each company's Movement/Hazard Phase. Each company has its own site path, draw and limit. The Dwarves' five-character size does not increase Gandalf's limit, and Gandalf's presence elsewhere does not increase the Dwarves' limit.

## The six rules in plain language

- The solo draw is based on the normal Hazard draw number selected by the ordinary movement rules.
- A move to a Haven uses the origin site's normal Hazard draw number.
- The base Hazard Limit is based on company size, with a minimum of two and special half-counting for Hobbits and Orc scouts.
- Hundreds of Butterflies increases the Hazard Limit by one and untaps its target; it does not change the solo draw.
- Twilight does not count against the Hazard Limit, but still needs a legal Environment target.
- A character eliminated after the base limit is set does not reduce that phase's base limit.

## One final table example

A five-Dwarf company moves to Moria. You draw eight cards. You have five ordinary slots. The Hero player uses Hundreds of Butterflies, so the limit becomes six. The Hazard hand contains six legal limit-counting plays and one Twilight with a legal Environment target.

The solo Hazard player can play the six legal limit-counting cards, plus Twilight. The draw remains eight cards; Twilight is not one of the six ordinary slots. Any drawn cards that remain unplayed are handled according to the selected solo procedure.

Do not treat this as a fixed sequence to copy. The actual draw, legality and card text determine what can be played in a real game.

## Official basis

- [Council of Elrond Official Game Rules](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) — base Hazard Limit (2.IV.iii), normal card draw (2.IV.v), and company-size definition.
- [The Wizards rules — Solitaire Rules](https://meccg.com/rules/by-expansion/the-wizards/) — separate Hazard deck, five-card solo hand and the 2 + twice draw formula.
- [Hundreds of Butterflies card text](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/MeCCG%20Full%20Spoiler%20List.pdf) — playable on a moving character during the Movement/Hazard Phase; untaps it and increases the Hazard Limit against its company by one.
