# 6L. Seized by Terror — Aftermath, Followers & Recovery

This lesson starts **after** Seized by Terror succeeds. The earlier labs taught when the card is legal and how likely a character is to split. This one follows the consequences: what travels with the character, what happens to followers, why Fellowship can be lost, and how the companies can reunite.

The active decks do not change. The fixed 30-card Hazard deck contains two Seized by Terror; the 36-card Hero deck contains two Fellowship among its 30 Resources.

## 1. Read the current card text precisely

The current Council of Elrond card database gives Seized by Terror these requirements:

- It can target a **non-Wizard, non-Ringwraith character** moving in a Shadow-land or Dark-domain.
- The target player's roll is 2D6 plus the target character's mind.
- A result **below 12** makes that character split into a different company.
- The new company immediately returns to the original company's site of origin.

A successful result is a **company split and return**, not an elimination, discard, wound or body check. The character remains in play. Do not remove the character or the cards it bears just because the company has changed. Seized by Terror is a short-event, so playing it uses one of the moving company's Hazard Limit slots.

Source: [Council of Elrond Official Card Database](https://www.councilofelrond.org/cards/), Seized by Terror (DM-88).

## 2. What goes with the split character?

Separate the character from the company and then check every card associated with that character.

### The target character and its own cards

The target character forms the new company and returns to the original company's site of origin. Items borne by that character remain borne by that character. Cards played on or placed with a character are under that character's control; a company split does not itself eliminate the character.

An ally also stays with its controlling character until that character leaves play. Seized by Terror changes the character's company; it does not say to eliminate the character. Therefore do not discard an ally merely because its controller was split off.

### Followers are a separate problem

Seized by Terror says that **the target character** splits off. It does not say that the target's followers join the new company.

A follower must remain stacked under its controlling character, and a character can control a follower only while both are at the same site. When Seized by Terror separates a controller from its followers, the followers left behind are no longer under that character's direct influence. The reverse can also happen: if the target is itself a follower, it is separated from the controller who remains with the original company.

The consolidated rules give a specific repair procedure for a follower removed from direct influence outside an Organization Phase:

1. Its mind is **not** immediately subtracted from general influence.
2. During the player's next Organization Phase, move it under general influence or under another eligible character's direct influence.
3. If it is still not controlled by general or direct influence at the end of that Organization Phase, discard it.

This is a delayed control problem, not an immediate discard. The follower must be dealt with at the next Organization Phase.

## 3. Company-wide Resources do not all behave alike

The current company-split rule distinguishes effects by what they target:

- A Resource permanent-event or ongoing effect targeting the original company as an entity stays with the original company.
- An effect targeting a specific character's company stays with that character.
- The Hazard player chooses which Hazard permanent-events played on the original company go with which subsequent company.
- Both resulting companies are considered to have faced attacks that the original company faced before the split.

Always read the individual card as well. **Fellowship has its own explicit departure clause**, so it does not simply remain with the original company.

### Fellowship is especially vulnerable

Fellowship is in the active Hero Resource list (two copies). It can be played at a Haven during the Organization Phase on a company with at least four characters and allies. It gives all characters and allies in that company +1 prowess and +1 to corruption checks.

Its text also says to discard it if a character or ally joins or leaves the company for any reason. A character split off by Seized by Terror has left that company. Therefore every Fellowship copy in play on that company is discarded. This can remove the company's combat and corruption protection without Seized by Terror wounding anyone.

Do not generalise this result to every company effect. Fellowship is discarded because its own text says so; other effects must be checked against their own wording and the company-split rule.

## 4. What happens to the two companies this turn?

### Default result: the split-off company returns to the origin

Without a relevant cancellation effect, the split-off company returns to the original company's site of origin immediately. It does not arrive at the declared destination.

The consolidated return-to-origin rule (2.IV.4) says a company returned to its site of origin during its Movement/Hazard Phase is no longer considered to have a site path or to have moved to a new site that turn, and it cannot initiate actions during its Site Phase. Apply this to the split-off company returned by Seized by Terror. Do not automatically treat the remaining company as having been returned too: it is still the company travelling to its declared destination unless another effect changes that.

The original company's base Hazard Limit was fixed at the beginning of its Movement/Hazard Phase. Do not recalculate it because the split changes the company's size.

### Important exception: Promptings of Wisdom is in the active Hero deck

The 36-card Hero deck contains two copies of Promptings of Wisdom. It is playable during the Organization Phase on a Ranger. The target Ranger may tap to cancel all Hazard effects for the rest of the turn that force **his company** to return to its site of origin or tap its current or new site. If he taps, he makes a corruption check. The card has 2 corruption points and cannot be duplicated in a given company. The check includes those 2 points and any other corruption points the Ranger controls. In the starting company, Óin also bears Cram (1 point) and has a -1 corruption-check modifier, so using Promptings on Óin means a check against 3 corruption points with -1. Gildor has no printed negative check modifier and no starting Cram, but recruiting him raises the company's Hazard Limit.

Council of Elrond Rulings Digest #210, Question 6 (9 August 2025), specifically resolves its interaction with Seized by Terror:

**If Seized by Terror targets the Ranger who has Promptings of Wisdom:**

- The split still happens. Promptings does **not** cancel the split.
- The Promptings effect follows the Ranger into his new company because it targets that Ranger's company.
- If the Ranger taps and Promptings resolves before the return effect, it cancels the return. The Ranger continues to the declared new site alone, without a separate Movement/Hazard Phase.
- He rejoins the original company at the end of all Movement/Hazard Phases, before the Site Phase.
- The original company is no longer protected by that Promptings effect for the rest of the turn.

**If Seized by Terror targets a different character in the Ranger's company:**

- The different character splits off and returns to the original company's site of origin.
- Promptings stays with the Ranger's original company. It does not cancel the return of the other character.
- The Ranger's company remains protected by Promptings from other qualifying return-to-origin or site-tapping Hazard effects for the rest of the turn.

Promptings does not cancel River: River requires a Ranger to tap and does not itself force a company to return to its site of origin or tap its current/new site. It cancels only the Hazard effects named by its text.

**One-Ranger example:** Óin is the company's only Ranger and bears Promptings. Seized targets Óin; he taps Promptings and passes the corruption check, so he continues to the destination and rejoins before the Site Phase. If River is also in play, Óin is already tapped and cannot satisfy it. The Site Phase is skipped, but Óin is with the company at the destination rather than stranded at the origin. If Gildor is also present and untapped, he can satisfy one River while Óin remains tapped.

If the split-off Ranger rejoins at the destination before the Site Phase, the companies are no longer separated for the Site Phase. Any Fellowship copy is still discarded: the character did leave the company, and rejoining does not undo Fellowship's discard clause. If the target returns to the origin instead, any follower/controller relationship left separated must be repaired at the next Organization Phase.

The two companies otherwise cannot rejoin while they are at different sites. Once both are at the same site, the normal joining rules apply: companies may be joined during an Organization Phase when they are at the same site; at the end of all Movement/Hazard Phases, companies at the same non-Haven site must join, while joining at a Haven is optional.

## 5. Worked example: the starting Dwarves

The Academy's starting control relationships are:

- Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli.
- Glóin controls Óin.

The starting company has seven points of unused general influence. Thorin's +2 direct influence against Dwarves lets him control Dori (mind 1) and Kíli (mind 3); Glóin's +1 against Dwarves lets him control Óin (mind 3). The starting-company setup places a Cram with Thorin and a Cram with Óin.

Assume the company takes the Lórien → The Wind Throne Starter Movement route. The Hero site path contains a Shadow-land, so Seized by Terror is legal. The site being a Ruins & Lairs is not what makes Seized legal; the region type in the path does.

The table below shows the **default result without Promptings of Wisdom canceling the return**. If Óin is the target and he bears Promptings, use the special result in section 4: he splits but continues to the destination and rejoins before the Site Phase.

| Target | Mind | Split chance | Control consequence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Dori | 1 | 33/36 = 91.7% | Dori separates from Thorin and must be reassigned at the next Organization Phase |
| Kíli | 3 | 26/36 = 72.2% | Kíli separates from Thorin and must be reassigned |
| Óin | 3 | 26/36 = 72.2% | Óin separates from Glóin and must be reassigned |
| Glóin | 5 | 15/36 = 41.7% | Óin is left with the travelling company but loses Glóin's direct influence |
| Thorin II | 8 | 3/36 = 8.3% | Dori and Kíli are left with the travelling company and lose Thorin's direct influence |
| Gandalf | Wizard | Not a legal target | Seized by Terror cannot target him |

### If Thorin is split off

Dori and Kíli remain with the company that continues to The Wind Throne. They are no longer at the same site as Thorin, so his direct influence cannot continue to control them there.

At the next Organization Phase, their combined mind is 4. In the untouched starting-company configuration, there are 7 points of general influence available, so both can be moved under general influence. If other characters have since used that spare influence, check the actual remaining total; otherwise move one or both under another eligible character at their site, or discard any follower still uncontrolled at the end of the phase.

### If Glóin is split off

Óin remains with the travelling company, but Glóin is now at the origin. Óin's mind is 3, so the untouched starting-company general-influence reserve of 7 is sufficient to take him under general influence at the next Organization Phase. Again, recalculate if the company has recruited additional characters.

### If a follower is split off instead

If Seized by Terror targets Dori, Kíli or Óin, the target is now at the origin while its controller remains with the company at the destination. The target's direct-influence relationship is broken by the separation. It must be reassigned at the next Organization Phase; it is not automatically discarded during the split.

## 6. Hero deck lesson: why Gandalf is the preferred Book bearer

The Book of Mazarbûl is a key objective in the first campaign. Gandalf is both a Sage and a Wizard. Seized by Terror expressly excludes Wizard characters, so it cannot split Gandalf away. The Hero deck also contains two Promptings of Wisdom; a Ranger carrying it can cancel the return part of Seized if that Ranger is the target, although the split itself still occurs.

That gives the Academy's existing Book-bearer recommendation a second benefit:

- Gandalf can satisfy the Book's Sage requirement.
- Gandalf cannot be selected by Seized by Terror.
- Promptings of Wisdom is a separate defence for a Ranger, but it must be tapped and causes a corruption check; it does not make the Ranger ineligible as a target.
- If a non-Wizard character bears the Book and is split off without the Promptings return cancellation, the Book remains with that character and returns to the origin with him, delaying the expedition.

This is not a reason to change the 36-card Hero deck. It is a reason to make the Book-bearer choice deliberately when the Book is played.

## 7. Route-planning comparison

| Route | Movement | Printed path | Seized by Terror |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lórien → The Wind Throne | Starter | Wilderness → Border-land → Shadow-land | Legal |
| Lórien → Iron Hill Dwarf-hold | Starter | Wilderness → Border-land → Border-land → Wilderness → Wilderness | Illegal |
| Eagles' Eyrie → Iron Hill Dwarf-hold | Region | Anduin Vales → Grey Mountain Narrows → Northern Rhovanion → Iron Hills | Legal: Grey Mountain Narrows is a Shadow-land |

The Wind Throne's site type is Ruins & Lairs, but Seized by Terror is keyed to the **movement region type**, not to the site's type. Conversely, the direct Iron Hill route is legal Starter Movement despite five printed region symbols, but none is a Shadow-land or Dark-domain.

Eagles' Eyrie remains a supplementary route exercise, not part of the locked 15-site first-play Location deck.
