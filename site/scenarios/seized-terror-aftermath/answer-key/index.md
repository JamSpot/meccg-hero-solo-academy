# Seized by Terror — Model Answer

Use this after completing [6L. Seized by Terror — Aftermath, Followers & Recovery]({{ '/scenarios/seized-terror-aftermath/' | relative_url }}).

## 1. Does a successful split discard the target?

No. Seized by Terror does not say to discard or eliminate the character. The character splits into a different company and that new company immediately returns to the original company's site of origin. The character remains in play.

Do not make a body check and do not remove items simply because the character has changed companies.

## 2. If Glóin is split off, what happens to Óin?

Óin is Glóin's follower in the starting company. The target named by Seized by Terror is Glóin; Óin is not named as joining the new company. Óin remains with the original travelling company, but he is no longer at the same site as his controller. His direct-influence control is therefore broken.

At the next Organization Phase, Óin must be placed under general influence or under another eligible character's direct influence at his site. If he remains uncontrolled at the end of that phase, he is discarded. In the untouched starting-company configuration, Óin's mind 3 fits within the 7 unused general influence. Because Óin starts with a Cram, that Cram travels with him to the origin if Óin is the target; if Glóin is the target, Óin and his Cram remain with the travelling company.

## 3. If Thorin is split off, what happens to Dori and Kíli?

Dori and Kíli remain with the travelling company; they are not automatically carried away with Thorin. Thorin is now at the origin while they remain at the destination, so they are no longer stacked with their controller and cannot remain under his direct influence there.

Their combined mind is 1 + 3 = 4. The untouched starting company has 7 points of unused general influence, so both can be moved under general influence at the next Organization Phase. Thorin's starting Cram goes with Thorin to the origin; it does not stay with Dori or Kíli. If other recruits have consumed that reserve, calculate the actual remaining influence and find another eligible controller or discard any follower still uncontrolled at the end of the phase.

The key timing point is that their mind is not immediately charged against general influence when direct influence is broken outside the Organization Phase. The reassignment deadline is the end of the next Organization Phase.

## 4. If Dori is split while carrying an item, does the item stay with the travelling company?

No. The item remains with Dori, the character who bears it. Dori and his borne item return together to the original company's site of origin. The item is not discarded by Seized by Terror.

The same principle applies to cards played on or placed with the target character, subject to their own text.

## 5. What happens to Fellowship?

Discard it. Fellowship's own text says to discard it if a character or ally joins or leaves the company for any reason. The split character has left the original company. If more than one Fellowship copy was in play on that company, each copy with that condition is discarded.

This is a direct card-text consequence, not a general rule that every company permanent-event is discarded when a company splits.

## 6. Can the split-off character enter the destination site this turn?

No. The new company returns to the original company's site of origin; it does not arrive at the destination. Under the consolidated return-to-origin rule (2.IV.4), a company returned to its site of origin is not considered to have moved to a new site and cannot initiate actions during its Site Phase.

The remaining company is still the company that continues to the declared destination, unless another effect returns it too. Its base Hazard Limit was fixed at the beginning of the phase and is not recalculated after the split.

## 7. Can the companies rejoin while at different sites?

No. They must first be at the same site. Companies at the same site can be joined during an Organization Phase. At the end of all Movement/Hazard Phases, companies at the same non-Haven site must join; joining at a Haven is optional.

A character returned to the origin cannot be treated as if it were already with the company at the destination.

## 8. Why is Gandalf safe, and why does that matter for the Book?

The current Seized by Terror card text excludes Wizard characters. Gandalf is a Wizard, so he is not a legal target. He is also a Sage, making him the preferred Book of Mazarbûl bearer under the Academy build. The Book therefore avoids this particular split threat while it is borne by Gandalf.

A non-Wizard Book bearer could be split away, taking the Book back to the origin and delaying the objective.

## 9. Why is Seized legal on the Wind Throne route but not direct Iron Hill?

The Hero Wind Throne site card lists a Starter Movement site path of Wilderness, Border-land, Shadow-land. That includes a Shadow-land, so Seized by Terror's region requirement is met.

The Hero Iron Hill Dwarf-hold site card lists Wilderness, Border-land, Border-land, Wilderness, Wilderness. That direct Starter path has no Shadow-land or Dark-domain, so Seized by Terror is illegal there. The fact that Iron Hill has five printed region symbols does not make it Region Movement; it remains legal Starter Movement because Lórien is the site's nearest Haven.

The supplementary Eagles' Eyrie → Iron Hill Region Movement route includes Grey Mountain Narrows, a Shadow-land, so Seized is legal on that leg.

## 10. What must happen to a follower whose control was broken?

By the end of the next Organization Phase, the follower must be moved under general influence or under another eligible character's direct influence. If it is still not controlled by either form of influence at the end of that phase, it is discarded.

The mind of a follower removed from direct influence outside an Organization Phase is not immediately charged to general influence. This temporary grace is not permission to leave the character uncontrolled after the next Organization Phase.

## 11. Which target is best for the Hazard player?

There is no universal answer. Use the objective and the company state:

- **Dori** is easiest to split (33/36, 91.7%). If the aim is the highest chance of removing a character from the travelling company, he is attractive.
- **Óin or Kíli** split on 26/36 (72.2%). Óin may also be important when River is present, because he is the starting company's only Ranger.
- **Glóin** splits on 15/36 (41.7%). If he is split, Óin is left without his controller.
- **Thorin II** splits on only 3/36 (8.3%), but if it succeeds, both Dori and Kíli lose their controller at once.
- If Fellowship is in play, any successful split can discard it, so the most likely target may be better than a low-probability controller target.

Choose the line that most damages the current objective, not simply the character with the lowest mind.

## 12. What does the general company-split rule add?

The current consolidated rule 2.II.3.6.1 distinguishes effects by what they target. Resource permanent-events and ongoing effects targeting the original company as an entity stay with that original company; effects targeting a specific character's company stay with that character. The Hazard player chooses which Hazard permanent-events go with which subsequent company. Both companies are considered to have faced attacks the original company faced before splitting.

This is why the player must identify whether an effect is company-wide, character-specific or governed by its own discard clause. Fellowship's own discard clause controls its result.

## Playtest note

For the first three baseline games, record each time Seized by Terror is drawn:

- Was the route condition actually met?
- Which eligible characters were available?
- Did the target control followers, carry an important item, or have an ally?
- Was Fellowship in play?
- Did the split change the Resource objective or only the company composition?
- How many turns did it take to regroup, and did any follower have to be discarded?

Do not change the two Seized by Terror copies or the two Fellowship copies until the baseline games are recorded.
