# Physical Card Acquisition Checklist — First Play Build

Use this page to check the exact physical cards needed for the Academy's first conventional Hero solo setup. It is a **genuine-card build**: no proxies, no printed substitutes and no Minion cards. You do not need to own complete expansions; the list below identifies the individual cards and quantities.

The active Academy list is derived from **Challenge Deck C — Dwarven Quest**, but it is not an untouched copy of that deck. The historical deck used three Pallando cards; the Academy version uses one Gandalf. Its original Dead Marshes site entry is Minion-alignment, so the Academy replaces it with the Hero version of The Wind Throne.

## 1. What must be on the table?

| Component | Exact quantity | What it is |
|---|---:|---|
| Hero play deck | **36** | 30 Resources, five non-Wizard characters and one Gandalf |
| Separate Hazard deck | **30** | The fixed Challenge Deck C-derived Hazard list |
| Location deck | **15** | Hero site cards; keep them separate and unshuffled |
| Starting company | **5** | Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin |
| Starting items | **2** | Cram, one with Thorin II and one with Óin |
| **Total physical cards** | **88** | Excludes the optional sideboard |

Only the 36-card Hero play deck and 30-card Hazard deck are shuffled. The 15 site cards form the Location deck and are selected as needed; they are not a random draw pile. The five starting characters and two Cram are placed into the starting setup and are not shuffled into the Hero play deck.

For the first session, leave the sideboard closed. The fixed baseline is enough to begin learning; you do not need to add Pallando, a second Wizard, specialist characters or optional Hazard swaps.

14. The Wind Throne — **Hero version**
15. Wellinghall

**Do not use Dead Marshes.** The historical Challenge Deck C list includes a Minion-alignment Dead Marshes site. A Wizard/Hero Location deck must use Hero sites, so the Academy replaces that entry with the Hero version of The Wind Throne. Do not accidentally buy or use the separate Minion version of The Wind Throne either.

## 4. Assemble the physical piles

1. Put the 36 Hero cards together and shuffle them as the Hero play deck.
2. Put the 30 Hazard cards together and shuffle them as a separate Hazard deck.
3. Keep all 15 Hero site cards in a separate, unshuffled Location deck.
4. Place the five starting Dwarves at Rivendell.
5. Put one Cram with Thorin II and the other with Óin.
6. Keep the Hero and Hazard discard piles separate.
7. Leave the sideboard out of the first game.

The Hazard cards never go into the Hero play deck. The Hero hand begins at five cards under the solo procedure; do not use the multiplayer eight-card hand.

## 5. Sleeves and storage — practical, not a game rule

Different sleeve colours are a useful safeguard against mixing the piles after play:

- One opaque colour for the **Hero play deck**.
- A different opaque colour for the **Hazard deck**.
- A third colour, or clear sleeves, for the **Location deck**.
- Keep the starting characters and Cram in a separate labelled section until setup.

This is only a physical organisation method; sleeve colours have no rules effect. If you already use one sleeve system, labelled deck boxes and separate dividers are equally suitable.

## 6. If a card is missing

Use the quantities on this page and the full [Hero Deck Construction guide]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}) and [Hazard Deck Construction guide]({{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }}). Mark a missing card as **to obtain** and buy the genuine single. Do not silently substitute another card or change the count: that would create a different test deck and make the Academy's worked examples harder to follow.

The 30 Resource list contains **one** Men of Dorwinion. Some online transcriptions duplicate that entry; use the checked 30-card Academy list rather than adding a second copy.
