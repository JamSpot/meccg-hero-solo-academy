## Alternative Wizard — not a backup in the same game

- Saruman

The active Academy uses **Gandalf** as its Wizard; the historical Challenge Deck C identity is associated with Pallando. Saruman is an alternative Wizard for a separate deck build, not a second Wizard to play alongside Gandalf. Only one Wizard may be in play at a time, and losing your Wizard ends the game.
