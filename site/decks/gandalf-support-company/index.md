# Gandalf and the Support Company

The active Academy is **Gandalf-led**. The historical Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C is Pallando-led, but Pallando is the historical benchmark, not the Wizard used in this Academy.

This lesson adapts the useful two-company idea to Gandalf without importing Pallando's card abilities.

## 1. The active Hero deck draft

The Academy keeps its locked **30-card Hero Resource list** and the five non-Wizard characters from the historical Dwarven Quest pool:

- Balin
- Boromir II
- Celeborn
- Fíli
- Gildor Inglorion

The active Wizard is **one Gandalf card**. That gives a working **36-card Hero play deck**: 30 Resources + five non-Wizard characters + Gandalf. The solo rules require a play deck of at least 30 cards, so the Academy does not need to retain the historical three Pallando copies simply to reach 38.

This is an Academy Gandalf-led adaptation, not the exact historical Challenge Deck C character list. The original Challenge Deck C includes Pallando ×3; those cards are not part of the active Gandalf build. Do not shuffle Pallando into the active deck as extra characters while Gandalf is your Wizard.

The 30 Resource list and 30 Hazard list remain the fixed teaching baseline. The starting Dwarves and two Cram items remain separate at setup.

## 2. Gandalf's actual card text matters

Gandalf is a Wizard with:

- Warrior, Scout, Sage and Diplomat skills;
- direct influence 10;
- prowess 6 and body 9;
- home site: **Any Haven**;
- **+1 to all of his corruption checks**;
- the ability to tap to test a Gold Ring.

A Wizard may be brought into play at his home site or at Rivendell, provided you do not already have a Wizard in play. Gandalf's home site is Any Haven, so he can be played at Grey Havens, Lórien or another Haven. He does not need influence to control himself.

Two important differences from Pallando:

- **Gandalf does not give you an extra card in hand.** The Academy's Hero hand target remains five.
- **Gandalf's corruption checks are harder by one.** Do not carry Pallando's hand-size advice into the Gandalf build.

The current 30 Resource list has no Gold Ring, so Gandalf's ring-testing ability is not used in the first Book of Mazarbul lessons. It becomes relevant only if a Gold Ring resource is added to a later build.

## 3. Keep Gandalf away from the first Dwarf expedition?

You have two legal broad approaches once Gandalf is in your hand and no Wizard is in play.

### Plan A — Gandalf joins the Dwarves

Play Gandalf at Rivendell and place him in Thorin's company.

- Five Dwarves + Gandalf = **six characters**.
- The company moves to Moria.
- Moria's normal Hazard draw number is 3, so the solo draw is **2 + (2 × 3) = 8**.
- The base Hazard Limit is **6**, not five.
- Gandalf is exposed to the Moria journey and its automatic attack of four Orc strikes at 7 prowess.

Gandalf's skills and prowess help the company, but the higher Hazard Limit gives the Hazard side one more ordinary play slot. Gandalf is also exposed to the site's danger, and his +1 corruption-check modifier is a real weakness.

### Plan B — Gandalf forms a separate company

Play Gandalf at **Grey Havens**, his legal Haven home site, as a separate one-character company. Move him to Lórien using Starter Movement; the two Haven site cards provide the connecting path. At the same time, Thorin's Dwarves can move Rivendell → Moria using Region Movement.

**Thorin's Dwarves**

- Five characters.
- Rivendell → Moria via Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.
- Moria normal Hazard draw: 3.
- Solo draw: **8**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.

**Gandalf**

- One Wizard.
- Grey Havens → Lórien using Haven-to-Haven Starter Movement.
- Lórien is a Haven, so use the site of origin's normal Hazard draw number.
- Grey Havens normal Hazard draw: 2.
- Solo draw: **6**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **2**.

| Company | Solo Hazard draw | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Thorin's five Dwarves to Moria | 8 | 5 |
| Gandalf alone to Lórien | 6 | 2 |
| **Total** | **14 cards across two phases** | **Separate limits: 5 and 2** |

The total of 14 is drawn from the shared 30-card Hazard deck, but it is not one combined hand and the limits do not combine. Resolve each company's Movement/Hazard Phase separately. Check each Hazard against that company's own route, targets, condition and timing.

This plan keeps Gandalf away from Moria's automatic attack, but it does not make him safe: he faces a separate six-card solo Hazard draw alone. A one-character Wizard company still has a Hazard Limit of two.

## 4. The support-company role

The historical Challenge Deck C Player Guide describes Pallando building a company at Lórien and pursuing allies and the Book. The Academy borrows the useful *company-development idea*, not Pallando's card text or a claim that the old guide was written for Gandalf.

A Gandalf support company can:

- recruit suitable characters at a Haven using Gandalf's direct influence;
- travel to **Wellinghall** to play Quickbeam;
- travel to **Goblin-gate** to play Gollum if the route and company condition make that worthwhile;
- travel to **Moria** to acquire Book of Mazarbul if Thorin's company has not done so;
- later help position for Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold and the Blue Mountain Dwarves faction.

Do not assume all these objectives fit into one short expedition. Check each destination's nearest Haven, the legal movement type, the site path and the solo Hazard draw before moving.

## 5. Sideboard access — Blue Mountain Dwarves

Blue Mountain Dwarves begins in the historical sideboard. During the Organization Phase, a Wizard in play may tap to use the normal sideboard procedure:

- bring up to five Resource and/or Character cards from the sideboard to the discard pile; or
- if the play deck has at least five cards, bring one Resource or Character card directly from the sideboard into the play deck and shuffle.

The faction does not go directly to your hand or into play. You must draw or retrieve it legally, reach a site where it is playable, and make the normal influence attempt.

If Gandalf taps for sideboard access, he remains tapped. A tapped character faces strikes with the normal tapped-character prowess consequence. Consider using the sideboard action while safely at a Haven and moving on a later turn.

Saruman in the historical sideboard is an **alternative Wizard for a separate build**, not a backup Wizard to play alongside Gandalf. You may have only one Wizard in play; losing your Wizard ends the game.

## 6. Route checks for Gandalf

- **Grey Havens → Lórien:** Starter Movement; both sites are Havens and their site cards list a path to one another. Lórien is a Haven destination, so use Grey Havens' normal Hazard draw number 2; solo draw 6.
- **Lórien → Wellinghall:** Starter Movement because Wellinghall lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Wellinghall's normal Hazard draw is 1; solo draw 4.
- **Lórien → Moria:** Starter Movement because Moria lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Moria's normal Hazard draw is 3; solo draw 8.
- **Wellinghall → Rivendell:** do not assume a direct one-turn Starter Movement. Wellinghall's nearest Haven is Lórien; use a legal intermediate Haven journey or prove a Region Movement route of four or fewer consecutive regions.

The site path is used for Hazard keying; it does not replace the movement rules. Use the [Free Movement guide]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) and [Expedition Planner]({{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}) before declaring the route.

## 7. Book of Mazarbul and Gandalf's corruption penalty

Kíli remains the recommended default Book bearer for the starting Dwarf company. He has a -1 modifier to all his corruption checks. Gandalf, by contrast, has +1 to all his corruption checks.

Do not move the Book to Gandalf merely because he is the Wizard. If Gandalf carries the Book, his own +1 modifier makes the storage check harder. Use the [Book bearer and corruption-support lesson]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}) to calculate the actual check and decide which characters can safely tap to support it.
