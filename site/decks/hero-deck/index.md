- **FIGHT:** Risky Blow, The Dwarves Are Upon You!

- **COMPRESS:** Bounty of the Hoard, Lucky Search, Thror's Map

Thror's Map is a stored-value card: its strategic value can be greater unplayed because it can compress a future expedition.
