# Hero Deck Construction — Beginner Guide

## The exact teaching card list

For **Game 1 and the first Academy lessons**, use the historical **Dwarven Quest (Pallando) Challenge Deck C** as our verified teaching baseline. The 30-card table below is the **Resource portion**, not the whole shuffled Hero play deck. Under the published solitaire procedure, Hazards are kept in a separate deck; resources and character/Wizard cards remain together in the Hero play deck. For the historical Challenge Deck C that means 38 cards. The active Academy adapts the character/Wizard portion for Gandalf: **36 Hero play-deck cards: 30 Resources + five non-Wizard characters + Gandalf**. The 30-card Resource list remains locked. The base Hero hand target is five. Under the solo rules, reset it after each company's Movement/Hazard Phase and again at End of Turn; the hand may temporarily exceed five after a movement draw. Apply active hand-size modifiers when resetting. The Hazard deck is separate and its cards never enter the Hero hand. See the [Solo Deck Cycle Lab]({{ '/scenarios/solo-deck-cycle-lab/' | relative_url }}) for a worked example. The hand size does not change either deck count.

This is a genuine-card list: **no proxies are intended**. If you do not own a card, mark it as a card to obtain rather than substituting a random card.

**The 36-card version is the current working first-play build.** The historical Challenge Deck C has 38 cards in its Resource-plus-character/Wizard play deck, but the printed solitaire rules require at least 30 cards, not exactly 38. The Academy's 30 Resources + five non-Wizard characters + one Gandalf is legal and is not waiting on two extra cards. Keep it unchanged through the first three playtests; only consider expansion if the playtest log identifies a real weakness.

### Wizard-copy rule and optional 37-card variant

The current 36-card Gandalf list is a valid working baseline, not the only legal size. The current **Council of Elrond consolidated rules (section 1.5)** allow up to **three avatar cards**, with any combination permitted except three different avatars. For a Wizard player, this allows up to three Wizard-avatar cards, including three copies of the same Wizard. Only one avatar can be in play at a time, and if it is eliminated you cannot reveal another avatar. The original printed *The Wizards* rulebook says up to two Wizard cards; this Academy follows the current CoE consolidated rule. A second Gandalf makes a 37-card variant; three Gandalf copies make a 38-card variant. These are legal deck constructions, not required upgrades.

The historical Challenge Deck C player aid lists Pallando ×3. This is consistent with current CoE rule 1.5 allowing up to three avatar cards (provided they are not three different avatars), although the original printed *The Wizards* rulebook contains an older two-Wizard limit. Under the current CoE consolidated rule, the original 38-card Pallando build is legal. The Academy's one-Gandalf list is a deliberate teaching adaptation, not a correction of an illegal Pallando list.

Before buying or changing Wizard cards, read the [Gandalf-versus-Pallando Wizard Choice Workshop]({{ '/decks/wizard-choice-workshop/' | relative_url }}). It compares Gandalf's corruption protection with Pallando's extra hand-size ability and gives the opening-hand odds for one or two copies. The active first-play baseline remains one Gandalf until the controlled comparison is complete.

### Starting-company legality check

The five starting Dwarves have a combined mind of **20**, exactly the normal starting-company ceiling. The Challenge Deck C player aid's direct-influence assignments also fit the printed character values:

| Character | Mind | Printed direct influence | Starting assignment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Thorin II | 8 | 2, with +2 against Dwarves and Dwarf factions | Controls Dori and Kíli |
| Dori | 1 | 0 | Thorin's follower |
| Kíli | 3 | 0 | Thorin's follower |
| Glóin | 5 | 2, with +1 against Dwarves and Dwarf factions | Controls Óin |
| Óin | 3 | 0 | Glóin's follower |
| **Total** | **20** |  | **Five characters** |

The control arithmetic is exact: Thorin has 2 + 2 = **4 direct influence against Dwarves**, matching Dori (1) + Kíli (3) = **4 mind**. Glóin has 2 + 1 = **3 direct influence against Dwarves**, matching Óin's **3 mind**. The race-specific direct-influence bonus is applied once, not once per follower. Under the control rules, Thorin and Glóin are the two non-follower characters, so their mind values (8 + 5 = **13**) are deducted from general influence; Dori, Kíli and Óin are accounted for against their leaders' direct influence. The five characters still count together for the **20-mind starting-company cap**. See [Official Rules 2.II.2.2.2 and 2.II.2.2.4](https://councilofelrond.org/rules/).

This is the original Challenge Deck C starting-company arrangement, retained for the Academy's Dwarven teaching company. The five starting Dwarves are set up from the starting character pool; they are **not** shuffled into the 36-card Hero play deck. For current card text and rules, use the [Council of Elrond official card database](https://www.councilofelrond.org/cards/) and [official rules](https://councilofelrond.org/rules/).

- Fíli

**Why Gandalf, not Pallando?** The Academy's current working Wizard choice is Gandalf. The historical Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C is Pallando-based and lists historical Pallando ×3, but those three Pallando cards are historical reference only; do not shuffle them into the active Gandalf-led deck. Gandalf is a unique Wizard with Warrior/Scout/Sage/Diplomat skills, DI 10, P/B 6/9, home site Any Haven, +1 to all his corruption checks, and the ability to tap to test a Gold Ring.

A Wizard may be brought into play at his home site or Rivendell if you do not already have a Wizard in play. Gandalf's home site is Any Haven. He does **not** increase your hand size: the Academy hand target remains five unless the Sage bearer uses the Book's own ability. His +1 corruption modifier is a bonus; Kíli's -1 is a penalty. ### What the five support characters actually add

The starting Dwarves already cover several skills, but **none is a Sage**:

- All five are Warriors.
- Kíli is the starting Scout.
- Óin is the starting Ranger.
- Glóin is the starting Diplomat.
- Sage access comes from Gandalf, Balin or Celeborn in the active 36-card deck.

This matters for Book of Mazarbûl and the deck's Sage-dependent Resource options. Gandalf is the most efficient all-round Sage, but he is not guaranteed in the opening hand.

**Recruitment recommendation for this Dwarf plan:**

1. **Gandalf** is the best all-round addition when drawn: he supplies Sage, Scout and Diplomat as well as his Wizard abilities, and his +1 corruption modifier makes him the preferred Book bearer.
2. If Gandalf is not yet in play, **Balin** is the strongest non-Wizard backup for this company. He supplies Sage without abandoning the Dwarf theme and is a better fit than Celeborn for the Iron Hill / Dwarf-faction plan.
3. **Fíli** is the cheapest combat-oriented recruit; **Gildor** is the stronger Orc-fighting option. Both duplicate a skill already present, so choose them for their combat text rather than skill coverage.
4. **Celeborn** is a Sage alternative, but uses six of the seven currently unused GI and his special influence bonus is irrelevant unless influencing Galadriel. **Boromir II** is a sound Warrior with a useful personal corruption modifier, but does not fill a missing skill.

The normal Organization Phase allows **one character action per turn**: play one Hero character or your avatar, or discard a non-avatar character. You cannot play Gandalf and Balin in the same Organization Phase. Once an avatar is in play, the rules also restrict where you may play further characters: they must be played at the avatar's current site or under direct influence with an existing company. Keep track of Gandalf's current site when recruiting. If he travels with the Dwarves, you can still play a new character at the company's current site; if he is elsewhere, the avatar's location and the existing companies' direct influence determine your legal options.

The five-card opening hand contains at least one of the three Sage-capable cards (Gandalf, Balin or Celeborn) only about **37.0%** of the time. Gandalf alone appears in the first five about **13.9%** of the time. Treat the Book's Sage benefit as a useful option, not something the opening hand guarantees.

**Company-size reminder:** the five starting Dwarves plus Gandalf and one recruited support character make seven characters. Companies at a Haven have no maximum size; a company away from a Haven may contain no more than seven characters. The base Hazard Limit is the current company size, or two if the company has fewer than two characters, and is fixed for that Movement/Hazard Phase. So the five-Dwarf company has a base limit of 5; adding Gandalf makes it 6; adding one support character as well makes it 7. A further character must wait, remain in another company, or require a reorganisation before the company moves away from a Haven.

## Book of Mazarbul — what the expedition is actually securing

The current card database gives Book of Mazarbul these important facts:

- **Unique Special Item**, with **2 corruption points**.
- **Only playable at Moria**.
- Worth **1 marshalling point while in play**.
- It may be stored at a **Dwarf-hold for 5 marshalling points**.
- **Storage timing and risk:** during the Organization Phase while the company is at a qualifying Dwarf-hold, the bearer makes the normal corruption check required for storing an item. The Book has two corruption points, so this is a real risk. If the check succeeds, it goes into the marshalling-point pile for **5 MP instead of its 1 MP in-play value**; do not add the values together. Because arrival happens during the Movement/Hazard Phase, the Organization Phase has already passed: store it at the **next turn's Organization Phase**, not during the arrival-turn Site Phase. You may still declare the next movement during that same Organization Phase after resolving storage.
- If its bearer is a **Sage**, you may tap the Book during your Organization Phase to increase your hand size by one until your next Untap Phase. This works only while the Book is borne. When stored, the Book moves to your marshalling-point pile and is no longer borne, so its corruption points no longer burden a character and its Sage hand-size ability is no longer active. **Do not call a stored card “removed from play”**: it remains in the marshalling-point pile.

For the later storage check, **a positive modifier helps and a negative modifier hurts**: corruption-check modifiers are added to the 2D6 roll. Dori, Kíli and Óin each receive **-1**, which is a penalty. Thorin II and Glóin have no printed corruption-check modifier. Gandalf has **+1**, which is a bonus. If Gandalf is in the company, he is the safest default Book bearer: with only the Book's 2 CP, his lowest possible modified roll is 3, already greater than 2. He is also a Sage, so he can use the Book's Sage-only hand-size ability. If Gandalf is not in the company, consider Thorin II or Glóin rather than choosing Kíli simply for his Scout skill. This is a recommendation, not a rule: calculate the actual bearer’s total corruption points and all check modifiers.

The starting company also has **Óin as its Ranger**. If the Hazard player uses River on a site the company has moved to, the current erratum lets the company tap a Ranger by the beginning of the Site Phase to avoid doing nothing during that phase. If Óin is already tapped or otherwise unable to tap, the company may lose its Site Phase options. This is a real reason to track his condition.

The five starting Dwarves in this teaching company have Warrior, Scout, Ranger and Diplomat skills, but **none is a Sage**. The Book's hand-size ability is not available while one of these Dwarves bears it. Gandalf is a Sage: if he later bears the Book, he may tap the Book during the Organization Phase to increase the Hero hand size by one until the next Untap Phase. This is the Book's ability, not a hand-size ability on Gandalf's Wizard card.

This creates two distinct objectives:

1. **Game 1 — ACQUIRE:** reach Moria and play Book of Mazarbul if the company can safely complete the site attempt.
2. **Later campaign — STORE:** move the Book to a Dwarf-hold, such as Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, to realise its 5 MP stored value.

The second objective is a new expedition, not an automatic continuation of the first. The direct Moria-to-Blue-Mountain route contains five regions, so it cannot be completed in one turn under the normal four-region limit. You do **not** have to use a Haven as the intermediate stop: the fixed 15-site deck contains Goblin-gate, allowing a two-leg non-Haven route (Moria → Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold).

## Book bearer: plan the storage corruption check

The Book has **2 corruption points**. A corruption check is **2D6 plus the character's check modifiers and any support**; a positive modifier helps, while a negative modifier hurts. Gandalf has +1 and is a Sage, making him the strongest default bearer when he is in the company. If Gandalf is absent, Thorin II and Glóin have no printed check modifier; Dori, Kíli and Óin each have -1. Kíli's Scout skill is useful, but his -1 is a penalty, not a benefit. The bearer is a strategic choice, not a fixed rule. The Book's Sage hand-size ability is **not passive**: the Sage must bear it and you must tap the Book during the Organization Phase. The +1 hand-size increase lasts only until the next Untap Phase. Do not raise the five-card target merely because Gandalf or another Sage is present. Once the Book is stored, it is no longer borne and its Sage ability no longer applies. The [Three-Turn Expedition — Worked Solo Run]({{ '/scenarios/three-turn-expedition/' | relative_url }}) shows the Book's first-turn acquisition, the next-Organization-Phase hand-size action and the final storage check in one sequence.

You can improve the storage check by tapping other **untapped characters in the same company** before the roll. Each helper gives the bearer +1 to that check. The bearer does not tap merely to make the storage check.

### Kíli carrying only the Book: a risk example

This is a worked risk example, not the recommended bearer. Assume Kíli has only the Book's 2 corruption points, his printed -1 modifier, and no other check modifiers.

| Other characters tapped to support | Final check modifier | Lowest possible modified 2D6 result | Outcome |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | -1 | 1 | Fails on natural rolls of 2 or 3 (3/36). |
| 1 | 0 | 2 | Fails only on a natural roll of 2 (1/36). |
| 2 | +1 | 3 | Guaranteed success against 2 CP. |

A corruption check succeeds only when the modified roll is **greater than** the character's total corruption points. For a Hero, the traditional printed categories discard the character on a result equal to or one below the CP total, and eliminate it on a lower result. **Current Council of Elrond rule 7.1 also contains a separate “modified roll is less than two” elimination clause**, which overlaps with the one-below category when the modified result is exactly 1. For the Kíli example above, a natural 2 becomes 1 after his -1 modifier: the current CoE wording treats that as elimination, while the older printed categories treat it as a discard. The failure probability is unchanged. Use the [Book Storage & Corruption Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/book-storage-corruption-workshop/' | relative_url }}) for the exact edge case. Always include every corruption point on cards the bearer controls, not just the Book. A +1 modifier raises the roll and is helpful; a -1 modifier lowers it and is harmful.

**The tactical price:** helpers tapped during the Organization Phase remain tapped when the company moves that turn. A tapped character has -1 prowess when facing a strike, so tapping everyone for a guaranteed check can make the next Hazard phase more dangerous. Decide whether to use one helper, two helpers, more, or postpone movement. If Despair of the Heart is still on Kíli, add its 2 corruption points before calculating; first consider whether he can attempt to remove it during this Organization Phase. Despair of the Heart cannot be played on Gandalf because he is a Wizard.

Complete the [Corruption Check Lab]({{ '/scenarios/corruption-check-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/corruption-check-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}) to practise choosing the bearer and calculating the risk. Then use the [Book Storage & Corruption Workshop]({{ '/scenarios/book-storage-corruption-workshop/' | relative_url }}) for the full storage timing, Despair, Fellowship, A Friend or Three and support-tap interactions.

Official basis: [Council of Elrond rules — corruption checks and support](https://www.councilofelrond.org/rules/) and the [official card database](https://www.councilofelrond.org/cards/).

| **Wormsbane** | 4 | 2 | +2 prowess to a maximum of 9; against a Dragon or Drake strike, +4 prowess to a maximum of 12 and -2 to the strike's body. | Its strongest bonus is specialist protection against Dragons and Drakes. It is not a special bonus against Moria's Orcs. |
| **Thrór's Map** | — | 1 | Discard it to untap a site with a Dragon automatic attack. | It has no printed MP value. Moria and Goblin-gate have Orc automatic attacks, so neither qualifies. |

**Corruption points and check modifiers are different.** Corruption points are added to a character's CP total; a positive or negative corruption-check modifier changes the roll. Fellowship's +1 is a check modifier, not a CP. Durin's Axe, Bow of Dragon-horn, Enruned Shield, Hauberk, Magical Harp, Wormsbane and Thrór's Map carry the CP values shown above. A bearer carrying several items must add their CP values before making a corruption check.

For the first Moria expedition, this changes the combat decision:

- **Enruned Shield** can make one strike against its bearer ineffectual.
- **Hauberk of Bright Mail** raises the bearer's body.
- **Durin's Axe** raises a Dwarf's prowess substantially, but adds three CP.
- **Bow of Dragon-horn** cannot reduce Moria's Orc automatic attack because it only modifies a Hazard creature attack not keyed to a site.
- **Wormsbane** is not a special answer to Orcs; its premium bonus is against Dragons and Drakes.
- **Thrór's Map** is not useful at Moria because Moria has an Orc automatic attack.

The best item is not always the one with the highest MP value. Decide who will carry it, what threat it answers, and whether that character can safely bear the resulting corruption burden.

### Hundreds of Butterflies — the real solo trade-off

The solo procedure's draw is calculated before the Movement/Hazard Phase card play. If Moria's normal Hazard draw number is 3, the Hazard side draws **8** cards. The five-Dwarf company's base Hazard Limit is **5**.

If you play **Hundreds of Butterflies** on a moving character, it untaps that character and increases the Hazard Limit against the company by one. The Hazard side can therefore play up to **six** limit-counting Hazards from the eight already drawn, if six legal plays exist. It does **not** cause two more cards to be drawn. If Twilight is also drawn and has a legal Environment target, Twilight remains a separate no-Hazard-Limit play.

This is the card's central bargain: regain an untapped character now, but give the opposition another legal slot. Practise the full arithmetic in the [Hazard Draw & Limit Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/' | relative_url }}).

### The actual learning sequence

**PLAN → PREPARE → AVOID → FIGHT → ACQUIRE → RECOVER → REASSESS**

- **Plan:** choose the mission and route before committing to a site.
- **Prepare:** use Haven-only resources such as Fellowship at the correct time.
- **Avoid:** preserve a Scout and Concealment for a genuinely dangerous attack; do not assume Not at Home works against every creature.
- **Fight:** use the Dwarf-wide bonus or a one-strike bonus only when the actual attack justifies the body risk.
- **Acquire:** attempt the Book, item, ally or faction that supports the current mission.
- **Recover:** use site-specific item effects and Smoke Rings only when their conditions are met.
- **Reassess:** after the site phase, decide whether to recover, continue or change the next objective.

### The hand-management test

Before discarding a card, classify it as one of three things:

1. **Needed now:** supports the next movement, site attempt or defence.
2. **Needed later:** depends on a future site, company state or objective.
3. **Low value for this plan:** unlikely to help the current campaign line.

Do not discard a card just because it is not playable this turn. Equally, do not keep every card indefinitely: the next expedition should determine what is worth holding.

## Plan the Hero hand by mission stage

The teaching deck is not a pile of cards that are all equally urgent. Before moving, label each useful card by the decision it supports. This is a planning aid; the printed text determines whether a card can actually be played.

| Mission stage | Ask yourself | Common mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Before movement** | Which card improves this trip, which protects against a likely problem, and which only matters at a later site? | Keeping every card “just in case” without a named future use. |
| **During movement / Hazards** | Is this resource legal now, and does it address the threat that is actually present? | Spending a defensive resource on a hypothetical threat while ignoring the current state. |
| **At the destination** | What does the objective require, and what resources or characters are still available after Hazards? | Treating arrival as an instruction to attempt the site regardless of wounds and taps. |
| **After the objective** | Which cards support the next planned leg, recovery, or a new objective? | Continuing the original route after the mission has changed. |

### The three-question keep test

For each card you are tempted to keep, finish these sentences:

1. **I am keeping this because…**
2. **I expect to use it when…**
3. **If that situation does not arise, I will reconsider it when…**

This prevents two opposite errors: discarding a situational card before its use matters, and hoarding cards with no plan. Do not infer a card's effect from its title; check the physical card text and the current rules.

## Opening-hand practice — plan the mission, not just the cards

Use this short exercise before Game 1, then repeat it after you have played once. Your solitaire hand size is five cards.

**Mission:** start at Rivendell and plan the expedition to acquire Book of Mazarbul at Moria.

1. **Name the objective.** Book of Mazarbul is the reason for this trip; do not let an optional item or ally automatically replace the mission.
2. **Sort the five-card hand.** Mark each card as (a) movement/planning, (b) avoid or survive a threat, (c) fight if needed, (d) a site-specific or later objective, or (e) uncertain until you read the card.
3. **Check the actual text.** A card's category does not prove it is playable now. Check its timing, target, site and any conditions.
4. **Keep a credible fallback.** Do not spend every defensive option on an imagined threat before you know what the Hazard side actually plays; equally, do not save a card that is the correct legal answer to a real threat.
5. **Plan the next hand.** After movement, site resolution and the normal hand adjustment for the selected solo rules, decide what you need for the next expedition rather than hoarding everything.

### Worked sorting example

Imagine your hand contains **Great-road, Concealment, Book of Mazarbul, Risky Blow, and Lucky Search**. Do not assume all five should be played immediately:

| Card | First question | Planning category |
|---|---|---|
| Great-road | Is the company at a Haven at the end of Organization, and is the extra Hazard draw worth a special return to that Haven? | Special return-to-Haven option; not general free movement |
| Concealment | Is a Scout available to tap to cancel one attack? Thorin II and Kíli are Scouts. | One-attack response; tapping the Scout is the cost |
| Book of Mazarbul | Is the company at the right site, and can the resource be played under its printed conditions? | Current mission objective |
| Risky Blow | Is there a relevant attack, and is the risk worth taking for the objective? | Fight only when justified |
| Lucky Search | Is the company at a Shadow-hold or Dark-hold, and is the Scout worth risking on its uncancellable search strike? | Risky site-phase item search; it cannot find the Special Item Book |

This is a **card-reading exercise, not a scripted play sequence**. Use the text on your physical cards to decide what is legal. If a card is not useful now, record whether it has a credible later use before discarding it.

## Gildor's second-Ranger role on the Wind Throne route

The support-character table above correctly notes that Gildor duplicates Óin's Ranger skill in the starting company. That does not make the skill redundant on every route.

The Wind Throne route can expose the company to **two copies of River**. The current ruling requires one Ranger to be tapped for each River; one Ranger cannot satisfy both copies. The starting company has only Óin as a Ranger, and Gandalf is not a Ranger. If two Rivers are played on the site, Óin can satisfy one, but the company's Site Phase is wholly skipped unless it has a second untapped Ranger. It cannot enter the site or face the site's automatic attack while the River requirement remains unsatisfied.

Gildor Inglorion is already one of the five non-Wizard characters in the active 36-card Hero deck. He is a Warrior/Ranger with mind 4, +2 prowess against Orcs, and costs four of the starting company's seven unused general-influence points if controlled by general influence. If he is legally recruited before the Wind Throne expedition and remains untapped, he can provide the second Ranger. His +2 prowess against Orcs is also relevant to the site's three Orc strikes at 7.

This is a **situational recruitment reason**, not a general recommendation to add Gildor to every company. If both Rivers are present, tapping Óin and Gildor satisfies them and allows the Site Phase to proceed. Both Rangers are then tapped before the company enters and faces The Wind Throne's three Orc strikes at 7. Gildor's Orc bonus partly offsets his tapped penalty, but does not remove it. Also, Gildor is not a complete answer to a Seized by Terror + two-River line: if Seized splits Óin, only Gildor remains and the two-River requirement is still unsatisfied. The Hero player must weigh access to Site Phase actions against the tapped-character combat penalty.

Practise the complete interaction in the [Shadow-land Route & Seized by Terror Lab]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/' | relative_url }}) and its [model answer]({{ '/scenarios/shadowland-route-lab/answer-key/' | relative_url }}). The active Hero deck remains 36 cards; no character substitution is proposed.
