# Pallando and the Support Company

The five Dwarves are the Challenge Deck C starting company. **Pallando is not one of those five starting characters.** He is the Wizard avatar represented by the Pallando cards in the Hero play deck. Once he is in play, he gives you a second strategic option: a separate support company that can develop at other sites while Thorin's company pursues Moria.

This is an advanced extension of Game 1, not an extra requirement for your first expedition.

## 1. What is actually in the Hero play deck?

The historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C list has:

- **30 Hero Resources**;
- **eight character/Wizard cards:** Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli, Gildor Inglorion and three Pallando cards;
- a separate **30-card Hazard list**;
- a separate Location deck and sideboard.

That gives the Academy's **38-card Hero play deck**. The three Pallando cards are Wizard-avatar cards, not three extra followers. A Wizard may be played only when you do not already have a Wizard in play. The three-avatar deck allowance permits the published three-copy Pallando combination; it does not permit three Wizards to be in play together.

Pallando's printed home site is **Grey Havens**. A Wizard may be brought into play at his home site or at Rivendell. He does not use influence to control himself. Once revealed, he acts as a character and can use his direct influence to control followers.

## 2. Why Pallando matters

Pallando has four skills — Warrior, Ranger, Sage and Diplomat — direct influence 10, prowess 6 and body 9. His card also allows you to keep one more card than normal in your hand.

The Academy's solo base hand size is five. While Pallando is in play, his printed hand-size ability means your hand target is **six**, not five. Apply that at each normal hand reset while his ability is active. His opponent-facing instruction to discard cards face-up has no practical opponent to affect in this solo format.

Do not treat Pallando as expendable. A Wizard's elimination ends the game. A one-character Wizard company is legal, but it is exposed: its normal Hazard Limit is still two, and the Wizard can be targeted by legal Hazards.

## 3. The published Challenge Deck C plan

The historical Challenge Decks Player Guide gives Pallando a distinct job:

1. Move him to **Lórien** and build a company there using characters drawn from the Hero play deck.
2. Use that company to play the two allies — **Gollum at Goblin-gate** and **Quickbeam at Wellinghall** — and to play Book of Mazarbul at Moria if Thorin's company has not already acquired it.
3. After playing Quickbeam, reposition Pallando's company toward Rivendell.
4. Tap Pallando to bring **Blue Mountain Dwarves** from the sideboard into the play deck or discard pile.
5. Position a company to reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, store the Book and play the faction.

This is the published deck's strategic plan, not a compulsory script. If the Dwarves acquire the Book early, Pallando's company can focus on the allies, faction and future positioning instead of duplicating the Book mission.

## 4. A legal first two-company example

Assume Pallando is in your hand and you decide to reveal him during the Organization Phase.

### Company A — Thorin's Dwarves: Rivendell to Moria

- Starting company: Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin.
- Movement: Region Movement.
- Site path: **Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**.
- Moria's normal Hazard draw number: **3**.
- Academy solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 3) = 8 cards**.
- Company size and base Hazard Limit: **5**.

The site path has three regions, including both endpoints, and is within the four-region limit. Check the adjacency against your physical Region cards before declaring it.

### Company B — Pallando: Grey Havens to Lórien

Pallando can be played at his home site, **Grey Havens**, as a separate one-character company. He can then use Starter Movement to move to Lórien: both are Havens, and their site cards provide a path to one another.

- Origin: Grey Havens.
- Destination: Lórien.
- Movement: Starter Movement, using the printed Haven-to-Haven site path.
- Lórien is a Haven, so the normal Hazard draw is taken from the **site of origin**, Grey Havens.
- Grey Havens' normal Hazard draw number: **2**.
- Academy solo Hazard draw: **2 + (2 × 2) = 6 cards**.
- Company size: **1 Wizard**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **2** (the minimum).

This route uses Pallando's printed home site and avoids needing a second Rivendell site card just to create a separate company. If you instead want Pallando to form a separate company at Rivendell while the Dwarves remain there, you need another physical Rivendell Haven card. The normal rules allow multiple Haven cards in the Location deck; do not invent or proxy a duplicate if you do not own one.

### Do not combine the two Hazard phases

If both companies move in the same turn, they have **separate Movement/Hazard Phases**:

| Company | Solo Hazard draw | Base Hazard Limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Thorin's five Dwarves to Moria | 8 | 5 |
| Pallando alone to Lórien | 6 | 2 |
| **Total cards drawn / separate limits** | **14** | **5 and 2** |

The total of 14 is drawn from the shared 30-card Hazard deck in two separate company phases. It is **not** one combined 14-card hand, and the limits do not combine into one limit of seven. Resolve one company at a time, check legality against that company's own route and condition, and apply that company's own limit. The solo procedure directs the Hazard side to play as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within that company's limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination.

This is a demanding opening. The separate company can increase your options, but it also makes the Hazard deck cycle faster and exposes Pallando to his own Hazard draw.

## 5. What if Pallando joins the Dwarves instead?

If Pallando joins Thorin's five-character company and they move together to Moria:

- the company has six characters;
- the base Hazard Limit becomes **6**;
- the Moria solo draw remains **8**, because the destination's normal Hazard draw number is still 3;
- the Wizard is now exposed to the Dwarves' Moria journey and its automatic attack.

This is not automatically safer just because the company is larger. The extra character may help in combat, but it also raises the Hazard Limit and puts your Wizard in the same danger as the Dwarves.

## 6. Sideboard timing — getting Blue Mountain Dwarves

**Blue Mountain Dwarves** begins in the Challenge Deck C sideboard, not in the 38-card Hero play deck.

During the Organization Phase, a Wizard in play may tap to use the normal sideboard procedure. You may either:

- bring up to five Resource and/or Character cards from the sideboard to your discard pile; or
- if your play deck has at least five cards, bring one Resource or Character card directly from the sideboard into your play deck and shuffle.

The faction does **not** go straight into your hand or into play. You still need to draw it or retrieve it through a legal card effect, then reach a site where it is playable and make the normal influence attempt.

Tapping Pallando has a cost: he remains tapped if his company moves that turn and will have the normal tapped-character consequences when facing strikes. Consider using his sideboard action while he is safely at a Haven, then moving on a later turn, rather than automatically tapping him immediately before a dangerous journey.

## 7. Route choices for Pallando's company

Use the printed site cards, not a guessed shortcut:

- **Grey Havens → Lórien:** Haven-to-Haven Starter Movement; both cards show the connecting site path.
- **Lórien → Wellinghall:** Starter Movement because Wellinghall lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Wellinghall's normal Hazard draw number is 1, so the solo draw is **4**; a lone Pallando company has a base Hazard Limit of 2.
- **Lórien → Moria:** Starter Movement because Moria lists Lórien as its nearest Haven. Moria's normal Hazard draw number is 3, so the solo draw is **8**; Pallando alone still has a base Hazard Limit of 2.
- **Wellinghall → Rivendell:** do not assume a direct Starter Movement. Wellinghall's nearest Haven is Lórien; use a legal intermediate Haven journey or prove a Region Movement route of four or fewer consecutive regions.

The site path is a Hazard-keying tool, not permission to skip movement requirements. Use the [Free Movement guide]({{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}) and [Expedition Planner]({{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}) before declaring the journey.

## 8. When to use the support company

| Situation | Sensible plan |
|---|---|
| Pallando is not in your hand | Do not stall the Dwarves' Book mission waiting for him. Continue the first expedition and play him when legally available. |
| Pallando is in hand and you have a useful second-company plan | Consider playing him at Grey Havens and moving to Lórien, accepting the separate six-card solo Hazard draw. |
| The Dwarves are already strong and you want to protect Pallando | Keep the Wizard away from the Moria automatic attack; do not merge him into the Dwarves just to increase prowess. |
| Pallando is tapped after sideboarding | Consider staying at the Haven for recovery/positioning rather than exposing a tapped Wizard to a new Hazard phase. |
| The Book is already acquired | Use Pallando's company to develop Quickbeam, Gollum, Blue Mountain Dwarves and the next campaign position; do not duplicate the Book objective without a reason. |
