# Historical Pallando Reference — Challenge Deck C

> **Historical reference only — not the active Academy build.** The conventional Hero Solo Academy is locked to **Gandalf** as its Wizard. Do not use Pallando as the Wizard in the active Gandalf-led deck, and do not shuffle Pallando ×3 into that deck.

The original Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C is Pallando-based. Its Player Guide describes a plan in which Pallando moves to Lórien, builds a support company from characters drawn from the play deck, and helps pursue the allies and Book of Mazarbul.

This page preserves that historical information so the Academy can compare the original published deck with its Gandalf-led adaptation.

## What the original Challenge Deck C contains

The historical list has 30 Resources and eight character/Wizard cards:

- Balin
- Boromir II
- Celeborn
- Fíli
- Gildor Inglorion
- Pallando ×3

The original 38-card Hero play deck is therefore 30 Resources plus those eight cards. This is the historical Pallando configuration, not the active Academy configuration.

## Pallando's printed ability

Pallando is a Warrior/Ranger/Sage/Diplomat Wizard with DI 10, prowess 6, body 9 and home site Grey Havens. His card lets his controlling player keep one more card than normal in hand. The opponent-facing face-up discard instruction has no practical opponent to affect in this solo format.

The Academy's Gandalf card is different: Gandalf has home site Any Haven and +1 to all of his corruption checks. Gandalf does not provide Pallando's hand-size bonus. Keep those card texts separate.

## The original Player Guide's support-company plan

The historical guide recommends:

1. Move Pallando to Lórien and develop a company there using characters drawn from the play deck.
2. Use that company to play Gollum at Goblin-gate and Quickbeam at Wellinghall.
3. Play Book of Mazarbul at Moria if the Dwarves have not already acquired it.
4. After Quickbeam, reposition Pallando's company toward Rivendell.
5. Tap Pallando to bring Blue Mountain Dwarves from the sideboard into the play deck or discard pile.
6. Position a company to reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold, store the Book and play the faction.

This is the original Pallando strategy. The Academy borrows the useful idea of a Wizard support company, but its active rules examples use Gandalf's actual card text and are documented in [Gandalf and the Support Company]({{ '/decks/gandalf-support-company/' | relative_url }}).

## Historical solo draw example

If a one-character Pallando company moves Grey Havens → Lórien:

- Lórien is a Haven, so normal rules use Grey Havens' Hazard draw number, 2.
- The Academy solo formula would draw 2 + (2 × 2) = 6 Hazard cards.
- The one-character company has a base Hazard Limit of 2.
- Pallando's hand-size ability would raise the Hero hand target from five to six while he is in play.

These calculations are preserved for historical comparison only. The active Gandalf-led example uses the same route/draw logic but Gandalf's own card text: +1 to his corruption checks and no hand-size increase.
