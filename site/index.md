# MECCG Hero Solo Academy

**You have your cards. Start here and follow the numbered steps in order.**

This Academy teaches **conventional solo Hero play** in Middle-earth CCG: you control a Hero company and run a separate Hazard deck against it. It is not the Arda format and its teaching decks are not Arda piles.

## Your first session — do these in order

<ol class="course-steps">
  <li><strong>Set up the table.</strong> Separate the 36-card Hero play deck (30 Resources plus six character/Wizard cards), 30-card Hazard deck, 15 site cards, starting company and two Cram items, sideboard, and separate discard piles. <a href="{{ '/academy/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 1: Start Here and Set Up →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Hero cards.</strong> Use the current working teaching list, check the names against your physical cards, and keep the list beside you. <a href="{{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 2A: Hero deck list →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Hazard cards.</strong> Build the complementary Hazard deck and keep it separate from the Hero cards. <a href="{{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 2B: Hazard deck list →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Play the first expedition.</strong> Start at Rivendell and follow the guided Book of Mazarbul mission toward Moria. Do each step in order; don't try to learn every rule at once. <a href="{{ '/games/game-1/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 3: Play Game 1 →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Use the turn workbook.</strong> Pause at the decision points, write down what you think should happen, then continue through the worked example. <a href="{{ '/games/game-1/turn-workbook/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 4: Game 1 Turn Workbook →</a></li>

## What to do today

**Only do Steps 1–5.** Open Start Here, prepare the two listed decks, play Game 1, and use the workbook. Do not start with advanced Hazard combinations or deck optimisation.

The Academy's first-game baseline is a Challenge Deck C-derived Hero teaching list: it retains the Dwarven Quest Resources and five non-Wizard characters, but currently uses one Gandalf instead of the historical three Pallando cards. The solo Hazard-draw procedure is documented separately. This is a conventional Hero solo teaching setup. It is not an Arda decklist or an Arda solitaire ruleset. Use the [solo rules page]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}) for the exact procedure used here.

## Check the solo draw before your next expedition

There are two separate movement draws: the Hero player draws from the Hero play deck, while the solo Hazard side draws from its own deck. The Hazard Limit is a third number. Complete the [Movement Draw Lab]({{ '/scenarios/movement-draw-lab/' | relative_url }}) to practise all three, then use the [Hazard Draw & Limit Lab]({{ '/scenarios/hazard-draw-limit-lab/' | relative_url }}) for Haven destinations, multi-company turns and Hundreds of Butterflies.

## After the first expedition

Once you can run the Dwarven company through the first Moria expedition, learn how Gandalf supports the Gandalf-led Academy. He is the Wizard avatar in the Hero play deck, not one of the five starting Dwarves. His separate company creates another Movement/Hazard Phase, so learn the draw and limit calculations before using it.

- [Gandalf and the Support Company]({{ '/decks/gandalf-support-company/' | relative_url }})
- [Wizard Choice Workshop — compare all five Wizards]({{ '/decks/wizard-choice-workshop/' | relative_url }})
- [Gandalf — One Company or Two? practice scenario]({{ '/scenarios/gandalf-company/' | relative_url }})

## The loop you are learning

**Prepare → Organise → Move → Draw and resolve Hazards → Enter the site → Attempt the objective → Recover or continue → Debrief**
