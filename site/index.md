# MECCG Hero Solo Academy

**You have your cards. Start here and follow the numbered steps in order.**

This is the practical route for learning solo Hero play with physical Middle-earth CCG cards. You do not need to read the research log, inventory methods, or every reference page before your first game.

## Your first session — do these in order

<ol class="course-steps">
  <li><strong>Set up the table.</strong> Separate the Hero Resource deck, Hazard deck, site cards, character pool, company area, and separate discard piles. <a href="{{ '/academy/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 1: Start Here and Set Up →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Hero cards.</strong> Use the fixed teaching list, check the names against your physical cards, and keep the list beside you. <a href="{{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 2A: Hero deck list →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Hazard cards.</strong> Build the complementary Hazard deck and keep it separate from the Hero cards. <a href="{{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 2B: Hazard deck list →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Play the first expedition.</strong> Start at Rivendell and follow the guided Book of Mazarbul mission toward Moria. Do each step in order; don't try to learn every rule at once. <a href="{{ '/games/game-1/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 3: Play Game 1 →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Use the turn workbook.</strong> Pause at the decision points, write down what you think should happen, then continue through the worked example. <a href="{{ '/games/game-1/turn-workbook/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 4: Game 1 Turn Workbook →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Check what you learned.</strong> Complete the short first-session workbook before looking at the answers. <a href="{{ '/scenarios/first-session/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 5: First Session Practice →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Learn movement and route choices.</strong> Only after your first game, study legal movement, free/player-directed movement and route risk. <a href="{{ '/reference/free-movement/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 6: Free Movement →</a> then <a href="{{ '/routes/expedition-planner/' | relative_url }}">the Expedition Planner →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Learn the Hazard side deliberately.</strong> Practise legality, targets, timing, combinations and Hazard Limit. <a href="{{ '/reference/hazard-player/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 7: Hazard Player →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Repeat and improve.</strong> Complete the decision lab, compare route risks, and record repeatable tests before changing the deck. <a href="{{ '/scenarios/expedition-decision-lab/' | relative_url }}">Open Step 8A: Decision Lab →</a>, then <a href="{{ '/scenarios/route-risk-workshop/' | relative_url }}">Step 8B: Route Risk Workshop →</a>, then <a href="{{ '/scenarios/solo-playtest/' | relative_url }}">Playtest and Debrief →</a></li>
</ol>

## What to do today

**Only do Steps 1–5.** Open Start Here, prepare the two listed decks, play Game 1, and use the workbook. Do not start with the card database, source comparisons, advanced Hazard combinations, or deck optimisation.

The Academy's current first-game baseline is the historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C teaching list with an explicitly documented solo Hazard-draw procedure. It is a teaching setup, not a claim that this list is identical to the entire Arda pool. The [solo rules page]({{ '/reference/solo-ruleset/' | relative_url }}) explains exactly which procedure is being used.

## The loop you are learning

**Prepare → Organise → Move → Draw and resolve Hazards → Enter the site → Attempt the objective → Recover or continue → Debrief**

If you get stuck, return to [Step 1: Start Here]({{ '/academy/' | relative_url }}). Use [Course Path]({{ '/academy/learning-path/' | relative_url }}) for the full sequence and [All Pages]({{ '/navigation/' | relative_url }}) only when looking up a specific reference.
