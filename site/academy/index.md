# Lesson 1 — Start Here and Set Up

**Goal:** prepare the table so you can begin the first conventional Hero solo expedition without hunting through your collection or mixing card zones.

> **Project boundary:** this is the conventional Hero-vs-Hazard solo format, not Arda. The 36-card Hero play deck and separate 30-card Hazard deck on this page are specific to this Academy's teaching setup. Do not use them as an Arda solo setup.

Do these steps in order. Do not skip ahead to advanced deck tuning.

## Step 1 — Separate the card zones

Prepare these distinct areas:

- **Hero play deck** — shuffle the 30 Hero Resources and six character/Wizard cards together: **36 cards total**, with no Hazards.
- **Hazard deck** — the separate 30-card opposing deck.
- **Location deck** — the 15 site cards, kept separate and not shuffled.
- **Starting company/setup cards** — the five starting Dwarves and two Cram items.
- **Hero discard** and **Hazard discard** — kept separate.
- **Sideboard** — separate and unused in Game 1.
- **Company area** — characters, followers, items and other cards currently associated with the company.

For the first lessons, keep the zones physically distinct even though you control both sides of the solo game.

## Step 2 — Know which card list this lesson uses

The Academy's first-game baseline is the historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C teaching list, combined with an explicitly identified solitaire Hazard-draw procedure. It is a fixed teaching setup to learn the conventional Hero solo game loop.

This is not an Arda pool, and Arda rules or Arda solitaire variants are not used in this lesson. If you are looking for Arda format, leave this course and use the [separate Arda Master Database](https://jamspot.github.io/meccg-arda-master-database/).

## Step 3 — Build the Hero play deck first

Assemble the locked 30-card Resource list and the six active character/Wizard cards: Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli, Gildor Inglorion and Gandalf. Shuffle them together to make the **36-card Gandalf-led Hero play deck**. Do not include Pallando ×3 in the active build. Keep the starting company and its two Cram items separate until setup. Do not tune the list before you have completed a first game and debrief.

[Build the Hero play deck →]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }})

## Step 4 — Build the complementary Hazard deck

Once the Hero list is assembled, build the Hazard deck from its own list. Keep the two decks and discard piles separate.

[Build the Hazard deck →]({{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }})

## Step 5 — Learn the table conventions

- Upright = untapped.
- Rotated 90° = tapped.
- Rotated 180° = wounded.
- Keep Hero and Hazard draws and discards separate.
- Keep the location deck accessible; it is not the normal shuffled play deck.

## Step 6 — Read the Game 1 mission brief

Your first objective is **Book of Mazarbul at Moria**. The first game teaches the conventional Hero solo expedition loop, not every rule in MECCG.

Read the mission, starting company and movement plan in the next lesson. Keep the official rules available for questions, but do not read the entire rulebook before starting.

## Gandalf is not a starting Dwarf

The five starting characters are Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin. Gandalf is the Wizard avatar in the Hero play deck. He must be drawn and played at a Haven (his home site is Any Haven) or at Rivendell if you do not already have a Wizard in play. Do not add him automatically to the starting company.

After the first Moria expedition, use [Gandalf and the Support Company]({{ '/decks/gandalf-support-company/' | relative_url }}) to learn the Academy's Gandalf-led support-company adaptation and the extra solo Hazard phase created by a second company.
