# Academy Learning Path

The Academy is a course, not an encyclopedia.

Every lesson follows:

**DO → STOP → DECIDE → EXPLAIN → CONTINUE → DEBRIEF**

## Level 0 — Set the table

Build the 30-card Hero teaching deck and 30-card Training Hazard deck. Keep the Location deck, character pool, sideboard and discards separate.

[Build the Hero deck](../../decks/hero-deck/)

[Build the Hazard deck](../../decks/hazard-deck/)

## Level 1 — Game 1

**Mission:** acquire Book of Mazarbul.

Start at Rivendell. Learn:

**MISSION → DESTINATION → ROUTE → HAZARDS → SITE → OBJECTIVE → REASSESS → RECOVER/PRESS ON**

[Game 1 teaching script](../../games/game-1/teaching-script/)

## Level 2 — Movement

Learn formal Region Movement: up to four regions, counting origin and destination, with no repeated region.

[Free / Player-Directed Movement](../../reference/free-movement/)

## Level 3 — Route Planning

For every expedition ask:

1. Where am I going?
2. Why am I going there?
3. Which legal route gets me there?
4. What does that route expose me to?
5. Where will I be afterwards?
6. Is the reward worth the risk?

## Level 4 — Hazard Player

Use:

**LEGALITY → TARGET → DISRUPTION → SEQUENCING → PRESSURE**

## Level 5 — Site Decisions

Arrival and site entry are different decisions. Learn when to enter, when to stop, and when to reassess.

## Level 6 — Recovery

Learn when pressing on is worse than returning to a Haven.

## Level 7 — Combat

Only now add attack resolution, strikes, prowess, body, wounds and combat resources.

## Level 8 — Influence and Recruitment

Learn direct influence, general influence, followers and company composition.

## Level 9 — Objectives and Compression

Connect individual expeditions into a campaign and learn to combine useful objectives and future positioning.

## Level 10 — Corruption and Campaign Risk

Learn to judge rewards against long-term risk.

## Level 11 — Sideboarding and Advanced Construction

Introduce sideboard access, larger decks and specialised resource/Hazard construction.

## Level 12 — Advanced Middle-earth

Dragons and difficult route/site interactions become advanced campaign material.

> **Academy rule:** introduce a rule when the player is about to need it, not merely because the rule exists.
