# Game 1 Turn Workbook — Hero and Hazard Decisions

Use this beside the physical cards. It joins the complete Hero play deck (30 Resources plus eight character cards), the separate Training Hazard deck, Region Movement, and the decision to enter, continue or recover.

This Academy uses a labelled teaching hybrid: historical Dwarven Quest / Pallando Challenge Deck C lists, normal MECCG rules for movement and card play, and a separately identified solo Hazard-draw procedure. The original Challenge Deck was not designed specifically for this solitaire procedure. Printed card text and official rules take precedence.

## 1. Set up the table

- [ ] 38-card Hero play deck assembled and shuffled: 30 Resources plus Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli, Gildor Inglorion and Pallando ×3.
- [ ] 30-card Training Hazard deck assembled and shuffled separately.
- [ ] Solo hand size set to 5 for the hand-reset step.
- [ ] 15 site cards kept as the Location deck; do not shuffle them.
- [ ] Starting company: Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin at Rivendell.
- [ ] Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin.
- [ ] Two Cram items placed as specified by the teaching list.
- [ ] Hero and Hazard discard piles separate; starting pool and sideboard separate.
- [ ] Book of Mazarbul identified as the first mission.

If a listed card is missing, record it as needed for the physical collection. Do not silently substitute a card during the scripted lesson.

## 3. Keep the solo draw and Hazard Limit separate

The Academy's selected solo procedure draws **2 + (2 × the normal Hazard draw number for the relevant site)**. For this move to non-Haven Moria, that number is 3, so the draw is **2 + (2 × 3) = 8 cards**. Use a five-card hand for this solo teaching setup.

A five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of **5** under normal rules.

| Question | Game 1 answer |
|---|---|
| Cards drawn by the Academy solo procedure | 8 |
| Base Hazard Limit for five characters | 5 |
| Does drawing eight allow eight ordinary Hazard plays that count against the limit? | No |
| Does Twilight count against the Hazard Limit? | No. It can be played at any point during either player's turn if it has a legal Environment target. |
| What does the selected solo procedure direct? | Play as many drawn Hazards as possible within the limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination |

Do not confuse this solo draw formula with the normal two-player draw rule. Also remember that Twilight is a special no-limit Hazard: it cancels one Environment card and does not use one of the ordinary Hazard slots.

## 4. Audit the Hazard draw

Look at all eight cards before choosing plays.

| Check | Question |
|---|---|
| Legality | Can the card legally target this company, its site path or new site? |
| Keying | Which region type/name or site condition allows it? |
| Requirements | Does it need another card, target or timing condition? |
| Consequence | How would it damage this expedition? |
| Critical character | Whose wound, tap or disruption matters most? |
| Sequence | Does printed wording require a particular order? |
| Limit | How many plays that count against the limit have been declared? |
| Combination | Which legal set creates the greatest disadvantage within the limit? |

**Most important legal Hazard:** ...........................................................

**Why it is legal:** ........................................................................

**Why it is more damaging than the alternatives:** ........................................

### The solo instruction

Do not deliberately make weak plays to help the Hero side. Choose the most disadvantageous legal combination and play as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within the Hazard Limit. An illegal card is never an option, and a card's requirements cannot be ignored. Do not judge only by attack prowess: disruption may be more damaging than a creature if it prevents the mission.

## 5. Resolve the expedition and decide whether to enter

Use this as a learning checklist, not a replacement for the complete rules.

1. Reveal Moria during the Movement/Hazard Phase.
2. Declare the Region Movement site path.
3. Establish the applicable Hazard Limit and resolve required effects in the correct order.
4. Draw according to the Academy's labelled solo procedure.
5. Play legal Hazards according to their text, timing and the selected solo instruction.
6. Finish the Movement/Hazard Phase and assess the company.
7. During the Site Phase, decide whether to enter; resolve the site's effects and automatic attack normally.
8. Attempt Book of Mazarbul only if the company and rules permit it.
9. Reassess before planning another expedition.

Before entering, ask: Can the company handle the automatic attack? Who is tapped or wounded? Can the objective still be attempted? What is the cost of failure? Is recovery more valuable?

**Decision:** Enter now / Delay or recover.  
**Reason:** ....................................................................................

Arrival and site entry are separate decisions. Do not use this worksheet to waive an official rule or invent an extra penalty.

## 6. After Book of Mazarbul

If acquired, the mission changes from **ACQUIRE** to **SECURE → CONSOLIDATE → REPOSITION**. Book of Mazarbul is worth 1 MP while in play and can be stored at a Dwarf-hold for **5 MPs**. It has 2 corruption points. Its hand-size ability only works if its bearer is a Sage; none of the five starting Dwarves is a Sage, so that bonus is not available during Game 1.

The direct Moria-to-Blue-Mountain route via Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador contains five regions. It is too long for one normal Region Movement. Plan a legal intermediate stop and verify adjacency against the region cards. See the [Moria → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold route example]({{ '/routes/moria-blue-mountain/' | relative_url }}) and the [Route Atlas]({{ '/routes/' | relative_url }}).

Press on if the company remains capable, the next destination has a real purpose, the hand supports it and the risk is justified. Recover or reposition if key characters are wounded, essential defence is spent, or the next reward does not justify the danger.

## 7. Five practice scenarios

Write your answer before reading the model response.

### A — Eight cards, five slots
You draw eight Hazard cards for Moria. Two are illegal, one is legal only if another card is played, and five appear individually legal. What do you do?

**Model response:** Exclude the illegal options, check the amplifier's requirements and sequence, then choose the most disadvantageous legal combination. Play as many as possible within the five-card base Hazard Limit; drawing eight does not permit eight ordinary plays.

### B — The powerful creature
A creature looks devastating, but its printed keying does not match the declared site path. Play it?

**Model response:** No. Legality comes before strategy.

### C — Five-region route
You want to move from Moria to Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold through Redhorn Gate, Hollin, Rhudaur, Arthedain and Númeriador. Is this one normal Region Movement?

**Model response:** No. Plan a legal intermediate stop and a second leg.

### D — Damaged at Moria
The best fighter is wounded and two characters are tapped. Must you enter immediately?

**Model response:** No. Arrival and entry are separate. Inspect the automatic attack, attempt requirements, company condition and mission value before making the legal Site Phase decision.

### E — The Book is secured
The Book is acquired and the next site is reachable. What is the first question?

**Model response:** “What does the company need now?” Reassess the mission, wounds, taps, hand and future position before choosing the next expedition.
