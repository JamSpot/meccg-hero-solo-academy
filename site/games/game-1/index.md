# Game 1 — First Expedition

## Mission

**Acquire the Book of Mazarbul.**

Start at **Rivendell**. Destination: **Moria**.

The first game is deliberately small. Its purpose is not to teach every rule. It teaches the core solo loop:

**plan → move → expose → survive → enter → attempt → reassess**

The follow-up is to secure the Book, with **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold** as the historical consolidation destination.

Use the new [Game 1 Turn Workbook]({{ '/games/game-1/turn-workbook/' | relative_url }}) beside the physical cards to record route, Hazard, entry and recovery decisions.

## Starting company

- Thorin II
- Dori
- Kíli
- Glóin
- Óin

Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli. Glóin controls Óin.

Use the two Cram items from the Challenge Deck C setup: one with Thorin II and one with Óin.

## Before the first turn

Prepare the two shuffled decks and the separate Location deck:

- **Hero play deck — 36 cards:** 30 Hero Resources plus Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli, Gildor Inglorion and one Gandalf Wizard card. Gandalf is the Wizard avatar, not part of the five-Dwarf starting company. Pallando ×3 belongs to the historical Challenge Deck C reference, not the active Academy deck. No Hazards go in this deck.
- **Hazard deck — 30 cards:** keep it separate from the Hero play deck.
- **Location deck — 15 sites:** keep separate and unshuffled.
- **Starting setup:** Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin, with the two Cram items assigned as listed above.

Use a **5-card hand** for this solo teaching setup, including the opening hand. Gandalf does not change the five-card Hero hand target; his card gives +1 to his own corruption checks. Keep the Hazard deck face down. The Hazard player is controlled by the learner.

For this first game, do not try to optimise every play. The objective is to learn why a decision is good or bad.

## Step 1 — Identify the objective

Put **Book of Mazarbul** where it can be seen.

Ask:

> What does this card require me to accomplish?

Then identify the destination:

**Moria.**

Do not start moving until you know what the expedition is trying to achieve.

## Step 2 — Plan the movement

Use **Region Movement** for the teaching game.

Example route/site path:

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

This contains three regions, so it is within the normal four-region maximum.

### The movement checklist

Before moving, say these aloud:

1. **Destination:** Moria.
2. **Movement type:** Region Movement.
3. **Route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.
4. **No repeated region:** confirmed.
5. **Region count:** three.
6. **Site path:** declare it during the Movement/Hazard Phase, after revealing the new site.
7. **Exposure:** consider what the named regions allow the Hazard player to key.

The lesson is that choosing a destination is only the beginning of movement planning.

## Step 3 — Draw from both decks

During a moving company's Movement/Hazard Phase, keep the two draws separate.

The Hero site-card numbers are commonly shown as **opponent draws / you draw**:

- The **first number** is the normal Hazard-player draw.
- The **second number** is the normal Hero/resource-player draw.

For Moria, the Hero site card is **3/2**.

### Hero play-deck draw

Your five-Dwarf company contains characters with a mind of at least 3, so it qualifies for the normal Hero draw. Draw **up to 2 cards** from the 36-card Hero play deck (at least one if the company qualifies). This is not a Hazard draw.

### Separate solo Hazard draw

The normal Hazard-player number for Moria is **3**, so the solo procedure draws:

**2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards.**

Draw those eight from the separate Hazard deck. Do not put them into your Hero hand.

### Keep the three numbers separate

| What is being counted? | Game 1 at Moria |
|---|---:|
| Hero cards drawn from the Hero play deck | Up to **2** |
| Hazard cards drawn from the separate Hazard deck | **8** |
| Five-Dwarf base Hazard Limit | **5** |

After the company's Movement/Hazard Phase has finished, reset your Hero hand to the solo hand size of **5**. If you drew two Hero cards, you may have seven in hand while resolving the phase; do not discard down to five before the Hazard and other phase actions are complete. Play only Resource cards whose timing and conditions permit them.

## Step 4 — Become the Hazard player

Look at the eight drawn cards and assess the full draw before choosing plays.

For every card ask:

### LEGALITY

Can it legally be played against this company, its movement, site path or new site?

### TARGET

Who is the most important character to affect?

### DISRUPTION

Would wounding, tapping, exhausting, corrupting or otherwise disrupting that character change the expedition?

### SEQUENCING

Is this card stronger before another card, or does its wording require a particular order?

### LIMIT

How many Hazard plays that count against the limit have already been declared?

### COMBINATION

Which legal combination creates the greatest disadvantage within the limit?

**Important solo rule:** the selected procedure directs the Hazard side to play as many of the drawn Hazards as possible within the Hazard Limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination. Do not deliberately leave a useful legal slot empty just because a card looks less dramatic. Equally, never play an illegal card or ignore its requirements.

## Step 5 — Decide whether to enter Moria

Arriving at the site and entering the site are separate decisions.

Before entering, ask:

> If I enter now, what can I accomplish?

Then:

> What is my worst reasonable outcome?

If the company is already badly compromised, recovery may be better than forcing the site. Resolve Moria's effects and automatic attack normally; attempt Book of Mazarbul only if the company and rules permit it.

## Step 6 — Resolve the site

Resolve the site's effects normally.

Then attempt **Book of Mazarbul** if the company can legally do so.

Do not immediately plan the next expedition.

First inspect the company's condition:

- Who is tapped?
- Who is wounded?
- Who has lost useful resources?
- Who is still capable of leading combat?
- What cards remain in hand?
- What does the company actually need next?

## Step 7 — The Book changes the expedition

Before the Book was acquired, the expedition was:

**ACQUIRE**

Once it is acquired, the question changes to:

**SECURE → CONSOLIDATE → REPOSITION**

This is one of the most important lessons in the Academy.

Do not automatically press on simply because another site is reachable.

Ask:

> What is the highest-value next position for the company?

## Step 8 — Recovery decision

At the end of the expedition, choose deliberately between:

### Press on

Use this when:

- the company remains strong;
- the next site is genuinely productive;
- the hand supports another expedition;
- the additional risk is justified.

### Recover

Use this when:

- characters are wounded or tapped;
- the company has spent key defensive cards;
- the next objective is not urgent;
- returning to safety protects more future value than another marginal site attempt.

The lesson is:

> **Recovery is not failure. Wasted recovery turns are not the goal either.**

## Game 1 decision points

Pause at these moments and decide before reading the lesson.

### Decision A — Route

**Question:** Why use Region Movement rather than simply following the safest familiar route?

**Lesson:** Region Movement gives more destination and route control, but the declared site path can create additional Hazard-keying opportunities. Choose with both effects in mind.

### Decision B — Hazard

**Question:** Which drawn Hazard is the most dangerous, and why?

Do not answer based only on attack strength. Consider the critical character, current wounds/taps, corruption, immediate objective and recovery options. Then follow the solo instruction to choose the most disadvantageous legal combination within the limit.

### Decision C — Entry

**Question:** Do you enter Moria immediately?

**Lesson:** arriving at a site does not itself force an immediate site attempt.

### Decision D — The Book

**Question:** Once Book of Mazarbul is acquired, has the expedition's purpose changed?

**Answer:** Yes. **Acquire** becomes **Secure/Consolidate**.

### Decision E — Recovery

**Question:** Is one more productive site worth the risk?

Compare immediate Marshaling Point gain with future campaign value.

## Game 1 success condition

For the first lesson, success means:

1. Reach Moria legally.
2. Understand why the declared route matters.
3. Survive the Hazard phase.
4. Enter Moria deliberately.
5. Acquire Book of Mazarbul if the company is capable.
6. Make an explicit press-on-or-recover decision.

Winning efficiently is **not** the objective of Game 1. Learning to make the decisions is.

## Gandalf after the first expedition

Gandalf is in the 36-card Hero play deck but is not one of the five starting Dwarves. When you draw him, decide whether to build the separate support company. Do not automatically add him to the Moria company: doing so raises that company's Hazard Limit from five to six and exposes the Wizard to the Moria journey. See [Gandalf and the Support Company]({{ '/decks/gandalf-support-company/' | relative_url }}) after you have learned the basic one-company loop.

## After Game 1

Record:

- starting company;
- movement route;
- Hazards drawn;
- important Hazard decisions;
- whether the Book was acquired;
- wounds/taps;
- resources spent;
- whether the company recovered or pressed on;
- what you would change next time.

Use the [Game 1 Turn Workbook]({{ '/games/game-1/turn-workbook/' | relative_url }}) for the full debrief, then replay Game 1 once. The second play should not be about trying a completely different deck. It should be about making **better decisions with the same teaching pool**.

## Captain's question

At the end of the game, answer:

> **Did I choose my route because it was legal, or because it was good for the whole expedition?**

That question is the bridge from learning the rules to actually learning the conventional Hero solo game.
