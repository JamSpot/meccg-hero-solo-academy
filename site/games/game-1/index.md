# Game 1 — First Expedition

## Mission

**Acquire the Book of Mazarbul.** Start at **Rivendell**. Destination: **Moria**.

The follow-up is to secure the Book, with **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold** as the historical consolidation destination.

## Starting company

- Thorin II
- Dori
- Kíli
- Glóin
- Óin

Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli. Glóin controls Óin. Thorin II has Cram; Óin has Cram.

## Movement

Use **Region Movement**.

Example route/site path: **Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**.

## Solo Hazard draw

For Moria, normal Hazard-player draw is 3. Solo draw is **2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards**. Hazard Limit for five characters is **5**.

## Table procedure

1. Draw five Resources.
2. Pick an objective.
3. Find its destination.
4. Choose move or stay.
5. If moving, choose movement type.
6. Check route legality.
7. Calculate the site's Hazard draw.
8. Draw solitaire Hazards.
9. Play the most strategically damaging legal combination.
10. Decide whether to enter.
11. Resolve the site attack.
12. Attempt one Resource.
13. Plan the next position.

## What Game 1 teaches

- Movement and Hazard phases are different decisions.
- Reaching a site does not mean entering it.
- Entering a site does not mean the expedition is complete.
- The critical character is not necessarily the strongest fighter.
- Defensive cards have future value.
- After acquiring the Book, the expedition changes from **Acquire** to **Secure**.
- Recovery is a strategic decision, not an emotional reset.

> Can I safely visit one more productive site before recovering?
