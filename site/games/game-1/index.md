# Game 1 — First Expedition

## Mission

**Acquire the Book of Mazarbul.**

Start at **Rivendell**. Destination: **Moria**.

The first game is deliberately small. Its purpose is not to teach every rule. It teaches the core solo loop:

**plan → move → expose → survive → enter → attempt → reassess**

The follow-up is to secure the Book, with **Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold** as the historical consolidation destination.

## Starting company

- Thorin II
- Dori
- Kíli
- Glóin
- Óin

Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli. Glóin controls Óin.

Use the starting equipment/resources specified by the chosen Dwarven teaching list.

## Before the first turn

Separate the two decks:

- **Hero Resource deck**
- **Hazard deck**

Keep the Hazard deck face down. The Hazard player is controlled by the learner.

For this first game, do not try to optimise every play. The objective is to learn why a decision is good or bad.

## Step 1 — Identify the objective

Put **Book of Mazarbul** where it can be seen.

Ask:

> What does this card require me to accomplish?

Then identify the destination:

**Moria.**

Do not start moving until you know what the expedition is trying to achieve.

## Step 2 — Plan the movement

Use **Region Movement** for the teaching game.

Example declared route/site path:

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

This contains three regions, so it is within the normal four-region maximum.

### The movement checklist

Before moving, say these aloud:

1. **Destination:** Moria.
2. **Movement type:** Region Movement.
3. **Route:** Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate.
4. **No repeated region:** confirmed.
5. **Region count:** three.
6. **Site path:** declared.
7. **Exposure:** consider what the named regions allow the Hazard player to key.

The lesson is that choosing a destination is only the beginning of movement planning.

## Step 3 — Calculate the solo Hazard draw

For this teaching variant, the solitaire Hazard draw is:

**2 + (2 × the number on the destination Site card).**

Moria has a 3, so:

**2 + (2 × 3) = 8 Hazard cards.**

Draw eight cards from the Hazard deck.

Do not automatically play all eight.

The normal Hazard Limit is a separate rules concept. With five characters, the company's base Hazard Limit is **5**.

For the teaching game, keep the distinction clear:

- **Solo draw** determines how many Hazard cards are available to the solitaire Hazard player.
- **Hazard Limit** determines the normal limit on Hazard play.

## Step 4 — Become the Hazard player

Look at the eight drawn cards.

For every card ask:

### LEGALITY

Can it legally be played against this company, its movement, site path or new site?

### TARGET

Who is the most important character to affect?

### DISRUPTION

Would wounding, tapping, exhausting, corrupting or otherwise disrupting that character actually change the expedition?

### SEQUENCING

Is this card stronger before another card, or should it be saved?

### PRESSURE BUDGET

You do not have to spend every legal card.

The goal is to create a meaningful challenge, not to kill the company.

## Step 5 — Decide whether to enter Moria

Arriving at the site and entering the site are separate decisions.

Before entering, ask:

> If I enter now, what can I accomplish?

Then:

> What is my worst reasonable outcome?

If the company is already badly compromised, recovery may be better than forcing the site.

For Game 1, however, the normal teaching objective is to attempt Moria unless the company has been seriously damaged.

## Step 6 — Resolve the site

Resolve the site's effects normally.

Then attempt **Book of Mazarbul** if the company can legally do so.

Do not immediately plan the next expedition.

First inspect the company's condition:

- Who is tapped?
- Who is wounded?
- Who has lost useful resources?
- Who is still capable of leading combat?
- What cards remain in hand?
- What does the company actually need next?

## Step 7 — The Book changes the expedition

Before the Book was acquired, the expedition was:

**ACQUIRE**

Once it is acquired, the question changes to:

**SECURE → CONSOLIDATE → REPOSITION**

This is one of the most important lessons in the Academy.

Do not automatically press on simply because another site is reachable.

Ask:

> What is the highest-value next position for the company?

## Step 8 — Recovery decision

At the end of the expedition, choose deliberately between:

### Press on

Use this when:

- the company remains strong;
- the next site is genuinely productive;
- the hand supports another expedition;
- the additional risk is justified.

### Recover

Use this when:

- characters are wounded or tapped;
- the company has spent key defensive cards;
- the next objective is not urgent;
- returning to safety protects more future value than another marginal site attempt.

The lesson is:

> **Recovery is not failure. Wasted recovery turns are not the goal either.**

## Game 1 decision points

The instructor should pause at these moments and ask the learner to decide before explaining the answer.

### Decision A — Route

**Question:** Why use Region Movement rather than simply following the safest familiar route?

**Lesson:** Region Movement gives greater destination and route control, but exposes more information to the Hazard player.

### Decision B — Hazard

**Question:** Which drawn Hazard is the most dangerous, and why?

Do not answer based only on attack strength.

Consider:

- critical character;
- current wounds/taps;
- corruption;
- immediate objective;
- recovery options.

### Decision C — Entry

**Question:** Do you enter Moria immediately?

**Lesson:** arriving at a site does not force an immediate site attempt.

### Decision D — The Book

**Question:** Once Book of Mazarbul is acquired, has the expedition's purpose changed?

**Answer:** Yes.

**Acquire** becomes **Secure/Consolidate**.

### Decision E — Recovery

**Question:** Is one more productive site worth the risk?

This is the first time the learner should explicitly compare immediate Marshaling Point gain with future campaign value.

## Game 1 success condition

For the first lesson, success means:

1. Reach Moria legally.
2. Understand why the declared route matters.
3. Survive the Hazard phase.
4. Enter Moria deliberately.
5. Acquire Book of Mazarbul if the company is capable.
6. Make an explicit press-on-or-recover decision.

Winning efficiently is **not** the objective of Game 1.

Learning to make the decisions is.

## After Game 1

Record:

- starting company;
- movement route;
- Hazards drawn;
- important Hazard decisions;
- whether the Book was acquired;
- wounds/taps;
- resources spent;
- whether the company recovered or pressed on;
- what you would change next time.

Then replay Game 1 once.

The second play should not be about trying a completely different deck. It should be about making **better decisions with the same teaching pool**.

## Captain's question

At the end of the game, answer:

> **Did I choose my route because it was legal, or because it was good for the whole expedition?**

That question is the bridge from learning the rules to actually learning to play Hero Arda.
