# Campaign Walkthrough — Model Answer

Use this after completing the [Campaign Walkthrough]({{ '/games/campaign-walkthrough/' | relative_url }}). The route choices are examples; the best decision depends on the actual wounds, taps, hand and Hazard draw.

## 1. Rules layers

The solo draw and five-card hand are from the solitaire rules in *The Lidless Eye* rulebook. The Academy's fixed Dwarven Quest lists and Book mission are a teaching adaptation. The Book mission does not replace the official solo scoring objective of accumulating as many MPs as possible by the second exhaustion of the play deck.

The current CoE rules should be used for movement, site paths, Hazard Limits, site entry, combat and corruption. Card text and current errata take precedence over the Academy's shorthand.

## 2. Turn 1 — Rivendell to Moria

**Region Movement** is the correct direct route because Moria's nearest Haven is Lórien, not Rivendell. The route Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate contains three regions, includes both endpoints, and has no repeated region. Confirm adjacency on the physical Region cards.

Moria's normal site-card draw is **3 Hazard / 2 Hero draw**. Because Moria is a non-Haven destination:

- Hero draws up to **2 cards from the Hero play deck**.
- Solo Hazard side draws 2 + (2 × 3) = **8 Hazard cards**.
- Five characters produce a base Hazard Limit of **5**.

The Hazard side must inspect all eight cards and choose as many legal plays as possible within five limit-counting slots. The route contains three Wilderness regions and names Redhorn Gate. A Hazard is not legal merely because it appears on the list: check its printed key, target, conditions and timing.

If the company enters Moria, it faces the site automatic attack of four Orc strikes at 7 prowess. This attack is not a Hazard play and does not consume a Hazard Limit slot. It is faced again on every later entry.

If the company does not enter, it avoids the automatic attack but cannot attempt the Book or take other site actions. The choice should be based on the actual arrival condition, not on hindsight.

## 3. Turn 2 — compare the two routes

### Route A: Moria to Goblin-gate

Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass.

Four regions, including endpoints. It is within the normal limit if the Region cards confirm every adjacency.

The Hero version of Goblin-gate is a non-Haven destination with normal draw **2 Hazard / 1 Hero draw**:

- Hero draws up to **1 card from the Hero play deck**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **6**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.

If the company enters, it faces three Orc strikes at 6 prowess. Declining to enter avoids that site attack but also means no Gollum attempt or other Site Phase action.

### Route B: Moria to Rivendell

Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur.

Three regions. Rivendell is a Haven destination, so use Moria, the site of origin, for both normal draw numbers:

- Hero draws up to **2 cards from the Hero play deck**.
- Normal Hazard number is 3, so solo Hazard draw: **8**.
- Base Hazard Limit: **5**.

This route is not safer simply because it ends at a Haven. It has the same eight-card solo Hazard draw as the first Moria arrival. Its advantage is the opportunity to heal at the Haven during the next Untap Phase.

## 4. Turn 3 — Blue Mountain

From Goblin-gate:

High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

From Rivendell:

Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador.

Both routes are legal Region Movement examples if each adjacency is confirmed and no region repeats. The first has four regions; the second has three.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a non-Haven destination with normal draw **1 Hazard / 1 Hero draw**:

- Hero draws up to **1 card from the Hero play deck**.
- Solo Hazard draw: **4**.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.
- No automatic attack on the Hero site card.

The number of regions travelled does not change the draw. The destination's normal draw number applies because Blue Mountain is not a Haven.

## 5. Store the Book at the next Organization Phase

The Book's card text specifically allows it to be stored at a Dwarf-hold. Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold qualifies by name, even though its site type is Free-hold.

Storage is an Organization Phase action. The company does not need to enter the site during the arrival-turn Site Phase solely to store the Book next turn.

For a bearer carrying only the Book's 2 CP:

| Bearer | Printed modifier | Natural rolls that fail | Failure probability |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Gandalf | +1 | None | 0/36 |
| Thorin II | 0 | 2 | 1/36 |
| Glóin | 0 | 2 | 1/36 |
| Dori | -1 | 2–3 | 3/36 |
| Kíli | -1 | 2–3 | 3/36 |
| Óin | -1 | 2–3 | 3/36 |

These figures change if the bearer has other corruption points, modifiers or effects. Other characters in the same company can tap to add +1 each to the check. That support tap has a tactical cost if the company also moves during the turn.

On success, the Book goes to the marshalling-point pile for **5 MP**. Do not add its 1 MP in-play value to the stored value.

## 6. Hazard-side discipline

The fair solo procedure is neither “play the first five” nor “make the most frightening play regardless of legality.”

For every drawn card:

1. Check the printed key and target.
2. Check that the required site path, region type, named region or target actually exists.
3. Check conditions and timing.
4. Count the card against the Hazard Limit if it does so under the rules.
5. Compare the legal combinations and choose the most disadvantageous one.
6. Play as many drawn Hazards as possible within the limit.

If no legal card remains for a slot, leave the slot unused. Do not invent a target or ignore a printed restriction.

## 7. What a successful tutorial run demonstrates

You have learned the campaign when you can explain:

- why Rivendell → Moria requires Region Movement;
- why the route is declared as a site path after the new site is revealed;
- why Moria produces an eight-card solo Hazard draw but only a five-card base Hazard Limit;
- why entering Moria is a separate decision from arriving;
- why Goblin-gate's automatic attack is separate from the Hazard Limit;
- why a Haven destination uses the origin site's normal draw numbers;
- why the direct Moria-to-Blue Mountain route needs an intermediate site;
- why Book storage occurs in the next Organization Phase;
- why a positive corruption-check modifier helps and a negative one hurts;
- why the Book's stored value is 5 MP rather than 1 + 5.
