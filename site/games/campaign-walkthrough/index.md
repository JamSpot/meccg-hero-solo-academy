# Campaign Walkthrough — Rivendell to the Book of Mazarbul

**Use this after Game 1 and the Second Expedition workbook.** It joins the deck, the route, the Hazard side and the Book's storage into one campaign sequence you can follow with your physical cards.

This is a teaching walkthrough, not a scripted game result. Shuffle normally, use the cards you actually draw, roll your own dice and record what happens. No Hazard draw, combat result or successful storage check is predetermined.

## What rules are we using?

Keep these three layers separate:

1. **Official MECCG rules:** movement legality, site paths, normal card draws, combat, site entry, corruption and card text.
2. **Official solitaire procedure:** a separate Hazard deck, a five-card hand, and the solo Hazard draw and play instructions from the solitaire rules in *The Lidless Eye* rulebook.
3. **Academy mission:** the Dwarven Quest teaching lists adapted to one Gandalf-led Hero play deck, with acquiring and storing Book of Mazarbul as the tutorial objective.

The official solitaire objective is to accumulate as many marshalling points as possible by the time the play deck has been exhausted for the second time. The Book mission below is a **scenario objective for learning**, not a replacement for that general solitaire scoring rule.

Sources: [The Lidless Eye — Solitaire Rules](https://meccg.com/rules/by-expansion/the-lidless-eye/) and [Council of Elrond Official Game Rules](https://councilofelrond.org/rules/).

## Prepare the table

Use the fixed Academy lists:

- **Hero play deck — 36 cards:** 30 Resources, five non-Wizard characters (Balin, Boromir II, Celeborn, Fíli and Gildor Inglorion), and one Gandalf Wizard card. The 36-card Gandalf-led build is the active teaching version; do not add Pallando to it.
- **Hazard deck — 30 cards:** the separate Dwarven Quest / Challenge Deck C Hazard teaching list.
- **Location deck — 15 sites:** keep these separate and unshuffled.
- **Starting company:** Thorin II, Dori, Kíli, Glóin and Óin at Rivendell.
- **Starting items:** Cram with Thorin II and Cram with Óin.
- **Hand size:** five cards for the solitaire game.
- **Control:** Thorin II controls Dori and Kíli; Glóin controls Óin.

Keep Hero and Hazard discard piles separate. Do not stack the Hazard deck in a known order, select a convenient draw, or return unplayed drawn Hazards straight to the deck.

See [Hero Deck Construction]({{ '/decks/hero-deck/' | relative_url }}) and [Hazard Deck Construction]({{ '/decks/hazard-deck/' | relative_url }}) for the exact quantities.

## Turn 1 — Rivendell to Moria

### Mission

Acquire **Book of Mazarbul**. It is a unique Special Item with 2 corruption points. It is only playable at Moria, is worth 1 MP while in play, and may be stored at a Dwarf-hold for 5 MP. If a Sage bears it, the Book can be tapped during the Organization Phase to increase your hand size by one until your next Untap Phase.

### Organization Phase — choose the destination and movement

Declare Moria as the destination. Use **Region Movement** for this direct journey.

**Declared route:**

**Rhudaur → Hollin → Redhorn Gate**

Count both endpoints: three regions. Check every adjacency on the physical Region cards, confirm that no region repeats, and make sure the route is within the normal four-region maximum.

Why not direct Starter Movement? Moria lists Lórien as its nearest Haven, not Rivendell. The direct Rivendell-to-Moria journey is therefore not a legal Starter Movement. Region Movement is the appropriate teaching choice.

Do not declare the detailed site path yet. During Organization, declare the destination and movement; declare the Region Movement site path after the new site is revealed in the Movement/Hazard Phase.

### Movement/Hazard Phase — resolve the steps in order

1. **Reveal Moria.** Rivendell becomes the site of origin.
2. **Declare the site path.** For this Region Movement, name Rhudaur, Hollin and Redhorn Gate. All three are Wilderness.
3. **Set the base Hazard Limit.** The five-character company has a base Hazard Limit of five. It is fixed for this phase, even if a character is later eliminated.
4. **Apply ongoing effects.** Resolve any effects that already depend on this movement.
5. **Draw cards.** Moria's site card says the normal draw is **3 for the Hazard player and 2 for the Hero player**. In this solo procedure:
   - Hero player: draw up to **2** Resource cards normally.
   - Solo Hazard side: draw **2 + (2 × 3) = 8** Hazard cards.
6. **Resolve passive conditions.** Follow the normal rules and card text.
7. **Play Hazards.** Inspect all eight drawn cards. Check keying, target, requirements and timing. Play as many as possible within the five-slot Hazard Limit, choosing the most disadvantageous legal combination. Do not play an illegal card to fill a slot.
8. **End the phase.** Discard drawn Hazards that were not played. Reset the Hero hand to the solo hand size of five, following the solitaire procedure.

The site path matters. The three Wilderness regions can satisfy double-Wilderness requirements, and the named region Redhorn Gate can matter to a creature keyed by region name. Check each actual Hazard card; do not decide legality from theme alone.

### Site Phase — decide whether to enter Moria

Arrival and site entry are separate decisions.

If you choose **not** to enter, the Site Phase ends immediately. You avoid Moria's automatic attack, but you cannot attempt the Book or take other site actions.

If you choose to enter, face Moria's automatic attack:

**Orcs — four strikes with 7 prowess.**

This attack is separate from the Hazard Limit. It must be faced each time the company enters Moria, even if it was defeated on a previous visit.

After successfully entering and resolving any attacks, you may attempt to play Book of Mazarbul if the site and card conditions are satisfied. The site taps when the Special Item is successfully played.

### Turn 1 debrief

Record:

- Did you enter Moria?
- Which Hazards were legal on the declared path?
- Which five (or fewer) limit-counting plays did you choose, and why?
- Which characters were wounded or tapped?
- Who bears the Book, if acquired?
- Is the company strong enough to press on?

If the Book was not acquired, the mission remains **acquire the Book**. Do not jump ahead to the storage route.

## Turn 2 — if the Book was acquired: choose the next route

The Book was acquired during the Site Phase, so that turn's Organization Phase has already passed. You cannot store it or declare a new destination retroactively. At the next turn, untap and then choose one of the following routes during Organization.

### Route A — non-Haven waypoint through Goblin-gate

**Moria → Goblin-gate**

**Region path: Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur → High Pass**

Four regions, including origin and destination. Check all three adjacencies on the Region cards. This is within the normal Region Movement limit.

Goblin-gate is a non-Haven site. Its normal draw is **2 Hazard / 2 Resource**:

- Hero player: draw up to **2** Resource cards.
- Solo Hazard side: draw **2 + (2 × 2) = 6** Hazard cards.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.

If you enter Goblin-gate, face its automatic attack:

**Orcs — three strikes with 6 prowess.**

You may decide not to enter. That avoids the automatic attack, but you cannot attempt Gollum or take other Site Phase actions. You remain at Goblin-gate and can move again next turn.

### Route B — Haven recovery at Rivendell

**Moria → Rivendell**

**Region path: Redhorn Gate → Hollin → Rhudaur**

Three regions. Check the physical Region cards and the four-region limit.

Rivendell is a Haven, so the normal draw uses the **site of origin**, Moria:

- Hero player: draw up to Moria's Resource number, **2**.
- Solo Hazard side: use Moria's normal Hazard number, **3**, so draw **8** Hazard cards.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.

This is not a low-risk shortcut: the Hazard draw is based on Moria because Rivendell is the destination Haven. The benefit is the chance to recover at a Haven during a later Untap Phase. Compare the company's wounds, taps and remaining defensive Resources before choosing it.

## Turn 3 — reach Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold

### If you used Goblin-gate

**Goblin-gate → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold**

**Region path: High Pass → Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

Four regions, including both endpoints. Confirm each adjacency and do not repeat a region.

Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold is a non-Haven site. Its normal draw is **1 Hazard / 1 Resource**:

- Hero player: draw up to **1** Resource card.
- Solo Hazard side: draw **2 + (2 × 1) = 4** Hazard cards.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.

The Hero version of Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold has **no automatic attack**. The company still faces the four-card solo Hazard draw on arrival.

### If you used Rivendell

**Rivendell → Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold**

**Region path: Rhudaur → Arthedain → Númeriador**

Three regions. Check each adjacency and the endpoints.

Blue Mountain is a non-Haven destination, so its own normal draw numbers apply:

- Hero player: draw up to **1** Resource card.
- Solo Hazard side: draw **4** Hazard cards.
- Five-character base Hazard Limit: **5**.

The two routes have different costs. The Goblin-gate route avoids a Haven stop but exposes the company to another four-region Wilderness site path and an optional Orc automatic attack if it enters. The Rivendell route gives a recovery opportunity but the Moria-to-Haven movement still produces an eight-card solo Hazard draw.

## The next Organization Phase — store the Book

When the company is at Blue Mountain Dwarf-hold at the start of a later turn, you may attempt to store Book of Mazarbul during the Organization Phase.

You do **not** need to enter Blue Mountain during the arrival-turn Site Phase solely to store the Book later. The Book's own text allows it to be stored at a Dwarf-hold; Blue Mountain is the named Dwarf-hold even though its site type is Free-hold.

The bearer makes the required corruption check. The Book has 2 corruption points. Add any other corruption points on the bearer and apply all relevant modifiers.

- Gandalf has **+1** to his corruption checks. With only the Book's 2 CP, his minimum modified roll is 3, so he succeeds automatically.
- Thorin II and Glóin have no printed corruption-check modifier. With only the Book's 2 CP, they fail only on a natural 2.
- Dori, Kíli and Óin each have **-1** to their corruption checks. With only the Book's 2 CP, they fail on a natural 2 or 3.
- Other characters in the same company may tap to give +1 each to the check. Those characters will be tapped if you then move during the same turn.

If the check succeeds, place the Book in your marshalling-point pile for **5 MP**. Its 1 MP in-play value is replaced by the 5 MP stored value; do not add them together.

If the bearer is Gandalf, remember the Book's Sage ability: while it remains in play, Gandalf can tap the Book during the Organization Phase to increase the hand size by one until the next Untap Phase. Once stored, it is no longer in play and cannot provide that ability.

## After storage — continue the campaign

The Book mission is complete. Do not treat the campaign as over unless the scenario you chose says so.

Return to the Hero and Hazard deck plans and select the next objective that matches the company's condition and the cards in your hand. Possible Dwarven Quest directions include Iron Hill Dwarf-hold, The Lonely Mountain, Ovir Hollow or another site supported by the actual Resources you hold.

The official solitaire game continues toward its general scoring condition: accumulate as many marshalling points as possible before the play deck is exhausted for the second time. The Academy Book mission is a learning milestone within that larger solo framework.
