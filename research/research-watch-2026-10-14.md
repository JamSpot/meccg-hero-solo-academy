# Arda Research Watch — 14 October 2026

## Summary

No newly found comprehensive Hero Arda card pool or quantity spreadsheet supersedes the current baseline. The useful addition this pass is a solo-Arda construction discussion that can inform the Academy's setup guidance, plus confirmation that official-card Arda remains in active use.

## New or revalidated sources

- **My Solo Arda Build with Session Report (BoardGameGeek, 2013):** https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/ — solo construction discussion describing an eight-turn game aiming for 25 Marshaling Points and separate pools for Marshaling Point cards, resources, hazards, minor items, characters, rings, and sites. Treat this as a design reference, not an authoritative inventory; it uses experimental rules and does not list every card quantity.
- **Gen Con 2026 MECCG Arda event:** https://www.gencon.com/ — indicates current organized play for Arda and a preference for official versions of cards. Event evidence is not a card list.
- **MeCCG lists and downloads:** https://www.meccg.net/ — useful source for card lists, character lists, and Challenge Deck material. These validate card identity but do not establish Arda membership or quantities.
- **BoardGameGeek MECCG files:** https://boardgamegeek.com/ — includes Arda examples, Hero Arda ruleset documents, and solo resources. Useful references, not a replacement master pool.
- **Council of Elrond card database:** https://github.com/council-of-elrond-meccg/meccg-cards-database — cards.json and changes guide are useful for card identity, text, and rulings; keep these fields separate from Arda membership and quantities.

## Quantity baseline retained

The explicit ARDA quantities in the original `GandalfGeneArda.xls` remain the authoritative baseline for this source:

| Expansion | Physical cards |
|---|---:|
| METW — The Wizards | 552 |
| METD — The Dragons | 105 |
| MEDM — Dark Minions | 41 |
| MEBA — The Balrog | 1 |
| **Total with explicit quantities** | **699** |

The source has 377 card rows; 375 rows have explicit ARDA quantities. Those 375 rows contain 374 unique names because Nenseldë the Wingild appears twice. Army of the Dead and The White Tree have blank ARDA quantities and must remain blank until evidence is found. The published summary total of 695 is not supported by the explicit quantities. The separate Desglose sheet sums to 691.

## Decision / next action

Do not continue spending research time on the already-reconciled 377/374 or 699/695 discrepancy. Build the master database around the 699 explicit quantity baseline, retain source provenance, and distinguish:
1. card identity and current text;
2. Arda membership evidence;
3. quantity evidence by expansion/source;
4. unknown or blank quantities.

Then use that database to publish usable physical Hero, Hazard, and resource lists and deck-construction instructions for the Academy. No user decision is required at this stage.
